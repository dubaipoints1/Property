  all-spend rankings. Conflicting product/booklet or bilingual rates remain held.
- ENBD and ADCB LuLu reward units are differently denominated. A shared programme
  name does not justify one conversion for both. The general valuation policy
  has not been silently changed.
- Joining, renewal, promotional waivers, VAT and issuer-only FX charges remain
  separate. FAB/SC upper bounds are not fixed all-in conversion rates.
- Ended voucher campaigns, restricted hotel certificates and talabat credit are
  not current unrestricted cash or transferable-points bonuses.
- Salary-or-credit-limit routes do not establish universal salary-transfer,
  residency or employment conditions. Original protected records are retained

- All11ADCB raw profiles lack fully verified typed benefit records. Public
  summaries say Pending, not None. This does not assert that the cards lack perks.

  percentage is held while VAT/scheme treatment remains unresolved.
- Eight dependent guides and curated comparisons remove obsolete fixed annual

fees/VAT cannot become zero-cost net rankings, break-even values or Product

or bonuses; manually authored chart amounts cannot bypass the source holds.
