## 3. ENBD — claims verified

All PASS against T&C clause numbers and the landing page:

- **Dates and figures.** Period 25 Sep–24 Dec 2026, both dates inclusive (T&C 2). "Up to AED 6,000" (landing-page meta). Table A / B / A+B: 1,500 / 500 / 2,000 and 2,000 / 4,000 / 6,000 (T&C 6a, 6b).
- **Account.** New-to-bank or new to liabilities (5a); first-ever account (4e); opened during the period (4b); existing products not considered (7e).
- **Salary.** Minimum AED 15,000 including fixed allowances, within 90 days (5c). WPS or UAEFTS only, with no cash, cheque or personal transfers (8l). Pension salaries count (8m). The approved-employer list appears on the landing page only and is attributed to it.
- **Card.** New card within 90 days (5d); AED 5,000 spend within 60 days of issue (5e); exclusions (7a, subset listed).
- **Loan or wealth product (6b).** The 90-day window is on the landing page only and is attributed to it.
- **Payout and clawbacks.** Payout after 150 calendar days (7b). Reversal without 12 consecutive salaries, from any account or card (8e). The 180-day closure rule (8f). One bonus only (7d, 8b). No other offers once paid (7c).

Fixes needed:

- "Be new to ENBD" wrongly excludes customers who are new to liabilities (E3).
- 150 days is a floor, not a date (E4).
- The 180-day clock runs from account opening (E5).
- The unconditional clause 8c is missing (E6).
