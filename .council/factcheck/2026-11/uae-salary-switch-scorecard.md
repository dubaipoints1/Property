# Fact check — UAE Salary Switch Scorecard

**Verdict: pass, 2026-11-04.** Independent Head of Research and Fact-Checker review of the amended article. [Issuer dossier](../../research/2026-11/uae-salary-switch-scorecard-dossier.md).

- Emirates Islamic: AED 1,000 account-only for the stated case, with the two named charges only; optional AED 500 requires qualifying retail spend of AED 12,000 within 60 days. The amount, enrolment, qualifying salary, timing and possible reversal agree with issuer evidence. Opening-fee evidence is the account KFS.
- FAB: the source illustration for AED 20,000 is AED 334 per eligible month; `334 × 12 = 4,008` and `334 × 11 = 3,674`. The article names the incremental balance or new-card test, payout timing, and forfeiture, without treating the illustration as approval or net profit.
- DIB: home-finance route has no net estimate. The 90-day and 60-day clocks are for the eligible valuation-fee reversal, not the joining-bonus payment. No unverified finance charges, bank approval claim, advertising or affiliate links.
