# Fee tax basis and Finder reward evidence

## Reader question

Does the annual fee already include VAT, and is there enough evidence for this card to match the rewards preference or fee ceiling I selected?

## Scope

Use the existing fee tax-basis field to label quotes that exclude VAT on card tiles, comparisons, review and fee surfaces. The Fees index displays such quotes without treating them as like-for-like samples. Do not publish an all-in offer price for a pre-tax quote. Preserve the distinction between an annual waiver and the total first-year cost.

Only use a known reward type for a Finder preference match. Retain explicit legacy cashback classification when the label is blank, not a cashback assumption for an otherwise unconfirmed card. Keep unconfirmed rates and salary qualification out of results that depend on those inputs. Server and browser use the same price filter evidence. Do not change the homepage ranking, underlying card data, tracking, or other releases.

## Acceptance

- Covered and unknown values remain distinct: a confirmed zero fee is not pending; a confirmed pre-tax fee is neither pending nor an all-in price.
- A pre-tax renewal remains labelled in compact tiles, review and Compare, and is excluded from fee statistics and Finder price comparisons.
- Missing or held rewards cannot earn a cashback/points/miles preference boost or a confirmed mismatch label. Known cards and default ordering continue to work.
- Require independent staged review, tests and a hosted check before any deployment. The three NBK issuer profiles are a separate scope, not approved or added by this brief.
