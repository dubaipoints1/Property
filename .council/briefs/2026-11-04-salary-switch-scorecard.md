## Source and arithmetic checks — 2026-11-04

- [Emirates Islamic terms](https://www.emiratesislamic.ae/en/terms-and-conditions/salary-transfer-offer-terms-and-conditions-oct-2026): CASA and Covered Card tables, non-national row `15,000 — 34,999`, read separately: `1,000` and `500` with `12,000` spend. Published sum `1,500` is simple addition, not a calculator output or estimate of net card value. Eligibility, fulfilment and reversal sections govern timing. The [account KFS, p3](https://www.emiratesislamic.ae/-/media/ei/pdfs/Key-Information/kfs_accounts_ea.pdf) lists free opening; the [account tariff](https://www.emiratesislamic.ae/en/soc/accounts) and KFS state the salary-transfer exclusion for the monthly balance fee. Only those charges are counted; no assumed closure, other service charges or financing. The existing campaign entry and `scoreOffer` distinguish the optional component.
- [FAB promotion](https://www.bankfab.com/en-ae/personal/promotions/20-percent-cashback-on-salary-transfers) links the [controlling PDF](https://www.bankfab.com/-/media/fab-uds/personal/promotions/2026/20-percent-cashback-on-salary-transfers/terms-and-conditions-en.pdf). Section 5 contains the issuer’s exact row `20,000 4,000 334 4,008`, also preserved in `.council/research/2026-10/salary-transfer-source-sweep-2026-10-13.md`. `334 × 11 = 3,674`. Section 5 specifies the increment from the previous month-end, qualifying new card, timing and forfeiture; §6 gives the terms precedence. This is an exact-salary worked example only; `scoreOffer` must still leave FAB conditional/unpriced because it cannot verify the monthly tests.
- [DIB XTRA terms](https://www.dib.ae/docs/default-source/pdf/dib-xtra-tc.pdf), §§2.1 and 4.13: require a qualifying bundle for the joining bonus and distinguish the eligible home-finance evaluation fee reversal. No fixed cash figure or finance quote. The 90/60-day home-finance milestones are distinct from the joining-bonus timetable. `scoreOffer` with a mortgage selection remains conditional/unpriced.
