# Is a salary-transfer bonus worth moving your payroll for?

## The reader question

Should I move my salary for this reward, given the conditions, account costs
and time before I can use the money? Help a UAE resident decide whether to
proceed, ask the bank a specific question or leave payroll where it is. A large
advertised maximum is not the answer.

## Why now

The owner commissioned a practical salary-bonus brief on 8 November 2026.
Our [payout-calendar article](/news/uae-salary-switch-payout-calendar/) already
separates entry, qualification and payment. This commission must go further:
compare the reward the reader could qualify for with the extra obligations
and costs of switching. It is not another calendar or a rewrite of the
[same-salary scorecard](/news/uae-salary-switch-scorecard/).

No new campaign facts have been verified for this commission. The target date
is provisional planning, contingent on current source capture and every
publication gate; an offer may change or close before then.

## The reporting commission

Ask Head of Research for current primary-source captures before drafting.
Choose campaigns only after establishing that new applicants can still enter.
Compare like-for-like salary and product choices using the existing model;
do not invent a customer, bank conversation, payout experience or quoted reply.
If a claim needs a bank clarification, commission a dated, attributable reply
through an authorised contact route; absent a reply, mark it unresolved.

For each selected campaign, capture a source-to-claim record covering:

- Application route, campaign start/end and which event must occur by the
  deadline; residence, new-customer lookback and employer/payroll eligibility.
- Gross versus credited salary definition, required credits, optional versus
  mandatory card or finance, qualifying spend/savings, caps and exclusions.
- Qualification trigger, payment window, ongoing retention and clawback;
  distinguish calendar days, working days and calendar months exactly.
- Account/card charges with VAT treatment, waivers and waiver conditions,
  minimum-balance requirements, closure/cancellation charges and timing.
- The comparison horizon and what is excluded. Unpriced finance, borrowing
  costs or unknown fees cannot become zero or a claimed net benefit.

Retain exact wording, document version, URL, capture time in Asia/Dubai, full
source text and page/line anchors. Typed financial numerics must come through
the deterministic parser/provenance process, not LLM extraction. Where the
existing model cannot represent a fact, stop for review rather than silently
adding a formula or changing canonical offers.

## Reader flow and existing interaction

Open with a short conditional verdict: worth considering if the verified
conditions fit; not worth changing payroll simply to chase a maximum.
Then present an eligibility checkpoint, a compact benefits/costs/unknowns
comparison and a short next-action list. Keep unknowns visible.

Use the existing [salary-transfer calculator](/salary-transfer/calculator/)
to let readers change salary and optional products. Explain that its reward
estimate is conditional, not approval, a guaranteed payment or a full net-cost
calculator. Reuse `SalaryTransferTimeline` and its verified offer adapter only
when the selected campaign still passes the existing timeline guard. Do not
copy dated timetable prose or calculate a personalised payday.

Close with a plain copyable checklist: confirm eligibility in writing; check
payroll cut-off; save the applicable terms; price unavoidable charges; confirm
what could reverse the reward. The checklist is advice, not a new application
form. Link to [Card Finder](/cards/finder/) only where a required card needs a
separate suitability check. No new input collection, storage or tracking.
