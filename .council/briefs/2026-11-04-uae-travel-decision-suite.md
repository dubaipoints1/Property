# UAE travel decision suite

Reader question: should I pay cash or miles for this flight, can I get the miles from the rewards I hold, and will my card still be worthwhile after its first year?

## Build and evidence

- **Cash or Miles:** a standalone calculator and an expandable version in the Skywards and Etihad Guest guides. Reader-entered, comparable, all-in AED quotes only. Integer fils arithmetic; optional personal target, not a universal redemption value. The tool prices neither seat availability nor earned rewards on the paid fare. Inputs stay on the page.
- **UAE Transfer Finder:** exact issuer rewards currencies and routes to Skywards, Etihad Guest and Qatar Privilege Club. Parse ratios, minimums, posting windows and bonus dates from short, linked primary excerpts, not competitor data. Distinguish base rates from bonuses and withhold conflicting or unpublished values. The existing 2026 guide keeps its URL and teaches the decision; its stale, competing rate table is retired.
- **Card value:** same eligible everyday UAE spend in Finder and Compare, modelled against the current structured card terms, caps and published valuation registry. Year-one and standard renewal amounts are separate; contingent waivers and welcome offers are not assumed. Unknown rates, conditions or fees can withhold an estimate. Existing card eligibility and ranking logic are unchanged.
- **Travel evidence:** a small, explicit evidence box for priced-not-booked, hypothetical and announcement-only coverage. The Etihad article archives the exact quote lines from the public booking screens, uses matching flight/cabin/passengers/baggage and says where change terms differ. No booking, login, account information or live fares API. Existing hypothetical examples and the flydubai Skopje announcement are labelled distinctly.
