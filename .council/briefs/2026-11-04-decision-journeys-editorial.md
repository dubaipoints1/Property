# Decision journeys and original reporting

- Correct gross annual-fee VAT labels without changing the stored Citi or ADIB amounts. For Citi, retain the first-year and spend-waiver distinction, put the issuer source beside the summary and attribute the welcome credit to the route verified. An unverified referral route must not inherit those terms.
- Keep the default Finder useful for public applicants. Its visual shortlist, DOM and keyboard order, and Compare action should agree. Put the selected comparison first; keep curated examples as secondary navigation.

## Source proof and acceptance

Spend-waiver threshold is inclusive. The welcome-credit figure is the product page's advertised offer, not an affiliate rate.
- [ADIB product page](https://www.adib.ae/en/personal/cards/cashback-visa-card/adib-cashback-visa-covered-card) and [issuer schedule](https://www.adib.ae/-/media/project/adib/adibsite/docs/personal/cards/adib-covered-cards-schedule-of-charges-and-fees.pdf): AED 99 base; existing AED 103.95 is the gross amount. Only the display basis changes.
- [Etihad Credit Bureau](https://etihadbureau.ae/en/Individual/CreditReport) supplies credit-report scope, not lender approval thresholds. Do not treat examples as underwriting guidance.
- Verify source, offer channel, fee tiles, keyboard order, selected cards, compact deal listing, issuer link/code placement, build and internal links. Require actual Council and Linux validation runs and read back the deployed site. Keep product terms, sponsor placements, analytics and bot-exclusion settings out of scope unless separately approved.

## Editorial queue (proposals, not scheduled or published)

1. Card Change Log: one material issuer-document change per edition; preserve source dates, before/after terms, audience and one worked impact. If older terms cannot be proven, label the first observation a baseline.
2. Salary Switch Scorecard: a worked comparison of a few already-tracked, re-verified offers. Show account-only vs required spending, deposit or finance; when a payout is due; fees only when verified; and what still needs confirmation. Reuse the Tracker/Calculator rules and do not create a second offer dataset.
3. Evergreen worked examples: break-even between a fee-paying and fee-free cashback card, and common ways a salary-transfer reward can fail. Check for overlap first; expand the closest existing guide rather than add a duplicate.
4. Suggested cadence: one substantive item per week on site and existing RSS. No newsletter, new automation, automated publishing, paid imagery, or paid search.
