# Competitor depth, card breadth and news operations

## Owner request and boundaries

Mostafa supplied LoyaltyLobby, Head for Points, The Points Guy, Visit Dubai's
Dubai Access Club, and two YallaCompare entry points on 2026-11-07. He asks
for one dedicated agent per website, comprehensive links/structure/content/
sublink review against DubaiPoints, substantially broader card coverage and
expanded, monitored, automated news. This supplements the authorised loading,
card and native utility work; it does not waive its test-before-live gates.

Five dedicated site reviewers will be queued within available concurrency;
the two YallaCompare URLs are one website assignment, not two independent
competitors. Each reviewer owns only its assigned site. Root synthesises the
cross-site comparison and inspects DubaiPoints' actual code/live state.

No claim that every historical article, authenticated interaction or dynamic
result has been inspected. The deliverable includes an observed URL inventory,
page-family map, representative meaningful sublinks, access dates, blocked/
unexamined areas and a feature-by-feature existing/partial/missing matrix.
Do not confuse an indexed snippet with a full page or source currency.

## Dedicated review assignments

1. LoyaltyLobby — programme promotions, news cadence, lifecycle and community.
2. Head for Points — editorial navigation, programme/credit-card journeys,
   guides, reviews, readers and monetisation.
3. The Points Guy — card/programme hierarchy, comparison/tools, content
   templates, trust/commercial separation and visual journeys.
4. Visit Dubai / Dubai Access Club — visitor/deal/experience discovery and
   programme positioning, with membership/access conditions kept explicit.
5. YallaCompare — both supplied URLs, UAE issuer/product taxonomy, discovery/
   comparison/application paths and public editorial/commercial disclosures.

## Common comparison contract

For each site record navigation/footer hierarchy, content families, observed
internal links and templates, card/product coverage semantics, news/deals
taxonomy and freshness, useful tools, UX/imagery/spacing, responsive and
interaction observations only where actually inspected, commercial/trust
signals, and applicable UAE adaptations. Distinguish observed fact, site
self-claim and practitioner inference. Prioritise P0/P1/P2 by reader benefit,
coverage and maintenance cost. Do not copy competitor prose, art, code or
rankings. Do not duplicate controls DubaiPoints already has.

## Card and news gates

Competitor product listings discover candidates; only current issuer sources
can substantiate financial fields. Preserve deterministic regex provenance,
VAT/unit/cap/eligibility scope, active versus archived and charge versus credit
distinctions. Unknown figures are not zeros; a larger headline count is not
the acceptance criterion. Produce an issuer/product coverage ledger with
captured, reconciled, qualified, held and blocked states.

Audit existing twice-daily free RSS discovery, press-room monitoring, digest
issues, routing/deduplication/expiry controls and editorial throughput before
adding another feed or worker. Propose automation for discovery, triage,
source/evidence packaging, draft preparation and expiry/correction alerts.
Publication remains subject to source/fact/editorial/Council checks; no silent
autopublication, scraping-policy bypass, paid provider activation or monitor
provisioning. Any new paid operation requires a specific quote and approval.
