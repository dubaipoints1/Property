## Authority and objective

Mostafa: “Do a whole review. We can change if we want even the home page. Animation”.
This opens the homepage to review and prototypes and supersedes the earlier
preservation restriction for exploration only. Current delivered tokens, type,

## Three rendered choices

1. **Editorial Atlas — recommended.** Image-led arrival, reporting before tools,
   numbered optional previews separate from one-tap headline links, compact task
   doors and useful offer/guide shelves. Calm article/tool continuity.
2. **Runway Journal.** Panoramic travel arrival and ordinary-scroll chapters.
   Strong travel identity; a longer opening risks repeating today's scroll toll.
3. **Decision Desk.** Task-led disclosures and contained real issuer artwork.
   Strong for card/salary intent, weaker as the sole daily editorial front door.

Private routes: `/dev/21st-site-directions/` and `/dev/whole-site-review/`.

21st references are inspiration, not downloaded source or licence clearance.

## Motion hierarchy and exclusions

- Immediate button, focus, selected-state and disclosure feedback.
- Optional reader-selected media transition, with direct destination links.
- Quiet current-section feedback, reusing existing reading navigation.
- A bounded photographic arrival, then ordinary native scrolling.
- Optional About/editorial special: never required to read or use a tool.

No autoplay rail, cursor chase, scroll locking, nested article scroller,
animated financial figures, fictional testimonials/metrics or decorative motion
on every paragraph. Private prototypes cancel tracked motion when reduced-motion
becomes active; source guards are not a runtime preference-change certificate.

## Phased plan and acceptance

1. **Correctness and measurement:** review/fix unit-mixed homepage ranking,
   calculator reset/bounds/readiness, expiry/freshness delivery and privacy-safe
   analytics reporting. Keep Google settings unchanged absent explicit approval.
2. **Choose and pilot:** Chairman selects a rendered composition and separately
   decides whether palette/type exploration is wanted. Pilot homepage plus one
   news/article journey and one decision journey in both themes.
3. **Family rollout:** apply the chosen hierarchy through all 19 families using
   shared templates and the route ledger; preserve earlier programme/hand-off fixes.
4. **Acceptance and release:** full build/tests/static-route/link checks, rendered
   phone/tablet/desktop/short landscape, keyboard/touch, no-JavaScript,
   reduced-motion including live changes, zoom/CLS, physical iPhone Safari and
   actual Council review before a protected normal merge and live verification.

Do not mix the three motion systems throughout the site or declare the whole
site QA-closed from a representative sample. Commercial activation, paid assets,
new analytics collection and publication require their respective authority.
