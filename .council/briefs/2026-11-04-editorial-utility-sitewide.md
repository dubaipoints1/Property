# Editorial Utility across DubaiPoints

Reader question: can I tell what this page is for, find the relevant answer or next action, and check the evidence without working through a large banner or a dense block of controls?

The Chairman selected Editorial Utility from rendered phone and desktop comparisons with Travel Magazine. Borrow the layout ideas of an asymmetric lead and dated news rows from [The Points Guy](https://thepointsguy.com/) and [Head for Points](https://www.headforpoints.com/), not their identity, photography, rankings, language or US/UK assumptions. Keep the established DubaiPoints homepage hero, type, palette and editorial voice. Reserve prominent photography for a useful visual subject with an approved asset.

## Template requirements

- **Shared shell:** no menu interactions lost; a keyboard skip link; visible focus; readable footer groups; aligned phone, tablet and desktop navigation. Search and not-found pages point readers to a next step.
- **News, guides and deals:** let readers reach the latest story, the first useful paragraph or the offer terms quickly. Keep dates, original sources, bylines, related tools and approved image attribution. Never call a chronological list “trending”. A real photograph can feature a travel story; a text-led item does not need decorative artwork.
- **Programmes, banks and cards:** useful facts beside or directly below a clear title; tools, eligibility, expiry and sources remain findable. Preserve licensed issuer artwork and exact issuer/offer references. Do not generate brand images or invent facts to fill an empty panel.
- **Finder, Compare, Salary Tracker and calculators:** bring results and primary fields forward; move optional filters into labelled disclosures where useful. Preserve formulas, ranking, fee treatment, eligibility and source links. Save no reader inputs beyond the existing behaviour.
- **Shared controls:** primary (one obvious next action), secondary (e.g. reset), quiet (low-emphasis navigation), field and filter. Use theme tokens for normal, hover, focus, selected and disabled states; selection is not colour alone. Aim for 44 CSS px on primary controls. Keep non-control text links recognisable.

## Verification

Check every generated route for horizontal reflow at 320, 390, 721, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920 CSS px, and local image sources for missing files. Review representative phone and desktop screens for each family; inspect dark mode, keyboard/focus, retained source and image credit, matching or empty tool states, and figures unchanged. Record only observed outcomes. Require genuine Council and Linux PR checks and verify the Cloudflare production deploy and live pages before calling the release live.

Not in scope: new analytics, ads, affiliate placements, scraping, paid imagery, publication of new financial terms, replacement of the homepage hero or hosting changes.
