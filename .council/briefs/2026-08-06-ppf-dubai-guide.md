# Brief — PPF category guide: costs in AED, warranty mechanics, who we'd use

**Date:** 2026-08-06
**Tier:** T3 (new guide page; widens the motoring bounded category by one
piece on explicit Chairman direction)

## Origin and scope ruling

The owner/Chairman asked whether to publish "a post on Modcare" or expand
the PPF block in `guides/expat-starter-car-costs`. Editorial advice was
that a vendor profile fails §10 1a (it would imply vetting depth that a
disclosed first-hand recommendation does not carry) and that the reader-
first version is a **category guide** — PPF costs, coverage, warranty
mechanics, installer questions — in which Modcare remains the disclosed
recommendation. Chairman direction, 6 August: "Do the PPF guide."

This widens the motoring **bounded category** (ratified with PR #309) by
one page. It does not create a motoring beat.

## COI and recommendation basis — carried over, unchanged

Identical to `2026-08-05-uae-car-running-costs-guide.md`: no payment, no
affiliate arrangement, no ownership interest. The recommendation rests on
the editor's first-hand experience as a paying customer, is disclosed
inline **above** the recommendation block (ordering per the 5 August
ruling), and explicitly states it is not a comparative test. Both Modcare
domains still serve `robots: noindex` (re-confirmed on the 6 August
fetch), so outbound links confer nothing; the guide's search value
accrues to dubaipoints.ae.

**§10 1a guard for this piece:** two other installers are named because
they publish prices. They are cited as published-price reference points
only, with an explicit statement that we have not used or tested them.
Naming them is what keeps the piece a market guide rather than an advert.
