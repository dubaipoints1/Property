# Reliable monitoring and card-source maintenance

## The reader question

Can DubaiPoints identify source changes and missed checks without presenting
unchecked data as current? The immediate user-facing value is dependable
maintenance of the existing site, followed by three useful article briefs.

## Authority and release condition

Mostafa's 2026-11-08 direction authorises the named implementation and normal
protected release only after testing and substantive Council sign-off. This is
not a waiver, paid-service approval or article-publication approval. The target
date is a planning target, not a promise to bypass a failed check.

## Why now

Read-only investigation found credit-starved checks omitted by the existing
poller, salary digests without a workflow delivery step and swallowed targeted
scrape-dispatch failures. Seventeen newer partial profiles deliberately follow
manual-primary maintenance; that coverage must remain explicit.

## Out of scope

No new financial figures, source-date promotion, article publication, paid
scrape activation, paid monitor/cadence/target expansion or new UI runtime.

## Done when

1. Realistic failure/partial/pagination/delivery/dispatch regressions exercise
   the production code, preserve retryable work and distinguish collection
   success from polling attempts.
2. Every current card has a deterministic coverage disposition; automated and
   manual routes, missing coverage, source/pin integrity and overdue reviews
   are visible without pretending every field is verified.
3. Three distinct unpublished article briefs specify source reading, current
   dates/terms, original comparison, existing interactive tool and fact-check/
   Council gates. No public article route is created.
4. Check, full tests, build and scoped independent Council review pass on the
   exact release candidate; normal protected CI/merge/deployment is observed.
5. Existing financial data, public routes, homepage/UI and spend controls are
   unchanged except where an explicitly reviewed repair is necessary.
