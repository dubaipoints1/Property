# Editorial imagery and the UAE reader proposition

## Scoped release

The image inventory reached 315 public HTML routes; 475 distinct on-page, same-origin image URLs passed HTTP, type and file-signature checks. The three affected News cards used generic title-only covers, not broken images. Replace those listing-only covers with a Cash or Miles pictogram, existing issuer-sourced Citi art, and a previously cleared calculator photo for the Salary Switch Scorecard. Where neither cleared art nor an assigned visual exists, use a compact text card. Keep the three articles' lightweight reading template. Use existing source-cleared programme photos on the hotel directory; retire the former Berlin Sofitel from live ALL imagery. Use exact issuer art in Compare where available, keep honest no-art states, and show the already-required Modcare credit beside the guide stage. No homepage, financial data, new tracking, paid imagery, sponsor placements or issuer/application changes.

## Positioning

Make DubaiPoints the independent first stop for UAE residents deciding which card fits them, how to earn and use points, what a flight or hotel costs in AED, and which deal is still worth acting on. Authority is earned through evidence and repeat use; it is not a claim that no other source exists. Keep the recognisable homepage. On interior pages, place one answer and its terms near the top, use compact purposeful visuals and comparison tables, and offer clear next steps instead of long detours or decorative full-screen images.

- [The Points Guy’s methodology](https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/points-miles-valuations-methodology) separates observed data from editorial estimates, and its [publishing standards](https://thepointsguy.com/legal/tpg-publishing-standards) explain disclosure and editorial independence. Adopt the evidence and transparency, not US card assumptions or advertising density.
- [Head for Points’ Skywards example](https://www.headforpoints.com/2026/01/23/redeem-emirates-skywards-miles-for-jet2-flights/) walks through a priced example and flags uncertainty. Do this for comparable itineraries from DXB, AUH or SHJ, recording total cost, miles, taxes, cabin and date; label hypothetical cases distinctly.
- [MoneyLuna](https://www.moneyluna.com/post/best-uae-salary-transfer-offers), [CreditSouq](https://creditsouq.ae/how-it-works/) and [Tilla](https://tilla.ae/) already cover pieces of this journey. Compete by connecting sourced eligibility, card value, redemption and current deals, not by claiming calculators or salary tables are unique.

## 30 / 60 / 90 days — proposals, not a publishing schedule

1. **Days 1–30:** Repair the visual system and verify the highest-value guides and deal terms. Use the corrected Skywards guide as the starting pattern: label editorial estimates, show the arithmetic, and separate programme benefits from paid products. Prioritise verified publication/expiry timestamps, conditions and next actions across Cards, Points & Miles and Deals.
2. **Days 31–60:** Build on the Card Change Log, Cash or Miles and Salary Switch Scorecard with one original, source-led story a week. Link each story to the relevant Finder, Calculator, Tracker, programme or live offer; route updates through the existing site and RSS. Expand into UAE-origin airport, lounge and hotel decisions only where there is an actionable takeaway.
3. **Days 61–90:** Compare like-for-like Cloudflare periods in Dubai time with bots excluded, keeping visits and page views distinct from unique visitors. Where available, use Search Console UAE non-brand query/page impressions and clicks, and measure which deal pages are current and source-complete. Aggregate reports do not establish individual journeys. Expand formats with evidence of demand.

Affiliate revenue remains a separate decision. Pilot one disclosed issuer-direct placement only once written terms and applicable approvals are in place. Keep rankings and the direct alternative independent of commission; keep application data with the issuer. Separate clicks, partner-validated outcomes and cash received. Do not add affiliate links, third-party tags or display ads in this release.
