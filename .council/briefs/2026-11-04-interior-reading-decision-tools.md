# Interior reading and decision tools

## Reader problem and visual comparison

Production browser review at a 1280 × 720 viewport: the Skywards photo header occupies most of the first screen, with the overview below it and a dense duplicate expiry policy in the right column. The long lounge-access guide devotes nearly the whole viewport to headline and deck; the Citi review’s photo and terms matter, but the hero's spacing delays the rest of the review. Review the shared interior shells and leave the homepage unchanged.

- [The Points Guy’s Skywards guide](https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/emirates-skywards) separates summary, considerations and detailed sections. Adopt the early answer and separate reading path, not the advert gap or commercial rail.
- [Head for Points’ Skywards article](https://www.headforpoints.com/2026/01/23/redeem-emirates-skywards-miles-for-jet2-flights/) makes a narrow central column and a worked calculation easy to find. Use the narrow measure and AED-first calculation, not its advertising or a copied layout.
- [MoneySavingExpert](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/banking/compare-best-bank-accounts/) sequences eligibility, actions and payout. Apply that sequence to UAE employer payroll without importing the UK switch guarantee. [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/credit-cards/methodology) explains rating inputs; use actual Finder filters and published card terms, with no prediction of approval.

## Release boundary

- Compact photo stages for programmes and editorial articles, shorter card-review padding, readable shared prose and native contents controls. Skywards puts earning, spending and expiry first; the programme template has a compact linked reference rail, with full policy accessible in the main flow. Retain existing navigation, canonical paths, design tokens and licensed photography. Do not retrofit every legacy story by hand.
- Salary Tracker and current bank pages: date/condition/payout/retention separately, with a published-terms fallback rather than inferred campaign promises. Reuse campaign records and history. The homepage layout stays unchanged; the existing Live Desk label uses the same confirmed deadline treatment. No reward calculations or eligibility decisions change.
- Card Finder: show the specific matching inputs and issuer checks. Do not change ranking, filters or shared links.
- Cash or Miles: one text-led, dated launch issue. Clearly identify hypothetical inputs unless a like-for-like public cash and award quote can be verified. Resolve the site’s conflicting descriptions of its existing baseline, without changing a rate. See the [issuer dossier](../research/2026-11/cash-or-miles-uae-flight-break-even-dossier.md).

## Acceptance gates

Inspect representative programme, hotel, guide, card, deal, news and tool routes on mobile and desktop; verify narrow reflow across the complete static site, keyboard-accessible disclosures and unobscured fragment targets. Keep figures, fees, charges, permissions, homepage and existing commercial/analytics configuration separate. Require genuine Council/CI and Cloudflare preview before normal merge. Verify production and both RSS feeds for the new issue. Readership comparisons, if made, use bot-excluded Cloudflare views and visits, not unique humans or approvals.
