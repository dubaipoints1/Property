## Pilot choice and launch decision

Prepare one issuer-direct **Citi UAE / Citi Cash Back** enquiry and, only if
approved later, one clearly disclosed contextual placement on the existing
card review. Keep the homepage, rankings, calculations and editorial verdict
unchanged. Do not monetise every bank link or turn the site into a lead form.

This is an operational pilot choice: the site already has the review,
issuer-source dossier and application-channel analysis. It is **not** a
judgement that Citi is the best card, the highest-paying partner or more
likely to accept DubaiPoints. No publisher commission or approval has been
verified.

**Current decision: do not launch.** Citi's live product page distinguishes
its general direct-website welcome offer from arrivals through third-party
links. A publisher link must not inherit the general offer by assumption.
Even an untracked link from DubaiPoints is a third-party route; do not label
it as bonus-eligible merely because it is non-commercial. Obtain written
campaign-specific terms and recheck the reader outcome before placement.
[Official Citi Cash Back product terms](https://www.citibank.ae/credit-cards/cashback/citi-cashback-credit-card),
read 2026-11-05, welcome-offer bullets.

## Written questions the agreement must answer

These are internal launch controls and negotiation questions, not statements
that a particular law or bank contract already requires each item.

| Area | Exact question / required written answer |
|---|---|
| Contracting parties | Which Citi UAE entity or authorised intermediary would contract, and which DubaiPoints legal entity may participate? Who has authority to approve the arrangement? |
| Publisher eligibility | Are independent editorial publishers accepted? Which business registration, licensing, residence, website ownership, audience and due-diligence evidence is required? Do not assume a personal banking referral programme permits publication-scale use. |
| Campaign and product | Which exact product, territory, audience, currency and approved destination are covered? What are the campaign start/end and withdrawal mechanics? |
| Reader offer and channel | What customer offer applies to the dedicated publisher destination? Does a third-party arrival affect the general direct-website offer? Which offers cannot be combined? Provide a current written comparison of relevant fees, eligibility, rewards and conditions. |
| Payable event | Is payment triggered by a validated lead, submitted application, approval, activation, spending condition or another event? Which conditions must all be met? No event or amount is agreed yet. |
| Commercial schedule | What is the fee/commission, denomination, tax treatment, invoice process, threshold, settlement timetable and dispute process? Supply a signed schedule; a directory headline is not a rate card. |
| Attribution | Which link/sub-ID identifies this approved placement, what attribution model/window applies, how are duplicate applications and other channels handled, and can privacy refusals break attribution? |
| Validation and reversals | What makes an event invalid or reversible? What validation status and evidence can the publisher see, what is the dispute window, and how are post-payment reversals handled? |
| Permitted activity | Are on-site editorial links permitted? Are search adverts, brand bidding, social posts, email, messaging, incentives, coupons, sub-publishers, redirects or browser extensions prohibited or approval-gated? The pilot proposes on-site editorial only. |
| Claims and creative | Which wording, disclosures, product/brand images and trademark uses are approved? Who supplies current product terms and change notices? Can factual reviews remain independent, including an unfavourable verdict? |
| Data and privacy | Which party receives applicant information and acts in each data role? What cookies, redirects, identifiers, consent signals, cross-border flows, retention, deletion and incident processes are involved? Can the pilot operate without DubaiPoints collecting personal application data? |
| Reporting | What aggregate partner reports and campaign identifiers are available? Can they distinguish attributed applications, approvals, validation, reversals and payable commission without returning applicant identities? |
| Suspension and exit | How can either party pause a link, correct an offer, remove assets or terminate? What happens to already attributed applications and outstanding validated payments? |
| Applicable permissions | Which advertising, financial-promotion, privacy and other applicable permissions/requirements have the parties assessed for this actual publisher, location, audience and channel? Which approvals and records must each party maintain? Obtain appropriately qualified advice where applicability is unresolved. |

## Proposed disclosure and reader protection

**Do not publish this wording until its facts are true and the exact payable
event has been agreed.** For an approved-application commission pilot:

> Affiliate link: DubaiPoints may earn a commission if you apply through
> this link and Citi approves your application under the agreed campaign
> terms. Commission does not change our verdict or ranking. Check the
> linked offer's eligibility, fees and conditions; it may differ from
> Citi's general website offer. Approval is Citi's decision.

Place the disclosure above the first commercial link and the main
recommendation, legible on mobile, not only behind an asterisk or footer.
Label the actual link, retain the applicable direct-source information and
make any campaign difference prominent. If the contract pays for a different
event, return for Standards/Chairman review and change the wording to that
event; do not leave an approval-only disclosure on a click/lead-paid link.

An internal component review found that `src/components/AffiliateLink.astro`
is dormant and supplies a marker and `sponsored` relation, not a complete
site-wide disclosure system. Before activation, reconcile the affected
review, `/partnership/`, `/how-we-make-money/` and editorial policy with the
actual commercial facts. Their present no-live-affiliate statements must
remain true until activation. The two public trust pages also describe
future sponsored content differently; do not treat this affiliate-only
pilot as permission to launch sponsorships or silently resolve that policy.

Do not present a referral as guaranteed approval, guaranteed income,
guaranteed customer benefit or bank endorsement. Do not strip referrers,
cloak links, manufacture direct-application status, set affiliate cookies
without a reader action or substitute another campaign without review.
Stop rather than imply parity if the proposed reader offer is worse or
cannot be compared with the direct alternative on source-backed terms.

## Measurement design — proposed, not collecting

| Stage | Evidence source | What may be reported | What it does not prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qualified reading | Existing bot-excluded aggregate readership, with period, timezone and source recorded | Visits/page views for the relevant article | Unique people, individual intent or completed applications |
| Outbound action | Future separately approved minimal on-site event | Commercial outbound clicks by approved placement/campaign | A unique person, an application, approval or commission |
| Partner outcome | Partner's reconciled aggregate campaign report | Reported application/approval event in its stated status | Payable income unless all validation conditions are satisfied |
| Validated commission | Final partner statement and reversal rules | Confirmed payable commission separately from pending/reversed amounts | Cash received |
| Settlement | Accounting/bank reconciliation | Cash received for the statement | Profit after all operating costs or evidence that rankings are correct |

Do not implement the outbound event under this pack. If later approved,
send only a fixed event name and allowlisted product/campaign/placement
identifiers. Do not transmit salary, spend, filter state, query strings,
full destination URLs with identifiers, email, phone, application details
or free text. An aggregate sub-ID is not permission to send personal data.
Any GA4 proposal in the parallel workstream remains review-only.

Compare complete like-for-like reporting periods and disclose partner
validation delays. Do not sum site clicks and bank conversions as if they
were the same event; do not claim a click-to-approval rate without aligned
scope, attribution and denominators. Keep editorial usefulness and complaint
signals alongside commercial reporting. No revenue forecast is established.
