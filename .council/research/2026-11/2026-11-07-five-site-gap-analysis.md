# Five-site benchmark: a more useful UAE publication

Reviewed 2026-11-07. Owner-requested research, not a claim of complete market
coverage, final Council acceptance or a production deployment.

## Conclusion

The priority is **broader verified issuer coverage and a repeat-reading
editorial service**, joined to the tools DubaiPoints already has. It is not
another calculator, a larger mega-menu, copied overseas card advice or an
automatically published headline feed. A larger count is useful only when
products are distinct, currently marketed and honestly described.

Five dedicated reviewers covered LoyaltyLobby, Head for Points, The Points
Guy, Visit Dubai and YallaCompare; the two supplied Yalla URLs were one site
assignment. Root supplemented Head for Points with seven current public
Chrome pages and bounded desktop/390px observations. Full reports and URL
ledgers are retained in the private evidence folders listed below.

## What to adapt, not copy

| Reference | Useful observed pattern | DubaiPoints application | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| [LoyaltyLobby programme hubs](https://loyaltylobby.com/hotel-promotions/hyatt/) | Programme-specific promotion types and current/history distinction, supported here by indexed evidence | Current-action panel on existing hotel hubs; separate registration, booking and stay conditions; on-site monthly programme brief | Claiming live visual superiority or copying worldwide/targeted offers into UAE eligibility |
| [Head for Points](https://www.headforpoints.com/best-uk-hotel-points-promotions/) | Monthly hotel briefing, programme questions and card-lifecycle explanations | Link existing news/deals to programme guidance; answer application-versus-bonus eligibility, fees, fulfilment and cancellation questions | Commercial density, copied UK rules, automatic newsletter/forum launch |
| [The Points Guy transfer bonuses](https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/current-transfer-bonuses) | Editor-owned current-offer reference and practical booking/service formats | UAE transfer-bonus overview when several genuine offers warrant it; monthly benefit-use checklist; UAE-origin booking walkthroughs | US products/valuations, generic AI adviser or compensation-influenced ranking model |
| [Visit Dubai public deals](https://booking.visitdubai.com/en/deals-and-offers?type=attraction) | Intent-led experiences with provider, party, booking and redemption conditions | Coherent existing local-value journey; eventually merchant-direct issuer type and precise book/use windows | Calling invite-only Access Club universal resident access; becoming a broad tourism booking engine |
| [YallaCompare](https://yallacompare.com/ae/en-us) | Bank/product-to-action acquisition paths and broad product discovery | Verified issuer census, clearer shortlist-to-compare journey and explicit unknown terms | Counting callback/alternative-product pages as currently applicable cards; lead harvesting or copied stale financial figures |

These are design/editorial inferences from the cited structures, not measured
traffic or conversion effects. Competitor financial values are discovery
leads, never DubaiPoints canonical card data.

## Existing strengths — not missing features

Read-only source checks confirm Finder, Compare, Salary Tracker, card-value,
payoff, fee index, cash-or-miles and Transfer Finder tools; programme hubs,
starter guidance and scenario-led best-of guides; three news desks; search,
manual editorial rails and reading progress; offer expiry/ending-soon/archive,
source/eligibility/limits/promo-code panels and sector/issuer/code filters;
public corrections, valuation methodology and a commercial firewall.

Salary Switch Scorecard/payout, card-change log and cash-or-miles are already
repeatable service formats. My Emirates Pass, DSF, brunch/iftar and specific
party/reservation/exclusion conditions already exist. The improvement is
their discoverability and extension, not claiming they are absent.

The newsletter was deliberately removed on14October. Current organisational
byline and team disclosures must not be embellished into fictitious people,
personal experience or a staffed newsroom.

## Prioritised programme

### P0 — trustworthy coverage and operation

1. Reconcile existing card fields and contradictory surrounding prose against
   actual issuer evidence. Source-read, field-reviewed and fully verified
   are distinct. Preserve old dates when a field was not reverified.
2. Maintain a product ledger by legal issuer/brand, active application state,
   personal credit versus charge/debit/supplementary, source capture,
   reconciled fields, held conditions and reason for exclusion. Do not call
   the57 active baseline the UAE market or treat56 intake names as additions.
3. Represent unknown salary/FX as null, not zero; a published variable maximum
   is not an exact cost. Keep unknowns out of salary-qualified, zero-FX and
   aggregate claims, with visible source/coverage limitations.
4. Finish loading acceptance with real production-like tags, cold mobile
   measurements, separate initial/video budgets and unchanged visible design.
5. Make news intake failures visible; distinguish healthy/no-new from failed
   feeds. Retain safe, bounded, explicitly unread leads and provenance.

### P1 — recurring reader value

6. Extend airline/hotel service journalism: what changed, effective date,
   affected UAE reader, conditions, confirmed versus attributed reporting,
   and next action. Do not fill a quota with weak stories.
7. Pilot a monthly hotel-loyalty action brief and monthly UAE card-benefit
   checklist, reusing canonical sources instead of duplicating numbers.
8. Connect existing hotel hubs to current checked offers. Add distinct
   registration/book/stay windows only through a reviewed content model;
   absence of a restriction must not mean universal eligibility.
9. Extend Transfer Finder's existing offer overlay into a current-bonus view
   only when real coverage warrants it; include target eligibility,
   irreversibility, timing and check date.
10. Deepen UAE-origin booking tasks and programme Q&A. Start with actual
    frequent decisions, not an arbitrary hundreds-of-pages target.
11. QA the staged native selection tray, filter tokens, differences view,
    filter pane and optional guide. Preserve explicit reader context, unknown
    facts, accessible direct controls and no-JS defaults.

### P2 — deliberate extensions, not automatic commitments

12. A merchant-direct deal issuer type could support no-card-required local
    offers. The present schema requires a bank OR programme, so this is real
    T3 model/editorial work, not just adding a coupon label.
13. A small local-value pathway can connect dining, family outings, staycations
    and free alternatives already covered. Venue/party/accessibility data
    must be sourced; provider booking stays external.
14. A consented, redacted reader-question clinic can grow into durable Q&A.
    A public forum is a separate moderation/privacy operation and is deferred.
15. First-hand regional reviews require genuine use, date, who paid/hosted,
    original or licensed evidence and clear limits. Stock/AI cannot supply
    an experience that did not occur.
16. Formal AED redemption sampling would improve valuation evidence. The
    existing methodology honestly says it has not begun; no empirical
    valuation claim or paid data/travel spend is inferred from this plan.

## News automation: safe operating boundary

Existing automation is real: the latest inspected scheduled run42283838379
successfully read all seven configured feeds and produced a new-item digest.
The gap is coverage/relevance, failure visibility and editorial throughput,
not a missing cron job. Free probe42364338274 found a potentially useful
official Accor feed; validation is required before activation.

Proposed/authorised bounded implementation uses the same twice-daily free
workflow: validated feed → safe canonical lead → desk/relevance reason →
explicitly unread triage snapshot → source/evidence review → original draft →
fact/editorial/Council check → normal protected publication. Expiry and
corrections continue after publication. No automatic article publication,
new paid monitors, provider generation, newsletter or silent policy change.

The27September Charter amendment permits fully read secondary news with
named inline attribution, own wording and UAE angle, with primary sources
preferred when readable. It does not relax primary deterministic extraction
for cards, guides or salary data. RSS headlines alone are not a verified
fact base. The current2–3 airline/1–2 hotel weekly ceiling remains until a
specific owner ruling changes it; the news agent will prepare a capacity/SLA
proposal rather than silently raise it.

## Acceptance and measurement

Measure distinct qualified active products and field completeness—not raw
page count. News measures should include healthy sources, relevant unique
leads, editorial turnaround, checked stories, corrections and expired-offer
handling. Reader measures can include successful shortlist→comparison and
guide→tool tasks using already authorised analytics; no new tracking events
or personal-data collection are authorised by this research.

Before live: exact-source tests/build/links/CSP, independent source/model and
utility review, rendered light/dark/mobile/landscape/desktop/zoom/no-JS/motion/
keyboard checks, performance budgets and actual executed protected CI/Council
gates. Report physical Safari limitations honestly. Merge normally only when
the release gates pass, then verify the Cloudflare production artefact and
canonical live pages. Research, staged code, deployed and QA-closed are not
interchangeable states.
