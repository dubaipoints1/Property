# Salary Switch Scorecard — issuer evidence, 2026-11-04

- **Emirates Islamic:** [October campaign terms](https://www.emiratesislamic.ae/en/terms-and-conditions/salary-transfer-offer-terms-and-conditions-oct-2026), definitions, current/savings eligibility, fulfilment and both reward tables. Compare the two non-national cells in the AED 15,000–34,999 row, not the nationality columns. The optional card’s qualification clock runs from disbursal. [Account KFS](https://www.emiratesislamic.ae/-/media/ei/pdfs/Key-Information/kfs_accounts_ea.pdf), Personal Banking Select on p3, confirms free opening and the monthly-balance charge exception. [Tariff](https://www.emiratesislamic.ae/en/soc/accounts) corroborates the balance-charge exception.
- **FAB:** [Conventional offer terms](https://www.bankfab.com/-/media/fab-uds/personal/promotions/2026/20-percent-cashback-on-salary-transfers/terms-and-conditions-en.pdf), §§2, 3 and 5–6. The exact issuer row matches the [deterministic archive](../2026-10/salary-transfer-source-sweep-2026-10-13.md). Each month's eligible increment is measured against the prior month end, not a static balance. No individual outcome assumed.
- **DIB:** [DIB XTRA terms](https://www.dib.ae/docs/default-source/pdf/dib-xtra-tc.pdf), §§2.1(b)–(g), 3.1 and 4.13. The home-finance valuation-fee reversal is distinct from the joining bonus, and depends on the actual eligible fee. Do not infer that a particular home-finance facility qualifies for the salary bonus or that the all-in finance cost is lower.

No new typed offer data. Arithmetic and relationship to the existing Tracker and Calculator are documented in the [brief](../../briefs/2026-11-04-salary-switch-scorecard.md).
