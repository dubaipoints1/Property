The minimum packet is two real public booking states: cash and miles-plus-cash,
with the applicable rules for each. A common URL does not deduplicate away
different booking states. Preserve full text/captures, URLs, Asia/Dubai and UTC
capture times, search inputs, request/result identity and hashes, while

Required semantic anchors before root types any financial payload:

| Capture | Scoped anchors / facts to bind | Failure disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Both quote headers | Origin/destination, travel date, selected flight, passenger count, cabin, fare family | A mismatch blocks the comparison. A route teaser is not a quote. |
| Cash final total | Explicit displayed currency and total including all mandatory charges for the selected party | No teaser, currency-symbol guess, per-person/party confusion or missing pay-later charge. |
| Reward final total | Miles currency/quantity plus the whole remaining cash payment, including tax/surcharge/co-pay | Indicative miles or taxes-only excerpts do not establish the payable total. |
| Both fare rules | Baggage, changes, refunds, no-show/redeposit, payment constraints and deadlines | Explain differences before deriving a winner; do not invent equivalence. |
| Offer terms, if any | Booking/travel windows, residence/status conditions, caps and fees/VAT | No claim that a promotion applies without its exact eligibility source. |
