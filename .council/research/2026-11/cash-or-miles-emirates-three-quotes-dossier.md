## Original illustrative inputs, not issuer pricing

The editor defines a fictional matched flight with an all-cash total of AED 1,000, a fictional Cash+Miles total of AED 800 plus 15,000 miles, and a fictional Classic Reward total of AED 300 plus 30,000 miles. These are teaching inputs only. No route, date, cabin availability, actual redemption quote or real passenger is claimed.

Outputs must be generated by `calculateCashOrMiles` in `src/lib/cashOrMiles.ts`, using `dpValueFils("skywards")`, not a second formula. The break-even result is conditional on the other inputs and the editorial baseline remaining fixed. The baseline is not an Emirates rate. Cash-fare earnings, change/refund costs and opportunity costs are excluded and must be disclosed rather than guessed.
