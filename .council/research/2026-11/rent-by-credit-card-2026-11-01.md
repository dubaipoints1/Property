Dubai Land Department Launches ‘Flexi Rent’ Initiative and Strengthens Strategic Partnerships to Advance Rental Market Accessibility
23 June 2026
As part of its ongoing efforts to develop a more flexible and sustainable real estate ecosystem that enhances quality of life and elevates customer experience, Dubai Land Department (DLD) launched the ‘Flexi Rent’ initiative and signed cooperation agreements with Wasl Properties, Deyaar Property Management, Dubai World Real Estate, Modern Real Estate, Dubai Investment Real Estate, SBK Real Estate, Rocky Real Estate, SRG Properties, Harbor Real Estate, Driven Properties, and Al Showaib Real Estate to support its implementation.
This step reflects DLD’s commitment to providing more flexible rental solutions that respond to evolving market needs, further strengthening Dubai’s position as a leading global real estate destination that offers more efficient and sustainable housing models for various segments of society.
The initiative focuses on introducing a flexi-rent model that broadens tenants' options through a variety of payment plans, including monthly, quarterly, and semi-annual instalments. It is further supported by incentives and value-added packages offered by participating entities, helping to enhance rental market stability, improve quality of life, and provide housing solutions tailored to the needs of diverse segments of society.
Under the cooperation agreements, the ‘Flexi Rent’ model will be applied to vacant or eligible rental units owned or managed by the participating partners. This will be achieved by offering flexible payment options and providing rental incentives, discounts, or promotional packages for new tenants, in accordance with the partners’ approved policies and in compliance with the laws and regulations in force in the Emirate of Dubai.
Dubai Land Department will provide the regulatory and coordination framework necessary for the implementation of the initiative, including supplying partners with relevant guidelines, updates, and requirements, supporting technical integration with approved systems, and monitoring the initiative’s
overall performance in coordination with participating entities. The Department will also support the visibility of partners’ participation through its official channels, including the Dubai REST app, the Department’s website, and its various digital platforms, in accordance with approved procedures and regulations.
Dubai Land Department affirmed that the “Flexi Rent” initiative is an extension of its ongoing efforts to develop innovative and adaptable real estate solutions that enhance the market’s ability to respond to changing dynamics while meeting the community's evolving needs. The Department noted that providing tenants with a range of payment options contributes to improving quality of life, strengthening the stability of the rental market, and supporting the development of a more sustainable and efficient real estate ecosystem.
It further emphasised that collaboration with the private sector is a key pillar in accelerating the adoption of new operational models that create tangible value for customers. The initiative is also closely aligned with the objectives of the Dubai Real Estate Strategy 2033, which aims to enhance the sector’s competitiveness and reinforce Dubai’s position as a leading global destination for investment and living through an advanced real estate ecosystem that places people and their quality of life at the heart of its priorities.
In turn, the partners will be responsible for implementing the initiative’s model across eligible units and managing related tenancy contracts, payments, and data through approved systems. They will also be tasked with raising tenants’ awareness of the flexible payment options and the process for benefiting from the initiative, while ensuring full compliance with the policies and directives issued by Dubai Land Department, as well as all applicable laws and regulations governing rental relationships, data protection, and transparency in dealings with tenants.
The collaboration also includes monitoring the initiative's outcomes during its pilot phase using a set of key performance indicators (KPIs). These include the number of rental units enrolled in the initiative, the number of tenancy contracts concluded under the ‘Flexi Rent’ model, occupancy rates, tenants’ payment compliance levels, the extent to which flexible payment options are utilised, and the number of incentives
offered to new tenants. In addition, the initiative’s performance will be assessed by monitoring feedback and complaints related to the programme, as well as the rate at which they are addressed and resolved.
With the launch of this initiative, Dubai Land Department continues to advance its vision of building a resilient and sustainable real estate sector founded on innovation and meaningful partnerships, while delivering practical solutions that enhance quality of life and respond to evolving economic and social needs. The initiative represents a further step within the Department’s integrated roadmap to develop a more future-ready and competitive real estate ecosystem, supporting the objectives of the Dubai Economic Agenda D33 and reinforcing the emirate’s position as a leading global destination for living, working, and investment.

# Rent in 12 with Property Finder: Monthly Rent in Dubai
# Split your annual rent into 12 monthly card payments.
October 6, 2026
Signing a new lease in Dubai usually means paying a large amount upfront before you move into your new home. For many tenants, paying the full amount upfront is inconvenient and requires a lot of saving. We offer a new way to rent in Dubai that solves for this: Rent in 12.
With Rent in 12, you can pay your annual rent in 12 monthly card payments, while your landlord continues to receive rent payments according to the agreed payment schedule.

## What is Rent in 12?
Rent in 12 is a service available exclusively on the Property Finder app that lets you split your annual rent into 12 monthly card payments. Instead of paying a large amount upfront, you can spread your rent across the year and align your monthly rent payment with your salary cycle.
Simply get approved, find your home, sign your tenancy contract, and start paying rent monthly.
Learn more about Rent in 12
## How does Rent in 12 work?
The entire process is completed in a few simple steps, exclusively through the Property Finder app:
1. Get approved
Connect your UAE bank account or upload your last 3 months of bank statements to get approved.
2. Find your home
Browse homes with the “Rent in 12” filter and find the one that’s right for you.
3. Sign your contract
Once approved, your agent prepares the tenancy contract, which is then signed by both you and the landlord.
4. Pay monthly
You’re all set to pay your rent monthly using debit or credit card. You can also sync your rent payments with your salary date and track all your payments directly in the app.
## Why use Rent in 12?
Here is what this service changes for tenants in Dubai:
- Keep your cash available for other plans or priorities: Money you would have tied up in rent stays available for moving costs, furniture, lifestyle upgrades, or unexpected expenses.
- Sync rent payments to your salary date: Payments are collected on a fixed date each month, so you can line them up with your payday and manage your monthly rent payment more easily.
- Never miss a due date: Automatic monthly card payments help you stay on top of the due dates, and you can follow every payment on the Property Finder app.
## Who is eligible for Rent in 12?
To apply for Rent in 12, you should meet the following basic requirements:
- A monthly income of AED 8,500 or more
- A steady income (you should be able to provide 3 months of your bank statements)
- A valid Emirates ID
- A UAE-issued credit or debit card
New arrivals to the UAE and self-employed or freelance applicants are reviewed on a case-by-case basis based on income consistency shown in bank statements. It also helps to have the other documents you need to rent a property in Dubai ready before you start viewing homes.
## How are the monthly payments structured?
A service fee applies based on the number of payments the landlord receives as per the tenancy agreement:

You can check the exact service fee through the monthly rent calculator in the app for any listing, making monthly rent calculation easier before you move forward.
### Other charges to know about
Late payments: Reminders are sent 7 days and 3 days before your payment is due. If you miss the payment, your saved card is retried daily for 30 days. After that, you will be contacted and a civil case may be initiated.
Early cancellation: If you end your tenancy early, you pay the early-termination penalty agreed with your landlord, plus the applicable interest fees. If your landlord has received more than is ultimately due, the excess amount is returned to you.
Rent is only one part of moving. Ask your agent any questions you may have—they’re there to guide you through the process. It’s also worth reading up on the additional costs of renting in Dubai so there are no surprises.
## How is Rent in 12 different from a personal loan?
Rent in 12 is not a loan. You are not borrowing money, and there is no interest. Instead, it is a payment management service with a service fee that allows eligible tenants to split their annual rent into 12 monthly card payments.
Rent in 12 uses a UAE-issued credit or debit card for the scheduled monthly payments and does not operate as a personal loan. Using the service does not affect your AECB credit score.
This gives eligible tenants another way to manage their rent monthly instead of making larger rent payments upfront.

Rent in 12 is applicable to Dubai residential rental properties listed on Property Finder between 30,000 AED and 400,000 AED.

Yes. The full journey happens in the app, including the income check, signing your contract, and tracking your monthly payments.
Can I cancel my Rent in 12 contract early?
Yes, but fees apply. You pay any remaining monthly payments plus the remaining digital management fees.

# How Tern Works — Rent Rewards, Fees & Setup FAQs | UAE
# Resources
Everything you need to know about paying rent with Tern. Can't find what you're looking for? Get in touch.
## How it works
What is Tern?+
Tern lets UAE residents pay rent with their credit card, earn rewards on every payment, and pay zero fees. Your landlord receives exactly what they're owed — nothing changes for them.
Who can use Tern?+
Anyone renting in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, or Sharjah with a valid tenancy contract and a UAE-issued Visa or Mastercard credit card.
Does my payment frequency matter?+
No. Tern supports monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, and annual payment schedules. We match your tenancy contract — we don't change it.
Does Tern work for commercial leases and office space?+
Not currently. Tern supports residential tenancies only.
What about short-term rentals or holiday homes?+
Not currently — Tern requires a registered tenancy contract (Ejari or Tawtheeq). Short-term and holiday home arrangements aren't supported.
Why didn't anyone do this sooner?+
We asked the same question. Credit card fees made rent payments commercially unviable for most platforms. We built the infrastructure to absorb them — which is why it took a fintech, not a property company, to figure it out.
## Fees
What does it cost me?+
Nothing. Paying your rent via Tern is free. No processing fees, Nada. We have some optional services from partners like Tern Split which do have fees. Those'll be shown to you before you confirm.
What does it cost my landlord?+
Nothing. Landlords pay zero fees.
How does Tern make money if it's free?+
We earn through merchant partnerships and our payments infrastructure.
No seriously, is there a catch?+
Genuinely, no. Your landlord gets paid on time. You earn rewards you were always entitled to. We cover the fees. If you ever want optional service like Tern Split, we'll tell you how much that costs upfront — no surprises. The only thing that changes is your rent starts working for you.
## Your rewards
What rewards do I actually earn?+
Two layers. Your credit card pays its normal rewards — miles, points, or cashback — exactly as it would on any other purchase. On top of that, you earn Tern points: 1 point for every 7 AED in rent.
What can I redeem points for?+
Rewards with 200+ merchants, cashback against your next rent payment, or points transfers to Air Arabia.
Do my bank's credit card rewards still apply?+
Yes — completely separate from Tern points. Your bank pays its normal rewards on the transaction. Tern points are on top of that.
Will my bank definitely give me rewards on a rent payment?+
Tern processes payments as a normal purchase, not a cash advance, so your bank's standard rewards apply. The exact reward category your bank assigns depends on their internal setup — most UAE banks treat it as a standard spend, but if you want certainty, a quick call to your bank will confirm it.
What happens to my points if I move or my tenancy ends?+
Points don't disappear immediately. You have 6 months after your tenancy ends to redeem them. If you start a new tenancy on Tern within that window, the clock resets.
## Your landlord
Does my landlord need to approve this?+
No. If you have a valid tenancy contract, you can set up Tern independently. Your landlord doesn't need to do anything.
What changes for my landlord?+
Nothing. They receive the same payment, the same amount, on time. The only thing that changes is how you pay.
Will my landlord be charged anything?+
No.
I already gave my landlord post-dated cheques — can I still use Tern?+
It depends on your landlord. Some landlords can void or replace the cheques and switch you to Tern mid-tenancy. For others, the switch happens at renewal. Either way, reach out and we'll tell you exactly where you stand.
Do I need my landlord's bank details to sign up?+
Yes — you'll need the IBAN your landlord uses to receive rent. The name on the IBAN needs to match your tenancy contract. If you don't have it, your property manager or leasing agent will.
## Getting started
How long does signup take?+
Under 2 minutes. Sign in with UAE Pass, upload your Ejari, add your card. Done.
What do I need to sign up?+
UAE Pass, your Ejari, and your landlord's IBAN. If your Ejari hasn't been issued yet, reach out — we'll work through it.
Which cards do you accept?+
All UAE-issued Visa and Mastercard credit cards. We don't currently support international cards, debit cards, or Amex.
When exactly does Tern charge my card?+
At 9:30 AM on your scheduled rent due date. You'll receive reminders 7, 5, 3, and 1 day before so you can make sure your card has the available limit.
What if my card limit isn't high enough to cover the rent?+
You'll get reminders ahead of every payment. If your limit is tight, most UAE banks can increase your daily or per-transaction limit on the spot — a quick call before the due date usually sorts it. You can also top up your card balance in advance if needed.
What if my payment fails?+
Tern retries after 48 hours. You'll be notified, and your landlord is informed — but late fees or penalties are governed by your tenancy agreement, not Tern.
## Trust & security
How do I know Tern is legitimate?+
Tern is registered with Dubai's Department of Economy and Tourism (License 1376768) and operates under the Dubai Land Department's REES program. Payments are processed by Paymob, licensed by the UAE Central Bank. We don't store your card data — Paymob does, under PCI DSS compliance.
Is my payment treated as a cash advance?+
No. It's processed as a normal purchase — so your bank's rewards apply and there are no cash advance fees.
