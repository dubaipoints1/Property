# Cash or Miles: launch evidence — 4 November 2026

## What the sources establish

- [Emirates Skywards FAQ](https://www.emirates.com/english/help/faq-topics/emirates-skywards/faq/where-can-i-find-out-how-to-spend-my-miles/), “What is a Classic Reward?” and “When I use Skywards Miles to buy a reward ticket, will I have to pay any extra charges?”: taxes are paid in cash; carrier-imposed charges apply and Emirates may let members pay those charges with additional Skywards Miles. Classic Rewards and Cash+Miles are distinct. [Emirates' public booking page](https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/) directs users to log in to book Classic Rewards. The [public Miles Calculator](https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/skywards/miles-calculator/) and [Dubai–Heathrow route page](https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/destinations/dxb/lhr/flights-from-dubai-to-london-heathrow/) do not establish matched all-in award and cash prices for an individual itinerary.
- [Etihad Guest UAE terms](https://www.etihad.com/en-ae/legal/terms-and-conditions), §§4.4.9.1–5 and 4.4.10.2: award space may be limited; the Promo seat calculator gives indicative miles and omits taxes, fees and carrier charges. The booking flow provides the exact applicable cash and miles combination and carrier charge. The [Abu Dhabi–Muscat route page](https://www.etihad.com/en-ae/flights/flights-from-abu-dhabi-to-muscat) is route and promotional fare evidence only. A read-only one-adult Economy search on the [public Etihad miles booking form](https://www.etihad.com/en-ae/etihadguest/spend-miles/fly-with-miles) for AUH–MCT on 25 November 2026 returned “Pardon Our Interruption” instead of quote results. No sign-in, booking or security-control bypass was attempted.
- `CLAUDE.md`, 12 June 2026, Chairman ruling 2, and `src/lib/valuations.ts`: Skywards and Etihad Guest each retain the **2 fils per mile** editorial cost-basis baseline. A booking-specific result is separate from that baseline and does not create a sampled floor, median, or ceiling. No published values changed.

## Worked examples only

Every cash quote, award cash charge and miles quantity in the launch edition is a deliberately hypothetical input, not copied from a carrier's route estimates. No live inventory or real redemption value is claimed. Both examples assume one adult, matched flight/date/cabin and comparable fare conditions. Cash ticket rewards are not priced into the examples.

| Case | All-in cash fare | Reward cash due | Miles due | Cash avoided | Fils per mile | Cash break-even at 2 fils |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| DXB–LHR Business return | AED 5,200 | AED 2,400 | 105,000 | AED 2,800 | 2.67 | AED 4,500 |
| AUH–MCT Economy one-way | AED 380 | AED 180 | 15,000 | AED 200 | 1.33 | AED 480 |

Cash avoided = all-in cash fare − all reward cash due. Fils per mile = 100 × cash avoided ÷ miles due, rounded to two decimal places. Cash break-even = reward cash due + miles due × AED 0.02. Use matched quotes only in future observed editions, with date/time, flight numbers, cash fare conditions, charge breakdown and seat count checked at capture. No recurring job was started.
