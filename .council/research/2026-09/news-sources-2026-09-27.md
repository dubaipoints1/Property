### 1a. My List Terms and Conditions
URL: https://www.qatarairways.com/en/Privilege-Club/terms-and-conditions/my-list.html

> **1. My List – Terms & Conditions.** These Terms & Conditions govern the use of the 'My List' feature offered to Qatar Airways Privilege Club members.
> My List — A feature within the Qatar Airways Privilege Club that allows a member to create a personalised list of up to four individuals for whom they can redeem Avios for award flights.
>
> **2. Eligibility**
> 2.1 Only active Qatar Airways Privilege Club members may create 'My List'.
> 2.2 Only adult individuals aged 18 years or above may be invited to join another member's List.
> 2.3 A member may only be part of one 'My List' at any given time.
> 2.4 Members who are part of an existing Family & Friends group are not eligible to be added to any 'My List'.
> 2.5 A Burgundy, Silver, Gold, or Platinum Member may create a "My List" only if they created their Privilege Club account at least thirty (30) days prior.
>
> **3. Structure & Participation**
> 3.1 A 'My List' owner may add up to four (4) members to their List.
> 3.2 Members added to 'My List' must remain on the List for a minimum of six (6) months from the date they are added to the List.
> 3.3 A Privilege Club member who is added to another List may still create their own List.
> 3.4 Only the 'My List' owner may initiate the addition of members. Invitations are sent using the member's membership number and either their last name or email address or through an invitation link.
> 3.5 The member must accept the invitation in order to be added to the List.
>
> **4. Redemption**
> 4.1 The 'My List' owner is solely responsible for redeeming Avios from their own Privilege Club balance on behalf of their members.
> 4.2 Gold and Platinum members may book award flights for members added to their List, and also have the flexibility to book award flights for any additional individual of their choice online or through our Contact Centre or Member Service Centre.
> 4.3 Redemptions for 'My List' members are restricted to Qatar Airways and partner award flights.
> 4.4 'My List' members cannot redeem Avios from the List owner's balance.
>
> **5. Removal**
> 5.1 After the six‑month lock-in period, the 'My List' member may leave or be removed by the List owner at their discretion.
> 5.2 Changes to 'My List' membership (adding or removing individuals) may be subject to system validations and verification processes to prevent unauthorised use.
> 5.3 In the event that a 'My List' owner or member deletes their Privilege Club account … b) If the deletion occurs during the lock‑in period, the user shall not be permitted to join another List until the completion of the six (6) month lock-in period.

Read via `fetch-sources.yml`, 27 September 2026, HTTP 200.

> Abu Dhabi: Hotels and hotel apartments across Abu Dhabi will be expected to weave Emirati culture, customs and traditions more closely into the visitor experience under a new set of hospitality standards that will be introduced across the emirate from early 2027.
>
> The Department of Culture and Tourism – Abu Dhabi has launched the Emirati Hospitality Standards, a framework comprising 65 measurable requirements covering eight stages of a visitor's journey, from communication before arrival through to departure.
>
> … Developed in cooperation with the National Projects Office and with the support of the National Identity Committee of the Education, Human Development and Community Development Council …
>
> Guests could, for example, be welcomed with Arabic coffee and locally sourced varieties of traditional Emirati dates.
>
> Hotels will also be encouraged to introduce visitors to traditional dishes including harees … and balaleet …
>
> The standards include a series of mandatory requirements for hotels.
> Properties will be required to provide explanations or links on their websites introducing guests to the history and culture of the UAE.
> They must also send guests information before their arrival offering guidance on ways to experience the country's culture, heritage and distinctive natural environment.
> At least one member of staff in the reception area will be required to wear Emirati national dress, while guest-facing employees, including restaurant staff, will receive training that enables them to introduce and explain at least three traditional Emirati dishes and their place in the country's culinary traditions.
> Hotels will also be required to incorporate at least one design element inspired by Emirati heritage into a public area regularly used by guests.
>
> The framework is designed to be flexible enough to accommodate differences between hospitality establishments rather than imposing an identical model on every property.
> Hotels will be required to implement at least 20 standards, including 10 mandatory requirements and another 10 selected by the property.
> Hotel apartments will operate under a simplified framework. The source information states that this will include five mandatory standards as well as five standards selected by the property.
>
> Participating establishments will receive detailed implementation guidance, specialised training in Emirati culture and a comprehensive operating manual …
>
> The standards will move into full implementation across Abu Dhabi in the first quarter of 2027.
> The Department of Culture and Tourism – Abu Dhabi also plans to introduce an Emirati Hospitality label for visitors, recognising properties that go beyond the minimum standards.
> The label will have three levels of distinction …
