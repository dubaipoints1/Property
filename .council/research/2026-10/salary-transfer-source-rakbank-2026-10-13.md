Terms and Conditions
1. This salary transfer cash reward promotion ("Promotion") is effective from 1st October 2026 to 31st October 2026 (both dates inclusive) ("Promotion Period"), unless otherwise extended or terminated in accordance with the terms of this Promotion. This Promotion is open to eligible customers of The National Bank of Ras Al Khaimah (P.S.C) a public joint stock company (the "Bank" or "Rakbank" or "Rakbank Islamic") who are primary individual account holders. By participating in this Promotion, you agree to be bound by these terms and conditions ("Promotion Terms").
2. To qualify for the Promotion, you must :
a. either have an existing individual Islamic AED current or savings account with the Bank opened prior to 1st October 2026 without your salary being transferred to such account or you must open a new individual Islamic AED Current or Savings account during the Promotion Period ("Eligible Account"); and
b. transfer 3 consecutive months' salary of at least D 10,000 per month to the Eligible Account or at least D 5,000 per month for customers enrolled under RAKempower Payroll solutions or RAKmore. The first such salary transfer being made during the Promotion Period in case of an existing Eligible Account or within 60 days from the date of opening the Eligible Account in case of a new Eligible Account; and
c. The Eligible Account should be in good standing with Rakbank and conducted in a proper and satisfactory manner at all times, including the time of crediting of any cash reward amount, as determined by the Bank in its sole and absolute discretion.
3. Salary transfer to Call Deposit is not eligible for the Promotion. Salary transfer for the purpose of availing personal finance shall not be eligible for the Promotion.
4. Salary credit transaction narration must include the word "Salary". Salary credits received in any Eligible Account held by self-employed individuals shall not qualify for the Promotion.
5. Rakbank customers holding individual account(s) (any type of personal bank account) whether closed or active, with Rakbank where their salary has been credited prior to 1st October 2026 are not eligible for the Promotion.
6. Rakbank employees are not eligible for the Promotion.
7. If you meet the eligibility criteria as mentioned above, you will receive cash reward as per the table below depending upon the salary transfer amount ("Reward"):

   D 10,000 to D 19,999                                          D 1,000
   D 20,000 to D 49,999                                          D 2,000
   D 50,000 and above                                            D 4,000

Rakbank Islamic reserves the right to claw back any cash reward paid to a customer from any account maintained by the customer with Rakbank, in the event it is established that the cash reward was made due to an error or mistake by Rakbank Islamic or in the event of any fraudulent activity undertaken to avail the benefits of the Promotion.
