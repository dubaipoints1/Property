## 1. Membership tiers — /loyalty/about-alfursan-program/membership-tiers-benefits/membership-tiers

> | Tier | Miles (To Attain) | Flights (To Attain) | Validity | Miles (To Maintain) | Flights (To Maintain) | Validity |
> | Green | Basic | Basic | Valid for life | Valid for life | Valid for life | Valid for life |
> | Silver "Elite" | 25,000 | 20 | End of December of the upcoming year | 20,000 | 15 | End of December |
> | Gold "Elite Plus" | 50,000 | 40 | End of December of the upcoming year | 40,000 | 30 | End of December |

> - Tier Credits or Credits are the status points earned by traveling on Saudia and airline partners.
> - "Flights" is the number of eligible flights that have been completed.
> - There are three tiers of membership in Alfursan Program: Green (basic), Silver (Elite) and Gold (Elite Plus).
> - Upgrading to the higher membership Silver (Elite) or Gold (Elite Plus) depends on the number of Tier Credits earned from qualified flights or the number of Flights during one calendar year.
> - In the case of a member being unable to maintain their membership tier level, the downgrade will be reflected on the 1st of January of the next year.

> | Benefits | Green | Silver Elite | Gold Elite Plus |
> | Reward Miles | - | 25% | 50% |
> | Family Miles | done | done | done |
> | Priority Boarding | - | done | done |
> | Seat selection * | - | done | done |
> | Additional Baggage allowance ** | - | 1piece | 1piece |
> | Rapid Check in Class | - | Business | First |
> | Lounge Access *** | - | done | done |

> *** On domestic flights only the Gold membership holder has access to any lounges and the Silver membership holder has access to Alfursan lounges. On international flights, the Silver membership holder can host up to three family members according to the class of travel. The gold membership holder can host up to three family members and one guest according to the class of travel.

## 2. Expiry policy — /earn-and-redeem/miles-types/expiry-policy

> As of 13 December 2022, the expiry policy has been changed … Now Miles expiry only occurs if a Member is not active in the AlFursan Program within a 2 years period. This means that Members who remain active will retain all of their Miles regardless of how long ago they were earned.

> The validity of all the Reward Miles in a Member's account will be extended for an additional 2 years period from the date of the last qualifying activity in the account. If a Member does not have any qualifying activity over the preceding 2 years period, then the total Miles balance in the member's account will expire. Miles cannot be re-credited after they expire.

> | Flights | Any earned Reward Miles transaction from Saudia or SkyTeam Partners |
> | Reward redemption | Any Reward Miles redemption transaction (redeeming Rewards such as Reward Tickets or Reward Upgrades) |
> | Member Referral | Bonus Reward Miles earned when a Member successfully refers a new Member to the AlFursan Program |
> | Earn Reward Miles from Partners | Where Reward Miles are earned on non-airline transactions (for example Reward Miles earned on an AlFursan-Partner credit card) |
> | Reward Miles Purchase | Transaction for purchasing Reward Miles |
> | Gifting/ Transferring Reward Miles to Another Member | Transaction for gifting or transferring Reward Miles to another Member |

## 3. Family program — /en-US/alfursan-information/family-member-program

> AlFursan family program offers a great opportunity to pool your Reward Miles with your family members, helping you reach your next reward sooner.
>
> You can nominate up to 8 family members to join your family program.

> Each family member will receive a separate membership number, allowing them to earn Tier Credits towards their own membership status. Children between the ages of 2 and 12 will receive 75% of the Miles earned on flights, while all other members will receive the full amount of Miles earned for their flights.
>
> Tier Credits are added to each family member's account independently … While Reward Miles earned by each family member will be collected in the family program pool
