> # World of Hyatt: Double Points at New Hotels, Up to 25,000 | Milesopedia
> As of October 1, 2026, World of Hyatt gives you double your base points at its new hotels, up to 25,000 bonus points per stay. The new offer replaces the 500 bonus points per night the program used to hand out at properties recently added to its portfolio.
> To earn these points, you book one specific rate, the "New Hotel Member Bonus Offer". We break down how the new World of Hyatt new hotel offer works, what it pays you depending on the nightly rate and when the old one was worth more.
> ## What changed at new Hyatt hotels
> The new version rewards spending: you earn double your base points on eligible rates, up to 25,000 bonus points per stay. It replaces the old one, which paid 500 bonus points per night with no registration, award stays included.
> The table below compares both versions.
> World of Hyatt new hotel offer: before and since October 1, 2026
> FeatureUntil September 30, 2026Since October 1, 2026
> Bonus500 points per nightDouble base points
> Cap per stayNone25,000 bonus points
> Rate to bookAny rate"New Hotel Member Bonus Offer" only
> Award staysEligibleNot eligible
> RegistrationNoneNone, but the rate is mandatory
> ## How to earn double points
> You earn double points by following the seven rules set out in Hyatt's terms. Check each one when you book, because a single mistake is enough to lose the bonus.
> - Be a World of Hyatt member when you book and when you stay, and provide your membership number
> - Book the "New Hotel Member Bonus Offer" rate at a participating hotel
> - Check in during the offer period set by that hotel
> - Earn Hyatt points: choosing airline miles for the stay cancels the bonus
> - Up to 3 rooms count, including the member's own room
> - Consecutive nights at the same hotel on this rate count as one stay, so one 25,000-point cap
> - Posting time: 2 to 4 weeks after checkout
> ## What it is really worth
> As a World of Hyatt member, you usually earn 5 base points per US dollar spent at the hotel. With this offer, you earn 5 more, for a total of 10 points per US dollar on eligible rates and incidental charges. Elite status bonus points, however, are not doubled.
> You hit the 25,000 bonus point cap after US$5,000 in eligible spending during a single stay. Based on our valuation of 1.8 cents CAD per World of Hyatt point, the extra 5 points are therefore worth about 9 cents CAD per US dollar spent.
> Compare with the old offer based on your nightly rate:
> - US$100 night: 500 bonus points now, the same as before
> - US$200 night: 1,000 bonus points, twice the old offer
> - US$80 night: 400 bonus points, less than the old 500
> - Award night: no bonus at all, while the old offer paid one
> In short, the new offer works in your favour on paid stays above US$100 per night. On cheap nights and award stays, however, it earns less than the old one.

> ## How To Earn the Bonus Points
> To qualify for the bonus points, you need to stay at an eligible property with a checkout date within the period listed on that specific hotel’s terms while it’s featured in the new hotels promotional page. Registration isn’t required, but you must book the “New Hotel Member Bonus Offer” rate.
> Only paid stays qualify to earn points, so award stays no longer count (which they did when the previous version of this offer provided 500 points per night on both paid and award stays). Group rates don’t qualify. And while the terms say it may take 2 to 4 weeks to receive your bonus points, it’s typically much quicker. On previous stays, the bonus points posted to my account a few days later, posting jointly with the stay credit.

> You’ll earn points if you check out during the eligible dates, and you need to book the “New Hotel Member Bonus Offer” rate to qualify.
> ## Final Thoughts
> If you stay at a new Hyatt hotel during certain dates, you can get 2x points per night. This is an updated version of Hyatt’s new hotel promotion, which previously provided 500 bonus points per night, whether you paid or redeemed points for your stay. Now, only paid stays qualify, and only the base rate earns double points — not any bonus points from your Hyatt elite status and not the taxes added to the base rate.
