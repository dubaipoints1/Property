Following the company's Member Day, Marriott International has announced two interesting updates that Bonvoy loyalists can look out for in the coming months.

Among the announcements is an exciting new app feature that will allow elite members to see where they stand in line for a complimentary upgrade — one of the most coveted perks for those with Marriott Bonvoy Gold status and above.

Here's what we know.

## Complimentary Upgrade List feature debuts in the Marriott app

TANNER SAUNDERS/THE PONITS GUY

In response to member requests for more visibility into the inner workings of complimentary room upgrades, Marriott is beta testing a new feature called the Complimentary Upgrade List in their app.

This feature will provide eligible members (those with Gold status and above) with the ability to see their exact position in the upgrade list before they arrive and check in to their hotel.

At the moment, the feature is only available for a small subset of hotels, but Marriott says it will expand to over 250 hotels throughout the U.S. and Canada by November.

It will certainly be interesting to see just how long these lists become at popular hotels during peak travel periods, how Nightly Upgrade Awards will be prioritized and whether or not the lists will function similarly to airline upgrade lists.

## A new lounge experience comes to select premium hotels Asia

ERIC ROSEN/THE POINTS GUY

In addition to the new app feature, Marriott announced a new lounge experience dubbed the Marriott Bonvoy Lounge. Debuting in February 2027, the experience will come to 100 premium-level hotels across Asia-Pacific, with 220 more hotels across Greater China joining in early 2027.

The participating lounges will offer elevated food and beverage programming, have dedicated associates on-site during peak lounge hours and serve locally-inspired offerings, according to the company's press release.

# Marriott Bonvoy to Roll Out An Upgrade List on Its App, Letting Members See their estimated chances of an upgrade - Live From A Lounge

# Marriott Bonvoy to Roll Out An Upgrade List on Its App, Letting Members See their estimated chances of an upgrade

October 15, 2026 by Ajay 1 Comment

Earlier this week, Marriott International held its second Members Day event at its headquarters in Bethesda. The company uses the event to give members an insight into what they are working on for members, in terms of product features and so on.

Marriott Bonvoy is testing a new feature that could make one of the most frustrating parts of hotel loyalty a little more transparent. The hotel group is experimenting with a Complimentary Upgrade List in its mobile app, allowing eligible members to see their position in the queue for a complimentary room upgrade and an estimate of how many upgrades might be available for their stay.

The feature is currently being tested at a limited number of hotels, with Marriott planning to expand it to more than 250 properties in the United States and Canada by early November 2026. If the rollout proceeds as planned, it will give Marriott Bonvoy members something they have largely been missing: visibility into where they stand when competing with other elite members for an upgrade.

Members first spotted the development in the Marriott Bonvoy app in October 2026. The feature remains in beta, and Marriott has not yet announced a wider international rollout.

### Marriott Bonvoy’s new Complimentary Upgrade List: How will it work?

The new screen, titled “Your Complimentary Upgrade Status”, appears in the Marriott Bonvoy app close to a guest’s arrival. It provides two key pieces of information: the estimated number of complimentary upgrades available for the reservation and the member’s current position in the upgrade queue. Here is a sampler.

For instance, a member might find themselves second in line for an upgrade, with the hotel showing no upgrades currently available. This doesn’t necessarily mean an upgrade is impossible, but it does give the guest a clearer sense of their position at that moment.

The app also indicates that Marriott will notify the member one day before arrival if it confirms a complimentary upgrade. However, the queue position can change, and the number of upgrades shown is an estimate, not a guarantee.

The system considers factors such as stay dates and the booked bed type when estimating upgrade availability. As a result, two members staying at the same hotel may see different information depending on their reservations.

There is an important distinction here. The feature does not promise a room upgrade simply because a member is near the top of the list. Instead, it offers a view of the upgrade process, which has traditionally been difficult for guests to understand from the outside. Also, I’d take this with a pinch of salt because, in my view, most upgrades are decided the night before check-in.

### Nightly Upgrade Awards will get priority

One detail worth noting: Marriott’s Nightly Upgrade Awards will be prioritised ahead of complimentary upgrades. The app’s estimated number of available complimentary upgrades does not include rooms allocated to Nightly Upgrade Awards. For Marriott Bonvoy members, Nightly Upgrade Awards are a separate benefit that can be earned through the programme’s Annual Choice Benefits at the 50-night and 75-night thresholds. These awards can be applied to eligible paid or award reservations to request an upgrade to selected premium rooms or suites, subject to availability.

### What does this mean for Marriott Bonvoy elite members?

Complimentary room upgrades are among the most visible benefits of hotel loyalty programmes. They are also among the least predictable.

Marriott Bonvoy offers room upgrades as a benefit for eligible elite members, subject to availability and the applicable programme terms. But members often have little information about how many other guests are competing for the same rooms, how much upgrade inventory is available, or whether their elite status will translate into a better room on a particular stay. Recently, many Marriott participating properties have started posting a counter showing how many members of each tier are checking in, to manage expectations around upgrades. That uncertainty can be particularly frustrating for frequent travellers who choose Marriott properties partly because of the benefits associated with their status.

An upgrade list could change that experience by offering members a clearer picture of their chances before they arrive at the front desk. Someone near the top of the queue might have reason to be optimistic, while someone further down could adjust their expectations accordingly.

Of course, a queue is only as useful as the rules governing it. Marriott has not publicly explained all the factors that determine a member’s position, so it remains unclear how the system will weigh elite status, reservation details and other considerations.

A high position also does not necessarily translate into a suite. The information currently described relates to the estimated availability of upgrades matching the member’s reservation, rather than a promise of a particular room category.

Still, making some of this information visible is a welcome development. It could also reduce guesswork around upgrades, particularly for members who frequently check in through the mobile app rather than speaking to the front desk.

### When will the Marriott Bonvoy upgrade list launch in India?

For now, there is no confirmed timeline for an Indian rollout. Marriott’s reported plans are centred on expanding the beta to more than 250 hotels in the United States and Canada by early November 2026. That is an expansion of the existing test, not confirmation of a global launch. India is an important market for Marriott Bonvoy, with a substantial portfolio spanning luxury brands such as The Ritz-Carlton, St. Regis and JW Marriott, alongside Marriott, Westin, Renaissance, Sheraton and other brands. For frequent travellers who regularly stay at these properties, an upgrade queue could provide useful additional transparency. But, it is not as big as a market as the US of A is. Additionally, with the focus on customer experience in India, I am unsure if this will even see the light of day in this part of the world.

However, until Marriott confirms the markets and properties that will participate, members should not expect the feature to appear on every reservation. Its eventual availability in India will depend on Marriott’s rollout decisions.

### Bottomline

Marriott Bonvoy’s proposed Complimentary Upgrade List will not create more suites or guarantee that every elite member receives an upgrade. It could make the process a little less opaque. For years, members have relied on experience and the occasional pleasant surprise at check-in to gauge their chances of getting a better room. An in-app queue adds transparency to that process, even if the final decision still depends on availability.

The feature is worth watching as Marriott expands its testing programme. The bigger question is whether the hotel group will eventually bring it to markets such as India and explain more clearly how it ranks members. For now, the upgrade list is a promising development for Marriott Bonvoy loyalists, but it remains a beta feature rather than a benefit available across the programme.

# Marriott Bonvoy Upgrade Waitlist: What the Beta Shows | Milesopedia

Marriott Bonvoy is testing an upgrade waitlist for complimentary upgrades in its mobile app. Spotted on October 11, 2026 on some elite members' accounts, the beta screen shows two numbers ahead of arrival: how many upgrades the hotel estimates it has available for your stay, and where you stand in line.

The idea borrows from airline upgrade lists. For a Canadian member who holds or is chasing Platinum Elite or Titanium Elite status in Marriott Bonvoy, it is the first time the chain has shown your position before you reach the front desk. Marriott has made no official announcement, and the feature is limited to select accounts.

## What the "Complimentary Upgrade Status" screen shows

The page appears in the app a few days before the arrival date, on the reservation itself. It brings together four elements:

- Estimated upgrades available: the number of better rooms the hotel expects to release to elite members for those nights.
- Your current position in line: where you rank among eligible members for the same stay.
- Confirmation the day before: the app says it will let you know 1 day before arrival whether your complimentary upgrade is confirmed, and asks you to turn on push notifications.
- Fine print: your position may change, and the estimate only counts rooms that match your booked stay dates and bed type.

In the screenshot that circulated, the hotel showed 0 upgrades available and the member was second in line. A top spot means little when the pool is empty.

A beta feature
As of October 2026, the upgrade waitlist only appears on select accounts or for select hotels. Marriott has published neither a general rollout date nor its allocation rules. The details described here may change before then.

## Nightly Upgrade Awards jump the line

Nightly Upgrade Awards, the upgrade certificates Platinum Elite members and above can pick as an annual benefit, are processed first. They are not reflected in the waitlist numbers.

In practice, a member who applies a certificate to the same dates moves ahead of everyone, including a Titanium Elite ranked first on the complimentary list. If a suite matters for a specific stay, the certificate is still the safest bet. Our guide to Marriott Bonvoy annual elite benefits walks through that choice.
