Terms & Conditions for Bonus on Salary Transfer
Details of Promotion
Mashreq customers can earn up to AED 2,500 cashback (“Bonus”) upon transferring

Terms & Conditions of the Promotion
   •   The promotion is open to Mashreq customers whose Current or Savings
   accounts were opened on or after 1 September, 2021 and who transferred
   first salary to their Mashreq account on or after 6th June 2024
   •   Only customers with a net salary greater than AED 5,000 per month are
   eligible for this promotion.
   •   The Promotion is applicable for Mashreq Gold customers of Mashreq.
   •   Bonus is awarded based on the following salary brackets:
 Salary Band   Bonus

   •   If the amount of salary transferred in subsequent months’ increases, the

•   The Bonus payments will be credited within ninety (90) days from the date of
   the first salary transfer in the Mashreq Account of the Customer, in the
   respective months.
•   If their salary is not transferred in any given month, the Customer will forfeit
   their Bonus payment in that given month. Similarly, if their salary transferred
   in a given month falls below AED 5,000, then the Customer will forfeit their
   Bonus payment benefit in that given month. However, during subsequent
   months, if salary retransferred/ returns to an amount that is greater than or
   equal to AED 5,000, the Customer will be credited the Bonus payment for
   that month where the salary transfer amount returned to more than or equal
   to AED 5,000 (based on the value of such salary). The forfeited bonus will not
   be paid in following months.
•   If salary is not transferred to the Mashreq Account for two (2) consecutive
   months during the 6 months period since the date of first salary transfer, all
   previous Bonus payments will be clawed back by debiting the Mashreq
   Customer’s account for such Bonus’ equivalent amounts.
•   Customer will receive the Bonus only once, paid over fixed period of 6 months
   where the eligibility is met, towards the Salary Transfer cashback.
•   Customer will not be eligible for the Salary Transfer cashback, if a Joining
   Bonus was availed earlier as part of the Happiness Account campaign.
