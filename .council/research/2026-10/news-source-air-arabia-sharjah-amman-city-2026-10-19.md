# Air Arabia expands Jordan network with new service to Amman City Airport

-

Last updated: October 8, 2026 | 14:24

-

Picture used for illustrative purpose only.

Air Arabia on Thursday announced the launch of its new non-stop service between Sharjah International Airport and Amman City Airport (ADJ), starting 15th November 2026.

The new service will operate four times weekly, marking the second airport in the Jordanian capital served by Air Arabia from Sharjah, complementing the airline’s existing services to Queen Alia International Airport and further strengthening connectivity between the two countries.

Adel Al Ali, Group Chief Executive Officer, Air Arabia, said, “The launch of our new service to Amman City Airport marks an important addition to our operations in Jordan, giving our customers access to a second airport in the capital.”

The launch builds on Air Arabia Group’s existing operations to Jordan, with non-stop flights from Zayed International Airport to both Amman City Airport and Queen Alia International Airport alongside existing services from Alexandria International Airport to Amman City Airport.

# Air Arabia expands Jordan network with new service to Amman City Airport

## Four weekly non-stop flights will connect Sharjah and Amman City Airport (ADJ), starting 15 November 2026.

Air Arabia, the Middle East and North Africa’s first and largest budget carrier operator, today announced the launch of its new non-stop service between Sharjah International Airport and Amman City Airport (ADJ), starting 15 November 2026.

The new service will operate four times weekly, marking the second airport in the Jordanian capital served by Air Arabia from Sharjah, complementing the airline’s existing services to Queen Alia International Airport and further strengthening connectivity between the two countries.

Adel Al Ali, Group Chief Executive Officer, Air Arabia, said: “The launch of our new service to Amman City Airport marks an important addition to our operations in Jordan, giving our customers access to a second airport in the capital. This new service reflects our commitment to enhancing travel options between the UAE and Jordan, while offering our customers greater convenience and flexibility.”

The launch builds on Air Arabia Group’s existing operations to Jordan, with non-stop flights from Zayed International airport to both Amman City Airport and Queen Alia International Airport alongside existing services from Alexandria International Airport to Amman City Airport.

Air Arabia operates a modern fleet comprising Airbus A320 and A321 aircraft and continues to expand its network from its hubs across the UAE and beyond. The airline offers a value-added onboard experience with ‘SkyTime’, its complementary in-flight streaming service, and ‘SkyCafe’, its onboard menu. Passengers can also benefit from ‘Air Rewards’, the most generous loyalty program in the region.

Customers can now book their direct flights between Sharjah and Amman City Airport by visiting Air Arabia’s website, calling the call centre, or through travel agencies.
