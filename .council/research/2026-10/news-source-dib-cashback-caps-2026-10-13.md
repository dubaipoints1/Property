## Arithmetic in the post (ours, from the figures above)

- Reward, 3%: the new AED 200 cap is reached at AED 6,667 of monthly spend in
  the category (was AED 10,000 for AED 300).
- Platinum, 4%: the new AED 300 cap is reached at AED 7,500 (was AED 10,000
  for AED 400).
- Reward category caps summed: before 300+300+100+100+100+25+300 = 1,225;
  after 200+300+100+100+100+25+200 = 1,025, against the unchanged AED 1,000
  cycle cap.
- Platinum category caps summed: before 400+400+150+150+150+50+400 = 1,700;
  after 300+400+150+150+150+50+300 = 1,500.
- Most a household spending at or above both old caps on groceries and school
  fees loses per month: Reward AED 200 (2 x 100); Platinum AED 200 (2 x 100),
  subject to the AED 1,000 cycle cap.
