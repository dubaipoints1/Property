## Benefits of the card:

- Earn Plus points up to 5% on your daily spends with instant redemption at POS, against miles and cash back.
  - 5% Plus points for AED 5,000 and above transactions
  - 2% Plus points for transactions between AED 2,500 – AED 4,999
  - 1% Plus points for transactions less than AED 2,500
  - 1% Plus points for Supermarkets and groceries, insurance, car dealerships, fuel, utility Payments, real estate, charity, education, quick service restaurant (fast-food restaurant), transit, government services, telecommunication transactions & spends originating in European Union (EU) countries.
- Maintain AED 50,000 quarterly spends and be the lucky winner to receive AED 5,000 cashback
- Enjoy free golf in the UAE and up to 40% discount at premium golf courses worldwide.
- Airport transfers up to 4 drops a year in the UAE
- Local courier service up to 12 times a year within the UAE
- Enjoy 4 complimentary valet parking services per month of hour each at selected locations in Abu Dhabi.

## Earn. Redeem. Relax. Calculate your rewards

Earn 5% On transactions above AED 5,000
Earn 2% On transactions between AED 2,500 – AED 4,999
Earn 1% On transactions below AED 2,500
Earn 1% On other Transactions*

**Please Note**

\*Other Transactions - Supermarkets and groceries, insurance, car dealerships, fuel, utility Payments, real estate, charity, education, quick service restaurant (fast-food restaurant), transit, government services, telecommunication transactions & spends originating in European Union (EU) countries

Plus Points Rewards earned on retail spends made on grocery and supermarkets, quick service restaurant (i.e. fast-food restaurant), insurance, car dealership, petroleum, transit, government services, utility payments, real estate, education & telecommunication payment will be capped at 1,000 points per statement cycle.

For Purchases made on retail spends at merchants located in European Union will be capped at 500 points per statement cycle.

Plus Points Rewards earned per statement cycle will be capped at 5,000 and maximum of 60,000 Plus Points Rewards can be earned per annum.
