# UX review: header, homepage, news, salary tracker (19 October 2026)

Requested by the Chairman in-session, 19 October 2026: "can you do a review of the website like, like from UI UX director team … can we do something to the menu maybe? also on the other pages as well like the news/salary tracker anything to enhance".

**Method.** I built the site from main at 2bde4fe and took Playwright screenshots at 390×844 and 1280×800 of the following pages, with reduced motion on:
- `/`
- `/news/`
- `/salary-transfer/`
- `/cards/`
- `/deals/`
- one news post
- one salary-band page

I also took one shot with the mobile drawer open.

## What works

- **One visual idiom.** Navy and gold, Newsreader with Geist, warm paper. It reads as one publication, not a stack of systems, which was the 7 October problem.
- **Homepage hero.** On phones the CTAs are on the first screen, and the H1 is strong.
- **Salary tracker.**
  - The desktop band picker, filters and sort are clear.
  - The "Ends in N days" urgency chips are useful.
  - The coverage note is honest.
- **News index.** The lead story plus "Trending now" works on desktop.

## Findings

1. **The menus disagree.**
   - Desktop shows Credit cards ▾ · Banks · Earn miles ▾ · Guides · Deals · 11 live · News.
   - The mobile drawer shows Deals · Cards ▾ · Banks · Points + Miles ▾ · Travel ▾ · Guides · News.

   A reader who moves between phone and laptop meets two different maps of the site.
2. **The salary-transfer tracker is missing from the desktop primary nav.** It is a flagship tool, but it only appears in a secondary strip.
3. **The secondary strip duplicates the primary nav.** "Salary offers · Card deals · Latest news" repeats Deals and News. On a 390px phone the third item is clipped at the edge.
4. **The deal count reads as body copy.** "Deals · 11 live" sits in the nav as running text; it would read better as a badge.
5. **Mobile drawer carets are off-palette.** They render in the legacy link blue rather than the navy accent.
6. **Mobile drawer tool subtitles are jargon.** "DP value" and "Sources watched weekly" don't tell a reader what the tool does.
7. **The homepage eyebrow on phones** wraps to two lines, has a stray dot offset to the left, and is low-contrast gold on the photograph.
8. **News filters on phones.** All news / Banking / Airlines / Hotels wraps, leaving "Hotels" alone on a second line next to RSS. On desktop the RSS pill floats mid-row, away from search.
9. **Salary tracker on phones.**
   - The page head (stats plus coverage box) pushes the band picker, the page's main control, below the first screen.
   - The band picker is a 2-column grid with an orphaned "AED 50k+".
   - Each offer card repeats three label rows (Reward type / Salary band / Validity), so eight offers run to about 1,600px.

## Not changed, deliberately

- **No new motion.** The 7 October ruling stands.
- **No palette or type changes.** The 2026-10-04 redesign rule says the current idiom stays until the Chairman ratifies a new direction.
- **The desktop salary-tracker table.** It already reads well.

## Worth considering next (not built)

- **"Ending soon" rail on the homepage.** A small rail drawing on the deals and salary offers with under 14 days left, built from data we already hold.
- **Sticky filter bar on the salary tracker.** The band picker would stay reachable while scrolling the list on phones.
- **News index dates.** A visible date in every list row, plus a "this week" divider, so returning readers can see what is new.
