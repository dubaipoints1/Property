## Emirates Islamic — NEW live banded offer (fills the open hand search)

- Offer page: <https://www.emiratesislamic.ae/en/offers/salary-transfer-cashback>
  (200, fetched 2026-08-22)
- Campaign T&C: <https://www.emiratesislamic.ae/en/terms-and-conditions/salary-transfer-offer-terms-and-conditions>
  (200, fetched 2026-08-22)
- "**The offer is valid from 1 June 2026 until 30 September 2026.**"
- CASA cashback bands (UAE Nationals / Non-UAE Nationals): 5,000–7,499 →
  100/50 · 7,500–9,999 → 200/100 · 10,000–14,999 → 500/400 ·
  15,000–34,999 → 750/600 · 35,000–49,999 → 2,000/1,500 · 50,000+ →
  5,000/3,000.
- Covered Card spend reward (Nationals / Non-Nationals · spend within 60
  days of disbursal): 200/200 · min 6,000 (both low bands) · 500/400 ·
  min 6,000 · 750/500 · min 12,000 · 1,000/750 · min 18,000 ·
  1,000/1,000 · min 18,000.
- T&C: "**New To Group Salary Transfer Relationship** means a customer
  must not have had an existing salary transfer relationship with the
  Emirates NBD Group, including Emirates NBD Bank and Emirates Islamic
  Bank within the last 6 months".
- T&C: three consecutive calendar months of salary credits qualify; "the
  lowest salary among the 2nd and 3rd Calendar months will be used".
- T&C: "Cashback will be processed within 60 days of meeting the salary
  transfer eligibility criteria."
- T&C clawback: "reverse the full salary cashback amount if the account
  doesn't receive any eligible salary credit for more than three (3)
  consecutive calendar months within twelve (12) months period following
  the month in which the salary transfer account cashback was credited."
- Exclusions: Super Savings Skywards / Etihad Guest accounts; active
  Personal Finance at payout; Emarati, Skywards Black, Etihad Guest and
  Amazon Covered Cards; EI staff; salary-enrolment reward in prior 12
  months. Alpha Youth Account adds AED 100. Enrolment form required.
