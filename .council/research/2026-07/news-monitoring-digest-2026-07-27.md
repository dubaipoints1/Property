## Story A (airline beat) — Skywards' summer push: DSS 5M-Miles raffle + enhanced redemptions

**Primary sources (both scraped in full, 27 July):**
- https://www.emirates.com/media-centre/emirates-skywards-launches-5-million-skywards-miles-giveaway-for-dubai-summer-surprises/ (8 July 2026)
- https://www.emirates.com/media-centre/make-every-mile-count-spend-smarter-to-unlock-more-summer-value-with-emirates-skywards/ (21 July 2026)

**Verified facts (from the 21 July release text, quoted where exact):**
- Enhanced redemption promotion runs **until 30 September 2026** across
  Skywards Everyday, Skywards Miles Mall, Emirates Official Store,
  Dubai Duty Free, marhaba Meet & Greet.
- Skywards Everyday during the promo: convert **350 Skywards Miles for
  AED 10 back** at "hundreds of participating retail, dining and
  lifestyle partners across the UAE, including Careem, Costa Coffee,
  MMI, BinSina Pharmacy, Al Jaber Optical, The Body Shop, NARS".
- Miles Mall: digital gift cards (Amazon AE, Deliveroo, Shukran, Apple,
  NIKE) "available from a minimum of 2,000 Skywards Miles".
- GCC-issued Emirates Skywards Visa credit cardholders: "up to 20% off"
  selected event redemptions on Skywards Exclusives ('Co-Brand Offer'
  label).
- DSS raffle (8 July release + restated 21 July): spend **AED 200+**
  with Skywards Everyday partners or Skywards Miles Mall UAE **until
  30 August 2026** → raffle entry per qualifying transaction;
  **500 winners × 10,000 Miles = 5,000,000 Miles** total.
- Also: "up to 1 Skywards Mile per US$1 spent at Dubai Mall, depending
  on membership tier"; weekly challenges on the Skywards Everyday app.

**Analysis hook (DP-native):** 350 Miles → AED 10 implies ~**2.86 fils
per Mile** — ABOVE our published 2-fil cost-basis baseline for
Skywards. That makes this cashback conversion an unusually honest
floor-value redemption while the promo runs; contrast with our standing
guidance that cashback conversions usually under-cut the baseline.
(Cross-link: /airlines/skywards/ and /valuations/.)

**Beat call:** airline (Skywards currency moves). Category:
programme-change or deal-update — desk's call. relatedPrograms:

**Facts as reported by LFAL (promo mechanics — mark as per-LFAL +

Rewards" partnership — verifiable at
https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/skywards/partners/marriott-bonvoy/

match our programme page):**
- Bonvoy→Skywards transfers 3:1; +5,000-Mile bonus per 60,000 points
  transferred.
- Skywards Silver/Gold/Platinum earn 1 Skywards Mile per USD on
  eligible Marriott stays; Bonvoy Gold Elite+ earn 3 points per USD on
  eligible Emirates flights; reciprocal elite courtesies.

**Analysis hook (DP-native):** 5,000 Bonvoy points at our published 2.5-fil

minutes — on a stay a UAE reader may already have booked for a summer
staycation. Note the trap: register FIRST, stay second.
