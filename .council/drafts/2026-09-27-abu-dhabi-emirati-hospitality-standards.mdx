Hotels and hotel apartments in Abu Dhabi will be expected to build Emirati
culture into the guest experience from early 2027, according to Gulf News.
In a report last updated on 25 September 2026, it said the **Department of Culture and Tourism – Abu Dhabi
(DCT)** has launched the **Emirati Hospitality Standards**. The report
says the standards cover **65 measurable requirements** across **eight
stages** of a stay, from before arrival to departure.

We have not found the standards on DCT's own site. Its circulars page,
read on 27 September, lists circulars up to No. 8/2026 and none on this
subject; its news listing and a search of WAM also turned up nothing. The details below are therefore **per Gulf News**.

## What hotels must do

Per Gulf News, **hotels must meet at least 20 of the standards: 10
mandatory ones plus 10 of their own choice**. Hotel apartments follow a
lighter version: five mandatory and five chosen.

The mandatory requirements Gulf News lists:

- An explanation of UAE history and culture on the hotel's website, or a
  link to one.
- Information sent to guests **before arrival** on ways to experience the
  country's culture, heritage and natural environment.
- **At least one reception staff member in Emirati national dress.**
- Guest-facing staff, including restaurant staff, trained to **introduce
  and explain at least three traditional Emirati dishes**.
- **At least one Emirati heritage design element** in a public area
  guests regularly use.

Gulf News also gives optional examples: a welcome with Arabic coffee and
locally sourced dates, and introducing guests to dishes such as harees
and balaleet. The report frames the coffee welcome as an example and the
dishes as encouraged; neither is among the mandatory requirements it
lists.

## When it applies

Full implementation is due in the **first quarter of 2027**, per Gulf News.
Gulf News reports that DCT also plans an **Emirati Hospitality label** with three levels of
distinction, for properties that go beyond the minimum.

## What it means for a stay

Per Gulf News, the standards cover hotels and hotel apartments across the
emirate, which takes in the chain hotels UAE residents book with points
for a staycation. The report does not mention loyalty programmes. What changes is what you should expect at check-in from 2027.

We will update this story if DCT publishes the standards themselves.
