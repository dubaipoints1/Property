Paying more for a hotel room to earn extra points can make sense. Paying more
without checking what those points buy does not. Start with the final bill and
the booking rules, then decide whether the rewards make up the difference.

That matters with a double-points offer. The headline tells you how a reward
changes; it does not tell you whether the room is a good buy.

**Research limitation:** We selected Novotel Dubai Al Barsha for a stay from
24–26 November 2026, with one room and two adults. We did not obtain matched,
final direct and third-party prices with the same room and cancellation terms.
There is no observed saving or winning rate in this draft. The method below
does not substitute for that missing comparison.

## Match the booking before comparing prices

Keep the direct hotel page and one alternative open together. Compare the room
you would actually book, not the cheapest headline on each site.

A superior double is not automatically equivalent to a room with a similar
name elsewhere. Check the bedding, board basis and occupancy. Then check when
you pay and what happens if plans change. A flexible room can reasonably cost
more than a non-refundable one, but that premium buys flexibility as well as
any points.

Use the total due for the whole stay. Include charges collected now and those
collected at the hotel. A nightly figure excluding taxes is not comparable with
an all-in total. If a charge is unspecified, the total is incomplete—not free.

| Check | What needs to match |
| --- | --- |
| The stay | Property, dates, occupants, room, bedding and meals |
| The final bill | Currency, included charges and amounts payable at the hotel |
| The risk | Payment timing, cancellation deadline, time zone and no-show terms |
| The reward | Eligible channel and rate, registration order and usable points |

## What Accor's offer changes—and what it does not

The [current Accor promotion conditions](https://all.accor.com/a/en/loyalty-program/promotions-offers/meapac-bonus-points-offer.html),
read on 9 November 2026, require registration before an eligible booking.
Registration and booking run through 13 December 2026, with the published
deadline stated in Central European Time. Eligible stays run through
31 March 2027 and require at least two consecutive nights at the same hotel.

The advertised multiplier is based on Classic status. Do not assume it doubles
every point an elite member would otherwise earn. Check the ordinary award
and the promotional addition separately.

Eligible direct Accor channels and connected traditional travel agencies are
covered by the conditions; third-party online travel agencies such as Expedia
and Booking.com are excluded from this offer. The programme's general terms
also exclude those OTA stays from ordinary eligible-stay points.

The participating-property list names Novotel Dubai Al Barsha. That supports
using it as a research candidate. It does not prove a particular rate qualifies
or that a room is available. The offer is subject to availability and cannot
be stacked indiscriminately with other promotions.

## Do not calculate points on the whole UAE bill

Under [ALL Accor's membership terms](https://all.accor.com/a/en/loyalty-program/legal/terms-and-conditions.html),
the Classic rate in the default participating-brand column is 25 Reward points
per €10 of eligible expenditure. The table treats some brands differently,
including Novotel Living; do not confuse that with an ordinary Novotel hotel.

Taxes, including VAT, and service charges are excluded from earning. For a stay
outside the euro area, Accor converts the eligible local-currency expenditure
to euros using the hotel's specified exchange-rate basis at checkout.

So multiplying the full AED bill by a headline earn rate is not a sound points
estimate. If the eligible spend or conversion is missing, leave the estimate
open rather than manufacture precision.

## Compare the premium with a reward you can use

The direct-booking premium is the final direct total minus the equivalent
alternative's final total. That is the extra cash you are considering paying.

Next, separate all rewards available only through the direct booking from the
narrower promotional uplift. Ordinary eligible-stay points may be part of the
direct-versus-OTA decision, but they are not a benefit created by the promotion.
Do not count them twice.

Accor's online redemption rules give a €20 discount for a minimum redemption
of 1,000 Reward points and a €40 discount for 2,000. Above that second block,
redemptions use increments of 2,000 points. Some taxes cannot be paid with
points; not every rate permits redemption.

Those are conditional booking discounts, not cash paid into your account.
The AED outcome depends on the applicable conversion and booking rules. A
balance below the minimum is not an immediately usable room discount.

Our [Valuations page](/valuations/) does not currently model Accor. Do not select
another programme as a proxy. We have not supplied an AED break-even figure
because the matched prices, eligible spend and applicable conversion are
not established.

## When taking the cheaper room is sensible

If the terms genuinely match, a higher direct rate needs a benefit you expect
to use. Keep unpriced preferences separate: dealing directly with the hotel,
flexibility and any status benefits may matter, but their cash value is not
established here.

Before booking, recheck the offer, rate eligibility and cancellation rules.
Keep the registration confirmation and original terms. Accor says promotional
points should post within ten days after checkout; that is not money available
to fund this booking.

For the background, see our [Accor programme guide](/hotels/accor-all/) and
[report on the current double-points offer](/news/all-accor-double-points-uae-hotels-book-by-13-december/).
The practical decision is smaller than the headline: pay more only when the
extra cost is justified by benefits you can actually use.
