[Double 11 page](https://www.ghadiscovery.com/ja-resorts-hotels/ja-hatta-fort-hotel/stay-offers/double-11)

That is a useful date to check if you are considering a UAE mountain break.

offer beats the price you could otherwise book.

The readable listing does not supply the actual deposit, cancellation,
blackout or included-charge conditions beneath its terms heading. Those
details matter before any payment. Ask for the complete conditions attached
to your dates, room and offer, then compare the all-in total with an equivalent
alternative.
