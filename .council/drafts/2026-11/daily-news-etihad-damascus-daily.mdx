**Etihad's Abu Dhabi–Damascus service is scheduled to go daily from
14 November 2026.** The airline set out the change in an
[Etihad Hub notice dated 8 October](https://www.etihadhub.com/en-ae/news/etihad-goes-to-from-damascus-daily),
which remained readable when checked on 9 November.

This is an upcoming service reminder, not a newly announced route. Etihad
attributes the frequency increase to demand; its timetable is subject to change.

## What it means for UAE travellers

If you are planning this route, check whether the daily pattern covers your
actual departure date rather than relying on an older weekly itinerary.
For a connecting trip, compare the whole journey, including the connection
and ticket conditions—not just the direct sector.

The announcement is not a live booking result. This report has not checked
seat inventory, fares, baggage rules, aircraft, reward availability, entry
requirements or destination travel advice. Confirm the current schedule
and applicable requirements before committing to a ticket.

Our [Etihad Guest guide](/airlines/etihad-guest/) provides programme background,
not evidence of reward seats on this route.
