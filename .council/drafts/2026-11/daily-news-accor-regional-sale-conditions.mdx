**Accor's winter sale does not carry the same cancellation terms everywhere.**
Its [public conditions](https://all.accor.com/a/en/offers/meapac/hotel-escape-sale.html)
place participating UAE hotels within the regional offer covering selected
premium, midscale and economy brands.

For that offer, section 3.4 requires payment upfront and rules out cancellation,
refunds and changes. It gives a booking deadline of 1 December 2026, stays
from 5 November 2026 to 19 April 2027.
Hotel-specific conditions take precedence.
Separate brand offers have different rules.

## What it means for UAE residents

Before paying, identify the campaign named against your room and compare its
final price with a genuinely flexible alternative. A discounted rate derived
from a flexible comparison price is not necessarily refundable.

The page also contains inconsistent membership and date wording between its
marketing copy and detailed conditions. Do not assume the worldwide headline,
member-rate stacking or another brand's rules apply to your reservation.
Check the exact hotel's participation, dates, minimum stay and payment terms.

No room availability, matched saving or qualifying individual rate was checked.
This is a conditions alert, not a recommendation to book.

Our [ALL Accor guide](/hotels/accor-all/) provides programme background,
not confirmation that a particular sale rate qualifies.
