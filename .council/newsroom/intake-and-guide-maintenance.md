# DubaiPoints: intake, routing and guide maintenance

Working procedure for the daily desk, subject to the existing Council editorial
standards. Discovery has no publication authority. Keep private drafts and
receipts out of the public collections and hosted trial outputs.

## Intake and selection

1. Combine the verified pilot receipt, the existing hosted digest and scoped
   official-page changes in the private queue. A feed item or changed selector
   is a lead, not a source read. Record the intended slot, actual start, last
   usable coverage, gaps and baseline-only rows separately.
2. Link each additional sighting to the same lead when the issuer/programme,
   product/route, effective date and material change match. Similar headlines
   alone do not establish a duplicate. Reconcile existing IDs and publication
   receipts before commissioning. Never reopen withdrawn items or earlier
   source holds merely because the title changed.
3. Prioritise UAE applicability, a reader action, deadline and full evidence.
   Do not commission Syria-related coverage or incident, safety, security or
   related disruption coverage. No quota-driven filler. Prepare at least five
   distinct, substantiated reviews per day when evidence permits, with no news
   ceiling and exact shortfalls recorded.

## Route each lead

| Reader question | Result |
| --- | --- |
| What just materially changed for me? | Original News quick hit, standard story or separately evidenced analysis. Link the relevant guide. |
| Is this specific offer usable by me now? | Deal only once current eligibility, conditions, participation and expiry are supported. If the rate or availability is not testable, say so or hold. |
| What should I do whenever this comes up? | Update the existing Guide first. Commission a new Guide only for a distinct decision without a current home. |
| Which card or account terms now apply? | Field-specific evidence/correction queue. Numbers enter typed data only from deterministic extraction tied to the exact primary source. |
| Is evidence contradictory, incomplete or not current? | Hold with a named missing check, next reviewer and recheck date. |

## Guide brief and update

Record the reader decision, relevant audience, current source receipts, what
already exists, which claims changed, and the proposed recheck date/owner.
Write an original summary and steps a reader can follow. Distinguish effective
dates, booking or application cutoffs and when the source was read. Keep
examples clearly hypothetical unless independently tested; do not imply
checkout prices, inventory, personal eligibility or first-hand experience.

In the private brief capture the next review date and reason before handing
off. Prioritise offer/route end dates and policy changes ahead of evergreen
copy. Update a guide's publication date only when it is genuinely new; use
the update date for substantive revisions. Do not count a News story and
cosmetic changes to its related Guide twice.

## Approval and hosted trial

Send one private review batch: exact copy/byline, sources, conflicts and
contributors, deadline and next editor per item. Require the owner's
article-specific acceptance and independent Council review. An advisory green
Council workflow is not approval. Publish only after required release checks
and verify the live page plus intended listing and feed.

The hosted workflow checks the fixed feed and page allowlist twice in one
ephemeral read-only job, with a pause and only the first in-memory baseline
for comparison. Its first use bootstraps once on creation of the exact owned
trial branch; it creates no recurring slot. No raw feed text, item URLs,
fingerprints, private queue, notifications, or draft files are retained.

Report baselines, genuine overlaps, newly seen URLs, changed prior items,
and gaps separately. A second-pass URL already seen in another source's
first pass is not a new lead; an entirely disjoint source window is a gap.
An overlapping window with zero changes is a valid quiet observation, not
proof of live change detection. The deterministic fixture tests verify new
lead detection and duplicate rejection even if real feeds stay quiet.

This trial does not replace either current monitor or test a missed
scheduled slot. Only after observing exact hosted logs and repeat receipts
should the desk propose persistence, delivery and missed-slot monitoring.
