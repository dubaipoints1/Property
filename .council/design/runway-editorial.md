# Runway Editorial: interior design system

Selected on 5 November 2026 after comparison of Runway Editorial, Linen
Journal and Blue Ledger. See the [rollout brief](../briefs/2026-11-05-runway-editorial-rollout.md).

- **Surfaces:** cool porcelain and deep ink in light mode; blue-green
  midnight and raised panels in dark mode. Petrol is the editorial and
  interaction accent. Gold remains reserved for the existing trust and
  expiry signals. Do not use it to decorate ordinary calls to action.
- **Typography:** retain Newsreader and Geist. Use visible hierarchy and
  a constrained reading column instead of reducing or cropping copy to fit.
  Display comparable figures with tabular numerals and stable row alignment.
- **Actions:** `dp-ui-primary` for the main action; `dp-ui-secondary` for
  a genuine alternative; `dp-ui-quiet` for lower-priority actions. Keep
  native semantics, keyboard focus and readable states in both themes.
- **Decision tools:** keep the inputs and first useful result close. For
  comparisons show at least two complete columns on narrow screens; additional
  columns scroll inside the comparison, not the document.
- **Discovery:** one lead item and a clear grid or list. Rich programme
  photography is manifest-backed and visibly credited. Manual mobile
  programme browsing, never autoplay or page-scroll interception.
- **Boundary:** the existing homepage, stylesheet links, poster and deferred
  reel are not restyled by this rollout. Do not promote the private prototype.
