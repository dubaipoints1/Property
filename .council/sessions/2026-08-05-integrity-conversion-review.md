# Council session: integrity and conversion sprint review

## Why convened

The Chairman asked the Council to review the completed working branch before
any commit, pull request, merge or deployment. The change is T3 because it
combines user-facing copy and layout changes, a newsletter integration,
production-route handling and a major Astro framework upgrade.

## Scope

- Review the exact uncommitted diff on `codex/integrity-conversion-sprint`.
- Test it against the Charter, editorial standards and the sprint brief at
  `.council/briefs/2026-08-05-integrity-conversion-sprint.md`.
- Return one role-specific verdict per required non-Chairman Council role:
  `pass`, `pass-with-edits` or `fail`.
- Identify only release-blocking findings and clearly separate later backlog.

## Out of scope

- Editing production or policy files during the review.
- Inventing primary-source verification, Buttondown configuration or data.
- Committing, pushing, opening a pull request, merging or deploying.
- Acting as the Chairman or granting publication approval.

## Agent assignments

- Editorial integrity panel: Head of Research, Fact-Checker, Standards Editor
  and Business & Real Estate Editor. Review claim force, provenance, voice and
  section fitness.
- Experience and discovery panel: SEO Strategist, Head of UX and Growth &
  Analytics Lead. Review redirects, indexability, responsive hierarchy and the
  newsletter journey.
- Release-governance panel: Managing Editor and Technical Lead. Review scope,
  tiering, evidence, build/dependency safety and readiness for the Chairman
  gate.

## Synthesis owner

Technical Lead convenes; Managing Editor principles control the synthesis. A
single Council recommendation will be written after all three panels return.

## Decision questions for the Chairman

1. Do all required non-Chairman roles pass the exact branch, or are edits
   required first?
2. Which findings are release blockers, and which belong in the post-launch
   backlog?
3. If the Council passes the branch, does the Chairman approve the framework,
   homepage, trust-copy, redirect and newsletter-posture changes for a PR?

## Renewed review after remediation

The Council re-reviewed the exact working tree after the Chairman selected the
organisational-byline route and the recorded copy, responsive, integration and
technical blockers were remediated.

All nine required non-Chairman roles now return `pass`: Head of Research, SEO
Strategist, Fact-Checker, Standards Editor, Head of UX, Technical Lead, Growth
& Analytics Lead, Business & Real Estate Editor and Managing Editor. The
Experience panel reported no release blocker. The Editorial panel accepted the
organisational-byline amendment and the clarified VAT and pre-sampling
boundaries. The Release panel accepted the Stage 7 evidence and the corrected
affiliate posture.

The branch is therefore routed to the Chairman gate. No commit, push, pull
request, merge or deployment is authorised by this Council verdict.
