2026-11-09; implementation staged, not deployed. The scarce-news diagnosis
was both a commissioning/output problem and a visibility problem. Removing
a weekly ceiling alone did not staff the hand-off from digest to reviewed
copy. Surfacing older reporting does not manufacture new output.

## Trade-offs

- More visible existing stories is useful discovery, not new journalism.
- Short timely copy can be practical without becoming a large comparison
  project; it still needs complete material evidence and review.
- Terms alerts are not rate recommendations or verified savings. Missing
  booking terms may justify a hold even near an offer deadline.
- A local queue makes ownership/blockers explicit but does not activate
  workers, authenticate source claims or approve publication.
- Viewport emulation and sampled accessibility checks are bounded evidence,
  not physical iPhone Safari or whole-site certification.
