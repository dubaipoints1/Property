# Council session: Travel news desks + editorial strategy

## Why convened

The site owner (Chairman authority) surfaced a screenshot of the Travel
mega-menu on mobile: it promised **Airline news**, **Airline reviews**,
**Hotel news**, **Hotel reviews**, **UAE staycations**, and
**Routes & destinations** — but every one of those links resolved to the
single mixed `/news/` feed (banking-dominated) or a generic index.
"Airline news" showed the owner credit-card stories. The navigation
promised sections the content model did not have.

Owner directive: (1) dedicated airline-news and hotel-news desks, each
owned by a specialist agent; (2) stories sourced by monitoring
headforpoints.com, thepointsguy.com, onemileatatime.com and
airline/hotel press channels, rewritten in the house voice with a
UAE/AED angle; (3) links/sections that actually work; (4) a
comprehensive editorial plan from the editorial team.

## Scope

- News taxonomy + routing: honest per-desk indexes wired to the nav.
- Two new agent definitions (airline-news, hotel-news desks).
- Sourcing policy: discovery via aggregators, verification via primary
  sources, attribution rules, plagiarism fence — discovery, not
  content laundering.
- Site-wide editorial strategy + cadence for all desks.
- Nav honesty for menu items whose content type does not exist yet.

## Out of scope

- Building hotel-review / staycation / route-page content (future
  briefs, scheduled in the strategy doc).
- Monetisation changes.
