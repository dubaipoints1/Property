An Emirates flight number doesn’t necessarily mean you’ll check in with Emirates. The two airlines sell some of each other’s flights. If you’re comparing tickets for a trip from Dubai, check who flies each leg, how much checked baggage your fare includes and how you can pay if you need more. A fare that looks cheaper before baggage may cost more for the trip you want.

## Open the flight details before paying

Look for **operated by Emirates** or **operated by flydubai** beside each flight instead of relying on the flight number. [flydubai explains the difference](https://www.flydubai.com/en/help/faq/?fc=emirates-and-flydubai-partnership&fn=interline): the airline selling the flight under its code isn’t always the one operating it.

Compare the checked allowance your booking shows in both directions. [Emirates says](https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/help/faq-topics/baggage-and-lost-property/faq/what-are-the-baggage-allowances-for-flights-with-other-airlines/) the allowance for your itinerary is displayed during booking and on the e-ticket receipt. If it isn’t clear before you pay, ask the airline selling the ticket to confirm it for the full itinerary. Don’t assume a general allowance table applies to every leg on a mixed booking.

## Check how you would buy extra baggage

[flydubai’s codeshare FAQ](https://www.flydubai.com/en/help/faq/?fc=baggage&fn=interline) says you can’t buy extra allowance online for a codeshare flight, but can pay for excess baggage at the operating airline’s departure terminal. [Emirates’ extra-baggage instructions](https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/before-you-fly/baggage/purchase-additional-baggage-allowance/) set conditions for its own purchase options. Don’t budget for an advertised advance-purchase discount unless the airline confirms it applies to your specific booking.

Before leaving for Dubai International, check the terminal on your confirmation and check in with the operating airline. [flydubai lists departures from Terminal 2 and some from Terminal 3](https://www.flydubai.com/en/help/faq/?fc=Airport+terminals&fn=interline). If you can’t confirm the excess charge before buying, treat the price of the extra bag as unknown, not zero. These checks cover a codeshare fare, not how bags move between separate tickets.
