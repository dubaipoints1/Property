**Start with where you’re staying, not the cheapest “Poland” result.** Air Arabia is adding two non-stop routes from Sharjah International Airport (SHJ) in December. Its [current UAE route page](https://www.airarabia.com/en/landing-pages/uae/shj-to-poland) lists different departure days for Gdańsk and Katowice, and the onward journeys are different too. The airline announced them in August; the December dates below are scheduled starts, not flights we’ve verified as operating.

| If your trip starts in… | Start by looking at… | Published flights from Sharjah |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gdańsk, Sopot or Gdynia | [Gdańsk Lech Wałęsa Airport (GDN)](https://press.airarabia.com/gdansk-becomes-the-latest-addition-to-air-arabias-poland-network-from-sharjah/) | From 14 December 2026: Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday |
| Katowice or the area around it | [Katowice International Airport (KTW)](https://press.airarabia.com/air-arabia-adds-katowice-to-its-growing-network-in-poland/) | From 17 December 2026: Monday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday |

## Work backwards from your hotel

[Gdańsk Airport](https://www.airport.gdansk.pl/en/before-travel/getting-to-the-airport/train) says to change at Gdańsk Wrzeszcz for trains into central Gdańsk. For Sopot or Gdynia, check whether the train on your date goes through or needs a change. If you are flying into Katowice, the [airport rail station](https://www.katowice-airport.com/en/for-passengers/directions/train) is called Pyrzowice Lotnisko. The airport lists trains that serve Katowice, but check the actual timetable rather than assuming a train lines up with your arrival. If Kraków is your destination, compare its separate Air Arabia route instead of treating Katowice as another name for the same airport.

Do the transfer check in reverse for the return. A flight that suits your arrival can still leave you with an inconvenient trip to the airport at the end of your holiday. Check both flight dates before paying for accommodation you cannot change.

## Compare what you’ll actually pay

For these UAE routes, Air Arabia's [fare table](https://www.airarabia.com/en/plan/reservation/fare-types) lists checked baggage as chargeable on Basic and included on Value and Ultimate. Compare the same bags on both legs, and check the inclusions for your chosen flights. Then put the final ticket and the airport transfers side by side. The lower headline fare is not necessarily the lower trip cost.

_Sources checked: Air Arabia’s two route releases, UAE booking and fare pages, and both airports’ rail directions, 15 November 2026._
