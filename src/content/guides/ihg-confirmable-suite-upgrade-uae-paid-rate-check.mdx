If you selected a Confirmable Suite Upgrade as an IHG One Rewards Milestone Reward, don’t book a more expensive stay just because you have the upgrade. Check the rate, the length of the stay and the hotel before you rely on it.

## Find the actual rate name

[IHG’s programme terms](https://www.ihg.com/content/gb/en/customer-care/member-tc/2nd-page) list Best Flexible, Member Discount and points Reward Night bookings as examples of eligible rates. A Free Night certificate is different and is excluded. IHG also excludes certain prepaid rates on which a deposit has already been taken, third-party online travel agency bookings and some properties, including Six Senses. An eligible stay can be up to five nights.

## Ask IHG to apply it before arrival

Contact IHG Customer Care from 14 days before arrival until 24 hours before check-in. The reward is for the member’s room. Depending on availability, IHG may confirm a premium room instead of a suite. Until IHG confirms the room, plan on the one you booked. An upgrade to a Club room does not by itself include the Club lounge or its other services.

A Confirmable Suite Upgrade is also different from paying with points. [IHG’s points guidance](https://www.ihg.com/content/us/en/customer-care/main/account/points-room-upgrade) says you can choose a higher room type when it is actually offered as a Reward Night. Don’t assume you can use extra points to add a suite to an existing booking.
