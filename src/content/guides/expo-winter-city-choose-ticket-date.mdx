If you are going to Expo City Dubai for Carols by Candlelight, choose the evening before you buy. [Winter City is at Al Wasl Plaza](https://www.expocitydubai.com/en/things-to-do/events-and-workshops/winter-city-and-disney-present-once-upon-a-holiday/) from [11 to 31 December 2026](https://dubai.platinumlist.net/event-tickets/winter-city/). The Carols programme is part of Winter City on specific dates, not a separate ticket to add to your basket.

## For Carols: choose 21, 22 or 23 December

The [2026 ticket seller](https://dubai.platinumlist.net/event-tickets/winter-city/) lists **Carols by Candlelight Under the Tree** on those three evenings. They have their own general admission price. Buy Winter City admission for the evening you want; viewing is first come, first served, subject to capacity. There is no separate Carols ticket, assigned seat or Express Entry on those dates. Check the programme for the timings of particular performances before setting off.

## For other dates: start with general admission

If you are going for the other Winter City activities, pick a date that works for your group and compare the ticket types for that date. On dates where Express Entry is offered, it applies at the **entrance**, not to queues inside. Don't pay for it expecting priority for individual activities.

Check the exact event name, date and total for your group in the basket. The seller lists tickets for a particular date as non-refundable and non-exchangeable; don't choose a night until you are sure the group can make it. Use the current Winter City listing rather than a standalone Carols page from a previous season.

_Sources: Expo City Dubai and its ticket seller, checked 15 November 2026. DubaiPoints did not test ticket availability or checkout._
