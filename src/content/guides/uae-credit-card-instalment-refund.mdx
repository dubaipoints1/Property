A merchant can accept a return while your card still shows an instalment plan. The refund tells you where the money is going; the plan tells you whether the bank will keep billing. Check both before changing what you pay this month.

## Find the plan name on your receipt

For [ADCB’s Slice checkout plan](https://www.adcb.com/en/get-in-touch/faqs/cards/slice.aspx), ADCB says the return follows the merchant’s policy. After the merchant processes a refund, the plan is adjusted or cancelled depending on the amount refunded. Look for Slice on the payment receipt and check your card statement for the matching plan.

The [noon Easy Installments help page](https://help.noon.com/portal/en/kb/articles/easy-installments-everything-you-need-to-know) describes a different arrangement: a return can be refunded to the card or to noon credits, and the customer must continue paying instalments under the bank’s terms. A retailer credit is not a card refund. Check the refund destination rather than assuming the money has reached your card.

## Ask the bank what will be billed next

Keep the refund receipt and find the original plan in your banking app or statement. Ask whether the refund has posted, whether the plan is still active, and what you need to pay by the next due date. If the plan is still running, ask whether it can be ended, whether that would make the remaining amount due sooner, and whether any fee would apply. [Visa’s UAE instalments FAQ](https://www.visa.com/en-ae/personal/products/installments) directs cardholders to their issuing bank for the treatment of a return.

Do not skip a payment because the merchant says the refund has been sent. Check the next statement for the refund and the revised plan, and follow the issuer’s confirmed payment instructions in the meantime.
