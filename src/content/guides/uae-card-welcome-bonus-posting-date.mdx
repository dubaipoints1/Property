You pay for a purchase you’d already planned. It goes through, but it’s still marked ‘pending’ in your banking app. If the offer counts purchases only once they’re posted to your card account, paying before the deadline isn’t enough: the purchase needs to post before the window closes. [HSBC UAE’s app guide](https://www.hsbc.ae/ways-to-bank/mobile/card-services-faqs/) explains the display: a transaction can appear straight away, but the pending label disappears when it settles. That explains the status, not the rules for any HSBC bonus.

## Check which clock applies

In [FAB’s November 2026 Cashback campaign terms](https://www.bankfab.com/-/media/fab-uds/personal/promotions/2026/cashback-credit-card-amazon-gift-card/acquisition_campaign_cashback_card_2026_en.pdf?view=1), the spending window starts when the card is issued, and eligible purchases have to post within that window. [Emirates Islamic’s Switch Cashback welcome offer](https://www.emiratesislamic.ae/en/offers/switch-cashback-credit-card-welcome-bonus) uses card approval to start its spending window. [Citi UAE’s digital acquisition terms](https://www.citibank.ae/content/dam/cgcpc/ae/prelogin/www-citibank-ae/doc/english/pdf/credit-cards/credit-card-offers-terms-and-conditions.pdf) give a separate window for the statement credit after you meet the spending requirement. Those are different events. If your offer doesn’t say whether the purchase date or the posting date counts, ask the bank before relying on a late purchase.

## What to check in the app

1. Save the terms for the offer you actually joined. Note what starts the clock and confirm the last qualifying day with the bank if it isn’t clear. Use the written terms that apply to your card and application route; a newer offer on the bank’s website may not be yours.
2. Match purchases against your posted activity and the offer exclusions. A posted purchase isn’t automatically eligible. FAB’s and Citi UAE’s example offers both exclude refunded purchases, so check your total again after a return.
3. Leave time for purchases you already planned to settle. If you come up short, don’t make an unplanned purchase just to earn the bonus. If you think you qualified but the reward hasn’t appeared, check the separate payout window, then take your receipts and statement dates to the bank.
