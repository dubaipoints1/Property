The best cashback card is the one whose qualifying purchases match your spending **after its fees and restrictions**. A large headline percentage is not enough: it may depend on a particular merchant, payment channel, account plan, spending tier or credit limit.

**Source review, 7 November 2026:** we have withdrawn universal annual-return rankings where the source conditions cannot be modelled reliably. Some issuer pages and linked documents disagree. The linked profiles identify those conflicts; a pending figure is not zero, and a retained source-record date is not a new whole-card verification.

These are comparison routes, not an ordered list of winners or a recommendation that you qualify.

## Build a spending check before a return estimate

Use a recent statement and separate restaurant bills, supermarket payments, fuel, subscriptions, bills and overseas purchases. Then check the issuer's definition of each category. A supermarket checkout is not necessarily the same category as a grocery order placed through a delivery platform.

For each card, establish:

- Which transactions qualify, including the payment channel and merchant classification.
- Whether the spending gate uses this statement, the previous month or another period.
- Which purchases count towards that gate and which earn a lower rate or nothing.
- Whether the cap applies per category, across rewards, to a credit limit or across account products.
- How cashback is redeemed, and whether redemption or expiry restrictions apply.

Only then calculate the reward on eligible spending, apply the relevant caps and deduct the costs that actually apply to you. Do not count the same purchase twice because it is both online and overseas.

<HotTip>
“Not estimated” means the available model cannot support that calculation. It does not mean the card earns nothing. A missing fee, cap or qualification condition must not be converted to zero to make a ranking look complete.
</HotTip>

## Treat account-linked and platform rewards separately

Liv's spending tiers and credit-limit cap, and Wio's account-plan and shared-cap conditions, need more than a generic salary or spending total. Confirm the actual plan and its costs before comparing their advertised returns.

Likewise, noon credits and talabat credit have a different redemption universe from cash. Compare them only against purchases you would make on those platforms anyway, and read the exact welcome-offer conditions rather than assigning the headline award a cash value.

## Before applying

Check the current application route, standard fee, any first-year or renewal waiver, and the issuer's own eligibility decision. An advertised minimum salary is not approval. For overseas use, distinguish an issuer charge from additional scheme or merchant conversion charges.
