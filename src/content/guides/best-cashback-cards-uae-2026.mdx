A cashback card's headline rate is the least useful number on its product
page. What a UAE cashback card actually pays is set by three other things:
the **spend floor** (many cards pay nothing in a month you spend under a
threshold), the **caps** (per category and per month), and the **annual
fee** after year one. A 6% card with an AED 5,000 monthly floor pays a light
spender less than a 1% card with none.

So this guide does not rank cards by headline. It runs the cards we cover
through three monthly spend profiles and shows the arithmetic, then names
the best card for each kind of spender. Every rate, cap, floor and fee
comes from our card database as verified on the dates shown on each linked
review. Annual fees are AED inclusive of 5% VAT.

This guide is for UAE residents with an AED salary. If you are choosing
your very first card, the
[entry-level roundup](/guides/best-entry-level-cards-2026/) ranks the same
market by salary band instead.

## The short answer

| If you are… | Take | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A light spender (around AED 3,500 a month) | [FAB Cashback](/cards/fab-cashback/) or [HSBC Live+](/cards/hsbc-live-plus/) | Low spend floors (AED 3,000), and 5% or 6% on the categories a small budget is made of |
| A household spending around AED 6,000 a month | [FAB Cashback](/cards/fab-cashback/), [HSBC Live+](/cards/hsbc-live-plus/) or [ADCB 365 Cashback](/cards/adcb-365-cashback/) | All three return roughly AED 1,950–2,100 a year net of fee on our profile |
| A heavy spender (AED 15,000+ a month) | [Liv Cashback+](/cards/liv-cashback-plus/) | 4% on everything once total monthly spend reaches AED 15,000, capped at AED 1,500 a month |
| A diner who wants no fee, ever | [Mashreq Cashback](/cards/mashreq-cashback/) | 5% on all dining, uncapped, free for life |
| Shopping Sharia-compliant | [ADIB Cashback Visa](/cards/adib-cashback-visa/) | 4% on five household categories with no monthly spend floor |
| Someone who wants one flat rate | [Citi Cash Back](/cards/citi-cashback/) | 1% on everything, 2% groceries, uncapped, no floor, fee waived at AED 9,000 a year |

The tables below show how each of those picks was reached.

## How we ran the numbers

Three monthly profiles, all spent in dirhams inside the UAE. None includes
government, utility or bill payments, which most cashback cards pay at or
near zero.

| Profile | Groceries | Dining | Fuel | Travel | Everything else | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Light | AED 1,400 | AED 700 | — | — | AED 1,400 | **AED 3,500** |
| Household | AED 2,000 | AED 1,500 | AED 500 | — | AED 2,000 | **AED 6,000** |
| Heavy | AED 4,000 | AED 3,000 | AED 1,000 | AED 2,000 | AED 5,000 | **AED 15,000** |

Each card's monthly cashback is the rate times the spend in each category,
with that card's caps and floors applied. The annual figure is twelve
months of that, **minus the annual fee you would pay in year two** — the
steady state, after any first-year waiver has run out. Where a card waives
its fee on annual spend and the profile clears the threshold, the fee is
zero. Welcome bonuses are one-off and are listed separately below.

## The household arithmetic, card by card

The AED 6,000 profile, worked in full so you can swap in your own numbers.

**[FAB Cashback](/cards/fab-cashback/)** — 5% on supermarkets, fashion and
dining, 1% on most other spend, AED 3,000 previous-month spend floor.
Groceries AED 2,000 × 5% = AED 100; dining AED 1,500 × 5% = AED 75; fuel
AED 500 × 1% = AED 5; other AED 2,000 × 1% = AED 20. **AED 200 a month,
AED 2,400 a year, AED 2,085 after the AED 315 fee.** Each 5% category stops
paying at AED 150 of cashback a month (AED 3,000 of spend). FAB lists a
small set of categories at 0.15%; if fuel is among them, take about AED 51
a year off. Cashback pays as FAB Rewards, redeemed in the FAB app.

**[HSBC Live+](/cards/hsbc-live-plus/)** — 6% dining, 5% fuel, 2%
groceries, 0.5% elsewhere, AED 3,000 monthly floor, AED 200 cap per bonus
category. Dining AED 1,500 × 6% = AED 90; groceries AED 2,000 × 2% = AED 40;
fuel AED 500 × 5% = AED 25; other AED 2,000 × 0.5% = AED 10. **AED 165 a
month, AED 1,980 a year.** The AED 313.95 fee is waived from year two above
AED 12,000 of annual spend, which this profile clears six times over.

**[ADCB 365 Cashback](/cards/adcb-365-cashback/)** — 6% dining, 5% fuel
and Salik, 3% groceries, 1% elsewhere, AED 5,000 monthly floor, AED 1,000
monthly cap. Dining AED 1,500 × 6% = AED 90; groceries AED 2,000 × 3% =
AED 60; fuel AED 500 × 5% = AED 25; other AED 2,000 × 1% = AED 20. **AED 195
a month, AED 2,340 a year, AED 1,957 after the AED 383.25 fee.** The fee has
no spend waiver. A month under AED 5,000 earns nothing.

**[ADIB Cashback Visa](/cards/adib-cashback-visa/)** — 4% on groceries,
fuel, dining, education and utilities, 0% on everything else. Groceries
AED 2,000 × 4% = AED 80; dining AED 1,500 × 4% = AED 60; fuel AED 500 × 4% =
AED 20; other AED 2,000 × 0% = AED 0. **AED 160 a month, AED 1,920 a year,
AED 1,816 after the AED 103.95 fee.**

**[Mashreq Cashback](/cards/mashreq-cashback/)** — 5% dining, 1% on most
other spend, 0.15% on the fuel, government, utilities and telecom bundle.
Dining AED 1,500 × 5% = AED 75; groceries AED 2,000 × 1% = AED 20; fuel
AED 500 × 0.15% = AED 0.75; other AED 2,000 × 1% = AED 20. **AED 115.75 a
month, AED 1,389 a year, no fee.**

**[Liv Cashback](/cards/liv-cashback/)** — one rate on all spend, set by
total monthly spend: 0.75% under AED 5,000, 1.5% from AED 5,000, 2% from
AED 10,000. AED 6,000 × 1.5% = **AED 90 a month, AED 1,080 a year, no fee.**

**[Citi Cash Back](/cards/citi-cashback/)** — 2% groceries, 1% everything
else, uncapped. Groceries AED 2,000 × 2% = AED 40; the other AED 4,000 ×
1% = AED 40. **AED 80 a month, AED 960 a year.** The AED 315 fee is waived
from year two at AED 9,000 of annual spend.

**[Liv Cashback+](/cards/liv-cashback-plus/)** — 1% under AED 7,000 of
monthly spend, 2% from AED 7,000, 4% from AED 15,000. AED 6,000 × 1% =
**AED 60 a month, AED 720 a year, AED 20 after the AED 700 fee.**

## Best for light spenders: FAB Cashback or HSBC Live+

At AED 3,500 a month the deciding number is the spend floor, not the rate.
ADCB 365's AED 5,000 floor rules it out entirely. FAB's AED 3,000 floor
(measured on the *previous* month's spend) and HSBC's AED 3,000 floor both
clear.

[FAB Cashback](/cards/fab-cashback/) comes out ahead on our light profile:
groceries AED 1,400 × 5% = AED 70, dining AED 700 × 5% = AED 35, other
AED 1,400 × 1% = AED 14 — AED 119 a month, AED 1,428 a year, AED 1,113 after
the fee. The catch is that the fee posts in full: FAB's year-one waiver ran
with a welcome cycle that closed on 31 May 2026, and FAB has published no
spend-based waiver.

[HSBC Live+](/cards/hsbc-live-plus/) pays AED 924 a year on the same
profile (dining AED 42 + groceries AED 28 + other AED 7 = AED 77 a month)
but costs nothing from year two, because AED 42,000 a year clears its
AED 12,000 waiver. If your monthly spend sometimes dips under AED 3,000,
both cards pay nothing for that month — FAB for the month *after*.

The [ADIB Cashback Visa](/cards/adib-cashback-visa/) is close behind at
AED 904 net, and it has no monthly spend floor at all, which makes it the
safest choice if your spend is uneven.

## Best for a household budget: FAB, HSBC or ADCB 365

On the AED 6,000 profile the three cards land within about AED 130 of each
other, so pick on the shape of your spend:

- **Groceries are your biggest line** — [FAB Cashback](/cards/fab-cashback/),
  the only card here paying 5% at the supermarket on an everyday budget.
- **Dining and fuel lead** — [HSBC Live+](/cards/hsbc-live-plus/) at 6% and
  5%, with a 2% FX fee that is low for the UAE if you also spend abroad.
  Its AED 200 per-category caps start to bite above about AED 3,300 of
  monthly dining.
- **You reliably clear AED 5,000 every month** —
  [ADCB 365 Cashback](/cards/adcb-365-cashback/), the broadest earn table
  (it also pays 5% on fuel, Salik and digital subscriptions). It is the
  riskiest of the three: one month under the floor earns nothing.

## Best for heavy spenders: Liv Cashback+

At AED 15,000 a month, [Liv Cashback+](/cards/liv-cashback-plus/) pays 4%
on everything: AED 15,000 × 4% = AED 600 a month, AED 7,200 a year,
AED 6,500 after the AED 700 annual fee. No category card on the ranked
list comes close, because their bonus categories are capped and the rest
of the spend earns 1% or less.

Two cautions, both from Liv's own terms. The 4% is a tier on **total**
monthly spend, not a rate you can count on: at AED 14,999 the whole month
earns 2%. And cashback is capped twice — at AED 1,500 a month, and at a
share of your credit limit (Liv's own example: an AED 20,000 limit caps
cashback at AED 800 a month). Liv also charges an AED 700 joining fee,
waived with an active Liv Max subscription, and issues either the
Cashback+ or the free Liv Cashback to a customer, not both.

Its points can also be converted to Emirates Skywards Miles, up to 10,000
a month; Liv does not publish the conversion ratio in the material we hold.

Among the category cards, [ADCB 365 Cashback](/cards/adcb-365-cashback/)
does best on the heavy profile (AED 420 a month, AED 4,657 a year net),
well inside its AED 1,000 monthly cap.

## Best free-for-life card: Mashreq Cashback

[Mashreq Cashback](/cards/mashreq-cashback/) never charges a fee and pays
5% on all dining — local, international and food delivery — with no cap
and no spend floor. On every profile here it beats Citi, and on the light
and household profiles it beats the free Liv Cashback.

What it does not do is pay well on anything else: 1% on general spend and
0.15% on fuel and bills since Mashreq's 13 June 2026 cut. If dining is a
small part of your spend, the [Liv Cashback](/cards/liv-cashback/) is the
better free card once you spend AED 10,000 a month (2% on everything,
capped at AED 750 a month).

## Best Islamic card: ADIB Cashback Visa

The [ADIB Cashback Visa Covered Card](/cards/adib-cashback-visa/) pays 4%
on groceries, fuel, dining, education and utilities, with no minimum
monthly spend. Its caps are AED 300 each on groceries, fuel and education,
AED 200 on dining and AED 100 on utilities, inside an AED 1,000 monthly
total. The fee is AED 103.95 a year.

It pays **nothing** outside those five categories, including all
international spend. Its closest Islamic rival in our data,
[DIB Consumer Cashback](/cards/dib-consumer-cashback/), pays 3% on
groceries and fuel only, behind an AED 3,000 monthly floor.

## Strong numbers, held out of the ranking

We do not rank a card while a field that decides the ranking is under
re-verification. These four would place well on the profiles above; the
reason each is held is stated plainly.

- **[Emirates Islamic Switch Cashback](/cards/emirates-islamic-switch-cashback/)**
  — on our household profile in its Lifestyle mode, it pays groceries
  AED 2,000 × 4% = AED 80, dining AED 1,500 × 4% = AED 60, fuel AED 500 × 8% =
  AED 40 and other AED 2,000 × 1% = AED 20: AED 200 a month, AED 2,400 a
  year, with the AED 313.95 fee waived above AED 30,000 of annual spend.
  That would top the household table. You pick Lifestyle or Travel mode
  each month and earn only that mode's rates, there is an AED 2,500 monthly
  floor, and the caps are tight (AED 100 fuel, AED 200 on each other
  category). **Held because its eligibility data is under re-verification.**
- **[Wio Credit](/cards/wio-credit/)** — 2% on all purchases, capped at
  AED 2,500 a month, no card fee. The price is a Wio plan: cashback needs
  the Salary plan (free with an AED 15,000+ monthly salary transfer), Plus
  or Family (AED 49 a month each). On the household profile that is
  AED 6,000 × 2% = AED 120 a month, AED 1,440 a year on the Salary plan, or
  AED 852 after twelve months of Plus. **Held because its eligibility data
  is under re-verification.**
- **[RAKBANK World](/cards/rakbank-world/)** — 10% on supermarkets, dining
  and travel in months you spend AED 10,000 or more, capped at AED 300,
  AED 300 and AED 400 a month; 1% elsewhere, capped at AED 100. On the heavy
  profile: groceries capped at AED 300, dining AED 300, travel AED 200,
  other AED 60 — AED 860 a month, AED 10,320 a year, with the AED 997.50
  fee waived above AED 100,000 of annual spend. Minimum salary AED 20,000.
  **Held because our review has flagged the earn grid for re-verification:
  RAKBANK's current page and older terms state different caps.**
- **[Standard Chartered Platinum X](/cards/sc-platinum-x/)** — **held
  because its earn rates are flagged for re-verification** in our data.

## What to check before you apply

1. **Your worst month, not your average one.** A card with a spend floor
   pays nothing in a month you miss it. If your spend swings, weight the
   floor-free cards (ADIB, Mashreq, Citi) more heavily than the tables do.
2. **Where the cap lands.** Divide the category cap by the rate to get the
   spend at which the bonus stops: HSBC's AED 200 dining cap at 6% stops at
   about AED 3,333 of dining a month.
3. **The fee after year one.** Most of these cards are free in year one.
   The tables use the year-two fee because that is what you keep paying.
4. **Spend abroad separately.** None of the profiles includes foreign
   spend. FX fees on these cards run from 1.99% (Liv) to 3.7% (DIB), so a
   1% or 3% foreign-spend rate is usually a net cost.

## Bottom line

There is no single best cashback card in the UAE; there is a best card for
how much you spend and where. Under about AED 5,000 a month, take a card
with a low or no floor: FAB Cashback if you can carry its AED 315 fee,
HSBC Live+ or ADIB if you cannot. Around AED 6,000, FAB, HSBC and ADCB 365
are within a rounding error of each other, so match the earn table to
your biggest line. From AED 15,000 a month, Liv Cashback+ is the card to
beat. Mashreq Cashback is the free card for anyone who eats out.
