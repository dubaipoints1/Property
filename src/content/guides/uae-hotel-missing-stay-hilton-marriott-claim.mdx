If you checked out of a UAE hotel and your stay isn’t in your Hilton Honors or Marriott Bonvoy account, pull up the booking confirmation and the final itemised hotel bill. Check who took the booking, whether the property participates and whether the rate was eligible. An ordinary booking through an online travel agency generally doesn’t qualify for stay credit under [Hilton’s rules](https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors/terms/) or [Marriott’s rules](https://www.marriott.com/loyalty/terms/default.mi), even if you added a membership number at reception.

## Hilton Honors

[Hilton says to allow up to two weeks](https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors/support-faq/) after checking out of a hotel outside North America, which includes the UAE. If your eligible stay still hasn’t appeared, sign in and follow [Hilton’s missing-stay instructions](https://www.hilton.com/en-gb/help-center/reservations/add-account-number-to-reservation/). Keep a readable copy of the hotel bill. Hilton requires it for a request for missing points or eligible spend, and must receive the request within six months of the stay.

## Marriott Bonvoy

Sign in through the [Marriott Missing Stay form](https://www.marriott.com/loyalty/myAccount/missingStayRequest.mi) and have the hotel bill to hand. Marriott’s programme rules generally require requests within one year of the eligible activity, with property and programme exceptions. If you need a copy of the bill, ask the hotel using your reservation details. Make sure you can identify the hotel and stay dates before starting. Compare the stay dates on the bill with the dates in your account.

## Check which credit is missing

If the stay appears but the promotional bonus doesn’t, check the promotion’s registration requirement and posting window separately. If neither appears, start with the stay itself and keep the confirmation and final bill together. For your next stay, confirm the right membership number is on the reservation at check-in. Keep the final bill until the activity appears, and don’t assume other stays at the same property will qualify if you booked them at a different rate or through a different seller.
