Fuel is where UAE card earn tables are least generous. Many banks class
petrol with government payments, utilities and telecom as a low-margin
category and pay a fraction of their base rate on it: 0.15% on the
Mashreq Cashback, 0.2% on most Emirates NBD Plus Points cards. A handful
of cards go the other way and pay 4–5% at the pump, but almost all of
them cap the fuel bonus and ask for a minimum monthly spend first.

This guide sets out which cards pay on fuel, how much at two monthly fuel
bills, and where each card's fuel cap lands. Every rate, cap, spend floor
and fee comes from our card database as verified on the dates shown on
each linked review. Annual fees are AED inclusive of 5% VAT.

The arithmetic is the rate times the monthly fuel bill, times twelve. For
HSBC Live+ at AED 1,000 a month: AED 1,000 × 5% = AED 50 a month, well
inside the AED 200 cap, so AED 600 a year. For DIB at AED 1,000:
AED 1,000 × 3% = AED 30, inside its AED 100 cap, so AED 360 a year.
LuLu Points are valued at their published face value of AED 0.01 a point
and redeem only at LuLu stores.

The spend floor is measured on **total** card spend, not fuel. A fuel
bill on its own will not clear HSBC's AED 3,000 or ADCB 365's AED 5,000,
so these cards pay their fuel rate only if they are also your main card.
In a month under the floor, neither pays any cashback at all.

## Best fuel rate: HSBC Live+

[HSBC Live+](/cards/hsbc-live-plus/) pays 5% on fuel, capped at AED 200
of cashback a month, so the bonus runs to AED 4,000 of fuel a month, more
than almost any household spends. It also pays 6% on dining and 2% on
groceries and entertainment, each under its own AED 200 cap, and 0.5% on
everything else.

The AED 313.95 fee is waived from year two if you spend AED 12,000 a
year on the card. The AED 3,000 monthly spend floor is the real condition:
in a month you spend less, the whole card earns 0.5%. The minimum salary
is AED 5,000 for employees of HSBC-approved companies and AED 12,500 for
everyone else, per our data.

## Best with Salik: ADCB 365 Cashback

[ADCB 365 Cashback](/cards/adcb-365-cashback/) also pays 5%, on fuel
**and** Salik toll top-ups, the only card in our data that names Salik in
a 5% category. Its other rates are the broadest on this list: 6% dining,
5% on digital subscriptions, 3% groceries and 1% elsewhere, within an
AED 1,000 monthly cashback cap.

The cost is a stricter floor and a fee with no waiver. It pays nothing in
a month you spend under AED 5,000, and the AED 383.25 fee applies from
year two whatever you spend. Take it if you reliably clear AED 5,000 a
month and fuel and tolls are a large part of it.

## Best with no spend floor: ADIB Cashback Visa

The [ADIB Cashback Visa Covered Card](/cards/adib-cashback-visa/) pays 4%
on fuel and automobile spend with no monthly minimum, capped at AED 300 a
month (AED 7,500 of fuel). At AED 1,000 a month it returns AED 480 a
year, AED 376.05 after its AED 103.95 fee, on fuel alone. Our data also
records free roadside assistance once a year.

It pays 4% on groceries, dining, school fees and utilities too, but
nothing on any other spend, including abroad. It is Sharia-compliant.

## Best for LuLu households: Emirates NBD LuLu 247 Platinum

The [LuLu 247 Platinum](/cards/emirates-nbd-lulu-247-platinum/) pays 4%
on fuel as LuLu Points, alongside 7% at LuLu and 2% on utilities. Our
data records no fuel cap and no spend floor. It costs AED 525 to join
and AED 262.50 a year, with no waiver published, and needs an AED 12,000
salary.

On fuel alone the fee is hard to justify: AED 1,000 a month of fuel
returns AED 480 a year in LuLu Points, AED 217.50 after the fee. The card
works when the LuLu shop is large too; our
[grocery-cards guide](/guides/best-grocery-cards-uae-2026/) covers that
side. Remember the points spend only at LuLu.

## Free cards that pay on fuel

Three free-for-life cards pay 2% on fuel in LuLu Points:
the [ADCB LuLu Titanium Gold](/cards/adcb-lulu-titanium-gold/) and
[Emirates NBD LuLu 247 Titanium](/cards/emirates-nbd-lulu-247-titanium/),
both from an AED 5,000 salary, and the
[ADCB LuLu Credit Card](/cards/adcb-lulu-platinum/) from AED 8,000. At
AED 500 a month that is AED 120 a year of LuLu credit. Small, but on a
card that costs nothing.

The [ADCB TouchPoints Platinum](/cards/adcb-touchpoints-platinum/) earns
2 TouchPoints per AED 1 on fuel. ADCB does not publish an AED value for
TouchPoints, so we cannot put a dirham figure on it.

## Cards that pay almost nothing on fuel

Worth knowing before you put petrol on your everyday card:

- [Mashreq Cashback](/cards/mashreq-cashback/): 0.15% on fuel, part of a
  government, utilities and telecom bundle, since 13 June 2026.
- Emirates NBD Plus Points cards ([Visa Platinum](/cards/emirates-nbd-visa-platinum/),
  [Visa Flexi](/cards/emirates-nbd-visa-flexi/),
  [Mastercard Platinum](/cards/emirates-nbd-mastercard-platinum/),
  [Visa Infinite](/cards/emirates-nbd-visa-infinite/)): 0.2%.
- [SHARE Visa Signature](/cards/emirates-nbd-share-visa-signature/) 0.1%
  and [SHARE Visa Infinite](/cards/emirates-nbd-share-visa-infinite/)
  0.15%.
- [Liv Cashback](/cards/liv-cashback/) and
  [Liv Cashback+](/cards/liv-cashback-plus/) pay 0.1% on "government,
  utility and other low-margin merchant categories". Our data does not
  say whether fuel is one of them, so we do not rank either card here.
- [FAB Cashback](/cards/fab-cashback/) pays 1% on most spend but excludes
  "select categories" at 0.15%. Our data does not say whether fuel is
  among them.

## Strong numbers, held out of the ranking

- **[Emirates Islamic Switch Cashback](/cards/emirates-islamic-switch-cashback/)**
  — 8% on domestic fuel in its Lifestyle mode, the highest fuel rate in
  our data, but capped at AED 100 a month, so the bonus stops at
  AED 1,250 of fuel. At AED 500 a month that is AED 40 a month, AED 480 a
  year; at AED 1,000, AED 80 a month, AED 960 a year. You earn the
  Lifestyle rates only in months you choose Lifestyle mode, and only once
  you spend AED 2,500 that month. AED 313.95 fee, waived above AED 30,000
  of annual spend. **Held because its eligibility data is under
  re-verification.**
- **[Emirates NBD Duo](/cards/emirates-nbd-duo/)** — 5% as Plus Points on
  fuel in months you spend AED 5,000, capped at 500 Plus Points a
  statement. No fee under the current promotion; the standard renewal is
  AED 840. **Abu Dhabi residents only**, so listed rather than ranked.

## Before you apply

1. **Price your real fuel bill.** At AED 500 a month, the gap between a
   5% card and a 2% card is AED 180 a year. A card fee can easily erase
   that.
2. **Check the floor.** HSBC, ADCB 365 and DIB pay their fuel rate only
   if total card spend clears a monthly minimum.
3. **Find the cap.** Divide the fuel cap by the rate: DIB stops paying 3%
   at about AED 3,333 of fuel a month, Emirates Islamic stops paying 8% at
   AED 1,250.
4. **Fuel bundles change.** Mashreq cut fuel from 0.33% to 0.15% on
   13 June 2026. Recheck your card's rate after any terms update.

## Bottom line

For most drivers the best fuel card is one that is also a good everyday
card: HSBC Live+ pays 5% at the pump and can cost nothing from year two,
and ADCB 365 Cashback adds Salik for households spending AED 5,000 a
month. If your spend is uneven, ADIB's 4% has no floor. If you want a
free card, the LuLu cards' 2% in LuLu Points is the most any
free-for-life card in our data pays on fuel.
