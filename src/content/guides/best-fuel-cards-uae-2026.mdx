A fuel reward matters only if **your petrol purchases qualify and the rest of your statement meets the conditions**. A headline rate cannot settle the choice without the eligible merchant definition, spending gate, cap, fee and redemption route.

## Check the whole statement, not just the petrol bill

A fuel bill may fit a reward category without meeting the card's total qualifying-spend requirement. Establish the measurement period, which purchases count towards it, and whether a previous month or statement affects the current reward.

Then check the fuel cap and any combined reward cap. A missing recorded cap is not evidence of unlimited earning. A limit tied to your assigned credit limit is also different from a public fixed monthly cashback allowance.

Do not assume the highest rate below a spending gate, or infer that the fallback must be zero. Where the source does not establish that outcome, ask the issuer.

For [ADCB 365](/cards/adcb-365-cashback/), keep fuel, Salik and subscription rules separate. Salik requires the stated direct-provider or Dubai Now route. Subscription boosts apply to eligible listed providers paid directly, not all digital spending or app-store payments.

## Keep the LuLu issuers and currencies separate

The similarly named ADCB and Emirates NBD LuLu products do not share one interchangeable points unit or redemption rule. Read each issuer's earning denominator and redemption terms together. Do not reuse one issuer's points-to-dirham conversion for the other.

The Emirates NBD LuLu profiles identify disagreements between the English and Arabic reduced-category rows. A disputed fuel or utility rate remains pending; it must not reappear as a settled pro or an annual-return example.

For ADCB, use the current named-store, other-store and reduced-category rows rather than the former broad fuel/base schedule. Restricted shopping rewards are not unrestricted cash, even if you already shop at LuLu.

## Check modes and redemption before estimating value

[Emirates Islamic Switch](/cards/emirates-islamic-switch-cashback/) has different Lifestyle and Travel modes. Do not combine their accelerated categories as if both operate in the same month. Qualification and foreign-currency uncertainties remain separate from the reward-mode choice.

Likewise, [Emirates NBD Duo](/cards/emirates-nbd-duo/) earns Plus Points, whose value depends on the redemption route. Native points are not automatically the same percentage of cashback, and the combined statement cap does not become a separate allowance for each physical card.

Keep welcome promotions and optional perks out of the basic fuel calculation. They need their own current eligibility and usage conditions; an unconfirmed lounge allowance cannot be used to make a fee look recovered.

## Before applying or moving your spending

Use a recent statement to identify eligible petrol purchases, then establish the exact rate, denominator, spending gate and cap. Deduct the costs that apply to your own arrangement, including joining and renewal charges where separately stated. Only calculate a return when those inputs are known.
