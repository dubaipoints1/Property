The 10-year **Golden Visa** is sold to readers as a residency
upgrade, but for the points-and-miles community its quieter benefit
is on the card-eligibility side. A bank reads a 10-year residency
as a sign that the customer is *staying*, but the premium banking
tiers that unlock the best cards are still gated by salary and
balance. This guide maps what the visa changes, and the thresholds
the banks actually publish.

## What the Golden Visa is (briefly)

The Golden Visa is a 10-year UAE residency, renewable, granted by
the **Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port
Security (ICP)** under one of several qualifying tracks: investor
(real-estate or business), specialised talent, scientist, top
student, humanitarian pioneer, and the "high-skill professional"
employee track introduced in 2022. The 2-year sponsored work visa
is the default for most expats; the Golden Visa is the upgrade.

For card-eligibility purposes the operative differences from a
2-year residency are:

- The visa does not require an employer sponsor, which means a
  job change does not trigger visa cancellation.
- Family sponsorship is broader and not capped by salary.
- The bank reads the visa as a 10-year residency horizon, which
  changes the underwriting view on long-tenor products (mortgages
  in particular).

The eligibility tracks themselves are detailed at the ICP site —
this guide focuses on the banking implications, not the
application process.

## How the banks' premium tiers treat the visa

None of the premium-banking pages we read on 10 October 2026 —
FAB Elite, FAB Private, Emirates NBD Priority and Private, Mashreq
Gold and Private, HSBC Premier — mentions the Golden Visa at all.
No bank publishes a Golden Visa route into its priority tier, and
we have found no published policy of flagging holders for card
upgrades. Read the visa as what it is to a bank: a residency
document for the KYC file, and a sign that you are staying.

The tiers themselves:

- **FAB (First Abu Dhabi Bank).** FAB Elite is the priority tier,
  served through FAB's Elite Centres; FAB Private is the
  private-banking tier. Neither page publishes an entry threshold.
- **Emirates NBD.** ENBD Priority and ENBD Private, with published
  thresholds below. The bank's Priority Banking Visa Infinite and
  Skywards Infinite cards are the ones its Priority page promotes.
- **Mashreq.** Mashreq Gold is the priority tier, with published
  thresholds below; Mashreq Private Bank publishes none. Solitaire,
  which earlier versions of this guide called a tier above Private,
  is a Mashreq credit card, not a banking tier.
- **HSBC.** HSBC Premier is a global premier tier with published
  UAE thresholds below.

## What thresholds actually gate the upgrade

The visa does not lower the bar. Each tier is gated by a salary or
balance threshold, and these are the ones the banks publish, read
from their own pages on 10 October 2026. FAB Elite, FAB Private and
Mashreq Private Bank publish none, so they are not in the table;
ask the bank.

ENBD and HSBC also admit customers through a home loan (AED 3
million or more at both), and Mashreq through a mortgage of AED 5
million or an annual insurance premium of AED 500,000. The ENBD
Private figure is published in dollars; the dirham equivalent uses
the 3.6725 peg. The Golden Visa does not substitute for any of
these thresholds.

## What the upgrade actually buys

The card-eligibility implication is the part most readers should
care about. A move from a standard current account to Priority /
Elite / Premier typically unlocks:

- Salary-transfer-required premium cards — the Emirates NBD Etihad
  Guest Elevate and Visa Infinite (AED 30,000 salary) and the
  [FAB Elite](/cards/fab-elite/) card (AED 40,000 minimum monthly salary on
  FAB's product page). Note that many premium
  cards, including the ENBD Skywards Infinite, need no salary transfer
  at all.
- Not, on our data, a lower FX fee: the FAB Elite card charges the same
  2.49% as FAB's other cards in our database, and ENBD's Visa Infinite
  the same 1.99% as its other cards.
- Concierge, lounge access (typically LoungeKey or DragonPass at
  the Priority tier, Priority Pass at Private), and travel
  insurance bundled at no extra cost.
- A dedicated relationship manager, which ENBD, Mashreq and HSBC
  all list as a tier benefit — the person to ask about a card
  upgrade or a fee waiver, though none of them publishes a waiver
  policy for tier customers.

## What it does not buy

Worth saying clearly: the Golden Visa does not change AECB
underwriting standards, does not lower the minimum-salary floor
for any specific card, and does not waive the security cheque
requirement. It is a residency document and a relationship
signal. It is not a credit-quality endorsement.

## What to do this week

1. If you hold the Golden Visa and have not informed your bank,
   send the updated visa page to your relationship manager. The
   bank's KYC record needs the new residency document and the
   update can trigger the tier-review conversation.
2. If you do not yet hold the Golden Visa but qualify under the
   investor or specialist-talent track, check the ICP eligibility
   page below and start the application before your next major
   credit-card move.
3. Re-read your existing card terms before you upgrade. We found
   no published policy at FAB or ENBD that waives a card's
   second-year fee for priority customers, so ask the relationship
   manager and get any waiver in writing.
