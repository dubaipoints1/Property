A point is not a standard unit of value. What matters is the programme that issues
it, the spending needed to earn it, and the redemption you can actually book.

This guide explains the decision, not a promised return. Our
[AED valuations](/valuations/) are editorial working estimates, not cash-out prices
or guaranteed minimum values. This is part of the
[DubaiPoints Expat Starter track](/guides/expat-starter/).

## Start with the currency you will use

**Emirates Skywards** earns Miles through eligible Emirates and flydubai activity,
co-brand cards and other programme partners. Start with the current
[Skywards guide](/airlines/skywards/), then check availability and the full payment
required for your intended trip.

**Etihad Guest** has its own earning, redemption and partner rules. A similarly
named Mile is not interchangeable with a Skywards Mile. Use the
[Etihad Guest guide](/airlines/etihad-guest/) and the programme's current booking
results rather than importing a Skywards valuation.

**Bank-proprietary rewards** may offer statement credit, shopping, travel or
transfers. Those are different exits, often with different rates. A point balance
is not an AED balance, and a transfer option is useful only when its current rate
and conditions suit a redemption you can use.

Neither bank points nor airline miles are protected from programme changes.
For example, [FAB's published Rewards terms](https://www.bankfab.com/-/media/fab-uds/personal/terms-and-conditions-consolidated/fab-reward-programmes/fab-rewards-terms-and-conditions-en.pdf?view=1)
reserve rights to change rewards benefits, eligible transactions, value and
redemption conditions. Holding bank points does not by itself remove devaluation
risk.

## Read the denominator before the headline

An earning rate must say what spend buys it. Miles per AED, Miles per USD
equivalent and points per a larger spending block are not directly comparable.
A multiplier sign alone does not tell you the cash return.

Check the rate that applies to your spending, not just the largest number on the
page. UAE cards may use different rates for foreign-currency purchases, particular
airlines, supermarkets, fuel, government payments or bill payments. Qualifying
spend and caps can matter more than the headline rate.

Our [Card Finder](/cards/finder/) and [comparison tool](/cards/compare/) retain the
listed earning basis. Read the conditions alongside the result; a ranking is not
confirmation that you meet the issuer's eligibility requirements.

## Compare a redemption with the cash ticket you would buy

Use a real, bookable itinerary and a cash alternative you would genuinely pay for.
Include taxes, carrier charges and other cash payments on the reward booking.
Check baggage, flexibility and refund conditions where they differ.

Then ask what cash payment the Miles actually avoid. An expensive published
premium-cabin fare can make a redemption look exceptional, but that is not the
same as saving money you would otherwise have spent.

The [cash-or-Miles calculator](/calculator/cash-or-miles/) helps compare your own
inputs. It is not an award-availability search, and it cannot turn an unavailable
seat into a usable redemption.

## Availability comes before a sweet-spot story

A quoted route price is not a permanent award chart. Programme changes, travel
dates, cabins and reward inventory can change both the Miles and cash required.
Do not apply for a card on the assumption that an old example will still be
available when its welcome Miles arrive.

Before transferring points, check the actual seats, the current transfer ratio,
the likely timing and whether the transfer can be reversed. Read the
[transfer-ratios guide](/guides/uae-transfer-ratios-2026/) for source and coverage
limits.

Shopping and gift-card redemptions are not automatically poor choices, nor is a
premium-cabin flight automatically good value. Compare the usable cash benefit,
the points required and the alternatives available to you.

## Choose the card after the use case

If you have a realistic airline redemption, compare cards that can earn the
relevant currency. Include the annual fee, any required account plan, the earning
rate on your actual categories and the conditions attached to perks.

If you mainly buy cash tickets or do not have a usable redemption in mind, compare
the cashback or statement-credit outcome as well. Do not pay for a complex
airline proposition solely because a large Miles balance looks attractive.

A welcome incentive is separate from the standing product. Check its current
deadline, qualifying spend, payment timing and exclusions. Do not assume a closed
campaign or an unpriced incentive is part of the ongoing return.

## Before applying

- Check the current issuer eligibility requirements. A minimum threshold is not
  guaranteed approval, and conflicting sources should be resolved with the bank.
- Compare fees on the same tax basis. An issuer foreign-currency fee may exclude
  additional scheme charges and the effect of the exchange rate.
- Read the current card review's source notes and verification date. A pending
  field is not a confirmed zero or an unconditional benefit.
- Keep a redemption in view, but do not spend more simply to earn points.
