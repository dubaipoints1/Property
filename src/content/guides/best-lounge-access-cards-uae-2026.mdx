Lounge access is the perk UAE banks advertise most and explain least. Card
pages say "complimentary airport lounge access" and stop there, without
saying which programme, how many visits a year, or whether the person
travelling with you gets in. Those three details decide whether the perk
is worth anything to you.

This guide collects what our card database records for every card we
cover that includes lounge access: the programme, the visit allowance,
the guest policy and any condition attached. Where a bank does not publish
one of those, we say so rather than fill the gap. Every figure comes from
our card database as verified on the dates shown on each linked review.
Fees are AED inclusive of VAT.

Two notes on what this guide does not do. It does not name specific
lounges at DXB, AUH or anywhere else: the programmes' lounge lists change,
and the bank pages do not commit to particular lounges. Check the
programme's app for your departure terminal before you rely on it. And it
puts a dirham value on a lounge visit only as our reviews do — an
**AED 200 walk-in equivalent per visit**, a modelling convention, not a
price quote.

For a sortable list of the cards whose lounge access is recorded in our
typed data, see [cards with lounge access](/cards/perks/lounge-access/).

## The short answer

- **Free, and unlimited:** [Emirates NBD Voyager World](/cards/emirates-nbd-voyager-world/)
  — unlimited Mastercard Travel Pass visits for the cardholder, no annual
  fee, AED 12,000 minimum salary.
- **Free, with a clear allowance:** [CBD One](/cards/cbd-one/) — eight
  visits a year via Visa Airport Companion, AED 5,000 minimum salary.
- **Low fee, with a guest allowance:** [FAB Cashback](/cards/fab-cashback/)
  — eight visits for you and six for a guest each year, AED 315.
- **Low fee, most visits:** [HSBC Live+](/cards/hsbc-live-plus/) — 12
  visits a year, fee waived from year two above AED 12,000 of annual spend.
- **Premium, unlimited with a guest:** [Emirates NBD Skywards Infinite](/cards/emirates-nbd-skywards-infinite/)
  or [FAB Etihad Guest Infinite](/cards/fab-etihad-guest-infinite/),
  depending on which airline you fly.

## What a lounge visit is worth to you

Our reviews value a visit at AED 200. A couple taking four return trips a
year and using a lounge on each departure from the UAE makes eight visits
— four for the cardholder, four for the guest:

- 8 visits × AED 200 = **AED 1,600 a year**, if the card covers the guest.
- 4 visits × AED 200 = **AED 800 a year**, if it covers the cardholder
  only and the guest pays.

That gap is why the guest policy matters as much as the visit count. On a
card that covers only the cardholder, a family of four gets one quarter
of the value.

At this level unlimited access with a guest is standard, so the lounge
alone should not decide between these cards. Choose on the airline or
loyalty programme first, then check the guest line:

- **You fly Emirates:** the [Skywards Infinite](/cards/emirates-nbd-skywards-infinite/)

At AED 200 a visit, a couple flying six return trips a year (12 visits)
gets AED 2,400 of lounge value at our convention: enough to cover a
AED 1,575 annual fee on its own, but not a AED 2,625 one.

The two ADCB Mastercard cards limit you to 14 visits a year. On the
Traveller, guests pay the Mastercard Travel Pass rate.

## Before you count on the lounge

1. **Find the programme's app.** Visa Airport Companion, Mastercard Travel
   Pass and DragonPass each run their own app and lounge list. Check your
   departure terminal there, not on the bank's page.
2. **"Unlimited" is the bank's word.** Several cards above say unlimited
   on the product page and leave the guest policy unstated. Ask before
   you bring someone.
3. **Meet the condition.** HSBC needs one international purchase a year;
   Citi needs non-AED spend; Emirates Islamic ties each perk to a monthly
   spend minimum.
4. **Banks change lounge providers.** Our reviews note that Emirates NBD
   has switched lounge providers on some cards before. Check after renewal.

## Bottom line

For most UAE residents the best lounge card is a free or low-fee one: the
Emirates NBD Voyager World for unlimited solo access at no fee, FAB
Cashback for a couple at AED 315, and HSBC Live+ for the most visits on a
card that becomes free from year two. Premium cards add unlimited guest
access, but at AED 1,000–2,625 a year the lounge should be a reason to
choose between them, not the reason to buy one.
