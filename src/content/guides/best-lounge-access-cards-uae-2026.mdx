An airport-lounge benefit is useful only if **you, your airport and your planned visit qualify**. The network name alone does not tell you the number of visits, whether a guest is included or whether a spending condition has been met.

**Source review, 7 November 2026:** several previously recorded lounge allowances and associated benefits were not established by the current captured terms. They are now withheld rather than reused in a “best value” ranking. A pending entitlement means unknown, not no access.

## Check access in this order

| Check | What to establish |
|---|---|
| Card and network variant | The exact product and physical-card network you hold |
| Registration | The correct benefit app or programme and any activation steps |
| Qualifying condition | Required card spending, eligible transaction currency and the measurement period |
| Visit allowance | Whether the source defines an allowance, its period and how visits are counted |
| Guests | Included guests, any separate allowance and charges beyond it |
| Lounge and journey | Participating airport, terminal, opening hours and visit-specific restrictions |

Do not turn an unlabelled visit count into an annual quota. “Unlimited” cardholder visits must not be read as unlimited complimentary guests.

## Allowances and costs belong together

Compare the confirmed card fee with the visits you would realistically use. Keep a joining fee separate from the renewal fee, and check whether a promotional waiver applies to your exact application. Do not assume the welcome award pays for either fee.

Then consider possible guest charges, visits outside the included allowance and any qualifying purchases needed before travelling. If the benefit requires a transaction in a specified currency, establish its timing and eligible amount directly with the issuer or programme. Avoid making an unnecessary purchase based on an incomplete summary.

## Do not infer benefits from card branding

Different network variants of a product family can have different benefits. The ADCB TouchPoints profiles now make those variant choices explicit rather than assigning every network entitlement to one universal card.

Similarly, an issuer page advertising a lounge programme does not by itself confirm every membership tier or guest rule. Current source notes identify where the recorded terms are incomplete or contradictory.

<HotTip>
Before leaving for the airport, check the exact card in the benefit app and the lounge for your terminal. An old screenshot of a card's benefits is not a substitute for current eligibility and visit conditions.
</HotTip>

## Keep lounge value separate from reward value

A lounge visit does not have a universal cash value. Count what you would otherwise pay for a visit or suitable alternative, not an assumed annual total for unlimited access.

Other travel benefits—airport transfers, insurance, hotel status and concierge services—need their own current terms. Do not add them into a package valuation merely because they appear beside the lounge headline.
