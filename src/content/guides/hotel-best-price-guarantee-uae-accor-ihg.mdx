A cheaper room on a booking site isn’t always the same booking. If you’ve booked directly with Accor or IHG and find a lower price elsewhere, first check whether it is a public rate for the same stay. Then check the chain’s deadline. It may arrive before your hotel’s free-cancellation deadline.

## Put both bookings side by side

Compare the hotel, dates, room category, guest count, meal plan and cancellation terms. Look at the total with charges, not just the nightly price in the search result. A cheaper prepaid room isn’t a match for a refundable one. Check that the lower rate is still bookable, and save the booking page and its conditions before the price changes.

## If you booked with Accor

Under [Accor’s terms](https://all.accor.com/a/en/information/best-price-guarantee-conditions.html), you need a confirmed direct booking and a lower public offer on the same type of channel: desktop website with desktop website, mobile website with mobile website, or app with app. Keep a dated screenshot showing the price and conditions to attach to the official claim. Submit within 24 hours of booking confirmation and no later than 48 hours before the planned arrival. Accor has separate rules for a price shown in another currency, so check those before treating the converted total as a match.

## If you booked with IHG

Under [IHG’s terms](https://www.ihg.com/content/ae/ar/customer-care/lowest-internet-rate-terms-conditions), the confirmed direct reservation must use its Best Available search. The other price must be bookable in the same currency, meet IHG’s minimum price difference and still be available when IHG checks. For a UAE hotel, don’t count on a member-only, card-only or coupon price qualifying. Submit within 24 hours of booking and leave more than 24 hours before the hotel’s standard check-in time. For the detailed rules in English, use [IHG’s published English terms](https://www.ihg.com/content/gb/en/customer-care/lowest-internet-rate-terms-conditions).

## Keep the booking you can afford to keep

If the cheaper rate isn’t comparable, decide whether you would still prefer it on its own terms. Don’t book a room you cannot afford on the assumption that the chain will lower the price, and check the existing cancellation deadline before switching.

*Accor and IHG’s published terms were checked on 20 November 2026. DubaiPoints did not file or test a claim.*
