If you’re flying from Dubai International (DXB) with a suitcase, you may be able to drop it off before heading to the airport. Emirates has desks in DIFC and Ajman; flydubai offers the service at its Sharjah Travel Shop. Start with your airline, then check whether the desk will be open when your flight is inside its bag-drop window.

## Find your airline’s desk

| Airline and location | Published desk hours | Bag drop before departure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emirates — [ICD Brookfield Place, DIFC](https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/manage-booking/difc-check-in/) | 08:00–22:00 daily | 24 to 4 hours |
| Emirates — [Ajman Central Bus Terminal](https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/manage-booking/check-in-options/) | Open 24 hours | 24 to 4 hours |
| flydubai — [Sharjah Travel Shop](https://www.flydubai.com/en/flying-with-us/check-in/city-check-in/) | 09:00–22:00 | 24 to 6 hours |

Count back from the departure time on your itinerary before making the detour. A desk can be open when it is already too late to drop your bag there. Check holiday hours before leaving home.

## Check who can use it

Emirates lists destination restrictions for DIFC and says everyone on the booking should be present. If you were planning to take the whole family’s cases in on your lunch break, check that before setting off. For Ajman, use Emirates’ own check-in page to confirm what the local desk requires.

Flydubai lists a handling fee per passenger and a route exception. It says the Sharjah desk serves eligible flights from both Terminal 2 and Terminal 3, and accepts bag drop after online check-in. The Sharjah shop is not where your flight leaves from: check the terminal on your booking, especially if another airline sold the ticket.

If the times don’t line up or your flight doesn’t qualify, use [Emirates’](https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/manage-booking/check-in-options/) or [flydubai’s](https://www.flydubai.com/en/flying-with-us/check-in/) airport check-in options. Dropping the bag early does not change your airport or gate arrival deadlines.
