The Abu Dhabi International Boat Show runs from 19 to 22 November at ADNEC Centre Abu Dhabi. Two details are worth getting right before you set off: the show is at **Marina Hall**, and two official listings disagree on when it opens.

## Head to Marina Hall

Use [ADNEC’s Boat Show listing](https://www.adnec.ae/en/eventlisting/abu-dhabi-international-boat-show-adibs-2026) for directions rather than relying on a generic convention-centre pin. If you’re driving, the [show’s ticket listing](https://tickets.virginmegastore.me/ae/event/34175/exhibition/abu) directs visitors to **Car Park D**. If you’re taking a taxi, specify **ADNEC Marina Hall**. Other events are listed at ADNEC on 19 November, so it’s worth being specific about your drop-off and where you’ll meet anyone joining you.

## Check the opening time before setting off

ADNEC lists **2pm–10pm**, while [Experience Abu Dhabi](https://visitabudhabi.ae/en/events/abu-dhabi-international-boat-show-2026) lists **3.30pm–10pm** for the same dates. If you want to arrive early, check the time on your ticket or with the organiser rather than assuming you can enter at 2pm. The shared 10pm closing time doesn’t establish when last entry is. The ticket seller also says there’s no re-admission, so if you’re meeting someone arriving later, agree to meet outside before either of you goes in.

## Check what your ticket covers

The [organiser’s announcement](https://www.adnec.ae/en/press-and-media/press-release-listing/abu-dhabi-international-boat-show-set-to-return-in-november-2026-with-expanded-offerings-and-exclusive-on-water-experiences) described new on-water experiences and a charter showcase. That tells you what the organiser planned, not what is included with every ticket. If a particular activity is the reason you’re going, check whether it runs on your chosen day and needs a separate reservation before paying or making the journey.

For a straightforward visit, save the Marina Hall directions, confirm your entry time and pick one thing you want to see first. For anything on the water, check access separately. If you’re choosing between days, compare the show schedule against the activity you want rather than assuming every activity runs on every day.
