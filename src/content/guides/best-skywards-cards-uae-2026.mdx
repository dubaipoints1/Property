Choose a Skywards card around **a redemption you can realistically use**, then compare the cost and conditions of earning towards it. A large Miles headline does not establish a good return on your spending, and a welcome award is not guaranteed value merely because it exceeds a card fee at an editorial valuation.

**Source review, 7 November 2026:** unrestricted annual-return examples have been withdrawn where reduced categories, statement caps or offer conditions cannot be modelled reliably. The linked profiles show the earning conditions and unresolved terms instead.

## Keep the spending denominator attached

A rate per US dollar is not the same as the same number per dirham. Read the reward currency, spending denominator and eligible merchant category together.

Then check reduced categories, geographical exceptions, excluded payments and statement caps. A premium tier's headline cannot safely be described as applying to every purchase. Where the sources give a qualified row, the comparison preserves that qualification rather than turning it into an all-spend estimate.

## Price the redemption before buying the earning route

Use the [Skywards programme guide](/airlines/skywards/) to check the programme context, then look up the actual flight and reward availability. Compare the cash fare with the Miles requirement, cash charges, booking flexibility and itinerary you would genuinely choose.

Our [Cash or Miles calculator](/calculator/cash-or-miles/) can help with a specific comparison. An editorial points valuation is a scenario input, not a guaranteed redemption value or an issuer promise.

## Separate welcome value from ongoing earning

Read the current offer for your exact application channel and customer category. “Up to” may combine different tranches with different conditions.

Keep months, days and billing statements distinct. A foreign-currency spending threshold must not be silently replaced by a rounded dirham amount. An ended gift-voucher campaign is not part of a current welcome offer.

Check the separately stated joining and renewal charges, any applicable waiver and the salary-transfer conditions. Where a qualification rule is unresolved, neither “required” nor “not required” is established.

## Other routes need their own conversion check

Cashback-to-Miles and transferable-points routes can be useful, but compare the actual conversion terms, account or plan conditions, transfer timing and reward usability. Do not assume a fixed transfer ratio or promotional bonus without checking the relevant current source.

Hotel points and Free Night Awards are also different instruments. A restricted hotel certificate cannot be counted as transferable airline Miles or unrestricted Bonvoy points.

Use the [UAE Transfer Finder](/transfer-finder/) to explore the recorded routes, then verify the current programme and issuer terms before transferring. The existence of a route does not establish that it is the best use of your points.
