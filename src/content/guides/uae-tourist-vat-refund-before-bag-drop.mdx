If you’re visiting the UAE and putting new purchases in your checked bag, leave the goods within reach until you’ve dealt with the tourist VAT refund. Planet can ask to inspect them. The time to find out is before you hand the bag to the airline, not at the refund counter on the other side.

## At the shop: check that a tax-free transaction exists

The [Federal Tax Authority’s tourist refund service](https://tax.gov.ae/en/services/tourist.vat.refunds.aspx) is for eligible visitors buying eligible goods from participating retailers. Ask a shop if it participates before treating any refund as part of the price. Use the same travel document you entered the UAE with.

Keep the original sales or tax invoice and check for the tax-free transaction. According to [Planet’s tourist FAQ](https://www.planetpayment.ae/tourists-landing/tourist-frequently-asked-questions/), that could be a tag with an invoice or a digital transaction you can see in its Shopper Portal. If the name or record is wrong, ask the retailer to fix it while you’re still in the UAE. An ordinary receipt alone is not proof that the shop issued a tax-free transaction.

## At the airport: go to validation before checking your bag

Have the original travel document, the goods, the invoices and your departure details ready. Start at Planet’s validation point before check-in. If the kiosk says the goods need inspection, take them to staff as directed rather than handing over the bag. Follow any further directions displayed at validation.

Validation and getting your money back are separate steps. The FTA says visitors choosing cash go to a participating cash partner after immigration once the transaction has been validated. If you choose a card refund, you can use [Planet’s refund status guidance](https://planetpayment.ae/tourists-landing/tax-refund/) to see what a pending or inspection-required status means. Don’t mistake a tag, a pending approval or a kiosk scan for confirmation money has been paid.

If you’re hosting visitors, send them the airport checklist before they pack: goods, original travel document, invoices and a checked tax-free record.
