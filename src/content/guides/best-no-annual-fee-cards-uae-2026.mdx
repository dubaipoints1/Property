"No annual fee" means three different things on UAE card pages. Some
cards are **free for life**: no fee is set at all. Some are **free under
a promotion**: the bank's schedule of charges lists a standard fee, and
the current offer waives it, which can end. And some are **free if you
spend**: the fee is waived each year you clear a spending threshold. All
three get called "free".

This guide sorts the no-fee cards in our database into those three
groups, then ranks them on a worked monthly budget. Every rate, cap,
spend floor and fee comes from our card database as verified on the
dates shown on each linked review. Fees are AED inclusive of 5% VAT.

## Free if you spend

Two cashback cards charge a fee from year two but waive it every year you
clear a spending threshold. For most people who use them as a main card,
that makes them free.

- **[HSBC Live+](/cards/hsbc-live-plus/)** — AED 313.95, waived from year
  two above **AED 12,000** of annual spend (AED 1,000 a month). The first
  year is free.
- **[Citi Cash Back](/cards/citi-cashback/)** — AED 315, waived from year
  two above **AED 9,000** of annual spend (AED 750 a month). The first
  year is free.

A waiver is conditional: in a year you spend less, the fee posts in full.

The working, card by card:

- **HSBC Live+:** dining AED 1,500 × 6% = AED 90; groceries AED 2,000 × 2%
  = AED 40; fuel AED 500 × 5% = AED 25; other AED 2,000 × 0.5% = AED 10.
  AED 165 a month. The AED 72,000 of annual spend clears the AED 12,000
  waiver.
- **ADCB LuLu Credit Card:** LuLu groceries AED 2,000 × 8% = AED 160;
  fuel AED 500 × 2% = AED 10; dining and other AED 3,500 × 1% = AED 35.
  AED 205 a month, in LuLu Points at AED 0.01 each.
- **SHARE Visa Signature:** Carrefour groceries AED 2,000 × 6% = AED 120;
  dining and other AED 3,500 × 1% = AED 35; fuel AED 500 × 0.1% = AED 0.50.
  AED 155.50 a month, in SHARE Points at 10 to the dirham.
- **Mashreq Cashback:** dining AED 1,500 × 5% = AED 75; groceries and
  other AED 4,000 × 1% = AED 40; fuel AED 500 × 0.15% = AED 0.75.
  AED 115.75 a month.
- **LuLu 247 Titanium:** LuLu groceries AED 2,000 × 3.5% = AED 70; fuel
  AED 500 × 2% = AED 10; dining and other AED 3,500 × 0.35% = AED 12.25.
  AED 92.25 a month.
- **Liv Cashback:** AED 6,000 of monthly spend sits in the 1.5% tier:
  AED 6,000 × 1.5% = AED 90. If Liv classes fuel among its 0.1%
  low-margin categories (our data does not say), it is AED 83 a month,
  AED 996 a year.
- **Citi Cash Back:** groceries AED 2,000 × 2% = AED 40; the other
  AED 4,000 × 1% = AED 40. AED 80 a month.
- **ADCB Essential Cashback:** AED 6,000 × 1% = AED 60.

Two conclusions. First, the best no-fee cash return on this budget is a
card that is only free because you spend on it: HSBC Live+ returns about
AED 590 a year more than Mashreq. Second, the store cards top the table
only if you would shop at that store anyway, and they pay in points that
work nowhere else. Measured in money you can spend anywhere, Mashreq is
the best free-for-life card on this profile.

## Best free-for-life cashback card: Mashreq Cashback

[Mashreq Cashback](/cards/mashreq-cashback/) has no annual fee on the
primary or supplementary cards and pays 5% on local and international
dining, including food delivery, with no cap and no spend floor. Other
spend earns 1%; fuel, utilities, government and similar payments 0.15%.
New customers get an AED 500 welcome bonus (AED 100 for existing Mashreq
card customers) on AED 5,000 of spend in the first two months.

If you eat out little and spend AED 10,000 or more a month,
[Liv Cashback](/cards/liv-cashback/) is the better free card: 2% on all
of that month's spend, capped at AED 750 a month.

## Best free card for travel: Voyager World or CBD One

- **[Emirates NBD Voyager World](/cards/emirates-nbd-voyager-world/)** —
  unlimited worldwide lounge access through Mastercard Travel Pass, and
  3 Voyager Miles per AED 1 on travel (6 on ENBD's Voyager Travel Portal),
  but 0.5 on everyday retail. AED 12,000 minimum salary, 1.99% FX fee.
  Welcome: 75,000 Voyager Miles on AED 35,000 of spend within the first
  three statements. Marketed free for life; see the promotion note above.
- **[CBD One](/cards/cbd-one/)** — eight lounge visits a year through Visa
  Airport Companion and multi-trip travel insurance up to USD 500,000,
  from an AED 5,000 salary. Its 3.5% FX fee makes it a poor card to spend
  on abroad.

## Before you apply

1. **Find out which kind of free it is.** Free for life, a promotion or a
   spend waiver. Only the first is certain at renewal.
2. **Price the points.** A store card's rate is real only at that store.
   Count it at zero for spend you would not have made there.
3. **Check the FX fee if you travel.** Free cards in our data range from
   1.99% (Emirates NBD and Liv) to 3.5% (CBD One).
4. **Compare against a waived card.** If you will spend AED 12,000 a year,
   HSBC Live+ costs nothing from year two and pays more than any
   free-for-life card on our household profile.

## Bottom line

For money back that spends anywhere, the best card that is free for life
is Mashreq Cashback, and the best card that is free in practice is HSBC
Live+, once you spend AED 1,000 a month on it. If one supermarket gets
most of your grocery budget, the free ADCB LuLu card for LuLu or SHARE
Visa Signature for Carrefour pays more, in points that stay in that
store. For travel, the Voyager World and CBD One are the free cards with
lounge access.
