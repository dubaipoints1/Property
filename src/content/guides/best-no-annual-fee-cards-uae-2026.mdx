"No annual fee" means three different things on UAE card pages. Some
cards are **free for life**: no fee is set at all. Some are **free under
a promotion**: the bank's schedule of charges lists a standard fee, and
the current offer waives it, which can end. And some are **free if you
spend**: the fee is waived each year you clear a spending threshold. All
three get called "free".

This guide sorts the no-fee cards in our database into those three
groups, then ranks them on a worked monthly budget. Every rate, cap,
spend floor and fee is taken from an existing card source record, not newly
re-verified by this bounded correction. Current profile source dates and Pending
qualification take precedence. Fees are AED inclusive of 5% VAT.

## Free if you spend

Two cashback cards charge a fee from year two but waive it every year you
clear a spending threshold. For most people who use them as a main card,
that makes them free.

- **[HSBC Live+](/cards/hsbc-live-plus/)** — AED 313.95, waived from year
  two above **AED 12,000** of annual spend (AED 1,000 a month). The first
  year is free.
- **[Citi Cash Back](/cards/citi-cashback/)** — AED 315, waived from year
  two above **AED 9,000** of annual spend (AED 750 a month). The first
  year is free.

A waiver is conditional: in a year you spend less, the fee posts in full.

## Before you apply

1. **Find out which kind of free it is.** Free for life, a promotion or a
   spend waiver. Read the current terms rather than assume that a label
   guarantees every future renewal charge.
2. **Price the points.** A store card's rate is real only at that store.
   Count it at zero for spend you would not have made there.
3. **Check the FX fee if you travel.** Free cards in our data range from
   1.99% (Emirates NBD and Liv) to 3.5% (CBD One).
4. **Compare against a waived card.** Confirm the waiver and qualification
   terms first. An older worked example does not establish that HSBC Live+
   outperforms a card whose total return is currently unestimated.

## Bottom line

Mashreq may suit dining-led spending, but its assigned-limit restriction and
Pending everyday local rate prevent an unconditional best-return conclusion.
The other historical examples illustrate the difference between a spend waiver
and a free-for-life card; HSBC qualification remains Pending. If one supermarket gets
most of your grocery budget, the free ADCB LuLu card for LuLu or SHARE
Visa Signature for Carrefour pays more, in points that stay in that
store. For travel, the Voyager World and CBD One are the free cards with
lounge access.
