If you need cash at a UAE ATM, check which account will be charged before you confirm. Drawing on your bank account and borrowing on your credit card are different transactions. When the cash is charged to your credit card, your bank may call it a **cash advance**. Don’t assume it works like paying for a purchase on the same card.

## Before you confirm the withdrawal

- **Look for the cash advance fee.** Check your card’s schedule of charges and whether there is a minimum fee. A small withdrawal does not necessarily mean a small charge. If you use a debit card instead, check the account and ATM charges separately.
- **Check when interest starts.** [Emirates NBD’s cash advance guidance](https://www.emiratesnbd.com/en/help-and-support/cash-advance-on-your-credit-card) and [Abu Dhabi Commercial Bank’s consumer terms](https://www.adcb.com/en/multimedia/tnc/adcb-cbg-tcs.pdf#page=88) both describe a withdrawal charge and interest from the transaction date. The usual interest-free treatment for eligible purchases does not cover the cash advance. Emirates NBD also says its separate advance fee still applies if you repay the advance in full.

## If you’ve already taken the cash

Ask your issuer what amount would clear the advance **on the day it expects to credit your payment**. If the card also has purchases or instalments, ask how that payment will be applied. [Abu Dhabi Commercial Bank’s payment terms](https://www.adcb.com/en/multimedia/tnc/adcb-cbg-tcs.pdf#page=92), for example, distinguish receiving cleared funds from applying them across different balances. Transferring the amount you withdrew does not by itself tell you whether you have cleared the charge and accrued interest.

After paying, check that the payment was credited and look for any remaining finance charge on the next statement. Check the amount and due date on your regular statement as well. If you are reading an ordinary Emirates NBD purchase statement, see [which amount is due](https://dubaipoints.ae/guides/emirates-nbd-first-card-statement-payment-check/).
