Before you move points, check two things: **how many miles will land** and
**what you give up by transferring**. Start with the
[UAE Transfer Finder](/transfer-finder/) for the published ratio and
minimum for your exact rewards currency. Check your bank’s final quote
before confirming. A route listed for one currency or card does not
automatically apply to the issuer’s other products.

## The currency map at a glance

Use the [UAE Transfer Finder](/transfer-finder/) to start with a bank
rewards currency or filter for
[Emirates Skywards](/transfer-finder/?to=skywards),
[Etihad Guest](/transfer-finder/?to=etihad-guest) or
[Qatar Privilege Club](/transfer-finder/?to=qatar-privilege-club).
The Finder links to each issuer’s evidence. If public sources disagree or
do not publish a condition, check the issuer’s live transfer screen.
A route missing from the Finder may still be available; this is not a
market-wide list. Direct-earning cards are not bank-to-airline transfers.

## Direct-earn vs transfer-earn — when each makes sense

A co-brand card normally earns the airline’s currency without a separate
bank-to-airline conversion. That can be useful when you mostly fly one
airline and already have a realistic redemption. A transferable card
can give you alternatives if you change plans, but its headline bank
points per dirham and the miles you actually receive are different things.

To compare two cards, multiply the bank points you can actually earn by
the verified miles received per bank point. Then compare that with the
direct-earn card after fees, spend conditions and caps. The
[Card Finder](/cards/finder/) and [Compare](/cards/compare/) show the
published conditions and separate first-year from ongoing spend value.

## The transfer-bonus trap

A bonus increases the miles received, not the likelihood of finding a
seat. Check the exact transfer date, participant list, minimum, cap and
posting time. Registration and posting are not the same as transferring.
An expired campaign is not a base transfer rate. Check the issuer and the
[Deals desk](/deals/) for dated offers rather than assuming a bonus repeats.

## What we'd actually transfer (and what we wouldn't)

1. Price the award, its cash charges, and the cash ticket you would
   otherwise buy. Use the [Cash or Miles calculator](/calculator/cash-or-miles/)
   to find the implied value in fils per mile.
2. Work out how many miles the transfer would add, including only a
   verified bonus you qualify for. Compare the value of those miles, less
   any transfer fee, with the cashback, statement credit or hotel stay
   you would give up. Keep all the figures in AED before dividing.
3. Confirm the seat and check whether the transfer can be reversed and
   when the miles should arrive. If the issuer does not publish a timing,
   do not assume the award will still be available when your miles post.

## Bottom line

Transfer for a specific redemption, not simply because a bonus exists.
