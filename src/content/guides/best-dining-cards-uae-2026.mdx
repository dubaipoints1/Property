Start by separating **restaurant bills, food delivery and named-platform orders**. They may look like the same spending to you but qualify differently under an issuer's reward rules. The best dining card depends on where the transactions are processed, the spending conditions you meet and the rewards you can use.

## Check the whole statement, not just the dinner bill

A dining reward may depend on total qualifying card spend, an assigned credit limit or a previous statement period. Ask which transactions count towards the gate, how refunds are treated and whether a reduced rate applies below it. Do not assume a fallback rate when the source does not establish one.

Next, establish the cap. A category cap, a combined monthly cap and a spending-eligibility limit are different constraints. Check what happens after the limit: a lower rate, no reward and an issuer-unpublished outcome must not be treated as interchangeable.

For overseas dining, retain the transaction-currency rules and all applicable conversion charges. A dining reward percentage minus an issuer fee alone is not an exact net overseas return.

## Delivery is not one category

A direct restaurant payment, a delivery-app order and a transaction through a named co-brand platform can follow different rules. The noon One profile therefore shows individual platform rows rather than calling its headline a rate for all dining. The talabat profile distinguishes restricted talabat credit from statement credit or cash.

Before applying for a platform card, check whether your usual merchant, order type and payment channel qualify. Count only the orders you would place anyway; do not increase spending to make a welcome threshold or a cap feel valuable.

<HotTip>
A dining discount is not cashback. Compare the final bill for the same meal, on the same terms, through the booking channel you would actually use. A membership's advertised reach is not proof that your preferred restaurant participates.
</HotTip>

## Keep optional perks out of the basic calculation

Cinema offers, dining memberships and buy-one-get-one programmes may make a card more useful, but their current entitlement must be verified separately. Do not add an assumed annual value for a perk whose outlets, allowance or eligibility remain unresolved.

For a restaurant shortlist, check the issuer's live offer and the restaurant's booking terms. A card image or a network logo does not establish a specific benefit.
