Dining is the category UAE cashback cards compete hardest on. Five cards
in our database pay 4–6% on restaurant bills, and one of them charges no
fee at all. The differences that decide which one you should carry are
the **cap** (several stop paying at a few thousand dirhams of dining a
month), the **spend floor** (some pay nothing unless total card spend
clears a minimum), and the **fee** after year one.

This guide works the dining cards through three monthly restaurant bills,
then sets out the dining discounts and 2-for-1 perks our data records.
Every rate, cap, spend floor and fee comes from our card database as
verified on the dates shown on each linked review. Annual fees are AED
inclusive of 5% VAT.

## The short answer

| If you… | Take | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Want one dining card with no fee and no fine print | [Mashreq Cashback](/cards/mashreq-cashback/) | 5% on all dining and food delivery, uncapped, no spend floor, free for life |
| Spend up to about AED 4,000 a month on dining | [HSBC Live+](/cards/hsbc-live-plus/) | 6% on dining up to AED 200 a month; fee waived from year two above AED 12,000 of annual spend |
| Spend over AED 5,000 a month on the card in total and dine out heavily | [ADCB 365 Cashback](/cards/adcb-365-cashback/) | 6% on dining including online orders, within an AED 1,000 monthly cap |

| Want restaurant discounts rather than cashback | [HSBC Live+](/cards/hsbc-live-plus/) | 12 months of Zomato Gold included, per our data |

## Three dining bills, worked

What each card returns on dining alone, for a year, at AED 1,000, 2,000
and 4,000 of restaurant and delivery spend a month. Caps are applied.

| Card | Dining rate | Dining cap | AED 1,000 a month | AED 2,000 a month | AED 4,000 a month | Spend floor | Year-two fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [ADCB 365 Cashback](/cards/adcb-365-cashback/) | 6% | AED 1,000 a month across all categories | AED 720 | AED 1,440 | AED 2,880 | AED 5,000 a month | AED 383.25 |
| [HSBC Live+](/cards/hsbc-live-plus/) | 6% | AED 200 a month (about AED 3,333 of dining) | AED 720 | AED 1,440 | AED 2,400 | AED 3,000 a month | Waived above AED 12,000/yr |
| [Mashreq Cashback](/cards/mashreq-cashback/) | 5% | None | AED 600 | AED 1,200 | AED 2,400 | None | Free |
| [FAB Cashback](/cards/fab-cashback/) | 5% | AED 150 a month (AED 3,000 of dining) | AED 600 | AED 1,200 | AED 1,920 | AED 3,000, previous month | AED 315 |
| [ADIB Cashback Visa](/cards/adib-cashback-visa/) | 4% | AED 200 a month (AED 5,000 of dining) | AED 480 | AED 960 | AED 1,920 | None | AED 103.95 |

The arithmetic is the rate times the monthly dining bill, times twelve,
with the cap applied. At AED 4,000 a month:

- **ADCB 365:** AED 4,000 × 6% = AED 240 a month, inside its AED 1,000
  total cap, so AED 2,880 a year.
- **HSBC Live+:** AED 4,000 × 6% = AED 240, cut to the AED 200 cap, so
  AED 2,400 a year. We count nothing above the cap; HSBC's terms in our
  data do not say over-cap dining earns the 0.5% base rate.
- **Mashreq:** AED 4,000 × 5% = AED 200 a month, AED 2,400 a year, no cap.
- **FAB:** the first AED 3,000 earns 5% = AED 150 (the cap); the other
  AED 1,000 earns the 1% base rate = AED 10, per our review. AED 160 a
  month, AED 1,920 a year.
- **ADIB:** AED 4,000 × 4% = AED 160 a month, under its AED 200 cap,
  AED 1,920 a year.

The spend floors are measured on **total** card spend. HSBC pays nothing
in a month you spend under AED 3,000 on the card, ADCB 365 nothing under
AED 5,000, and FAB nothing in the month after one under AED 3,000.

## Best for most diners: Mashreq Cashback

[Mashreq Cashback](/cards/mashreq-cashback/) pays 5% on local and
international dining, including online food delivery, with no monthly cap
and no minimum spend. It has no annual fee on the primary or
supplementary cards and needs an AED 5,000 salary.

It is not the highest dining rate, but it is the one with nothing to
manage. HSBC and ADCB 365 pay 6%, but both need a monthly spend floor
met, and ADCB charges a fee: on dining alone ADCB 365 only beats Mashreq
above about AED 3,194 of dining a month (where 1% of the bill, times
twelve, exceeds its AED 383.25 fee). Mashreq's weakness is everything
else: 1% on general spend and 0.15% on fuel and bills.

Our data also records 2-for-1 offers through The Entertainer under Visa's
MENA offers programme, and DragonFly dining discounts. Cashback can be
redeemed from AED 100 and expires 36 months after it is earned.

## Best rate up to AED 4,000 a month: HSBC Live+

[HSBC Live+](/cards/hsbc-live-plus/) pays 6% on dining, capped at AED 200
of cashback a month. Up to about AED 3,333 of monthly dining that is the
highest rate on the list, level with ADCB 365; at AED 4,000 a month it
pays the same AED 2,400 a year as Mashreq, and above that Mashreq's
uncapped 5% pulls ahead.

It also pays 5% on fuel and 2% on groceries and entertainment, under
their own AED 200 caps. The AED 313.95 fee is waived from year two above
AED 12,000 of annual spend, which AED 1,000 of dining a month clears on
its own. Our data records a complimentary 12-month Zomato Gold membership
(up to 50% off at 4,000+ UAE restaurants) and 12 months of Careem Plus.
The minimum salary is AED 5,000 for employees of HSBC-approved companies
and AED 12,500 for everyone else.

## Best for heavy spenders: ADCB 365 Cashback

[ADCB 365 Cashback](/cards/adcb-365-cashback/) pays 6% on dining,
including online orders, with no separate dining cap: the only limit is
the AED 1,000 of cashback a card can earn each month across every
category. For a household that dines out heavily and puts AED 5,000 or
more a month on the card, it is the highest dining return on our list:
AED 2,880 a year at AED 4,000 a month, AED 2,496.75 after the AED 383.25
fee. A single month under AED 5,000 earns nothing.

## Delivery and noon Food

[Mashreq](/cards/mashreq-cashback/) and [ADCB 365](/cards/adcb-365-cashback/)
both include food delivery in their dining rate. Beyond that:

- **[Emirates NBD Noon One](/cards/emirates-nbd-noon-one/)** — 20% back
  on noon Food, paid as noon Credits that spend only on noon platforms.
  This is the "dining" rate in our data, and it applies to noon Food
  orders, not restaurant bills, which earn 1%. The accelerated rates are
  capped at a combined AED 2,000 of noon Credits a month. Free for life,
  AED 5,000 minimum salary.
- **[FAB Cashback](/cards/fab-cashback/)** — 40% off Talabat orders, four
  times a month, up to AED 40 off an order with an AED 100 minimum, on
  top of the 5% dining cashback.
- **[Citi Cash Back](/cards/citi-cashback/)** — 20% off Talabat once a
  month, up to AED 14.
- **[Liv Cashback](/cards/liv-cashback/)** and
  **[Liv Cashback+](/cards/liv-cashback-plus/)** — 20% off one Talabat
  order a month.

## Miles and points cards on dining

Travel cards advertise dining as a bonus category, but the return is
small next to a cashback card:

- [Emirates NBD Skywards Infinite](/cards/emirates-nbd-skywards-infinite/):
  2 Skywards Miles per USD on dining, about 1.09% at our 2-fils-per-Mile
  baseline (2 ÷ 3.6725 × 2 fils).
- [Emirates NBD Skywards Signature](/cards/emirates-nbd-skywards-signature/):
  1.5 Miles per USD, about 0.82%.
- [Emirates NBD Marriott Bonvoy World Elite](/cards/emirates-nbd-marriott-bonvoy-world-elite/):
  3 Bonvoy points per USD on dining, about 2.04% at our 2.5-fils Bonvoy
  baseline; the free [Bonvoy World](/cards/emirates-nbd-marriott-bonvoy-world/)
  earns 1.5 points per USD, about 1.02%.
- [Emirates NBD Diners Club](/cards/emirates-nbd-diners-club/): 2.5% as
  Plus Points on dining, subject to a per-statement cap our data does
  not quantify. AED 420 fee, salary transfer required.

Our [Skywards guide](/guides/best-skywards-cards-uae-2026/) explains the
valuations. On dining alone, none of these comes near Mashreq's free 5%.

## Before you apply

1. **Check the floor against your quiet months.** If your card spend dips
   in summer, a floor-free card like Mashreq or ADIB pays every month.
2. **Find where the cap lands.** HSBC's 6% stops at about AED 3,333 of
   dining a month, FAB's 5% at AED 3,000, ADIB's 4% at AED 5,000.
3. **Watch the foreign-currency fee if you eat abroad.** Mashreq pays 5%
   on international dining but charges a 2.89% FX fee; HSBC's is 2%.
4. **Discounts are not cashback.** A 2-for-1 is worth something only if
   the outlet is one you would have chosen anyway.

## Bottom line

For most UAE diners the answer is the free Mashreq Cashback: 5% on every
restaurant and delivery bill, with no cap and no floor. HSBC Live+ pays
more up to about AED 4,000 of dining a month if you clear its AED 3,000
monthly spend floor, and adds Zomato Gold. ADCB 365 Cashback is the
highest return for heavy diners who spend AED 5,000 a month on the card.
Miles cards and 2-for-1 perks are extras, not reasons to choose a dining
card.
