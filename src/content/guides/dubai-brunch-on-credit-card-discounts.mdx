The Dubai brunch market resettled after the 2022 weekend shift —
the headline brunches now split between Friday (the legacy slot,
still the loudest) and Saturday (quieter, slightly cheaper at
several hotel groups). Covers run from the AED 295 entry band at
mid-tier hotels to AED 795+ at destination properties. The single
lever that moves the bill is not the day, the dress code, or the
seafood station; it is which UAE-issued card you pay on, and
whether it is enrolled in a dining-discount programme that the
host hotel participates in.

This guide is for the reader who already knows roughly which
brunch they want, and wants to know which card to pay on. It is
not a venue ranking. It is the AED maths on the card side.

## The brunch market in three bands

- **AED 295–395 / adult, soft beverage.** Mid-tier hotel brunches
  and the lighter four-star options. Often the best
  per-dirham experience if food matters more than the room. Bank-
  card 2-for-1 programmes appear here irregularly — the smaller
  properties prefer to discount directly to drive volume.
- **AED 495–595 / adult, house beverage.** The mainstream
  five-star band — Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt city properties, and
  the second-tier Atlantis and Madinat Jumeirah venues. Most of
  the bank-card dining programmes are aimed here. A 2-for-1 in
  this band saves AED 495–595 a cover; nothing else on the bill
  moves the maths comparably.
- **AED 695–895 / adult, premium beverage.** Atlantis Saffron and
  Bread Street Kitchen, Madinat Jumeirah's destination venues,
  FIVE's headline brunches, the Burj-view rooftop properties.
  Bank-card 2-for-1s exclude this band frequently — and where
  they are included, the premium-beverage upgrade is usually
  carved out.

Children's pricing is usually half-cover for ages 6–12, free
under 6, but cut-offs vary. Several venues have moved to a flat
AED 95–145 children's rate regardless of age. UAE-licensing rules
on unaccompanied minors apply at brunches with a beverage package
— check the venue's family policy before booking; some properties
are family-friendly only at the first seating.

## How the card discount actually arrives

UAE-issued cards pay back on brunch through three distinct
channels, and a reader who confuses them ends up overpaying.

1. **The card's own dining earn rate.** This is the percentage
   the issuer returns on the AED actually charged at the venue.
   It is small in absolute terms (1–5% in the UAE market) but it
   is the only channel that fires on every transaction, with no
   programme enrolment and no participating-venue list. The
   [FAB Cashback Mastercard](/cards/fab-cashback/) earns 5% on
   dining per the card's published earn-rate table, subject to a AED 3,000
   minimum monthly spend in the prior month, an AED 150 monthly cap on
   each 5% category (so dining stops earning the bonus rate above AED
   3,000 of dining spend a month) and a AED 1,000 overall monthly
   cashback cap. The
   [Mashreq Cashback Credit Card](/cards/mashreq-cashback/) earns
   5% on local and international dining, with no minimum spend
   and no monthly cap per the published terms — a different ceiling shape that
   suits a household with multiple brunches a month.
2. **The card's bundled dining programme.** The 2-for-1s that
   actually halve a hotel brunch cover come from The Entertainer
   (bundled with several Emirates NBD cards) and the Mastercard
   Buy 1 Get 1 app that FAB lists on its World Elite card. The
   banks' own dining lines are mostly percentage discounts rather
   than 2-for-1s: ADV+ on the FAB Elite card (up to 40% off at
   hotel restaurants, per FAB's product page) and Emirates NBD's
   Bon Appétit restaurant deals (15–25% off at the venues ENBD
   listed when we checked on 10 October 2026). Each is a
   participating-venue list that changes through the year, so we
   do not publish a snapshot here; the current list is always in
   the programme's own app or on the bank's offers page.
3. **The hotel-loyalty side.** Marriott Bonvoy and Hilton Honors
   credit F&B spend at participating UAE properties as base
   points. The points credit to the post-discount AED amount —
   if the 2-for-1 brings two covers to a single AED 525, the
   points credit on AED 525, not on the rack AED 1,050. Treat
   the points as a top-up, not the lever.

The headline mistake on the reader side is treating these as
substitutes when they stack. A cardholder dining at a Marriott
property can redeem a 2-for-1 where the venue participates, pay
the remaining cover on the card, earn the card's rewards on the
AED actually charged, *and* earn Bonvoy base points on the same
post-discount amount by attaching the Bonvoy number at check-in.
Several channels can fire off one transaction.

## Where each card type pays back hardest

- **FAB premium cards — [FAB World Elite](/cards/fab-world-elite/)
  and [FAB Elite](/cards/fab-elite/).** FAB does not run a
  bank-branded 2-for-1 dining programme on these cards. The World
  Elite page points holders to the Mastercard Buy 1 Get 1 app
  (Mastercard's programme, with its own merchant list), and the
  FAB Elite page lists ADV+, with up to 40% off at hotel
  restaurants. The FAB World Elite carries no annual fee per the
  published schedule, earns a flat 5 FAB Rewards per AED 1, and is
  open to FAB Private Banking customers by invitation only. Check
  the venue is in the app before you book, not at the table.
- **Emirates NBD cards — [Emirates NBD Marriott Bonvoy World
  Elite](/cards/emirates-nbd-marriott-bonvoy-world-elite/),
  [Emirates NBD Visa Infinite](/cards/emirates-nbd-visa-infinite/),
  others.** ENBD's own dining line is Bon Appétit, a list of
  percentage discounts (15–25% at the venues listed on 10 October
  2026) rather than a 2-for-1; the Bonvoy World Elite product page
  lists no dining 2-for-1 at all. The Bonvoy World Elite is still
  the strongest single card for the reader brunching at Marriott
  venues regularly: 3 Bonvoy points per USD 1 on dining (about 0.8
  points per AED 1) per the card's published earn-rate table, *plus*
  Bonvoy base points on the same paid-AED line. The fee — an AED
  1,575 joining fee in year one and AED 1,575 a year after, per the
  published schedule — is the gating question, and brunch alone will
  not carry it; the hotel benefits (Gold Elite status, the annual
  Free Night Award) have to.
- **The Entertainer (bundled with several UAE cards in 2026).**
  The widest four- and five-star hotel coverage of the 2-for-1
  routes, in the "buy one main brunch, get one free" shape.
  Premium-beverage upgrades are typically not included, and peak
  destination brunches are routinely excluded. The Entertainer is
  the right answer for a reader who eats across the four-star and
  mid-five-star band and does not concentrate on one chain.

For a reader paying on a flat-rate cashback card with no bundled
dining programme — the [FAB Cashback Mastercard](/cards/fab-cashback/)
or [Mashreq Cashback](/cards/mashreq-cashback/) — the only channel
firing is the dining earn rate. On a AED 525 cover that is AED 26.
Useful as the underlying earn, but not the brunch lever.

## The stacking rules

You cannot stack two 2-for-1s on the same cover — pick the
deepest. You can stack:

- A 2-for-1 with the hotel's own early-bird or Saturday off-peak,
  if the venue publishes it as a separate promo and the small
  print does not exclude card-discount combinations.
- A 2-for-1 with hotel-loyalty point earning on the cash you do
  pay. Marriott Bonvoy and Hilton Honors credit F&B spend at
  participating UAE properties as base points; points credit to
  the post-discount AED amount.
- A card 2-for-1 with that card's own category earn rate on the
  AED actually charged.

What you cannot do is point-redeem the brunch itself and also
claim the 2-for-1 — the 2-for-1 needs a paid base cover. Point-
redeeming an F&B charge at a UAE Bonvoy property typically lands
around 0.29 fils per point at the local F&B-redemption rate,
versus 2.5–3.5 fils per point on a good hotel-night burn. Spend
the points on the room and the cash on the brunch.

## The AED maths on a typical booking

Two adults, mainstream five-star, AED 525 / adult, house beverage:

- **Cash, no card benefit:** AED 1,050.
- **A 2-for-1 (The Entertainer or the Mastercard Buy 1 Get 1 app)
  at a participating venue:** AED 525 — a clean 50% saving, with
  the AED 525 still earning the card's underlying rewards rate.
- **No 2-for-1 at a Marriott property, paid on Bonvoy World
  Elite:** AED 1,050, plus Bonvoy base points on the AED 1,050,
  plus the card's 3 points per USD 1 dining earn — roughly 860 Bonvoy
  points, about AED 21 at our 2.5-fil Bonvoy baseline. The cover
  lands at an effective AED 1,029.
- **Flat-rate cashback card, no 2-for-1, dining at a non-
  participating venue:** AED 1,050, less roughly AED 53 of
  dining cashback at 5%. Net AED 997.

The 2-for-1 is the lever. The earn rate is the rounding.

## The cap mechanics, in plain language

For the regular brunch-goer, the cap on the card programme matters
more than the headline rate. Three cap shapes appear in the UAE
market:

- **Monthly cashback cap on the card.** The FAB Cashback Mastercard
  caps each 5% category at AED 150 a month and total monthly cashback
  at AED 1,000 per the published terms; a household already putting
  AED 3,000 a month of dining on the card earns nothing extra on the
  brunch. If brunch is the headline use, a card with an uncapped
  dining bucket — like the Mashreq Cashback — is the cleaner fit.
- **Rotation on the participating-venue list.** The Entertainer,
  the Mastercard app and the banks' own dining lists all change
  their participating venues through the year. A reader who picked
  a card for one specific brunch should re-check the list before
  each booking — the venue may have dropped out. The two-for-one offer typically reads as "valid
  while the venue is in the programme," not "valid for the year."
- **Per-month redemption count on the programme.** Bundled
  Entertainer offers cap the number of redemptions a holder can
  claim in a calendar month or year. Confirm the cap before
  building a four-brunches-a-month habit on the Entertainer alone.

## What to do this weekend

1. Pick the hotel group you want — Marriott, Hilton, Atlantis,
   Madinat Jumeirah, FIVE.
2. Cross-reference against your Entertainer or Mastercard Buy 1
   Get 1 app, and your bank's own dining list (FAB's ADV+, ENBD's
   Bon Appétit), for the venue's current participation.
3. Pay on the card that gives the deepest cover discount, not
   the highest base earn rate — the discount is worth multiples
   of the earn-rate gap.
4. Book the first seating; confirm dress code on the venue's
   reservations page before you leave the house.
5. Attach the hotel-loyalty number at check-in to capture base
   points on the AED actually charged.

## Pay smart

For a reader brunching once a month at a mainstream five-star,
the [FAB World Elite](/cards/fab-world-elite/) is the lowest-
friction choice if FAB offers it to you — it is invitation-only, with
zero annual fee per the published schedule and the Mastercard Buy 1
Get 1 app.
For a reader brunching at Marriott Bonvoy properties specifically,
the [Emirates NBD Marriott Bonvoy World
Elite](/cards/emirates-nbd-marriott-bonvoy-world-elite/) is the
strongest single card — 3 Bonvoy points per USD 1 on dining per the published schedule, plus Bonvoy
base points on the same line. For
the reader who refuses an annual fee and wants flat dining
cashback with no participating-venue list, the
[Mashreq Cashback Credit Card](/cards/mashreq-cashback/) returns
5% on dining at zero annual fee per the published schedule, with no monthly cap.
