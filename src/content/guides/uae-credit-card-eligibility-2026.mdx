Meeting a UAE credit card’s published salary minimum is a starting point, not approval. Check whether the card also requires a salary transfer, a particular employer or an invitation. The issuer decides after reviewing your application and may consider your credit history, income and existing commitments.

## What "salary transfer" actually means

A card’s salary minimum and its salary-transfer requirement are separate. A bank may accept proof of income for one product and require your salary to be credited to a particular account for another. Ask whether the condition applies to the card, a welcome offer or both. Our [salary-transfer guide](/guides/salary-transfer-mechanics-2026/) explains the process; the [tracker](/salary-transfer/) separates ongoing account conditions from temporary offers.

## AECB and credit history for new UAE arrivals

[Etihad Credit Bureau](https://etihadbureau.ae/en/Individual/CreditReport) says its Credit Report includes your score, reported income, credit commitments and payment history. It lets you request corrections if the information is wrong. A short credit history is not the same as a missed payment, but neither a score nor a clean report guarantees the bank will approve you. Check the issuer’s process rather than assuming a universal score cutoff or waiting period.

## Documentation banks ask for

Start with the issuer’s checklist. It may ask for an Emirates ID, passport and residency details, a salary certificate or other income evidence, and bank statements. Self-employed applicants should confirm which business and income records the bank accepts. For an example of an issuer-specific checklist, see the [ADIB Cashback Visa product page](https://www.adib.ae/en/personal/cards/cashback-visa-card/adib-cashback-visa-covered-card).

## What to do if you are below the minimum

Shortlist cards whose published requirements fit your circumstances. If the condition is unclear or your income is not salaried, ask the issuer which documents or products it accepts before applying. If you are considering a deposit-backed card, check the deposit, fees and release conditions first. A salary-transfer bonus does not waive the card’s requirements unless the issuer says so.

## Worked example

Suppose you meet a card’s published minimum salary but your employer pays your salary into another bank. If the card requires a transfer, meeting the income threshold does not meet the transfer condition. If the card does not require one, that removes one condition only; the issuer still decides whether to approve the application.

## Bottom line

Check the published salary requirement, any additional conditions and the application checklist. Compare the fees and benefits only after that. A match on paper is a shortlist, not a decision from the bank.
