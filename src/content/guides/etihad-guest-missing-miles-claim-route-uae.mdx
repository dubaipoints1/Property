Back in the UAE and your Etihad Guest miles haven’t appeared? Find the ticket and boarding pass before filing anything. Look for the **operated by** line as well as the flight number. Then separate a missing credit for flying from miles you expected on a partner purchase.

## If Etihad operated the flight

For travel after you joined the programme, Etihad’s [Guest FAQ](https://www.etihad.com/en-ae/help/faq/etihad-guest) directs members to **Claim missing miles** in their account. Use your flight details and keep the boarding pass. If the name on the ticket does not match your membership record, check that discrepancy before sending the same claim again.

## If another airline operated it

The same FAQ directs partner-airline claims through the Etihad Guest feedback form linked there, with the boarding pass and supporting documents. Don’t assume every fare on a partner earns miles. If it was a codeshare, check the separate codeshare guidance for that booking: the name that sold the ticket and the airline that flew it may differ. Etihad also treats flights taken before you joined separately, so don’t apply the ordinary member claim route to an older trip without checking first.

## If you did not fly

If the missing credit comes from a purchase with a programme partner, Etihad says to contact that partner. Keep the purchase receipt or confirmation rather than sending a flight claim without a boarding pass. For rewards you expected from a UAE bank card, start by checking the issuer’s own rewards record and terms. The missing item may be bank points, not a flight credit. Work out which account should show it before opening a case.
