Historical record, typed 6 August 2026 from the campaign's own T&C
(Ver.02/April2026, promo codes SW10ST/SW10TRB) after the coverage

This replaces guesswork with a verified record of what ADCB's Switch
cycle actually paid — useful the day cycle 11 appears, which is why the
page stays on the salary-transfer monitor.

Worth remembering about this campaign's shape: the advertised
"up to AED 12,000" bundled a second, funding-based leg (AED 1,000–5,000
for balances of AED 25,000–500,000+ plus two products) on top of the
salary leg's AED 7,000 national / 5,500 expat maximum; the salary reward
was conditional on AED 2,500 of credit-card spend; the reward came as an
electronics voucher with a 3-month fuse, not cash; and the 12-month
salary lock-in carried a full-value clawback. A headline built for the
top band, paid in vouchers, with the real commitment in the small print
— the pattern the tracker exists to decode.
