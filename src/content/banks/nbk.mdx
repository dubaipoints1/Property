This page covers the UAE versions of [Visa Classic](/cards/nbk-visa-classic/), [Visa Gold](/cards/nbk-visa-gold/) and [Visa Platinum](/cards/nbk-visa-platinum/). NBK UAE advertises a first-year annual fee waiver for all three. It lists separate renewal fees before VAT. Joining charges and other costs have not been established by this review.

## Which terms apply to you?

The bank says you need an NBK account to request a card. Its linked disclosure describes different eligibility routes. An account alone does not confirm approval, and these profiles do not use a single salary figure to rank applicants. Confirm your route with [NBK UAE](https://www.nbk.com/uae/personal/cards/credit-cards/) before you apply.
