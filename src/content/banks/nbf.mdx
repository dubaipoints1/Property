This page covers two conventional NBF cards and two [NBF Islamic](https://nbfislamic.ae/) cards. Their names are similar, but the source documents are separate. Use the profile that matches the card you intend to ask about.

## Conventional cards

- [NBF Visa Infinite](/cards/nbf-visa-infinite/) lists salary or Priority status as alternatives, subject to the bank’s other conditions.
- [NBF Platinum Exclusive](/cards/nbf-platinum-exclusive/) has a separate published salary floor. Do not assume it shares Infinite’s Priority route.

NBF discloses no standard annual fee on these conventional cards, but its tariff says the bank can levy fees or replace the card if a customer stops meeting Priority conditions. Check the terms of your own offer.

## NBF Islamic cards

- [NBF Islamic Platinum Exclusive](/cards/nbf-islamic-platinum-exclusive/) has its own published salary floor for salaried applicants.
- [NBF Islamic Visa Infinite](/cards/nbf-islamic-visa-infinite/) lists salary or NBF Priority status as alternatives.

None of the profiles makes salary alone a guarantee of approval. If you are applying against a deposit, confirm the relevant terms with the bank. A card disclosure also does not establish a separate salary-transfer promotion.
