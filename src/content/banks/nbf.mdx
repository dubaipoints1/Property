This page covers two cards from NBF Islamic. It does not list the bank’s conventional range. The profiles use each card’s named key facts statement for the standard annual membership fee and the bank’s published eligibility wording.

## Which card should you look at first?

- [NBF Islamic Platinum Exclusive](/cards/nbf-islamic-platinum-exclusive/) has its own published salary floor for salaried applicants.
- [NBF Islamic Visa Infinite](/cards/nbf-islamic-visa-infinite/) lists salary or NBF Priority status as alternatives.

Neither makes salary alone a guarantee of approval. If you are applying against a deposit, confirm the relevant terms with the bank. A card disclosure also does not establish a separate salary-transfer promotion.
