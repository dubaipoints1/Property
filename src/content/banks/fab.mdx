First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB) is the federal-scale balance sheet of the UAE
banking system — the largest lender in the country by assets, formed in 2017
by the merger of First Gulf Bank and National Bank of Abu Dhabi, and the
default high-street bank in the capital. For an expat household FAB is the
Abu Dhabi mirror of what Emirates NBD is to Dubai: dense branch network,
government-payroll partnerships and a range of cashback and travel cards.
The World Elite profile is retained with new-application availability
unconfirmed; invitation-only wording does not establish open invitations.

What sets FAB apart is the structure of its proprietary points currency.
FAB Rewards do not auto-credit; they pool in the FAB Mobile app and you
redeem on demand — statement cashback, partner shopping, or transfer to
selected airline programmes. Transfer ratios are not generous, but the
flexibility is real — the right structural choice for a reader who does
not yet know which airline they will fly most.

## Reward currency, in plain English

The covered FAB Rewards profiles earn Points, distinct from the Etihad Guest
Infinite's Miles. Points accrue on eligible spend at the card-specific
rate, pool in the FAB Mobile app, and sit there until you redeem — statement
cashback, partner-merchant catalogues, FAB Rewards Shop merchandise or eligible
airline transfers. Redemption values and conditions vary: do not assume par
cashback or that a transfer is automatically better value. Read each card's
source-qualified earn rows and the applicable redemption terms.

## Customer-service notes

The call centre on **+971 2 681 1511** runs 24/7 in English and Arabic with
reliable Hindi/Urdu cover in office hours. Branch density is highest in Abu
Dhabi, respectable in Dubai (Sheikh Zayed Road, DIFC, JLT, Dubai Mall),
thinner in the northern emirates than ENBD or RAKBank. The FAB Mobile app
handles card controls, FAB Rewards redemption, salary-account opening and
most service requests without a branch visit; loan restructures and large
statement disputes still funnel to a branch officer. Missed payments can affect
your credit record; confirm reporting and repayment terms directly with FAB.

## Bottom line

FAB suits the Abu-Dhabi-anchored expat, the government-payroll household
that wants its bank one bus stop from work, or the reader who values
redemption optionality over a single locked-in airline currency. Take the
Cashback Card if its eligible categories fit; the Etihad Guest Infinite if
you have a use for those Miles; the Elite only after checking qualifying
spend, caps and provider terms. Keep unresolved costs and availability out
of any return estimate, and assess account promotions separately.
