Wio Bank PJSC is the UAE's most consequential independent digital bank —
not a legacy lender's app-only brand but a standalone institution,
licensed by the Central Bank of the UAE and backed
by a consortium of ADQ, Alpha Dhabi, e& and FAB. Headquartered in Abu
Dhabi, it launched retail banking as a plans-and-spaces model: you pick a
plan, organise money into
Saving Spaces, and run credit through a single Wio Credit facility rather
than a shelf of differently-branded cards. For the expat reader the
appeal is coherence — one app, one plan, transparent pricing — and the
cost is that almost every benefit, including the headline cashback rate,
is gated by which plan you're on.

## Credit and the current source limits

Read the [Wio Credit review](/cards/wio-credit/) for the source-qualified
profile. Cashback depends on the eligible plan and a combined cap across the
relevant facilities; salary alone does not establish the reward calculation.
The general model remains pending rather than assuming every transaction
earns the headline rate.

The captured tariff omits an annual-card-fee row. That is not proof of a zero
fee, and account-plan costs are a separate question. Finance rates also vary
by plan; a previously stored generic rate is not a confirmed charge for every
customer. Check the current KFS and plan-specific tariff before using credit,
borrowing cash or settling another card. None is described here as free money
or a unique market-wide advantage.

## Salary plan and eligibility

Check the current [Salary plan](https://wio.io/salary-plan) terms for account
pricing, employer conditions, qualifying transfers and saving products.
Account-plan eligibility is not the same as approval for Wio Credit.

Use the current status above and the [salary-transfer tracker](/salary-transfer/)
for covered account promotions. Compare a deposit rate with a one-off cash
bonus only after accounting for the balance, period and qualifying actions.
This card-source refresh does not certify the current savings or loan tariffs.

## Customer-service notes

Wio is app-only: support runs through in-app chat, with
[wio.io](https://wio.io) as the public reference for plans, rates and
the fee documents. There are no branches; cash handling is the usual
digital-bank workaround of ATM networks and transfers rather than a
teller. The app itself is the product — plan management, Saving Spaces,
Easy Cash and Installments all live there — and the pricing pages are
unusually plain-spoken by UAE banking standards. Standard UAE credit
infrastructure applies: AECB reporting on credit products and the
Central Bank's Debt Burden Ratio rules.

## Bottom line

Wio may suit a reader who wants app-based plan management and can meet the
specific plan and credit conditions. Compare the separate account cost,
card-fee uncertainty, reward limits and finance terms before choosing it.
Use the [Wio Credit review](/cards/wio-credit/) for recorded eligibility and
pending terms, then confirm the applicable requirements with Wio. A published
threshold or an account subscription does not guarantee credit approval.
