Wio Bank PJSC is the UAE's most consequential independent digital bank —
not a legacy lender's app-only brand but a standalone institution,
licensed by the Central Bank of the UAE, regulated by the SCA, and backed
by a consortium of ADQ, Alpha Dhabi, e& and FAB. Headquartered in Abu
Dhabi, it launched retail banking as a plans-and-spaces model: you pick a
plan (free with a qualifying salary, paid otherwise), organise money into
Saving Spaces, and run credit through a single Wio Credit facility rather
than a shelf of differently-branded cards. For the expat reader the
appeal is coherence — one app, one plan, transparent pricing — and the
cost is that almost every benefit, including the headline cashback rate,
is gated by which plan you're on.

## Credit, and why there is no card table here

Wio Credit advertises **up to 2% cashback on all spend** — flat, no
category juggling — for customers on the Salary, Family or Plus plans,
capped at AED 2,500 a month. The genuinely unique feature sits next to
it: **paying other banks' credit cards through Wio Credit earns 0.5%
cashback** (same AED 2,500 monthly cap). Nobody else in the UAE market
pays you to settle a competitor's card bill, and for a household running
a high-fee card elsewhere it is free money on a payment you were making
anyway. Around the card sit Easy Cash — borrow from your credit limit at
AED 1 per day per AED 1,000 borrowed, repay within 30 days, with a 6-day
grace period and an AED 199 late fee from day 36 — Easy Installments
over 3–48 months, and credit interest from 2.49% a month on the Salary
plan.

The full spec is now on our [Wio Credit review](/cards/wio-credit/):
the Key Fact Statement v5 (effective August 2026) and the Schedule of
Fees Retail v8 (July 2026) were read the same day this hub published —
Mastercard World, a 2% FX fee, no annual card fee (the plan is the
price), an AED 2,500/month cashback cap, and a roll-over rate that
drops from 3.25%/month to 2.29%/month on the Salary plan. Every figure
traces to those two documents.

## Salary plan and eligibility

Wio's plan structure is the eligibility system. The [Salary
plan](https://wio.io/salary-plan) is **free with a salary transfer of
AED 15,000 a month or more** — or **AED 5,000** if your employer is one
of Wio's partner companies, a materially lower bar worth checking before
you assume you don't qualify. While your salary is still in transit you
sit on the paid Plus plan, and Wio switches you to the free Salary plan
automatically once the salary lands within two months.

The benefits are rate-based rather than a cash lump: **6% p.a. on
one-month Fixed Saving Spaces** (which requires at least AED 5,000 of
monthly card spend), 3.25% p.a. on ordinary Saving Spaces, personal
loans from 4.75%, credit interest from 2.49% a month, and the up-to-2%
credit cashback above. There is **no banded cash bonus**, which is why
Wio appears in our [salary-transfer tracker](/salary-transfer/) coverage
note as checked-with-no-live-banded-offer rather than as a table row.
Whether 6% on a savings balance beats a one-off AED 3,000-class bonus
elsewhere depends entirely on how much you'll actually hold in the
space — do that arithmetic before choosing a salary home on either
basis.

## Customer-service notes

Wio is app-only: support runs through in-app chat, with
[wio.io](https://wio.io) as the public reference for plans, rates and
the fee documents. There are no branches; cash handling is the usual
digital-bank workaround of ATM networks and transfers rather than a
teller. The app itself is the product — plan management, Saving Spaces,
Easy Cash and Installments all live there — and the pricing pages are
unusually plain-spoken by UAE banking standards. Standard UAE credit
infrastructure applies: AECB reporting on credit products and the
Central Bank's Debt Burden Ratio rules.

## Bottom line

Wio suits the expat earning AED 15,000+ (or AED 5,000 at a partner
company) who wants one coherent digital bank: free plan, strong savings
rates, flat plan-gated cashback, and the only mechanism in the market
that pays you for settling other banks' card bills. It is the wrong
choice if you want a branch, a banded salary-transfer cash bonus, or a
published salary bar you can check before applying — Wio's credit
eligibility is assessed in the app, which is why the
[Wio Credit review](/cards/wio-credit/) shows its salary minimum as
"Pending" rather than a number no document states.
