HSBC UAE's card range offers two different choices: direct cashback or Emirates
Skywards Miles. Start with the reward currency you can actually use, rather than
assuming that a higher card tier or a premium bank account is automatically the
better deal. The issuer makes the lending decision; a DubaiPoints shortlist is
not an approval or eligibility guarantee.

## Card ladder at a glance

- **[HSBC Live+ Credit Card](/cards/hsbc-live-plus/)** — category cashback with
  qualifying spend and statement-cycle caps. The current product page and KFS
  give different eligibility requirements, so the review marks eligibility
  Pending rather than presenting a single salary threshold as confirmed.
- **[HSBC Cash+ Credit Card](/cards/hsbc-cash-plus/)** — a flat base cashback
  rate, with additional periodic spend bonuses that are not included in the base
  calculation. Eligible purchases are limited by the assigned credit-card limit;
  until that limit is known, we do not calculate a total-return example.
- **[HSBC Emirates Skywards Infinite](/cards/hsbc-skywards-infinite/)** —
  Skywards Miles with different domestic and non-AED earning rates. Reduced-rate
  AED categories and direct-carrier bonuses need checking separately.
- **[HSBC Emirates Skywards Signature](/cards/hsbc-skywards-signature/)** —
  the other covered Skywards option. Its product page has employer-dependent
  income conditions, so no universal salary qualification is asserted here.

This is the set of HSBC profiles covered by DubaiPoints, not a claim that every
relationship-priced or private-banking card has been reviewed. The new profiles
use a manual primary-source maintenance route; pending terms remain visibly
labelled and are not treated as confirmed calculation inputs.

## Reward currency, in plain English

The covered cashback cards and Skywards cards do not share a reward unit. Direct
AED cashback can be compared with a fee in AED; a Skywards Mile is not cash and
its value depends on the redemption you can book. Use the
[Skywards guide](/airlines/skywards/) and
[cash-or-miles calculator](/calculator/cash-or-miles/) for that second decision.
Do not compare the headline cashback percentage with a headline number of Miles
without keeping the spending currency and earning denominator aligned.

## Salary transfer and eligibility

Bank-account promotions and credit-card eligibility are separate decisions. Read
the current **[HSBC salary-transfer offer](/salary-transfer/hsbc/)** for its
qualification steps, timing and potential clawbacks; those campaign terms are not
a standing card benefit.

The Live+ product page and KFS disagree on eligibility. Signature also publishes
employer-dependent income conditions. Confirm the applicable income, employer
and age requirements with HSBC; the earlier Live+ minimum should not be treated
as a confirmed qualification threshold. The Cash+ and Skywards reviews show the
source-specific requirements available for each product, not a bank-wide approval
promise.

## Customer-service notes

Use **[HSBC UAE's official website](https://www.hsbc.ae)** for current service
channels and application requirements. Before applying, obtain the current KFS
and ask which conditions apply to your employer, residency and chosen product.
For overseas spending, the shared card fee block labels HSBC's issuer processing
fee separately from scheme charges that may also apply; it is not an all-in FX
cost quotation.

## Bottom line

Choose the reward unit first, then check the fees, qualifying spend and exclusions
on the specific card. Live+ may suit category spending, Cash+ may suit a simpler
base rate, and a Skywards card may suit an existing Emirates redemption plan.
None is a recommendation to spend more, carry a balance or assume that meeting
an advertised income condition guarantees approval.
