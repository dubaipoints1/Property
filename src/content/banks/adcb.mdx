Abu Dhabi Commercial Bank (ADCB) is the capital's commercial-banking workhorse —
formed in 1985 and consolidated again in 2019 through the absorption of Union
National Bank and Al Hilal Bank, which lifted it into the country's top three
lenders by assets. For an expat household ADCB is the Abu-Dhabi-anchored
alternative to FAB: dense branch footprint in the capital, decent Dubai
coverage, and a TouchPoints rewards programme that converts to both Skywards
and Etihad Guest from one wallet — rare optionality among UAE issuers.

The bank's distinguishing move in 2026 is its [combined salary-transfer and
card-spend offer](/salary-transfer/adcb/), which stacks a cash account bonus
on top of a separate card-spend bonus. The headline AED 12,000 figure at
the AED 50,000+ band is real but conditional; the trap is below.

## Card ladder at a glance

ADCB's consumer line-up is unusually wide — eleven live cards as of August 2026 —
and most are marketed as **AED 0 annual fee** through automatic spend-based
waivers rather than a true free-for-life guarantee. The ADCB Schedule of Fees
(Ver.46, February 2026) lists real fees behind several of them — AED 383.25 on
the 365 Cashback (no spend-based waiver), AED 1,050 on the TouchPoints
Infinite, AED 1,575 on the Traveller. Read the fee tile on each card page
before assuming a waiver applies.

- **[ADCB 365 Cashback Credit Card](/cards/adcb-365-cashback/)** —
  category cashback on eligible dining, groceries, fuel and specific digital
  subscriptions. General entertainment and online spending are not interchangeable
  with the listed subscription categories. Check the current table, monthly
  qualification and merchant/payment-channel exclusions in the review.
- **[ADCB Essential Cashback](/cards/adcb-essential-cashback/)** — everyday
  cashback to compare with the more category-led cards. Confirm eligibility;
  the earlier salary-transfer requirement is not treated as established.
- **[ADCB Talabat Credit Card](/cards/adcb-talabat/)** — a talabat co-brand.
  Credits, order limits and qualification conditions need comparing with direct
  cashback, rather than treating them as the same reward unit.
- **[ADCB LuLu Titanium Gold](/cards/adcb-lulu-titanium-gold/)** and
  **[ADCB LuLu Platinum](/cards/adcb-lulu-platinum/)** — LuLu Points co-brands
  for grocery-heavy spending. The Platinum has a current salary-or-credit-limit
  route; check each product's separate qualification rules.
- **[ADCB Shukran Credit Card](/cards/adcb-shukran/)** — the
  Shukran co-brand. Confirm the current salary-or-credit-limit route directly
  with ADCB rather than assuming a universal salary threshold.
- **[ADCB TouchPoints Platinum](/cards/adcb-touchpoints-platinum/)** and
  **[Titanium Gold](/cards/adcb-touchpoints-titanium-gold/)** — entry
  TouchPoints earners; compare the dated card-specific qualification and fee records.
- **[ADCB Traveller Credit Card](/cards/adcb-traveller/)** —
  TouchPoints with a travel-skew earn table; check the full product conditions.
- **[ADCB Betaqti Credit Card](/cards/adcb-betaqti/)** — a UAE-national
  TouchPoints card with anniversary rewards. Its visible product wording has a
  salary-or-credit-limit route; do not infer a compulsory salary transfer.
- **[ADCB TouchPoints Infinite](/cards/adcb-touchpoints-infinite/)** — the
  flagship; the review's qualification block is the source for its current stored threshold.

## Reward currency, in plain English

ADCB TouchPoints are a flexible in-app currency rather than a cashback
auto-credit. Points post to the TouchPoints wallet on statement and stay
there until you redeem — statement value, partner merchants, travel through
the TouchPoints portal, or transfer to **Emirates Skywards or Etihad Guest**
at the bank's published ratio. The convertibility is the differentiator:
ADCB is among the few UAE issuers with credible both-airline routing from
one proprietary wallet. Transfer ratios are not aggressive and change
without notice, so check the in-app rate the day you intend to move points.
For everyday simplicity the ADCB 365 pays straight cashback and bypasses
the points layer; for travel optionality, take a TouchPoints card and
defer the airline choice until redemption.

## Salary transfer and eligibility

Read the **[current ADCB salary-transfer offer](/salary-transfer/adcb/)**
for campaign eligibility, qualifying actions, reward timing and clawbacks.
Account-promotional terms are not standing credit-card eligibility rules.

Current captured product wording for the 365, Essential, LuLu Platinum,
Shukran, talabat and Betaqti offers a salary OR credit-limit qualification
route. Those reviews withhold the earlier scalar qualification fields pending
confirmation; neither a listed income nor a credit limit guarantees approval.
The remaining product eligibility panels were not asserted read in this source
review. Use each card's dated source notes, not this hub as an application promise.

## Customer-service notes

The call centre on **+971 600 50 2030** runs daily in English, Arabic, Hindi
and Urdu. Branch density is highest in Abu Dhabi, with reliable Dubai
coverage on Sheikh Zayed Road, Business Bay and Dubai Mall, and reasonable
presence in Al Ain. The ADCB mobile app and the companion Hayyak app handle
card controls, TouchPoints redemption, salary-account opening and most
service journeys without a branch trip. ADCB reports to AECB per Central
Bank rules; missed payments register within 30 days and the 50% Debt
Burden Ratio cap applies on all unsecured exposure.

## Bottom line

ADCB suits the Abu-Dhabi-resident expat earning AED 15,000+ who can credibly
hit the monthly card-spend tier to clear the combined
salary-transfer-and-spend bonus, and who wants both Skywards and Etihad
Guest optionality from one wallet. Take the ADCB 365 for simple cashback
at the entry tier; the Talabat or LuLu cards as accent cards; the
TouchPoints range if you want flexibility without committing to one
airline. ADCB is the wrong choice if you fly Emirates exclusively (ENBD's
Skywards depth is greater), if you cannot reliably hit the monthly spend
tier, or if you want a no-spend-condition salary-transfer bonus —
RAKBANK is simpler there.
