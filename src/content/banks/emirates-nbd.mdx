Emirates NBD is Dubai's hometown bank — the largest lender by assets headquartered
in the emirate, formed in 2007 by the merger of Emirates Bank International and
the National Bank of Dubai. For an expat household it is also the default
"airline bank": Emirates' frequent-flyer programme is anchored here, and ENBD's
Skywards ladder is the most direct route into the miles currency that actually
matters in the UAE. Branch network is dense across Dubai and respectable in Abu
Dhabi and the northern emirates, the mobile app is mature, and the bank carries
a [salary-transfer cashback offer](https://www.emiratesnbd.com/en/promotions/payroll-cashback)
running to 24 December 2026.

The trade-off is that ENBD's pricing on premium cards is not the cheapest on
the market, and the customer-service experience is unevenly digital-first. If
you bank only through the app you will find ENBD competent; the moment you need
a branch officer to fix a statement entry you discover the queues are not short.

## Reward currency, in plain English

Plus Points have redemption-specific values: statement cashback is not at the
same value as selected non-cash redemptions. Do not treat a native Points
figure as an equal AED amount or assume the same conversion across co-brands.
Read the linked card's current reward unit, redemption conditions and source
notes before choosing between Plus Points and a direct airline or hotel card.

## Salary transfer and eligibility

ENBD's current salary-transfer promotion, the
**[Salary Transfer Welcome Bonus](https://www.emiratesnbd.com/en/promotions/payroll-cashback)**,
runs **25 September – 24 December 2026** and pays fixed cashback. Open your
first-ever ENBD account, transfer a salary of **AED 15,000 or more** within 90
days, and take a new ENBD credit card with **AED 5,000 of spend** within 60
days of issue: ENBD pays **AED 1,500** (salary AED 15,000–49,999) or **AED
2,000** (AED 50,000+). A personal, auto or home loan, or a wealth product,
lifts that to AED 2,000 or AED 6,000. The cashback lands about five months
in, and ENBD can reverse it if twelve consecutive salaries don't arrive. Full
terms are on our [ENBD tracker page](/salary-transfer/emirates-nbd/). The
previous cycle (1 February – 30 June 2026) paid bonus interest on the
balance instead; it is in the
[offer history](/salary-transfer/history/emirates-nbd/).

Account-promotion requirements are separate from credit-card qualification.
Earlier salary-transfer flags for individual cards are not all substantiated
by the current product records. Read each profile's pending terms and confirm
the applicable route with ENBD; a listed salary minimum is not an approval
promise.

## Customer-service notes

The call centre on **+971 600 540 000** runs daily and is reachable in English,
Arabic, Hindi and Urdu — wait times are short on weekday mornings and long on
the first working day of the month. The ENBD mobile app handles card
controls, statement requests, salary-account opening for existing customers,
and the Plus Points redemption flow without a branch visit. Branch density is
highest in Dubai (Sheikh Zayed Road, Deira, JBR, JLT, Business Bay all
covered), thinner in Abu Dhabi where ADCB and FAB dominate the high street.
Missed payments can affect your credit record; confirm reporting and repayment
terms directly with ENBD.

## Bottom line

ENBD suits the Dubai-based expat who flies Emirates, wants Skywards exposure
without a Skywards-only card. Compare any dated salary-transfer promotion on
its own terms rather than assuming it is always available. It may be a less
useful fit if you are
Abu-Dhabi-anchored, fee-sensitive at the premium tier, or want a single
transferable points currency — for those readers FAB Rewards or HSBC
Premier are closer to the brief.
