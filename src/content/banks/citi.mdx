Citibank UAE is the local arm of Citigroup — the US-headquartered global
franchise that has run a UAE consumer business since 1964, longer than HSBC or
Standard Chartered have been on the high street here. For an expat household
the appeal is shape-similar to HSBC's: a recognisable global brand, Citigold
tier that cross-recognises across Citi markets, and a card range built for
set-and-forget cashback rather than the perks-stacking complexity of FAB or
ENBD. Branch density is the thinnest of the four conventional banks in this
batch — a small footprint concentrated in Dubai (DIFC, Bur Dubai, Dubai Marina)
and Abu Dhabi.

For the covered Cash Back card, keep the assigned credit limit and payment
channel exclusions alongside the earning rate. A headline cashback percentage
is not an unrestricted return. Compare fees using the VAT basis labelled in
each product's current fee block, not a bank-wide quoting assumption.

## Card ladder at a glance

Citi's [UAE credit-card directory](https://www.citibank.ae/credit-cards/multiplecards)
lists the cards below for public application. Publicly listed does not mean
everyone qualifies.

- **[Citi Simplicity](/cards/citi-simplicity/)** — no annual membership fee.
  Other borrowing and transaction costs are separate.
- **[Citi Cash Back Credit Card](/cards/citi-cashback/)** — spending above
  the assigned credit limit earns no cashback,
  and utility payments through bank channels are excluded. The linked review
  distinguishes current fees and waiver terms from the incomplete general
  reward model; no unrestricted all-spend return is estimated.
- **[Citi Rewards](/cards/citi-rewards/)** — a ThankYou points card with a
  first-year waiver and a separate spending condition for renewal.
- **[Citi Premier](/cards/citi-premier/)** — a travel points card. Check the
  current partner and redemption terms before valuing the points.
- **[Citi Prestige](/cards/citi-prestige/)** and **[Citi Ultima](/cards/citi-ultima/)** —
  premium travel cards. Compare their annual fees and confirm the benefits
  you expect to use before applying.

## Reward currency, in plain English

Citi UAE does not run a single unified loyalty currency the way FAB Rewards or
ENBD Plus Points do. The cashback line pays direct AED cashback automatically.
The points line earns Citi ThankYou Points, redeemable through the Citi app
catalogue. Check the applicable statement-credit, merchant, travel and transfer
terms for the exact card instead of assuming a fixed points value or the
same redemption options across the range. The new source profiles cover
published membership fees and minimum income; they do not estimate rewards
value or confirm every application condition.

## Salary transfer and eligibility

Use the current salary-transfer status above and the [tracker](/salary-transfer/)
for covered account promotions. Account terms, Citigold relationship conditions
and a particular card's application requirements are separate. The linked Cash
Back review gives its recorded eligibility; meeting a threshold is not an
approval guarantee. Read any campaign retention and clawback conditions before
moving your salary.

## Customer-service notes

Citi's UAE call centre and digital channels are documented on
**[citibank.ae](https://www.citibank.ae)**; the mobile app handles card
controls, statement requests, ThankYou Points redemption and Cash Back tracking.
Branch density is the thinnest of the four banks in this batch — fewer than ten
branches across the UAE — which is the practical cost of choosing an
international franchise here. The flip side is that Citi's global call centre
network and the Citigold cross-recognition give a frequent-traveller household
something the locally headquartered banks cannot replicate. Note the
**VAT basis** shown on each card's fee block before comparing costs. Citi
reports to AECB. Missed payments can affect your credit record; confirm reporting
and repayment terms directly with Citi.

## Bottom line

Citi suits the expat who values a globally recognised brand, expects to move
between Citi markets over a career, or wants to compare direct cashback with
points. Consider Cash Back only after checking the eligible purchases, assigned
limit, fees and waiver conditions. Compare any account promotion separately;
this is not a claim that Citi is the simplest or highest-return UAE option.
