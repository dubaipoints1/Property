This page covers the conventional Titanium, Platinum and World Mastercard profiles and the separately named Islamic Titanium. Compare the standard annual fees in the four profiles, without treating them as the total cost of holding the card.

## Compare the annual fee period first

Platinum and World have separate first-year and standard renewal terms. The issuer also lists an annual spending waiver for those cards. The two Titanium profiles do not carry a standard primary annual fee, but that does not mean every other charge is zero. Check the exact product and the applicable tariff rather than carrying terms from one tier or product family to another.

## What the issuer lists

The [UAB forms and policies page](https://www.uab.ae/help-center/forms-and-policies) links separate conventional and Islamic applications and pricing documents. The cards shown here are named in the relevant application. That is not a guarantee of approval, a verified online tier selector or a complete eligibility test.
