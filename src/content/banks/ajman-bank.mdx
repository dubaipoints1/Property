<h2 id="bank-fee-sources">BRight fee profiles</h2>

These Ajman Bank profiles cover the published primary annual fee for [BRight Titanium](/cards/ajman-bank-bright-titanium/), [BRight Platinum](/cards/ajman-bank-bright-platinum/) and [BRight World](/cards/ajman-bank-bright-world/). They are not application recommendations. No eligibility, rewards return or current salary-transfer offer is assessed here.

Use the [credit-card Key Facts Statement](https://www.ajmanbank.ae/site/files/KFS_CREDIT_CARD_MAY_2025.pdf) and [retail tariff](https://www.ajmanbank.ae/site/tariff-of-charges-retail.html) to check the bank’s fee disclosures for the exact tier. The fee profile does not establish a separate joining fee or the total cost of using the card.
