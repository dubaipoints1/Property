<h2 id="bank-fee-sources">CBI card fee profiles</h2>

Commercial Bank International (CBI) publishes separate primary annual-fee terms for [Mastercard Titanium](/cards/cbi-mastercard-titanium/) and [Rewards Platinum Mastercard](/cards/cbi-rewards-platinum-mastercard/). These are fee references, not application recommendations.

Use the bank’s [credit-card tariff](https://www.cbiuae.com/media/uqziuyjp/cbi-credit-card-schedule-of-charges-nov-2026-v1-1.pdf) to check the applicable charges. Do not use the separate Visa Platinum annual fee to price the Rewards Platinum Mastercard. No eligibility, rewards return or current salary-transfer offer is assessed here.
