IHG One Rewards is the loyalty programme of IHG Hotels & Resorts:
InterContinental, Six Senses, Regent, Kimpton, Vignette Collection,
Hotel Indigo, voco, Crowne Plaza, Holiday Inn, Holiday Inn Express,
Staybridge Suites and the rest. For UAE residents its strength is on
the ground: IHG says it runs **39 hotels across eight brands in the
UAE**, with **110 more** due over the next three to five years, per its
29 September 2026
[news release](https://www.ihgplc.com/en/news-and-media/news-releases/2026/ihg-hotels-and-resorts-to-bring-its-midscale-brand-garner-hotels-to-the-middle-east-with-a-dual-signing-in-the-uae).
That makes it a currency many residents earn on work trips and
staycations without meaning to.

A sourcing note up front: tier thresholds, tier bonuses, earn rates
and the expiry rule on this page were read from IHG's
[Membership Terms and Conditions](https://www.ihg.com/content/us/en/customer-care/member-tc)
on 19 October 2026. Reward Night prices are dynamic and are not quoted.

## Tier structure

Every member starts at **Club** level. Above it sit four elite tiers,
earned in a calendar year on qualifying nights or Elite Qualifying
Points:

| Tier | Qualifies at | Bonus on points |
|---|---|---|
| Silver Elite | 10 nights | 20% |
| Gold Elite | 20 nights or 40,000 qualifying points | 40% |
| Platinum Elite | 40 nights or 60,000 qualifying points | 60% |
| Diamond Elite | 70 nights or 120,000 qualifying points | 100% |

Status earned in one year lasts to the end of the next. Three rules
make the ladder easier to climb than it looks:

- **Reward Nights count.** IHG's terms count nights paid for with
  points as qualifying nights.
- **Rollover nights.** Nights above what Gold, Platinum or Diamond
  needed roll into the next year and count towards status there.
- **Milestone Rewards** are earned every 10 nights, from 20 nights up
  to 100, on top of the tier itself.

Only one night per day counts, so two rooms on the same night earn one
qualifying night, not two.

## How Reward Nights are priced

IHG publishes no award chart. Its terms say the points price of a
Reward Night "may vary according to several factors, including but not
limited to brand, hotel, and day of week". There are no blackout dates,
but rooms are limited.

So the only way to judge a redemption is on the day: compare the points
price with the cash rate for the same room and date on ihg.com. If you
are short, **Points & Cash** tops up the balance with points bought at
booking. You can also buy up to 200,000 points a year outright.

## What an IHG point is worth

**We have no house valuation for IHG One Rewards yet.** It sits as
"Pending" on our [valuations table](/valuations/). Our
[methodology](/valuations/methodology/) samples the best off-peak award
price at a UAE property against the cash rate for the same night, and
we have not run that sampling for IHG. No UAE card earns IHG points, so
no card review has needed a figure either. Until the sampling is done,
this page publishes no fils figure.

For a single stay, divide the room's cash price in AED by its points
price on the same date. That tells you what the redemption is worth to
you.

## Who IHG One Rewards suits

IHG suits the UAE resident whose stays are already in its hotels:
Holiday Inn Express or Crowne Plaza on work trips around the Gulf,
InterContinental or a voco for a staycation. Silver at 10 nights is
reachable for most, and it turns the 12-month expiry clock off.

It suits you less if you want hotel points from a credit card. No UAE
card earns IHG points. Without an award chart, the programme also rewards
checking prices more than planning ahead.

## How to earn from the UAE

- **Paid IHG stays.** Per IHG's terms, 10 points per USD 1 or local
  currency equivalent at most IHG hotels, and 5 at Staybridge Suites
  and Candlewood Suites. The elite bonuses above come on top.
- **Promotions.** IHG runs registration-based promotions; we covered
  its [pick-your-points promotion](/news/ihg-one-rewards-choice-points-promo-ends-31-august/),
  which ran to 31 August 2026.
  Register before you stay.
- **New UAE hotels.** IHG's first two
  [Garner Hotels](/news/ihg-garner-hotels-uae-dubai-al-jaddaf-ras-al-khaimah/),
  in Dubai and Ras Al Khaimah, are expected to open in 2026.
- **UAE credit cards:** none. No card in our data earns IHG One
  Rewards points or transfers into them.

## What to watch

- **The expiry clock stops at Silver.** Club-level points expire after
  12 months with no earn or redeem transaction. A stay only protects
  them if it changes your balance, up or down.
- **No chart, no anchor.** Points prices move with the hotel and the
  day. Run the cash-versus-points check every time.
- **Conversions out.** Moving points to Qatar Avios is worth a look
  only during a bonus, and only once you have seen the rate in your
  account.
