AirRewards is the loyalty programme of Air Arabia, the low-cost carrier
based in Sharjah. Its registration page lists joining routes for
Sharjah and Abu Dhabi alongside Morocco, Egypt and Saudi Arabia, so for
UAE residents it is the home programme of the budget airline at both
ends of the country. It works less like an airline miles scheme and more
like a cashback wallet: points are earned on what you pay and spent at a
fixed rate against the fare.

The name has changed in spelling: Air Arabia now writes it
**AirRewards**, and the older `airewards` address on its site returns a
"page not found".

A sourcing note up front: everything on this page was read from Air
Arabia's [programme page](https://www.airarabia.com/en/airrewards/discover-airrewards/the-airrewards-program),
its [AirRewards Terms and Conditions](https://www.airarabia.com/en/airrewards/air-rewards-terms-and-conditions)
and its [RAKBANK card page](https://www.airarabia.com/en/airrewards/rakbank-air-arabia-platinum-credit-card)
on 20 October 2026.

## How AirRewards works

There are **no elite tiers**. Neither the programme page nor the terms
describe status levels, lounge benefits or tier bonuses: every member
earns and spends at the same rates.

**Earning.** Per the terms, members earn:

| On | Points per USD 1 |
|---|---|
| Flights (base fare and surcharge) and baggage allowance | 5 |
| Meal selection, insurance, seat selection and Hala services | 10 |

Points are not earned on government taxes and fees, or on other
travellers' flights, even when they are on your booking and you paid for
them. Each member's AirRewards ID has to be entered for that person to
earn. Charter, special Hajj/Umrah and repatriation flights do not earn.
A missing-points claim must reach AirRewards within 90 days of the
flight.

**Spending.** Points pay the base fare and surcharge of any Air Arabia
flight, and eligible add-ons. Air Arabia says there are no blackout
dates and that every seat is available every day. The number of points
a flight costs follows the published fare at the time of booking, so a
peak-date flight costs more points, just as it costs more cash. Taxes
and fees are always paid in cash. The minimum balance to redeem against
a flight and baggage reward is **3,000 points**, and **Cash + Points**
lets you combine the two.

## Family account

Up to **eight family members** can pool into a Head of Household's
account. The head must be at least 21. Eligible relatives are siblings,
parents, grandparents, spouses and children. A family member cannot be
swapped out for 12 months once listed.

There is a catch, and it is in the terms rather than on the programme
page: **50% of the points a family member earns go to the Head of
Household, and the other 50% are forfeited.** The head controls all
redemptions. For a family that flies Air Arabia together, each person
earning in their own account and redeeming their own points keeps every
point, at the cost of smaller balances.

## What a point is worth

Air Arabia publishes the value itself: **100 points = USD 1**, "the same
value anytime of the year". At the dirham's peg of AED 3.6725 to the
dollar, that is about **3.67 fils per point**. This is our arithmetic on
Air Arabia's own figure, not a DubaiPoints valuation, and it applies
only to the part of a booking points can pay for: the base fare and
surcharge, and add-ons. Our [valuations table](/valuations/) lists
AirRewards as "Pending" with no house figure.

On the same stated value, the earn rates above come to 5% back on the
fare and 10% back on meals, seats, insurance and Hala services.

## Who AirRewards suits

AirRewards suits the UAE resident who already flies Air Arabia out of
Sharjah or Abu Dhabi: short-haul family trips, visits home to the
subcontinent, Egypt or Morocco. Because points are worth a fixed amount
off the fare, there is no award chart to learn and no saver seat to hunt
for.

It suits you less if you want a programme that rewards loyalty with
status, lounges or upgrades. There are no tiers. It also suits a
travelling family less than the "Family account" heading suggests,
because pooling forfeits half of every family member's points.

## How to earn from the UAE

- **Flying Air Arabia.** 5 points per USD 1 on the fare and baggage,
  10 on meals, seats, insurance and Hala services, per the terms.
- **The RAKBANK Air Arabia Platinum Credit Card.** Air Arabia's page for
  the card lists 1.75 points per AED 5 spent on airarabia.com and
  outside the UAE, 1.25 points per AED 5 inside the UAE, and 0.30 points
  per AED 5 in "special categories", with conditions on RAKBANK's site.
  At Air Arabia's stated value, 1.25 points per AED 5 is about 0.9% back
  and 1.75 points about 1.3%. The page also advertised a 20,000-point
  welcome bonus, an annual free return ticket and no joining fee on
  20 October 2026. **We have not reviewed this card**, and it is not in
  our card data, so check the current terms with
  [RAKBANK](/banks/rakbank/) before applying.
- **Partners.** The programme page lists UAE and travel partners
  including Union Insurance (200 points per AED 100 on motor insurance),
  Kalyan Jewellers (up to 40 points per AED 100), Buzzpay (3 points per
  AED 10 on mobile recharges), Sixt (up to 6 points per USD 1) and
  Kaligo (up to 15 points per USD 1 on hotels).
- **Bank promotions.** Some UAE bank offers pay out in Air Arabia
  travel rather than points, such as Emirates NBD's
  [new-account Air Arabia return ticket](/news/emirates-nbd-new-account-air-arabia-return-ticket-31-december/)
  to 31 December 2026.
- **Other UAE credit cards:** none. No card in our data earns AirRewards
  points or transfers into them.

## What to watch

- **Points expire 24 months after you earn them.** This is a hard
  expiry per transaction, not an inactivity rule: flying again does not
  extend older points. Spend them on a regular basis.
- **Pooling halves family earnings.** Read the 50% rule before you set
  up a Family Account.
- **Taxes are cash.** Points cover the base fare and surcharge, not the
  taxes and fees, so a "free" flight still has a cash bill.
- **No conversion rate published.** The Etihad Guest conversion is real
  per Air Arabia, but check the rate in your account before you move
  points.
