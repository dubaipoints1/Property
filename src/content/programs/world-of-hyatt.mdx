World of Hyatt is the loyalty programme of Hyatt's brand family: Park
Hyatt, Grand Hyatt, Hyatt Regency, Andaz, Alila, Hyatt Centric, Hyatt
Place and the rest, plus the all-inclusive resort brands. It is a
smaller estate than Marriott's or Hilton's, and for UAE residents it
has one feature neither of them has: a **published award chart**. You
can look up what a free night costs before you earn the points for it.

A sourcing note up front: tier thresholds, tier bonuses and the award
chart on this page were read from Hyatt's own
[programme overview](https://world.hyatt.com/content/gp/en/program-overview.html)
and [free-nights page](https://world.hyatt.com/content/gp/en/rewards/free-nights-upgrades.html),
and the earn rate and expiry rule from Hyatt's
[programme terms](https://www.hyatt.com/help/terms/world-of-hyatt), all
on 19 October 2026. Hyatt's site lists its hotels on an interactive map
we could not read, so this page does not count Hyatt's UAE hotels.

## Tier structure

World of Hyatt has four levels: **Member**, **Discoverist**,
**Explorist** and **Globalist**. You qualify in a calendar year on
nights or on **Base Points**. Bonus points from promotions and tier
bonuses do not count.

| Tier | Qualifies at | Bonus on points |
|---|---|---|
| Discoverist | 10 nights or 25,000 Base Points | 10% |
| Explorist | 30 nights or 50,000 Base Points | 20% |
| Globalist | 60 nights or 100,000 Base Points | 30% |

At the base rate of 5 points per USD 1, the points routes mean USD
5,000, USD 10,000 and USD 20,000 of eligible Hyatt spend in a year:
about AED 18,360, AED 36,725 and AED 73,450 at the dirham's USD peg.
For most UAE residents the nights route is the realistic one. Nights
count when you pay an eligible rate or redeem a free night award.

What the tiers deliver, per Hyatt's overview:

- **Discoverist**: 2 p.m. late checkout on availability, complimentary
  premium internet where available and a daily bottle of water.
- **Explorist**: upgraded rooms on availability at check-in, 2 p.m.
  late checkout and early access to award-night inventory.
- **Globalist**: upgrades up to standard suites, Club lounge access or
  complimentary breakfast, 4 p.m. late checkout and early access to
  award nights. Hyatt also has a **Lifetime Globalist** level at
  1,000,000 lifetime Base Points.

Separately, **Milestone Rewards** begin at 20 qualifying nights and
continue every 10 nights up to 150. The choices include points, Club
access and suite upgrades.

## The award chart

Hyatt sorts its hotels into eight categories and prices a standard-room
free night at one of five levels per category. Per Hyatt's free-nights
page, points per night for a standard room:

| Category | Lowest | Low | Moderate | Upper | Top |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3,000 | 4,500 | 6,000 | 7,500 | 9,000 |
| 2 | 6,000 | 7,500 | 10,000 | 12,000 | 15,000 |
| 3 | 8,000 | 12,000 | 15,000 | 17,500 | 20,000 |
| 4 | 12,000 | 15,000 | 20,000 | 22,500 | 25,000 |
| 5 | 15,000 | 20,000 | 25,000 | 30,000 | 35,000 |
| 6 | 20,000 | 25,000 | 30,000 | 35,000 | 40,000 |
| 7 | 25,000 | 30,000 | 35,000 | 45,000 | 55,000 |
| 8 | 35,000 | 45,000 | 55,000 | 65,000 | 75,000 |

Club rooms, standard suites and premium suites have their own, higher
tables on the same page, and the all-inclusive resorts have a separate
chart. Hyatt says standard-room free nights have no blackout dates.

The chart sets the range. Which of the five levels applies on a given
date is Hyatt's call, so check the hotel's category and the date's level
on hyatt.com before you count on a price. We have not mapped the UAE
hotels to categories.

## What a Hyatt point is worth

**We have no house valuation for World of Hyatt yet.** Our card reviews
value hotel currencies at a cost-basis baseline in fils per point (2.5
for Bonvoy, 1.5 for Hilton), and no UAE card earns Hyatt points, so
there has been no review that needed one. We have not sampled UAE award
prices against cash rates either, which is how our
[valuations methodology](/valuations/methodology/) sets a hotel figure.
Until we do, this page publishes no fils figure.

What you can do yourself: divide the cash price of the room (in AED,
after tax) by the points price on the same date. That is the value you
are getting for that stay. The chart makes the sum easy, because the
points price is known in advance.

## Who World of Hyatt suits

World of Hyatt suits the UAE resident who travels to cities where Hyatt
is strong and pays for those stays: the business traveller on the
nights route to Discoverist or Explorist, or the family that wants a
fixed price for a resort stay a year ahead. Because the chart is
published, it rewards planning rather than luck.

It suits you less if you want your hotel points to come from a credit
card. No UAE card earns Hyatt points, so every point is earned on the
ground. If you would rather earn hotel points on everyday spend, look at
[Marriott Bonvoy](/hotels/marriott-bonvoy/), which UAE cards do earn.

## How to earn from the UAE

- **Paid Hyatt stays.** Per Hyatt's terms, 5 Base Points per USD 1
  (or the USD equivalent) on an eligible rate, and 2.5 at Hyatt
  Studios hotels. The tier bonuses above come on top.
- **Spending at the hotel.** Eligible dining, spa and other charges
  billed to your room earn Base Points too.
- **Promotions.** Hyatt runs registration-based offers, such as its
  [3,000 bonus points every three nights](/news/world-of-hyatt-3000-bonus-points-every-three-nights-15-december/)
  promotion to 15 December 2026 and a
  [double-points offer at new hotels](/news/world-of-hyatt-new-hotels-double-points-25000-cap/).
  Register before you stay.
- **UAE credit cards:** none. No card in our data earns World of Hyatt
  points or transfers into them. The World of Hyatt credit card that
  Hyatt's site promotes is a Chase product.

## What to watch

- **Inactivity forfeits the balance.** Twenty-four months with no
  earning or redemption and every point goes. One paid night or one
  redemption resets the clock.
- **Price levels move.** The chart is fixed, but where a date sits on
  it is not. A Moderate night today can be Upper next season.
- **Conversions are final.** Points moved to Qatar Avios or another
  partner cannot be moved back, per Hyatt's terms.
- **No UAE footprint count yet.** We have not published how many Hyatt
  hotels operate in the UAE, because Hyatt's own hotel list did not
  render for us. This note comes out when we have a count from Hyatt.
