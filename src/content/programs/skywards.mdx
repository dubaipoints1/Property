## How to earn Skywards Miles

There are three main routes for UAE residents:

### From UAE-issued co-branded credit cards

The Emirates NBD Skywards range is one direct-earning option. The
[Skywards Infinite](/cards/emirates-nbd-skywards-infinite/) earns
**2 miles per USD 1** (roughly 0.54 miles per AED) on Emirates, flydubai,
duty free, and eligible UAE food-delivery and car-booking apps; the
[Skywards Signature](/cards/emirates-nbd-skywards-signature/) earns
**1.5 miles per USD 1** on the same partner categories. Both post miles
directly into your Skywards account with no transfer step or ratio loss.

Check each card’s current welcome offer and price an eligible upgrade before counting on the bonus.

### From flying Emirates and flydubai

[Emirates says](https://www.emirates.com/english/skywards/earn-miles/) earnings vary by route, fare type and cabin. Your tier can add bonus Skywards Miles. Use the [Miles Calculator](https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/skywards/miles-calculator/) to check a specific flight on Emirates or flydubai.

### From transfer partners

Marriott Bonvoy and Accor ALL can top up a Skywards balance. Check the [transfer ratios](#transfer-partners) and confirm the award is available before moving points.

## What a Skywards Mile is worth

**For comparing cards:** DubaiPoints uses a **2 fils (AED 0.02) per mile** editorial baseline. It is not a live redemption quote.

| Redemption type | What to compare |
|---|---|
| Economy-to-Business upgrade | Eligible Economy fare, upgrade miles and cash charges versus the Business fare |
| Long-haul Business on Emirates | Check the live award and equivalent cash fare |
| First on the A380 | Check aircraft, eligibility, availability and the full cash charge |
| Economy on Emirates or flydubai | Compare the award with a cash fare you would actually pay |
| [Cash+Miles](https://www.emirates.com/english/skywards/cash-plus-miles/) | Use the cash saving and miles displayed at checkout; there is no fixed conversion rate |

**For booking a flight:** take the cash fare you would otherwise pay, subtract the cash charges on the reward, then divide by the miles required. Try the [Cash or Miles worksheet](/news/cash-or-miles-uae-flight-break-even/) or read the [valuation methodology](/valuations/methodology/).

## Best sweet-spot redemptions

The four documented sweet spots are listed in the Programme reference block
below the prose. Two of them are worth additional context:

### Economy-to-business-class regional upgrade — the canonical break-even

For a regional trip from Dubai, compare the price of a paid Business ticket with an upgrade-eligible Economy fare plus the miles and any cash charges. Confirm upgrade eligibility and availability with Emirates before buying the Economy ticket.

For illustration, at our 2-fils baseline, 30,000–50,000 miles represent AED 600–1,000 for card comparisons. That arithmetic does not tell you the mileage price or how much cash an upgrade will save.

### DXB → LHR in Business

Emirates lists [one-way Business Classic Rewards as Flex Plus only](https://www.emirates.com/english/help/faq-topics/emirates-skywards/faq/where-can-i-find-out-how-to-spend-my-miles/).
Check the [Miles Calculator](https://www.emirates.com/english/skywards/miles-calculator/) and then price your actual dates in the award search.
Subtract the full cash payable on the reward from the fare you would
otherwise buy, then divide the difference by the miles quoted. That is
your cash-fare comparison, not our published cost basis.

## Tier structure (Blue / Silver / Gold / Platinum)

Tier Miles count towards status; Skywards Miles pay for rewards. Check [Emirates’ tier rules](https://www.emirates.com/english/skywards/membership-tiers/) for qualification and [lounge terms](https://www.emirates.com/english/experience/our-lounges/partners/terms-and-conditions/) for access and guests.

| Tier | Qualification |
|---|---|
| Blue | Entry tier |
| Silver | 25,000 Tier Miles or 25 qualifying flights |
| Gold | 50,000 Tier Miles or 50 qualifying flights |
| Platinum | 150,000 Tier Miles and at least one qualifying Business or First Class flight |

Emirates says Tier Miles are earned mainly on Emirates, flydubai and eligible Emirates-marketed codeshares. [Emirates NBD’s Skywards Infinite](https://www.emiratesnbd.com/en/cards/credit-cards/skywards-infinite-credit-card/) lists Silver status while the card remains active; check the bank’s current conditions before relying on it.

## Transfer partners

Two [hotel transfer routes published by Emirates](https://www.emirates.com/au/english/skywards/partners/?partner=hotels):

- **Marriott Bonvoy → Skywards:** published base ratio of 3:1. Compare the flight you can actually book with using your points for a Marriott stay.
- **Accor ALL → Skywards:** 4,000 ALL Reward points convert to 2,000 Skywards Miles. Compare the flight saving with using the points directly at Accor.

For UAE bank programmes, check the [transfer ratios guide](/guides/uae-transfer-ratios-2026/) and the issuer’s current terms before moving points.

## Mile expiry mechanics

Per clause 9 of Emirates' published
[Programme Rules](https://www.emirates.com/english/skywards/emirates-skywards-programme-rules/),
Skywards Miles are **valid for three years from the date of the flight or
partner transaction that earned them**, and then expire on the **last day
of your birthday month** that follows the end of that three-year period.
Emirates' own worked example: a validity period ending 15 January 2026,
for a member born on 14 January, expires on **31 January 2027**.

**Only Platinum stops the clock.** Under clause 6.10.1, Miles held by
Platinum members do not expire for as long as Platinum status is retained;
Emirates shows them an "Adjusted Expiry" date tied to the tier review
instead. Silver and Gold do **not** change when Miles expire. If you drop
from Platinum, Miles carrying an Adjusted Expiry date expire on that date.

Activity does **not** extend Skywards Miles. Earning a single new mile does
not reset the expiry on older miles, unlike some competitor programmes
(e.g. AAdvantage). Emirates does sell extra validity: Miles due to expire
within 90 days can be extended by 12 months through its paid Extend Miles
product (clause 10), at whatever price Emirates is charging at the time.

**A change has been reported for miles earned from 1 June 2027.** Our [news report](/news/skywards-miles-expiry-birthday-month-rule-ends-2027/) covers the move to expiry at the end of the earning month three years later. Check Emirates’ rules and the expiry shown in your account before planning a redemption.

## Watch out for

- **Terms and availability can change.** Look up the trip before transferring points. An indicative mileage price does not reserve a seat.
- **Taxes and carrier charges can change the answer.** Compare the full cash charge on the award with the cash fare, not just the miles shown.
- **Reward pricing depends on the itinerary.** Compare the actual mileage
  and cash charges. In Emirates Business, one-way Classic Rewards are Flex
  Plus only; check return itineraries separately for Saver availability.
- **Cash+Miles and Classic Rewards are different.** Compare each available quote on its own terms; do not assume the same value per mile.

## Is Skywards worth chasing?

- **You fly Emirates or flydubai regularly:** compare the [UAE earning cards](#programme-cards) if you have a realistic use for the miles before they expire.
- **You rarely fly either airline:** compare a [cashback card](/cards/fab-cashback/) or transferable points before paying a co-brand fee.
- **You mainly fly Etihad:** compare [Etihad Guest](/airlines/etihad-guest/) for the routes you actually book.

## Bottom line

Price one flight you would actually book before choosing a Skywards card. The miles are only useful if the dates, cash charges and expiry work for you.
