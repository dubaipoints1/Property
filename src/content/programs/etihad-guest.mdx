Etihad Guest is worth comparing if you regularly fly from Abu Dhabi
or have a specific Etihad reward in mind. Before moving points or
choosing a card, check the award you can actually book and the
expiry date shown in your account.

## Etihad Guest in 60 seconds

- **Earn:** credit an eligible Etihad or partner flight, use a UAE
  co-branded card on qualifying spend, or transfer from an eligible
  partner once you know what you want to book.
- **Spend:** compare the cash quote with the miles plus taxes and
  fees for the same itinerary. DubaiPoints uses a **2 fils per mile**
  baseline; that is not a promise about any particular seat.
- **Expiry:** Etihad's FAQ says qualifying flight activity can
  extend the balance for Bronze, Silver and Gold. Don't rely on
  buying something with a co-brand card to keep your miles active.

## How to earn Etihad Guest Miles

### From UAE-issued co-branded credit cards

Examples include the [Emirates NBD Etihad Guest Visa Inspire](/cards/emirates-nbd-etihad-guest-inspire/),
[Emirates NBD Etihad Guest Visa Elevate](/cards/emirates-nbd-etihad-guest-elevate/)
and [FAB Etihad Guest Infinite](/cards/fab-etihad-guest-infinite/).
Use the linked reviews for the current fee, qualification conditions
and separate earn rates for general and Etihad spending. A card
welcome bonus or status benefit is not a substitute for reading its
conditions.

### From flying Etihad

Use the [Etihad Guest Miles Calculator](https://www.etihad.com/en-us/etihadguest/miles-calculator)
for the route, booking class and fare you are considering. Etihad's
[programme terms](https://www.etihad.com/en/legal/terms-and-conditions)
distinguish qualifying flights and fare classes. Check the operating
airline and your fare rather than applying the Etihad rate to a
partner ticket.

### From transfer partners

Start with the [UAE Transfer Finder](/transfer-finder/?to=etihad-guest).
It shows documented routes and flags when a bank's rate or minimum
cannot be confirmed. [Marriott Bonvoy](https://www.marriott.com/en-gb/loyalty/redeem/travel/points-to-miles.mi)
and [ALL Accor](https://all.accor.com/loyalty-program/partners/use/etihad/index.en.shtml)
also publish transfers to Etihad Guest. Check the sending programme's
minimum, account conditions and timing before transferring. An
expired points-exchange promotion does not set the rate for a
transfer made today.

## What an Etihad Guest Mile is worth

Use two different numbers for two different decisions. DubaiPoints'
**2 fils (AED 0.02) per mile** baseline is a conservative comparison
figure for earning. To decide whether to spend miles you already
hold, compare the cash fare you would actually buy with the fees
and taxes payable on the award. Divide the difference by the miles
required. Neither the baseline nor a past example confirms current
award availability.

## Best sweet-spot redemptions

<ProgrammeReference programme="etihad-guest" kind="redemptions" />

For a Business award, check the complete itinerary and the cash
amount due on the award. For a short-haul flight, start with the
cash quote you would pay rather than assuming that an award offers
a saving.

For an upgrade, start in **Manage My Booking**. Etihad's
[booking FAQ](https://www.etihad.com/en-ae/help/faq/manage) says
eligibility depends on the fare, route and availability. An upgrade
quote is not the price of a standalone award and does not make the
original ticket's conditions disappear.

## Tier structure (Silver / Gold / Platinum / Emerald)

Etihad separates standard qualification from renewal, and
publishes a reduced-criteria offer for some existing members.
Check the [Tiers and Benefits page](https://www.etihad.com/en/etihadguest/tiers-and-benefits)
and the requirements shown in your account rather than applying the
same threshold to everyone. The published Silver, Gold and Platinum
criteria include qualifying flight activity; earning Tier Miles on
an eligible UAE card does not by itself meet that part of the rule.
Emerald is based on qualifying flight spend.

## Transfer partners

<ProgrammeReference programme="etihad-guest" kind="partners" />

A transfer into Etihad Guest is different from booking an award on
a partner airline. Check the award first and do not assume you can
send the points back to the original programme if the seat goes.

## Mile expiry mechanics — the Skywards differentiator

<ProgrammeReference programme="etihad-guest" kind="expiry" />

For Bronze, Silver and Gold, Etihad's [UAE member FAQ](https://www.etihad.com/en-ae/help/faq/etihad-guest)
says miles are valid for **18 months** and can be extended by flying
with Etihad or a partner airline. Check that your flight qualifies
and has credited; a card purchase, a hotel-point conversion or an
award redemption should not be used as a substitute. For Platinum
and Emerald, [clauses 4.1.11–4.1.15](https://www.etihad.com/en/legal/terms-and-conditions)
explain the protection while the tier is held and the expiry rule
after downgrade.

[Skywards' programme rules](https://www.emirates.com/english/skywards/emirates-skywards-programme-rules/)
use a separate standard expiry tied to when miles were earned, with
an adjusted date for Platinum. If you fly occasionally, compare the
expiry dates shown in your accounts rather than assuming either
balance will last longer.

## Watch out for

- **Award availability can change.** A chart or calculator is not
  a reservation; check the live price and seat before transferring.
- **Compare total costs.** An award can have taxes and carrier
  charges. An upgrade also needs an eligible original ticket.
- **Separate earning from expiry.** Miles earned on a card and
  qualifying flying activity are not interchangeable.

## Is Etihad Guest worth chasing?

Compare it against the airline you actually use. Abu Dhabi-based
travellers may find the most useful redemption from their home
airport. If most of your flights leave Dubai, compare equivalent
Etihad and Skywards itineraries and the cost of reaching either
airport before building a second balance.

## Bottom line

Start with the specific trip, check live awards and account
validity, and compare the cash price. Spend or transfer only once
those three checks support the decision.
