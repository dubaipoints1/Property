AlFursan is the loyalty programme of Saudia, Saudi Arabia's national
airline and a SkyTeam member. For UAE residents it is a niche currency:
no UAE card earns it, and it pays off mainly on Saudia's own flights
to and through the Kingdom. It earns its place for residents who fly to the
Kingdom often, for work, family or Umrah, and for families who want one
pooled balance.

A sourcing note up front: tiers, the expiry rule, the family program
and the redemption tables on this page were read from Saudia's own
[membership-tiers page](https://www.saudia.com/loyalty/about-alfursan-program/membership-tiers-benefits/membership-tiers),
[expiry policy](https://www.saudia.com/earn-and-redeem/miles-types/expiry-policy),
[family-program page](https://www.saudia.com/en-US/alfursan-information/family-member-program)
and [redemption table](https://www.saudia.com/en-SA/loyalty/about-alfursan-program/alfursan-miles/miles-redemption/redemption-table-new)
on 20 October 2026. Saudia's main AlFursan address blocked our reader,
and we did not read its per-flight earn chart, so **this page states no
earn rate for flights**.

## Tier structure

AlFursan has three tiers: **Green** (basic), **Silver** ("Elite") and
**Gold** ("Elite Plus"). You qualify in a calendar year on **Tier
Credits**, earned on Saudia and partner-airline flights, or on the number
of eligible flights:

| Tier | To attain | To keep | Bonus Reward Miles |
|---|---|---|---|
| Silver Elite | 25,000 Tier Credits or 20 flights | 20,000 or 15 | 25% |
| Gold Elite Plus | 50,000 Tier Credits or 40 flights | 40,000 or 30 | 50% |

Status runs to the end of December of the following year. Miss the
maintenance target and the downgrade takes effect on 1 January.

Per Saudia's benefits table, both elite tiers get priority boarding,
seat selection, an extra piece of baggage and lounge access. On
international flights a Silver member can bring up to three family
members into the lounge, according to the class of travel, and a Gold
member up to three family members and one guest. Gold checks in at the
First counter, Silver at Business.

## The Reward Ticket table

AlFursan prices Saudia reward flights on a published zone chart. There
are two prices per cabin: a **Reward** seat and, when those are gone, a
**Rewards+** seat at a higher price. One-way, per Saudia's table:

| Zone | Economy | Economy+ | Business | Business+ | First |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gulf (GCC), incl. UAE | 5,000 | 10,000 | 24,000 | 48,000 | 35,000 |
| Domestic Saudi | 4,500 | 9,000 | 15,000 | 30,000 | 22,500 |
| Middle East (Jordan, Lebanon, Egypt, Iraq) | 6,500 | 13,000 | 28,000 | 56,000 | 40,000 |
| Sub-continent (A): Pakistan, North India | 9,000 | 18,000 | 34,000 | 68,000 | 47,500 |
| Sub-continent (B): South India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Maldives | 12,000 | 24,000 | 37,000 | 74,000 | 52,500 |
| Europe (B): Europe except Turkey | 12,000 | 24,000 | 44,000 | 88,000 | 62,500 |

The table covers more zones (Africa, Turkey, the Far East, North
America) on Saudia's page. Taxes, service charges and carrier-imposed
charges are paid on top. Reward tickets can be booked for anyone, are
valid for one year from issue, can be refunded or rebooked for a fee,
and earn no miles or Tier Credits.

## Family program

You can nominate **up to 8 family members**. Each keeps their own
membership number and earns Tier Credits towards their own status,
while the Reward Miles everyone earns collect in one family pool.
Children aged 2 to 12 earn 75% of the miles on their flights; Saudia's
page says all other members "receive the full amount of Miles earned for
their flights". Unlike some family schemes, nothing on that page takes a
cut of what goes into the pool.

## What a mile is worth

**We have no house valuation for AlFursan.** It is not on our
[valuations table](/valuations/), no UAE card earns it, and we have not
sampled Saudia's cash fares against the reward chart, which is how our
[methodology](/valuations/methodology/) would set a figure. This page
publishes no fils value.

For a single redemption, divide the cash fare you would otherwise pay
(in AED, less the taxes you pay either way) by the miles price. That is
what the miles are worth to you on that ticket.

## Who AlFursan suits

AlFursan suits the UAE resident who flies Saudia to the Kingdom several
times a year: the chart makes a Gulf-zone Economy seat cheap in miles,
and the flights route counts towards Silver at 20 flights a year, which
a regular commuter to Riyadh or Jeddah can reach. The family pool suits
households with relatives who fly Saudia too.

It suits you less if most of your flying is on Emirates, Etihad or a
oneworld carrier. Saudia is in SkyTeam, which has no UAE member
airline, and nothing you spend on a UAE card reaches AlFursan.

## How to earn from the UAE

- **Flying Saudia.** Miles and Tier Credits per flight depend on route
  and fare; we did not read Saudia's earn chart, so check the figure
  on saudia.com before you book. Elite members get the 25%
  or 50% bonus on Reward Miles.
- **SkyTeam partner flights** earn Reward Miles and count as qualifying
  activity, per Saudia's expiry-policy page.
- **UAE credit cards:** none. No card in our data earns AlFursan miles
  or transfers into them.

## What to watch

- **Two years of silence wipes the balance.** Any qualifying activity
  extends every mile by two years; none in two years and all of them
  go, with no reinstatement.
- **Rewards+ costs double in Economy.** On the Gulf zone it is 10,000
  against 5,000. Check for a Reward seat on another date first.
- **Upgrades are Saudia-only and fare-dependent.** Not on codeshares
  operated by another airline, not on Guest Saver (U class) or special
  fares.
- **Status is calendar-year.** Tier Credits reset on 1 January, so a
  push for Silver has to land inside one year.
