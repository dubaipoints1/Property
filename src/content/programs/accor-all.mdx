ALL Accor Reward Points can reduce the price of an **eligible paid
hotel booking**. Start by signing in, checking whether your room rate
accepts points and comparing the final AED price with the same rate
without points. Booking directly is not, by itself, proof that points
will apply.

## How to earn from the UAE

- **Eligible paid stays.** Book through an eligible channel at a
  participating property. Accor's [programme terms](https://all.accor.com/a/en/loyalty-program/legal/terms-and-conditions.html)
  give the earning rules and explain how brand and tier affect the
  points credited.
- **Dining without a stay.** Some Accor venues participate in a separate
  earning and redemption scheme. Ask the venue which expenses qualify
  before ordering; the hotel booking rate does not apply to that bill.
- **Transfers from HSBC UAE.** Accor [publishes a transfer route and a minimum](https://all.accor.com/a/en/loyalty-program/partners/conditions/hsbcuaeandqatar.html)
  for eligible HSBC Rewards members. Check the rate, minimum and availability
  in HSBC Rewards before transferring. These are Reward Points, not a way
  to qualify for ALL status.

## What an ALL point is worth

For eligible hotel bookings through ALL.com or the ALL Accor app,
[section 10 of Accor's terms](https://all.accor.com/a/en/loyalty-program/legal/terms-and-conditions.html)
lists **1,000 Reward Points for EUR 20 off** or **2,000 for EUR 40 off**.
After that, online redemptions use multiples of 2,000. This is an
eligible hotel discount, not a cash value or a promise that points will
apply to every rate, tax or expense. Accor applies its own exchange
rate when the charge is not in euros; use the actual AED reduction
shown for your booking.

Accor also publishes transfers to [Emirates Skywards](https://all.accor.com/a/en/loyalty-program/partners/airlines/emirates/points-transfer.html)
and [Etihad Guest](https://all.accor.com/loyalty-program/partners/use/etihad/index.en.shtml).
Check the current transfer minimum and the award you intend to book
before giving up a hotel discount. Keep dining and airline transfers
out of your hotel-value comparison: their terms are different.

## Tier structure

Reward Points are for redemptions. Status Points and Status Nights
are for tier qualification. Check Accor's [membership benefits and terms](https://all.accor.com/a/en/loyalty-program/legal/terms-and-conditions.html)
for the status you are trying to reach and the stay you plan to book.
A benefit on one brand or rate does not show it applies to another.

## What to watch

<ProgrammeReference programme="accor-all" kind="expiry" />

- **Check the expiry date before booking.** Accor's terms distinguish
  the checkout date for a qualifying hotel stay from the credit date
  for other qualifying transactions. Review your account activity and
  do not assume that making a booking or redeeming for a room extends
  the balance.
- **Check cancellation and prepayment.** Some prepaid rates require a
  card payment, and Accor's rules restrict when redeemed points are
  returned. Check the rate conditions before replacing a flexible cash
  booking.
- **Check the property and charge.** The presence of a room for sale
  does not guarantee an eligible points discount. Some rates and
  brands are excluded, and dining without a stay follows separate terms.

## Who Accor ALL suits

ALL is worth comparing if you already book eligible paid stays at
participating hotels and can use points before expiry. If you are
changing hotels to earn or spend points, compare the actual AED
saving with the alternative booking before paying more. If your
goal is an airline redemption, check live seats and the transfer
rules first.
