**ALL Accor** is paying **triple Reward points** on stays of two nights or
more at participating hotels in the UAE and eleven other markets. The
deadline that matters is close: you must **register and book by 25 October
2026**. The stay itself can be any time up to **31 January 2027**, which
covers National Day and the Dubai Shopping Festival season.

Everything below comes from Accor's own
[offer page and conditions of sale](https://all.accor.com/a/en/loyalty-program/promotions-offers/mea-3x-bonus-points-offer.html),
read on 19 October, and its
[participating-hotel list](https://all.accor.com/a/content/dam/all/hubs/meapac/offers-campaigns/2026/2026-08/ME_3X_HotelList.pdf).

## The rules that matter

- **Register first, then book.** Registration runs to 11:59 p.m. Central
  European Time on **25 October 2026**. Only bookings made *after* you
  register count, so a stay you booked before registering does not
  qualify.
- **Book by 25 October, stay by 31 January.** Stays run from 1 September
  2026 to 31 January 2027.
- **Two nights minimum**, consecutive, at the same hotel. A booking for
  several rooms counts as one stay.
- **Book direct.** Accor's websites and app, the ALL call centre, the hotel
  itself, or a travel agent connected to Accor's booking system. Expedia,
  Booking.com and other third-party sites are excluded. Give your ALL number
  when you book.
- **Any status.** The offer is open to every ALL member. The terms say the
  bonus is worked out "based on Classic status".
- **No stacking.** It cannot be combined with other promotions and does not
  apply to groups. Where a stay qualifies for several offers, you get the
  most generous one only.

Points arrive in two parts: your normal points first, then the bonus.
Accor's worked example is a stay that earns 250 Reward points, which gets
**an additional 500**. The terms say Reward points are credited within 10
days of checkout.

## The draw

Every completed, paid stay of two nights or more is also one entry in a
prize draw, with no limit on entries. UAE residents are eligible. The draw
takes place after 31 January 2027.

- **One grand prize:** 100,000 ALL Reward points, Diamond status and
  partner airline miles. Accor's offer page names the miles as **200,000
  Avios with Qatar Airways Privilege Club** and values the 100,000 points at
  EUR 2,000. The conditions of sale say the airline and the number of miles
  are still "to be confirmed".
- **Nine runner-up prizes:** 100,000 Reward points and Platinum status each.

## Which UAE hotels

By our count, Accor's list carries **96 UAE hotels**: 73 in Dubai, 12 in
Abu Dhabi, 6 in Ras Al Khaimah, 3 in Sharjah and one each in Ajman and
Fujairah. It runs from ibis and Novotel to Fairmont The Palm, Raffles
Dubai, Sofitel Dubai The Obelisk and Rixos Premium Saadiyat Island. The
other markets are Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, Qatar, Egypt,
Lebanon, Jordan, Iraq, Pakistan and Türkiye.

Not every Accor hotel in the UAE is on the list, so search the PDF for the
exact hotel name before you book.

## Is it worth it?

Accor's own prize valuation, 100,000 points for EUR 2,000, works out at 2
euro cents a point. On that basis the bonus is worth twice your normal
earn on the stay. It is a reason to book a staycation you were already
planning directly with Accor rather than through a booking site, and to do
it this week. It is not a reason to book a stay you did not want.
