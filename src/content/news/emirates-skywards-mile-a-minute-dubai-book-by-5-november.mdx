**Emirates Skywards** has brought back its **Mile-a-Minute** offer. It pays
**one bonus Skywards Mile for every minute a visitor spends in Dubai**, up
to **7,200 miles**. Emirates says that equals five days in the city.

The first thing to know if you live here: **it is not for you.** The terms
exclude members whose Skywards profile shows UAE residence. They also
exclude tickets issued in the UAE and journeys that start here. The offer
is for people flying *in*: family, friends and colleagues visiting between
now and mid-December.

Everything below comes from Emirates' own
[offer terms](https://www.emirates.com/us/english/special-offers/earn-a-mile-a-minute-in-dubai/)
and its
[media-centre release](https://www.emirates.com/media-centre/emirates-skywards-brings-back-popular-mile-a-minute-offer-rewarding-visitors-for-every-minute-spent-in-dubai/),
read on 26 October. When we tried the UAE edition of the offer page that
day, it returned "page not found". That fits the residency rule.

## The rules that matter

- **Register, then book, by 5 November.** Registration and ticketing must
  both happen by **11.59 p.m. Dubai time on 5 November 2026**. The booking
  window opened on 21 October. You can register on emirates.com, in the
  Emirates app, on flydubai.com or through Emirates call centres. New
  members can join up to the same deadline.
- **Fly by 13 December.** The whole journey must start and finish between
  21 October and **13 December 2026**. One aggregator headline gives
  31 December. Emirates' terms say 13 December.
- **Return fares only.** One-way tickets do not count. On Emirates, Special,
  Saver, Flex and Flex Plus fares in every cabin qualify. On flydubai,
  Lite, Value and Flex in Economy and Business do.
- **EK or FZ flight numbers only.** Codeshares and interline flights are
  excluded.
- **Stay at least 24 hours and at most 30 days.** The minutes are counted
  from the scheduled arrival and departure times on the ticket, not from
  when you actually land.
- **Once per member.** Only the first qualifying stay earns the bonus. If
  another promotion also pays a bonus, you get whichever is higher. Miles
  post within 30 days of the end of the stay.

## What it is worth

The minimum 24-hour stay earns **1,440 miles**. The 7,200 cap arrives at
**120 hours**, five full days. At our
[2-fils Skywards baseline](/airlines/skywards/), the cap is worth about
**AED 144** and the minimum about **AED 29**. That comes on top of the miles
the ticket earns anyway.

It is a modest sweetener, not a reason to fly. Its real use for UAE readers
is as a nudge for visitors. If relatives are already planning a November or
early-December trip, have them register and book a return ticket before
5 November, and stay at least a day.

Emirates' page also reminds visitors to keep their boarding pass for
partner discounts around the city. See our
[My Emirates Pass story](/news/my-emirates-pass-winter-2026-boarding-pass-discounts-31-march/)
for how that works.
