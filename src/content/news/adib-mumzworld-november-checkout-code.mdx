If you already have a Mumzworld order planned, [the Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank (ADIB) offer for UAE shoppers](https://www.adib.ae/en/offers/mumzworld) runs through **30 November 2026**. Enter `ADIB` at checkout and pay with an eligible ADIB Visa Covered Card.

The [offer terms](https://www.adib.ae/-/media/Project/ADIB/ADIBSite/docs/offer/2026/Nov/Mumzworld-Offer-TCs.pdf) set a minimum order and cap the discount. The offer can apply to items already marked down, but it cannot be combined with another offer or promotion. School uniforms, infant formula and gift certificates are excluded.

Check whether the discount appears in your final total before paying. If your planned basket does not qualify, compare the cost as it is before adding items just to meet the minimum.
