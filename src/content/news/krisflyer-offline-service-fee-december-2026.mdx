If you’re a UAE-based KrisFlyer member booking or changing a Singapore Airlines award, try the website or app before calling. From **1 December 2026**, Singapore Airlines says its additional offline service fee for selected award and membership transactions rises from **USD 25 or 2,500 KrisFlyer miles** to **USD 50 or 5,000 miles**.

This is not an increase in the miles needed for an award seat. It is an extra charge for selected services handled by an airline office or reservations hotline, such as booking or changing a redemption, processing an upgrade or changing your nominee list. Separate ticket-related charges may still apply.

The airline’s [fee table](https://www.singaporeair.com/en_UK/sg/ppsclub-krisflyer/forms-and-fees/service-fees/) says the offline fee is waived when the transaction cannot be performed online, and for PPS Club members and PPS Club supplementary cardholders. If you cannot make the change online, ask whether the waiver applies to the specific service you need. Check the total, including any separate ticket charge, and confirm the currency if the airline quotes in AED.
