If you’re heading to Ain Al Thuraya in Al Ain, check which sights your pass actually covers. The [Abu Dhabi Media Office](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/arts-culture/ain-al-thuraya-festival-2026-brings-generations-together-to-celebrate-al-ain-regions-heritage-through-music-and-traditional-performances/) lists the crafts, design and performances festival across Al Ain Museum and Al Ain Oasis from **27 November to 6 December**, with festival hours of **4 pm to 11 pm daily**.

The [festival ticket listing](https://al-ain.platinumlist.net/event-tickets/ain-al-thuraya-festival) separates the exhibition-only day pass from the one that adds the museum and oasis. If you only want the exhibition, look for the first option. For all three on the same day, check that your pass names the museum and oasis as well.

The pass covering the full festival excludes the concert, according to the ticket listing. If you’re going for a particular performance, check its ticket separately. Confirm the entry conditions and availability for your date before travelling; the advertised festival hours don’t establish last admission or the opening hours for ordinary museum visits.
