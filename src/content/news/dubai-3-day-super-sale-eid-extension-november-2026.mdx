If you’re waiting until the Eid Al Etihad break to buy something in Dubai, check whether the offer you want lasts that long. [Visit Dubai’s current Arabic campaign page](https://www.visitdubai.com/ar/festivals-and-events/3-day-super-sale) puts the main 3 Day Super Sale on **27–29 November 2026**, with offers continuing through **3 December**. That doesn’t mean every weekend price will carry over to the holiday.

Dubai Festival City Mall is one example of why the dates matter. Visit Dubai lists the mall’s special sale programme for **27–29 November**, with hours of **10 am to 2 am** on those dates. Check your shop’s own hours if you’re going late.

The [English Visit Dubai listings](https://www.visitdubai.com/deals-and-offers/shopping-deals) show the longer shopping window. Before making the trip, check the exact item, branch and offer end date with the retailer. If you’ve found the same item online, compare what you’d actually pay, including delivery, and check whether the return terms differ.
