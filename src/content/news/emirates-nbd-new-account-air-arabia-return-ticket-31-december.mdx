**Emirates NBD** is offering new customers a **guaranteed Air Arabia return
ticket** for opening an account in its **ENBD X** app and funding it with
**AED 50,000 within seven days**. The promotion runs from **1 October to
31 December 2026**, per its terms and conditions.

A smaller commitment enters a **draw instead**. ENBD's offer page says
**90 tickets** are to be won.

## Two ways in

| Route | What you need to do | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Guaranteed** | Fund the new account with **AED 50,000** in total within **7 days** of opening it | An Air Arabia return ticket, per the offer page |
| **Draw** | Transfer a salary of at least **AED 5,000**, or keep an average balance of at least **AED 10,000**, within 30 days of opening | One raffle entry; the page says 90 tickets are to be won |

Air Arabia is named on ENBD's offer page, not in the terms. The terms speak
of a "guaranteed prize" and say raffle prizes "may include cash rewards,
gadgets, travel experiences, vouchers" or other rewards.

## Who qualifies

- **New customers only.** You must be new to ENBD, or new to "liabilities"
  with the bank. Existing products do not count.
- **The app only.** The current or savings account must be opened through the
  ENBD X app during the promotion period. No separate registration is needed.
- **Salaries count only by WPS or the UAE Fund Transfer System.** Cash,
  cheques and transfers from a personal account do not count. Pension credits
  do.
- **Loan money does not count** towards the balance.

## The catches

- **No route, class or value is published.** ENBD confirms the ticket's
  details when you redeem it, and your dates depend on availability and
  "the booking partner's terms and conditions". You cannot know in advance
  what the ticket is worth.
- **It can take up to six months.** ENBD says it will hand out prizes within
  180 days of the account being opened.
- **Six months or ENBD can take it back.** ENBD may reverse the reward,
  without notice and from any of your ENBD accounts or cards, if you do not
  receive six consecutive salary payments or keep the required average
  balance for six consecutive months.
- **You lose it if you leave early.** The reward can be forfeited if the
  account is closed, the salary stops or a condition is broken before the
  prize is handed out.
- **Does it combine with ENBD's salary offer? The terms point both ways.**
  One clause says the promotion is not valid with any other ENBD promotion.
  Another says payroll customers who join through it are enrolled in ENBD's
  salary-transfer offer. Ask ENBD in writing before you assume you can have
  both.

## Is it worth it?

For most people, the AED 50,000 route means parking a large sum in a current
or savings account for six months in exchange for a ticket of unknown value.
Price an Air Arabia return on the route you would actually fly, and set it
against what that money would earn elsewhere over the same six months.

If you are moving your salary anyway and it is AED 15,000 or more, compare
this with ENBD's separate salary-transfer cashback, which pays
[AED 1,500 or more until 24 December](/news/emirates-nbd-salary-transfer-cashback-up-to-aed-6000-24-december/).
That offer comes with its own card and spending conditions. Both are on our
[salary-transfer tracker](/salary-transfer/).
