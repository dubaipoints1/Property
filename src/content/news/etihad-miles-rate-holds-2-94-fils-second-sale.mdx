Etihad's UAE offers page opened a **"Last chance for summer"** sale with a
**15 August 2026** booking deadline. Six round-trip Economy routes out of
Abu Dhabi, each shown with a cash fare and an Etihad Guest Miles price
alongside it.

The interesting part is not the sale. It is what the two prices reveal when
you divide one by the other.

## Six routes, one number

| Route | Cash | Miles | Implied value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Krakow | AED 1,840 | 62,620 | 2.94 fils |
| Palma de Mallorca | AED 2,190 | 74,530 | 2.94 fils |
| Al Alamein | AED 2,050 | 69,765 | 2.94 fils |
| Nice | AED 2,290 | 77,935 | 2.94 fils |
| Mykonos | AED 2,300 | 78,270 | 2.94 fils |
| Santorini | AED 2,390 | 81,333 | 2.94 fils |

Six unrelated destinations across Europe and North Africa, spanning fares
from AED 1,840 to AED 2,390, and every one lands on the same figure to two
decimal places.

## And it is the same number as last month

We ran this arithmetic on Etihad's [previous sale](/news/etihad-fare-sale-book-by-31-july-2026/),
which closed on 31 July with an entirely different route list — Mumbai,
Cairo, Rome, Bangkok, New York, London. That sale also returned a flat
**2.94 fils** per Mile.

Two sales, twelve routes between them, no overlap, one rate. That is enough
to stop calling it a coincidence. Etihad is not pricing Miles per promotion;
it is applying a standing conversion at roughly 2.94 fils and letting the
cash fare drive the Miles number. These are fixed-rate "Miles as currency"
prices — the redemption equivalent of paying by card — not award-chart
seats.

## What it means for your Miles

A standing rate is easier to plan around than a moving one, and the plan is
the same both months: **do not spend Miles at 2.94 fils.**

Our published cost basis for Etihad Guest is 2 fils, so 2.94 looks generous
on its face. It is not, because it is the wrong comparison. The number that
matters is what else the same Mile could do. Saver Business redemptions on
this programme return roughly **15–23 fils per Mile** — five to eight times
what the fare page is offering. Every Mile spent on a AED 1,840 Krakow seat
is a Mile not spent on a business cabin worth several times more.

The move is unchanged: if these fares suit you, **book them in cash**, earn
Miles on the spend, and keep the balance for a premium-cabin redemption.
Our [Etihad Guest programme guide](/airlines/etihad-guest/) lists the
sweet spots, and the [valuations methodology](/valuations/) explains where
the cost-basis figures come from.

## Before you book

These are ex-Abu Dhabi departures — Dubai-based readers should price the
transfer into any comparison with an equivalent DXB flight. Etihad states
that the schedule is subject to change and to local operating conditions,
and sale fares carry their own change and cancellation rules. Check the
fare conditions on the booking page before paying.
