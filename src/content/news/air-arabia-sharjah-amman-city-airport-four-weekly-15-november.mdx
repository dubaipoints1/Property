**Air Arabia** will fly **Sharjah to Amman City Airport (ADJ)** from
**15 November 2026**, four times a week. The airline announced the route in a
[press release on 8 October](https://press.airarabia.com/air-arabia-expands-jordan-network-with-new-service-to-amman-city-airport/).

Air Arabia already flies from Sharjah to Amman's main airport, **Queen Alia
International**. Amman City Airport is the capital's second airport, so this
gives Sharjah passengers a choice of two airports in Amman.

## The schedule

All times are local, as published by Air Arabia:

| Flight | Route | Departs | Arrives | Days |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G9 169 | Sharjah → Amman City | 08:00 | 10:30 | Mon, Wed, Fri, Sun |
| G9 170 | Amman City → Sharjah | 11:20 | 15:15 | Mon, Wed, Fri, Sun |

The airline says it flies Airbus A320 and A321 aircraft.
[AeroRoutes lists](https://www.aeroroutes.com/eng/261012-g9nov26adj) a mix of
A320, A320neo and A321neo on the route.

## Why it matters if you live in the UAE

The Friday and Sunday flights are the useful pair. You can leave Sharjah at
08:00 on a Friday and be back by mid-afternoon on Sunday, which makes a
weekend in Amman possible without taking leave. Both legs run on the same
four days.

Air Arabia's release says the Group also flies to both Amman airports from
**Zayed International** in Abu Dhabi. Abu Dhabi residents already have that
option, so the new route is mainly for Sharjah and the northern emirates.

The release gives no fares, so check the price for your dates on Air
Arabia's website before you compare it with a Queen Alia flight. Tickets are
on sale now through the website, the call centre or travel agents.

## Loyalty

Air Arabia's own programme is **Air Rewards**. The release does not mention
a launch offer or bonus points on the route.
