Hilton now lists [The Mangroves Abu Dhabi](https://www.hilton.com/en/locations/uae/abu-dhabi/lxr-hotels/) in its Abu Dhabi directory under LXR Hotels & Resorts. The [hotel’s own listing](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/auhptol-the-mangroves-abu-dhabi/) puts it in Eastern Mangroves, so make sure you’ve selected the right property before comparing rooms.

If you have Hilton Honors points, search your dates on Hilton and note the total cash price for the room you want. Then use the [Use Points & Money option](https://www.hilton.com/en-gb/help-center/reservations/booking-a-reservation-using-points/) under Special rates and look for that same room with the same guest count. Hilton says [the points price can vary by hotel and date](https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors/support-faq/). Check the cancellation rules on both quotes; the cheaper option is less useful if you can’t cancel it when you need to.

We checked the hotel listing and booking instructions, not reward availability. If no points option appears for the room you want, don’t buy points on the assumption that it will open up.
