Dubai Islamic Bank (DIB) has agreed to buy Emirates Islamic from Emirates NBD. In their [joint announcement](https://dib.ae/about-us/news/2026/11/25/dubai-islamic-bank-pjsc-and-emirates-nbd-bank-pjsc-sign-agreement-for-dib-to-acquire-emirates-islamic-bank-pjsc-for-new-shares-in-dib?type=news) on 25 November, the banks said DIB would issue new shares to Emirates NBD in exchange. That is an agreement, not a completed takeover. The [Dubai Financial Market filing](https://feeds.dfm.ae/documents/2026/Nov/25/e8361c1e-e64a-41ba-8840-46160a35d93f/DIB%20BODRES%2025%2011%202026.Pdf.pdf) says the proposed acquisition still needs shareholder and regulatory approvals. The joint announcement names the Central Bank of the UAE and the Capital Markets Authority among the authorities whose approvals are required. It does not give a completion date.

## What to do about your card

The announcement does not say that a card will be replaced, that a rewards balance will move or that you should change where you send a payment. Keep using the payment instructions from your issuer unless it tells you otherwise. If you are comparing a card from either bank, use the terms published for that card today. The agreement is not a new fee schedule or a promise that either bank’s rewards will carry over.
