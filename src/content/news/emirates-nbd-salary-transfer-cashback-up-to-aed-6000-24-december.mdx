**Emirates NBD** has a new salary-transfer offer, running from
**25 September to 24 December 2026**. It pays **cashback**. The last ENBD
cycle we covered, 1 February to 30 June 2026, paid
[bonus interest on the balance](/news/enbd-bonus-interest-salary-transfer-explained/)
instead. The headline is "up to AED 6,000", but that figure needs a
salary of AED 50,000 or more *and* a loan or wealth product.

## What it pays

Per ENBD's terms and conditions:

| Monthly salary | Salary transfer + credit card | …plus a loan or wealth product |
|---|---|---|
| AED 15,000 – 49,999 | **AED 1,500** | **AED 2,000** in total |
| AED 50,000 or more | **AED 2,000** | **AED 6,000** in total |

The right-hand column is the total, not an extra amount: ENBD's table adds
AED 500 or AED 4,000 to the base figure.

## What you have to do

1. **Open your first ENBD account.** The offer is for new-to-bank
   customers, or customers new to "liabilities" with the bank. The account must be your
   **first-ever** current or savings account with ENBD, opened during the
   offer period. Existing accounts and products do not count.
2. **Transfer a salary of at least AED 15,000** (fixed monthly allowances
   count) within **90 days** of opening the account. It must arrive
   through the UAE's WPS or Fund Transfer System. Cash, cheques and
   transfers from a personal account do not count. Pension credits do
   count. ENBD's offer page adds that only salaries from its **approved
   list of employers** qualify.
3. **Get a new ENBD credit card** within 90 days of opening the account,
   and **spend AED 5,000** on it within **60 days** of the card being
   issued. Cash withdrawals, cash advances, fees and refunds do not count
   towards the spend.
4. For the higher figure, **take a personal, auto or home loan, or any
   wealth product**. The offer page says this must be within 90 days of
   opening the account.

## The catches

- **You wait about five months.** ENBD pays the cashback after **150 calendar
  days**, counted from your first salary credit.
- **Twelve salaries, or ENBD can take it back.** ENBD may reverse the
  cashback if you do not receive **12 consecutive salary payments**. It can
  take the money from any of your ENBD accounts or cards.
- **Keep the card and any loan open until 180 days after account
  opening.** If you close the credit card or loan within **180 days** of
  opening the account, you lose eligibility and ENBD can reverse the
  cashback without notice.
- **One bonus, and no combining.** You can earn only one salary-transfer
  welcome bonus. Once it is paid, you are not eligible for other offers
  under this promotion or for ENBD promotions running at the same time.
  ENBD's terms also say the promotion is not valid with any other ENBD
  promotion, campaign or offer.
- **A loan bought for the bonus is the expensive part.** At the top tier,
  AED 4,000 of the AED 6,000 depends on a loan or wealth product. Price
  that product on its own terms before counting the cashback.
