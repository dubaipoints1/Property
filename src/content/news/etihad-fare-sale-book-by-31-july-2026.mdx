Etihad's UAE offers page ran a fare sale with a **book-by 31 July
2026** deadline for travel until **November 2026**. The booking window
has since closed; the analysis below stands as filed. The published
sample fares were round-trip Economy
**ex-Abu Dhabi**, inclusive of taxes and surcharges, and each one
carried an Etihad Guest Miles price alongside the cash price.

One caution before the numbers: earlier coverage this month described
a wider Etihad sale on a different booking window. That window has
lapsed and the AE offers page does not carry it today. What is
verifiable on 27 July is the book-by-31-July sale below.

| Route (round trip, ex-AUH) | Cash | Etihad Guest Miles |
|---|---|---|
| Mumbai | AED 1,050 | 35,735 |
| Cairo | AED 1,060 | 36,082 |
| Rome | AED 2,290 | 77,942 |
| Bangkok | AED 2,650 | 90,192 |
| New York (JFK) | AED 3,480 | 118,243 |
| London (LHR) | AED 3,520 | 119,806 |

## These are fixed-rate Miles prices, not award-chart redemptions

Divide the cash price by the Miles price and the same figure comes
back every time. London: **AED 3,520 ÷ 119,806 Miles = AED 0.0294**,
or **2.94 fils per Etihad Guest Mile**. Mumbai: AED 1,050 ÷ 35,735 =
2.94 fils. Cairo, Rome, Bangkok and New York all land on 2.94 too.

A flat rate holding across six routes and a threefold fare spread is
the signature of a fixed conversion. Etihad is selling these seats at
a set fils-per-Mile price rather than pricing an award seat off a
chart — so none of these Miles prices reflects award availability,
and none of them is a saver award.

That is the whole story for a Miles holder, because 2.94 fils is a
ceiling. It clears the 2-fils cost basis we publish for an Etihad
Guest Mile on [our valuations page](/valuations/) and the
[Etihad Guest hub](/airlines/etihad-guest/), so it is not a poor rate
against what a Mile costs to earn. But the same hub records saver
Business redemptions returning roughly 15–23 fils per Mile — five to
eight times more from the identical balance. Paying the cash fare on
an Etihad Guest co-brand card also earns Miles and Tier Miles; paying
with Miles earns neither and empties the balance at the programme's
weakest rate.

## What it means for UAE members

These are Abu Dhabi departures. Dubai readers should price the drive
or transfer to AUH into the comparison before treating AED 1,050 to
Mumbai as a Dubai fare — on the cheaper routes that transfer is a
material share of the ticket, and a DXB departure at AED 200 more may
be the better buy.

If one of the six routes matches a trip you were already taking, book
the **cash** fare before 31 July. If you were weighing the Miles
price, don't: hold the balance for saver premium-cabin space, where
Etihad Guest arithmetic actually works. And if none of the six is on
your list, there is nothing to do here — this is a fare sale, not a
programme change, and no earn rate, expiry rule or award chart has
moved.
