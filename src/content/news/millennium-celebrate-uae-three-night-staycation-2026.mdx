[Millennium’s Celebrate UAE offer](https://www.millenniumhotels.com/en/offers/middle-east/celebrate-uae-the-art-of-being-together/) gives UAE residents the last night of an eligible stay at participating hotels when they book at least three nights. The last-night benefit applies once per stay, and Millennium averages the confirmed rate across the booking. Compare the final total with the normal rate for the dates you actually want. A longer break is not a saving if it costs more than the shorter stay you planned.

## Use the resident code, not the Black Friday code

Millennium lists stays from **28 November to 6 December 2026**. Use `CELEBRATE UAE` when booking online and bring a valid Emirates ID to check-in. [Millennium Airport Hotel Dubai](https://www.millenniumhotels.com/en/dubai/millennium-airport-hotel-dubai/offers/) lists the resident offer; check whether it appears for your chosen dates. Millennium’s [separate Black Friday sale](https://www.millenniumhotels.com/en/offers/middle-east/black-friday-2026/) excludes UAE stays on **2–5 December**. Don’t assume the Black Friday rate applies on those nights.

Before paying, compare the checkout total, cancellation terms and meal plan. We checked the published terms on 22 November, not live rates or availability. The resident offer does not apply at every hotel.
