**No text from Standard Chartered? Do not budget for this cashback.** The bank's [UAE campaign terms](https://www.sc.com/ae/cashback-tncs/) restrict the offer to customers it contacted directly by SMS. The qualifying products listed are Journey (formerly Visa Infinite), Smart Saadiq, SimplyCash and Manhattan. Holding one of those cards is not enough on its own.

## What to check if you were invited

The offer runs from 13 November to 31 December 2026, inclusive. The bank says qualifying spending of AED 1,000 by 31 December can earn AED 100, credited to the primary card by 28 February 2027 if the conditions are met. The public terms we read do not list a separate registration step; check the terms and your invitation before moving any spending.

Domestic and overseas retail purchases in stores or online can count. The terms combine eligible primary and supplementary spending under the same card relationship. Cash withdrawals, exchange-house transactions, balance transfers, Fixed Payment Plans, Smart Payment Plans and school-fee cashier orders do not count. The terms also say cardholders eligible for this offer cannot qualify for another Standard Chartered UAE credit-card campaign during the same period.

A card that is cancelled or terminated when the bank calculates eligibility does not qualify. If the card is overdue, blocked or suspended, the offer is forfeited unless the bank reinstates it at its sole discretion.

The useful test is whether spending you already planned will qualify before the deadline. Do not apply for a card or move new spending just because the public terms are online.

_Source checked: Standard Chartered UAE campaign terms, 15 November 2026. We did not verify an individual invitation, transaction or payout._
