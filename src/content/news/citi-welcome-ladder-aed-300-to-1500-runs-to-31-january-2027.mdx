Citibank UAE has a joining-bonus ladder across its card range, and for
once the bank has put the whole thing in one table. The "Key Conditions"
block at the foot of its
[credit-cards page](https://www.citibank.ae/credit-cards), read on
15 September 2026, sets out a statement credit per card against a spend
target to be met within 60 days. The
[welcome-offer terms](https://www.citibank.ae/content/dam/cgcpc/ae/prelogin/www-citibank-ae/doc/en/pdf/credit-cards/credit-card-offers-terms-and-conditions.pdf),
dated July 2026, give it an end date: "available until January 31, 2027,
or discontinuation of the offer, whichever comes first."

## The ladder, as published

| Card | Joining bonus | Spend within 60 days | Annual fee | Minimum salary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Citi Ultima | AED 1,500 | AED 25,000 | AED 3,000 | AED 36,750 |
| Citi Prestige | AED 1,000 | AED 15,000 | AED 1,500 | AED 30,000 |
| Citi Premier | AED 750 | AED 10,000 | AED 750 | AED 15,000 |
| Citi Rewards | AED 300 | AED 6,000 | AED 0 in year one | AED 8,000 |
| Citi Cash Back | AED 300 | AED 6,000 | AED 0 in year one | AED 8,000 |

Fees and salary floors are from the same page's card tiles. Five per
cent VAT applies on top of every fee. The Rewards and Cash Back cards
are free in year one and free from year two on AED 9,000 of annual
spend; otherwise AED 300 applies.

One gap in the bank's own table: the Citi Rewards row is printed with
no amount. The card's
[product page](https://www.citibank.ae/credit-cards/rewards/citi-rewards-credit-card)
states AED 300 on AED 6,000, the same as the Cash Back card, and that is
the figure above.

## What the bonus is worth once the fee is paid

The bonus is a statement credit. The fee is a charge. Put them on the
same line for the first year:

- **Ultima:** AED 1,500 credit against an AED 3,000 fee. You are
  AED 1,500 down before VAT, having spent AED 25,000 to get there.
- **Prestige:** AED 1,000 against AED 1,500. AED 500 down.
- **Premier:** AED 750 against AED 750. Level, before VAT.
- **Rewards and Cash Back:** AED 300 against nothing. AED 300 up.

On the premium cards the joining bonus is a partial fee rebate, not a
gain, and the case for those cards rests on what you would use the
lounge, hotel-night and Miles benefits for anyway. On the two entry
cards the AED 300 is real money for AED 6,000 of spend you would have
made regardless, which is a 5 per cent return on that spend, once.

## The conditions that disqualify people

The same three restrictions as the
[Cash Back bonus we covered in August](/news/citi-cashback-joining-bonus-doubles-aed-300/),
plus one from the terms document:

- **Direct applications only.** The page states it on every card:
  "Applicants reaching Citi website through a web search advert or any
  third party link do not qualify." Type the address in yourself.
- **New to Citi cards only.** Existing cardholders and staff are out.
- **No Citi card closed in the last 24 months** (terms, clause 5).
- **One sign-up offer per person.** The ladder does not stack with any
  other promotion, and the Simplicity card is excluded.

Payout timing is stated two ways. The page says the credit posts
"within 45 days of meeting the minimum spend"; the terms say "within 30
working days". Those are close to the same thing in practice. Treat
45 calendar days as the outside case and query it after that.

## The page furniture has not caught up

The offer copy on each card is consistent. The metadata around it is
not. The page title for the Rewards card still says "AED 150 Joining
Bonus"; the Premier's description says "up to AED 350"; the Prestige's
says "AED 600"; the credit-cards page describes itself as "up to
AED 1,250" while its own hero says AED 1,500. Those are search-result
strings from earlier cycles. Go by the Key Conditions table and the
bullet on the product page, which agree with each other and with the
terms.

## The practical read

If you earn AED 8,000 or more and do not hold a Citi card, the Cash Back
or Rewards card is a clean AED 300 for spend you were going to put on a
card anyway, with no fee to claw it back. Our
[Cash Back review](/cards/citi-cashback/) covers the earn rates. The
premium tiers are a different decision: pay the fee for the benefits,
and treat the bonus as a discount on it. Nothing here needs doing this
week; the window runs to the end of January.
