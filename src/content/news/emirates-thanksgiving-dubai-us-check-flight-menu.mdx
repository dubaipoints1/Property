If you’re already flying between Dubai and the US with Emirates, look up your onboard menu before you travel. [Emirates announced on 19 November](https://www.emirates.com/media-centre/emirates-celebrates-thanksgiving-with-a-taste-of-gratitude/) that it plans to serve a Thanksgiving menu on selected flights from **23 to 30 November 2026**. The announcement covers all cabin classes on the selected services, but it doesn’t list individual flights. The menu varies by direction and cabin.

## Check the flight, then the lounge

Use [Emirates’ dining page](https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/experience/dining/economy-class/) to look up your route or flight number and check each leg separately. Emirates also announced seasonal dishes in its Dubai lounges during the promotion. The menu does not change who can get in. Check the lounge for your departure and confirm whether your [fare or Skywards status includes access](https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/experience/our-lounges/) before planning to eat there.
