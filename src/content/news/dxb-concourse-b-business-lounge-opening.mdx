[Emirates 24/7](https://www.emirates247.com/uae/new-business-class-lounge-opens-in-concourse-b-at-dubai-international-airport/7936?ot=ot.AMPPageLayout) reported on 18 November that a new Business Class lounge in Concourse B at Dubai International (DXB) had begun receiving passengers. The outlet cited Dubai Aviation Engineering Projects, which worked with Emirates on the Terminal 3 lounge. That is a report of an opening, not a new entitlement for everyone booked in Business Class.

We did not find separate confirmation from Emirates of the opening or this lounge’s access rules; its published guidance below applies to Dubai lounges generally.

## Check the fare name, not just the cabin

[Emirates’ Dubai lounge table](https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/experience/our-lounges/paid-lounge-access/) lists Business Class Saver, Flex and Flex Plus for Business Class lounge access. Business Class Special is in the paid-access row by fare alone. Skywards status is a separate route. [The airline’s tier terms](https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/experience/our-lounges/partners/terms-and-conditions/) list Business Class lounge access in Dubai for eligible Silver, Gold and Platinum members travelling onwards on Emirates. Silver covers the member only; check the tier and same-flight rules before counting on bringing a guest.

If you are using a bank card’s lounge benefit, check the exact lounge in your card programme. A newly reported airline lounge isn’t evidence that your pass has added it.

## Start with the lounge on your boarding pass

[Emirates’ Business Class lounge page](https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/experience/our-lounges/business-class/) tells passengers to check the lounge shown on the boarding pass if the one nearest their gate is unavailable. The airline says its [Wayfinder tool](https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/help/faq-topics/at-the-airport/) can show directions to gates and lounges in Terminal 3. Check your gate first. If your pass points you to another lounge, don’t plan a detour to Concourse B on the strength of the opening report alone.
