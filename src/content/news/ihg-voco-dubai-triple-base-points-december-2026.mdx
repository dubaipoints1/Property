IHG’s [voco promotion](https://www.ihg.com/content/us/en/deals/member-offers/voco3x26) lists **31 December 2026** as the last day to register and earn the bonus on qualifying nights. IHG’s [UAE voco directory](https://www.ihg.com/voco/content/gb/en/support/hotels) includes voco Dubai, voco Bonnington Dubai and voco Dubai The Palm. If you have a December booking at one of them, check whether your own IHG One Rewards account can register for the offer before you stay.

## Check the rate and the bonus separately

The voco offer brings the total to three times the base points you’d normally earn, before any separate elite or promotional bonuses. IHG excludes Reward Nights, Points & Cash and online travel agency bookings. The cash rate also needs to meet IHG’s published minimum spend. Choose a qualifying direct rate and check the terms for the nights you intend to stay.

This isn’t the [broader IHG app promotion](/news/ihg-one-rewards-triple-points-app-second-stay-31-december/). The voco terms describe qualifying nights after registration rather than requiring a first stay to unlock the bonus. We did not verify whether one booking could earn both bonuses. Compare the cash price and cancellation terms first; don’t treat unconfirmed points as a discount.
