The DubaiPoints news desk files its first post today.

Coverage focuses on the four things the publication exists to track:

- **Salary-transfer offer movements.** Tier additions, bonus changes, and time-windowed promotions at the UAE banks currently covered. The live [salary-transfer tracker](/salary-transfer/) is updated when a verified material change is incorporated; it does not represent complete market coverage.
- **Card launches and benefit changes.** New products, refreshed welcome bonuses, fee changes, perk additions or removals. Reviewed against applicable issuer product pages, Key Facts Statements, terms or fee schedules before publication.
- **Programme changes.** Award-chart movements, transfer-partner additions or pauses, expiry-rule revisions on Skywards, Etihad Guest, Qatar Privilege Club, Marriott Bonvoy and the regional hotel currencies.
- **Regulatory updates.** AECB, Central Bank of the UAE, DLD and DET decisions that affect points-and-miles behaviour for residents — corporate-tax interactions, FX-fee rule changes, Wages Protection System guidance.

The editorial standard is the same as the rest of the site: **AED-first** for monetary comparisons, with source denominations retained where conversion is unsupported. Material claims link to dated sources where available. Expired offers are removed from `/deals/` when their stated end date passes — they do not remain under "still available" copy. The same applies here: a piece that turns out to be wrong gets a [correction notice](/corrections/) on the page, not a silent edit.

If you spot a UAE points-and-miles story we have not filed, the [tip line](/tip/) is open. We do not pay for tips and we do not run press-release rewrites.

New posts publish only when the source basis and effective date are clear.
