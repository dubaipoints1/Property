**Air Arabia** is bringing back its **daily nonstop flight between Ras Al
Khaimah and Kozhikode** (Calicut), on the Kerala coast. Flights restart on
**16 October 2026**, the airline said in a press release on 6 October.

## What changes

- **Route:** Ras Al Khaimah (RKT) to Kozhikode (CCJ), nonstop.
- **Frequency:** daily.
- **First flight:** 16 October 2026.
- **Booking:** on Air Arabia's website, through its call centre or a travel
  agent. Tickets are on sale now.

Air Arabia's release names **Cairo, Islamabad, Lahore and Peshawar** among
the airline's other nonstop routes from Ras Al Khaimah.

## Why it matters if you live in the UAE

For residents of Ras Al Khaimah and the northern emirates, this is a direct
flight to Kerala without the drive to Sharjah or Dubai first. The release
gives no fares and no schedule times, so check the booking page before you
plan around it.

Flights booked direct with Air Arabia earn points in its **Air Rewards**
programme. The release does not mention any launch offer or bonus points for
the route, and we have not seen one.
