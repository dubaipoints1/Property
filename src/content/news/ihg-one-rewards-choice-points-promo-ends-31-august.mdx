IHG One Rewards is in the final nine days of its **Choice Promotion**:
members register for **one of two offers** — "Earn 2,000 points every
2 nights" or "Earn 8,000 points every 4 nights" — and both carry
"unlimited earning ability", so there is no cap to allocate stays
against. The Promotion Period covers stays "beginning May 20, 2026 and
ending on August 31, 2026", which is now a countdown.

The ordering rule comes first, as it always does: members "must first
register their member number for one of two available offers".
Register first, stay second — a night completed before you register
earns nothing. Residents of "a country in the Middle East" are on the
eligible list (China, Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan are not), so a
UAE-based member registering from here is fine. What counts as a
Qualifying Room Night is defined by the offer page's terms — read them
before assuming a rate qualifies.

## The pick is arithmetic, not preference

You register for one option, so choose against the IHG nights you
actually have left before month-end:

- **4 or more nights by 31 August** — take the 8,000-per-4-nights
  option. That is 2,000 points per night effective, double the other
  option's rate, and it repeats without a cap: 8 nights is 16,000
  points.
- **Two or three nights** — take 2,000-per-2-nights. Three nights
  under the 4-night option pays nothing; under the 2-night option the
  first two nights pay 2,000.
- **One night** — neither option triggers. Register anyway if a
  second night might materialise; registering costs nothing.

The trap is picking the bigger headline number with a two-night
staycation booked: 8,000 points you will never trigger is worth less
than 2,000 you will.

## What it means for UAE members

We are not putting a dirham figure on this one: IHG One Rewards is
not yet on our valuations table, and we do not guess. The honest
framing is unchanged regardless — this pays on IHG nights you were
staying anyway, at a Holiday Inn, Crowne Plaza, InterContinental or
voco already in your calendar before 31 August. Manufacturing a stay
to chase an unvalued currency is the wrong side of the arithmetic.

If a qualifying stay is booked, the whole task is a two-minute
registration done before check-in, with the option matched to your
remaining night count. More hotel-desk coverage is on the
[hotel news index](/news/hotels/); live offers we track sit on the
[deals page](/deals/).
