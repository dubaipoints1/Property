**Hilton Honors** is paying bonus points on every stay completed between
**15 October and 31 December 2026**, and UAE hotels are included. Every
Hilton hotel earns **1,000 bonus points a stay**. **Sixteen UAE hotels**
on Hilton's luxury, lifestyle and resort list earn **3,000**, among them
both Waldorf Astorias in Dubai, Conrad Dubai and Conrad Abu Dhabi Etihad
Towers. You have to register first.

The rules below come from Hilton's own
[promotion terms](https://www.hilton.com/en/offers/promotions/more-points-to-play/)
and its [list of 3,000-point hotels](https://www.hilton.com/en/p/honors-promotions/more-points-play/eligible-properties/),
both read on 19 October.

## The rules that matter

- **Register before you check out.** Registration is on the promotion
  page. Hilton's terms ask you to register "prior to completing a stay",
  so a stay you already booked counts as long as you register before it
  ends. Stays you finished before registering do not count.
- **The bonus is per stay, not per night.** A one-night stay and a
  week-long stay at the same hotel earn the same bonus. Checking out and
  back in again does not help: Hilton counts consecutive nights at one
  hotel as one stay.
- **The stay must finish by 31 December.** A stay that started before 15
  October counts if it ends on or after that date. A stay that runs past
  31 December does not count.
- **No cap.** Hilton sets no limit on the number of bonus points you can
  earn in the promotion. Separate limits on base points at some brands
  still apply.
- **Exclusions.** Group bookings do not qualify. The offer cannot be
  combined with "other select offers", which Hilton does not name.
- **Bonus points do not count towards elite status.** They can take six
  to eight weeks after checkout to post.

The terms say nothing about where you book or how many nights you stay.
Hilton's FAQ asks "Are all rates included in this promotion?", but its
answer did not load when we read the page.

## The 16 UAE hotels on the 3,000-point list

Hilton's list gives hotel names but not countries. We searched the full
list for UAE locations and found 16. LoyaltyLobby's country-sorted copy of
the list has the same 16 under the UAE.

- **Dubai (9):** Al Seef Heritage Hotel Dubai, Curio Collection; Canopy by
  Hilton Dubai Al Seef; Conrad Dubai; DoubleTree by Hilton Dubai – Jumeirah
  Beach; Hilton Dubai Jumeirah; Hilton Dubai Palm Jumeirah; Hilton Dubai
  The Walk; Waldorf Astoria Dubai International Financial Centre; Waldorf
  Astoria Dubai Palm Jumeirah.
- **Abu Dhabi (4):** Conrad Abu Dhabi Etihad Towers; Royal Rose Hotel Abu
  Dhabi, Curio Collection; The Mangroves Abu Dhabi, LXR Hotels & Resorts;
  The WB Abu Dhabi, Curio Collection.
- **Ras Al Khaimah (3):** DoubleTree by Hilton Resort & Spa Marjan Island;
  Hampton by Hilton Marjan Island; Waldorf Astoria Ras Al Khaimah.

Any other Hilton-brand hotel in the UAE earns the 1,000-point bonus.

## Is it worth it?

We value a Hilton Honors point at
[1.5 fils](/hotels/hilton-honors/), so 3,000 points are worth about
**AED 45** and 1,000 points about **AED 15**. The bonus is the same
however long you stay, so it adds the most to a short stay. A one-night
staycation at one of the 16 hotels, or a weekend on Marjan Island, gains
proportionally more than a week-long stay.

It is not a reason to book a stay you did not want. But if a Hilton stay
is already planned before 31 December, including over National Day, it
takes two minutes to register. Our
[Hilton Honors guide](/hotels/hilton-honors/) explains how the programme
works. Accor is running a separate offer on UAE hotels, with a
[triple-points promotion](/news/all-accor-triple-points-uae-hotels-book-by-25-october/)
that must be booked by 25 October.
