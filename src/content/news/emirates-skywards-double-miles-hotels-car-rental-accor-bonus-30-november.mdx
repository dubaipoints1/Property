**Emirates Skywards** is paying bonus Miles on almost everything around a
trip except the flight: hotels, holiday packages and car hire. Most
partners pay **double Miles**. Emirates' own hotel and holiday products pay
**50% extra**. And **ALL Accor** points converted into Skywards Miles earn a
**25% bonus**.

The window is short. Hotel and car bookings must be made between **29
October and 30 November 2026**. Emirates Holidays packages must be booked by
**26 November**. Stays, rentals and holidays must all be completed by **31
January 2027**, which covers National Day and the Dubai Shopping Festival
season.

Everything below comes from the Emirates Group's own release of 29 October
2026, distributed through APO Group. Emirates' media-centre site would not
load for our reader, so we read the release on APO's
[Emirates newsroom](https://emirates.africa-newsroom.com/press/emirates-skywards-rewards-members-with-bonus-miles-on-hotels-holiday-packages-and-car-rentals-this-winter?lang=en).

## What earns what

Per Emirates:

| Partner | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Emirates Skywards Hotels | **50% bonus Miles** on hotel stays |
| Emirates Holidays | **50% bonus Miles** on holiday packages |
| Booking.com | **Double Miles** on hotel stays |
| IHG Hotels & Resorts | **Double Miles** on eligible hotel stays |
| SIXT, Europcar, Avis, Budget, Hertz | **Double Miles** on car rentals |
| ALL Accor | **25% bonus Miles** on Reward points converted to Skywards |

The release does not give base earn rates for any partner, or say whether
members must register first. Emirates links to an offers page for the
details; check it, and each partner's own terms, before you book. We have
not read those terms.

## Who it suits

**Anyone already booking a winter trip.** If you were going to rent a car
abroad or book an IHG stay for December anyway, doubling the Miles costs
nothing extra. Book through the route that earns Skywards, and give your
Skywards number.

**IHG guests who earn Skywards Miles on their stays.** Emirates lists IHG
as a double-Miles partner for this window. Whether Miles beat IHG's own
points on a given stay is a separate sum; our
[IHG One Rewards hub](/hotels/ihg-one-rewards/) explains the programme.

## The Accor transfer: usually not worth it

Our [ALL Accor hub](/hotels/accor-all/) records the transfer to Skywards at
**2 ALL points to 1 Mile** (4,000 points to 2,000 Miles). With the 25%
bonus, 4,000 points would land 2,500 Miles. That ratio is ours, not the
release's.

At our [2-fils Skywards baseline](/airlines/skywards/), 2,500 Miles is
worth about **AED 50**. The same 4,000 points spent as Accor's fixed hotel
discount are worth about **AED 320** at our 8-fils working (two blocks of
EUR 40 off a stay). The bonus narrows the gap; it does not close it. It is
for a balance that would otherwise expire, or for topping up a Skywards
redemption that is a few thousand Miles short.

_Verified on 1 November 2026 against the Emirates Group's release of 29
October. AED figures are ours, at our published baselines._
