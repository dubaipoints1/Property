If you collect **Avios with Qatar Airways Privilege Club**, including
through UAE bank point transfers, there is a lesser-known way to spend
them: convert them into **ALL Accor** hotel points. Head for Points set out
the terms on 10 November 2026, and they are worth knowing mainly so you can
avoid the swap in most cases.

## The terms

Per [Head for Points](https://www.headforpoints.com/2026/11/10/convert-avios-into-all-accor-hotel-points/):

| | |
|---|---|
| Rate | **3.5 Avios to 1 ALL Accor point** |
| Minimum | 3,500 Avios, which gives 1,000 ALL Accor points |
| Annual cap | 100,000 Avios |
| Where | Through the Qatar Airways website |

Head for Points adds that the rate improved from 4.5 to 1 in November 2025.
We have not confirmed these terms on Qatar's own site, which blocks our
reader, so check the figures on the transfer page before you move anything.

## The arithmetic

ALL Accor has no award chart. As Head for Points explains, a point is worth a
fixed **2 euro cents** against a hotel bill, so:

- 1,000 ALL Accor points = **EUR 20** off a stay
- that costs you **3,500 Avios**
- so each Avios buys about **0.57 euro cents** of hotel

That last figure is our own division of EUR 20 by 3,500. For comparison, the
3,500 Avios you give up are two thirds of a 5,250-Avios one-way Economy award
of the kind Qatar listed from Doha to Sharjah in
[October's Privilege Deals](/news/qatar-privilege-deals-october-sharjah-book-by-14-october/).
Flights are where Avios hold their value.

## When it can still make sense

Head for Points gives two cases, and both apply to UAE members:

- **Keeping Accor points alive.** Per Head for Points, ALL Accor points
  expire after 12 months with no account activity. A minimum transfer of
  3,500 Avios counts as activity and resets the clock, which is cheaper than
  losing a large balance.
- **Topping up for something specific.** If you are a few thousand points
  short of an Accor redemption you have already chosen, a small transfer can
  close the gap.

## What it means for UAE members

- **Do not treat this as a general use for Avios.** Price a Qatar award
  first; the [Qatar Privilege Club hub](/airlines/qatar-privilege-club/)
  covers how.
- **If you hold ALL Accor points and have no stay planned,** note your last
  activity date. A stay, or a minimum transfer like this one, keeps the
  balance from expiring.
- **The cap is per year.** 100,000 Avios would give about 28,571 ALL Accor
  points, or roughly EUR 571 of hotel stay, by the same arithmetic.

_Published 10 November 2026. Rate, minimum, cap, the 2-euro-cent point value
and the 12-month expiry rule are per Head for Points, as marked, and are not

annual-cap figures are our arithmetic from those numbers._
