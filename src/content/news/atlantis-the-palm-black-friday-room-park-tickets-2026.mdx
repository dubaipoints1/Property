[Atlantis, The Palm’s Black Friday page](https://www.atlantis.com/dubai/atlantis-the-palm/offers/black-friday) advertises **35% off** rooms and suites for new reservations made by **29 November 2026**. The resort lists stays through **18 December 2027**, but blackout dates may apply and availability is not guaranteed. This is the offer for Atlantis, The Palm, not Atlantis The Royal.

## Price the trip, not just the room

The listed stay includes daily access to Aquaventure Waterpark and access to the Lost World Aquarium. If you would otherwise buy both, compare the total with a room elsewhere plus the tickets you need. Check which guests and days are included for your booking before buying anything separately.

The advertised room rate is subject to taxes and charges. Atlantis also lists an **AED 500-per-night** card hold or cash deposit at check-in for incidentals, separate from the room price. Check the exact cancellation deadline before replacing an existing reservation. The offer page mentions free cancellation, but its terms say refundable rates follow their usual policy.
