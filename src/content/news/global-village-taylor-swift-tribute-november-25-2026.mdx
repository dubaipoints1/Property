[Global Village is listing two Taylor Swift tribute performances](https://www.globalvillage.ae/en/shows-and-events/taylor-swift-tribute-show-global-village) for **Wednesday, 25 November 2026**, at **6pm and 8pm**. The organiser says show access is included with park entry. This is a tribute act, not an appearance by Taylor Swift.

Check which park ticket you need before buying. Global Village lists its [standard Weekday entry](https://www.globalvillage.ae/en/park-rules) at **AED 25 including VAT**, valid Monday to Thursday except public holidays. The park’s [terms](https://www.globalvillage.ae/en/terms-conditions) say the ticket type can change for special events. The show listing does not promise a reserved seat, so leave time to get from the gates to the performance area.

If you’re planning to see the later performance and travelling by bus, check the return timetable before you set out. The [park’s visit planner](https://www.globalvillage.ae/en/plan-your-visit) lists routes from stations including Union and Centrepoint. Recheck the official show listing on the day before travelling.
