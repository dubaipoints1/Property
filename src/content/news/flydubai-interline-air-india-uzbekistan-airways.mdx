flydubai has announced **interline agreements** with **Air India** and
**Uzbekistan Airways** on 16 September 2026. You can now buy a
flydubai flight plus an onward flight on either airline as **one ticket**,
with your bags **checked through** to the final stop.

flydubai says the two deals add **more than 80 destinations** that its
passengers can book. It now has interline agreements with **46 airlines**.

## Where they reach

- **Through Air India:** Indian destinations such as Bengaluru, Chennai,
  Chandigarh, Goa, Jaipur, Pune and Varanasi. Beyond India: Bali, Ho Chi
  Minh City, Hong Kong, Kuala Lumpur, Mauritius, Melbourne, Seoul,
  Singapore, Sydney and Tokyo.
- **Through Uzbekistan Airways, via Tashkent:** Irkutsk, Krasnoyarsk and
  Moscow. In Europe, Frankfurt, London, Milan, Munich, Paris and Rome.
  Inside Uzbekistan, Bukhara, Fergana and Urgench.

You can book these tickets on flydubai.com, in the flydubai app, through
its contact centre and travel shops, or on the partner airline's own site.

## What an interline ticket is — and is not

An interline ticket lets two airlines sell each other's flights on one
booking. It is **not a codeshare**: you fly on the partner's own flight
number and its own aircraft. It covers tickets and bags. It does not, on
its own, make the two airlines **loyalty partners**.

flydubai's release does not mention **Emirates Skywards**, miles or tier
credit. Do not assume the Air India or
Uzbekistan Airways part of the journey earns Skywards Miles. If earning
matters to you, check Skywards' own list of partner airlines before you
book. Our [Skywards hub](/airlines/skywards/) covers how the programme
works.

## Why it is worth knowing

For UAE residents, the useful part is one booking
through Dubai to destinations beyond flydubai's own network, with bags
checked through, instead of two separate tickets you have to connect
yourself. The Tashkent connections also add a Central Asian routing to
parts of Russia and Europe.
