[Caravan Hatta by Our Habitas](https://all.accor.com/hotel/C593/index.en.shtml) is on the participating-hotel list for ALL Accor’s new four-times Reward points offer. If you were already planning a night in Hatta, open the offer in your ALL account and register **before** booking. The previous round is closed, so make sure you’re looking at the new terms that name Caravan Hatta.

## Check the offer before paying

The new Accor terms give **17 January 2027 at 11.59pm Central European Time** as the deadline to register and book. The stay period runs from **23 November 2026 to 16 May 2027**. The offer applies once per member, to the first eligible stay booked and completed after registration. The multiplier applies to Reward points calculated at Classic status, not to Status points.

Book an eligible rate through Accor’s website or app and include your ALL membership number. Third-party booking sites such as Booking.com do not qualify. Compare the total price and cancellation conditions with any other rate you would book. Accor lists the property, but we did not verify availability for any particular night.
