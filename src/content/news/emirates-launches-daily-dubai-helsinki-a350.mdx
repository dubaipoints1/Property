Emirates flew to Helsinki for the first time on **2 October 2026**. The
airline says the daily service is the **only year-round direct
connection between Finland and the UAE**, per its press release of the
same day.

## The schedule

Per Emirates, with all times local:

- **EK167** departs Dubai at **08:15** and arrives in Helsinki at **14:30**.
- **EK168** departs Helsinki at **16:20** and lands in Dubai at **00:20**
  the following day.

The route is flown by the **Airbus A350**, which Emirates configures with
**298 seats** across **Business, Premium Economy and Economy**. Business is
a **1-2-1** layout with lie-flat seats; there is no First Class on this
aircraft. The release says the timings are built for onward connections
through Dubai to East and Southeast Asia, the Indian Ocean, Africa, the
GCC and Australasia.

## Who should care

- **Finnish residents of the UAE** and anyone with family in Finland: a
  non-stop replaces a one-stop via a European hub, and the overnight
  return lands before the working day.
- **Skywards members chasing Premium Economy.** The A350 carries
  Emirates' newer Premium Economy cabin, so Helsinki joins the list of
  routes where that seat is available. Award pricing for the route is not
  given in the release; check the
  [Emirates Skywards hub](/airlines/skywards/) for how Skywards prices
  Europe redemptions, and the live award search before planning a
  transfer.
- **Winter travellers.** The release points to Lapland and Rovaniemi
  through Emirates Holidays packages. Finland's Northern Lights season
  overlaps the UAE's National Day and winter-break weeks.

Nothing here changes an existing booking, a fee or an earn rate. It is a
new route, not a programme change.

Tickets are on sale on emirates.com, the Emirates app and through travel
agents, per the release.
