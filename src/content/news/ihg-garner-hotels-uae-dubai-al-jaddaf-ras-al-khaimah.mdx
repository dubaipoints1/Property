**IHG Hotels & Resorts** has signed the first two **Garner Hotels** in the
Middle East, both in the UAE, it announced on 29 September 2026. Garner is
IHG's midscale conversion brand, designed for owners converting existing
hotels, and IHG calls it its fastest-scaling brand worldwide.

Both hotels are signed with **Nooa Holdings** and are **expected to open in
2026**, per IHG's release.

## The two hotels

| Hotel | Rooms | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Garner Hotel Dubai Al Jaddaf** | 81 | Near Al Jaddaf Waterfront, between Downtown Dubai and Dubai International Airport, with a rooftop pool overlooking Dubai Creek |
| **Garner Hotel Ras Al Khaimah Downtown** | 204 | By the creek in Al Nakheel, near Al Naeem Mall, about 15 minutes from Ras Al Khaimah International Airport |

Together they add 285 rooms. IHG says it already has 39 hotels operating across eight
brands in the UAE, with 110 more due to open over the next three to five
years.

## What Garner is

IHG pitches Garner as quality stays at an affordable price: a convenient
location, a good night's sleep and a good breakfast. IHG describes the Ras Al
Khaimah hotel as a conversion of an existing property.

## For IHG One Rewards members

The release says Garner gives owners access to IHG One Rewards, but it does
not spell out how IHG One Rewards
earning or elite benefits will work at these two hotels, so check the
hotel's listing on ihg.com once it is bookable.

For UAE residents, the Ras Al Khaimah hotel is the more interesting of the
two, as an IHG option for weekends in the northern emirates. IHG places the
Dubai hotel between Downtown Dubai and DXB.

IHG has given neither hotel an opening date more precise than "2026". We will report when bookings open.
