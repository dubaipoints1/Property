If you collect Avios with **Qatar Airways Privilege Club** and want to
spend them on an award ticket for someone else, Qatar now offers a feature
it calls **My List**. Its terms and conditions, read on 27
September 2026, are specific enough to plan around.

## What the terms say

- You can add **up to four** people to your List and redeem your own Avios
  for their award flights.
- Everyone on the List must be a **Privilege Club member** and **aged 18
  or over**. They have to accept your invitation.
- Each person can be on **only one** List at a time. Anyone already in a
  Family & Friends group cannot be added.
- To create a List, your own account must be **at least 30 days old**.
- Once someone is added, they stay for **at least six months**. Neither
  of you can remove them before then.
- My List redemptions are limited to **Qatar Airways and partner award
  flights**.
- Only you spend from your balance. People on your List cannot redeem
  your Avios themselves.

## Gold and Platinum are the exception

Gold and Platinum members can book award flights for **anyone**, not just
the four people on their List. They can do it online, through the Contact
Centre or at a Member Service Centre. Neither of Qatar's pages extends that
to Burgundy or Silver members.

## Do not confuse it with Family & Friends

Qatar runs a second, separate feature on the same page.
**Family & Friends** is for **collecting** Avios as a group. You can add
up to nine people who are **not** Privilege Club members and pool what you
earn. **My List** is for **spending** your Avios on other members' award
flights. Someone in a Family & Friends group cannot be put on a My List.

## What the terms do not say

Qatar's pages do not say when My List was introduced or what rule, if any,
it replaced. Two points blogs,
[Points, Miles & Bling](https://pointsmilesandbling.com/qatars-avios-redemption-rules/)
and [Upgraded Points](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/qatar-airways-privilege-club-redemption-rules/),
reported in September 2026 that Qatar had removed a qualifying-activity
requirement for redeeming for others.
We could not confirm that from Qatar. The terms we read contain no such
requirement; the only waiting period is your account's 30 days.

The terms also limit My List to adults. They do not say how a member
below Gold books an award for a child.

## What to do

Choose your four names carefully. Each one is locked in for six months,
so a List built in a hurry costs you half a year of flexibility. If you
regularly book for more than four people, note that Qatar's pages name only
Gold and Platinum as free of the four-person List.
