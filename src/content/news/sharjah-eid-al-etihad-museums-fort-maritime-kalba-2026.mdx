If you’re planning an Eid Al Etihad outing in Sharjah, choose the venue before setting off. The [Sharjah Museums Authority programme](https://www.sharjahmuseums.ae/en-US/events/%D8%B9%D9%8A%D9%80%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8055) lists Sharjah Fort in the city on Saturday 28 November, [Sharjah Maritime Museum](https://www.sharjahmuseums.ae/en-us/museums/sharjah-maritime-museum) in Al Khan from 1 to 3 December, and [Bait Sheikh Saeed bin Hamad Al Qasimi](https://www.sharjahmuseums.ae/en-US/museums/bait-sheikh-saeed-bin-hamad-al-qasimi) near Kalba Fort on 2 December. The programme includes family workshops and traditional performances; check the organiser’s listing for workshop timings before travelling.

For a museum visit on 2 December, both Al Khan and Kalba have scheduled celebrations and their venue pages list free museum admission that day. The Maritime Museum is next to Sharjah Aquarium; do not assume the museum’s admission terms apply to a combined visit. Check the organiser’s listing and venue page before making a special trip.
