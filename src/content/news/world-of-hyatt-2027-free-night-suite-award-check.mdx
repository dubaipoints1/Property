If you are planning a Hyatt stay in Dubai or Abu Dhabi for 2027, check the name of the upgrade you intend to use. [Hyatt announced on 18 November](https://newsroom.hyatt.com/worldofhyatt2027) that members will be able to combine eligible awards from 1 January 2027. That does not make every award interchangeable. In Hyatt’s [programme update and FAQ](https://world.hyatt.com/content/gp/en/rewards/world-of-hyatt-enhancements.html), an eligible Free Night Award can be paired with a Suite Upgrade Award. It cannot be paired with a Premium Suite Credit.

## Check the award, then check the room

A Suite Upgrade Award is what you would look for if you want to pair an upgrade with an eligible free night. A Premium Suite Credit is a different reward: Hyatt says it applies to eligible paid rates at participating hotels with premium suites. If the credit is the one in your account, price the paid suite booking separately rather than assuming you can attach the credit to a free night.

Before changing an existing booking, check the exact award name and its expiry in your account, and verify availability and eligibility for your dates and hotel. The policy announcement does not confirm that any particular UAE property or room will accept your combination. Keep your current booking if the alternative is not confirmed, especially if you would face a cancellation charge.

## Use the right chart for the year

Hyatt has also published new Milestone Rewards options for 2027, including an earlier opportunity to choose a Tier-Qualifying Night Credit. Treat the upcoming chart as a planning reference, not a reason to move a December stay. Check the expiry shown on any award you already hold; the new expiry rule is tied to when an award is issued, not when you plan to travel.
