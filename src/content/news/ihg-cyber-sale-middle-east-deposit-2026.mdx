**IHG’s Cyber Sale is showing up at UAE hotels, but it won’t appear for every hotel or every date.** We searched [IHG’s own sale page](https://www.ihg.com/content/us/en/deals/hotel-offers/cybersale) on 17 November for 27–29 November 2026, with two adults in one room. At least 35 UAE properties displayed the named **Cyber Sale Member Exclusive** rate. That’s a snapshot for one stay, not a complete list of participating hotels.

## Where we found the rate

We saw the rate at 24 Dubai properties, including Hotel Indigo Dubai Downtown, Ciel Dubai Marina, InterContinental Dubai Festival City and Holiday Inn Express Dubai Airport. In Abu Dhabi, it appeared at InterContinental Abu Dhabi, InterContinental Residences Abu Dhabi, Holiday Inn Abu Dhabi and six hotels in Yas World Resort. InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort and Spa and InterContinental Fujairah Resort also showed it.

Search from the sale page for your own dates. IHG separates hotels offering the promotion from those displaying their best available fallback rates. Look for the named sale rate on the property card, then open the room’s rate details. A hotel appearing in search results is not enough. This check used IHG’s member-rate filter; it did not test non-member inventory or whether park tickets come with any Yas rate.

## Check what you would lose if you cancel

IHG lists bookings through **3 December 2026**, for eligible stays from **21 November 2026 to 30 April 2027**, and requires booking at least four days before arrival. It compares the sale against the hotel’s Best Flexible Rate before taxes and fees. That does not make the sale booking flexible: the [Hotel Indigo Dubai Downtown](https://www.ihg.com/hotelindigo/hotels/us/en/dubai/dxbbp/hoteldetail) room we opened showed a non-refundable first-night deposit due at booking.

Compare the same room and guest count at the final AED total, including any local charges, and read the cancellation terms. If your dates may move, check what you would lose before choosing the cheaper-looking rate. We did not reserve a room or verify a saving against another rate.
