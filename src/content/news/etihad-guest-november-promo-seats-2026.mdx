[Etihad’s UAE member-offer page](https://www.etihad.com/en-ae/offers/promotion/member-only-deals) lists discounted Promo seats for Etihad Guest members who book by **27 November 2026**, for travel from **18 November to 15 December 2026**. The airline shows one-way examples to and from Abu Dhabi, including direct and connecting itineraries. Etihad specifies UAE time for the dates but does not give a closing hour.

## Compare the full price before using miles

The discount applies to the base fare. Check the taxes, surcharges and any cash you’d still owe, then compare that total with a cash ticket you could actually buy. Etihad requires most of the Promo seat base fare to be paid in miles, so check that your balance covers the required portion before relying on the offer.

Search only if you already have dates in mind. The example price doesn’t mean a seat is available on your flight; compare the dates and fare rules for both directions if you need a return. Don’t move or buy miles solely on the strength of the advertised examples.
