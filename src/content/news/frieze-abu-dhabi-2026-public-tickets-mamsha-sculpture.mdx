The first Frieze Abu Dhabi art fair opens to the public at **2pm on Friday, 20 November**, at Manarat Al Saadiyat. [Abu Dhabi Media Office](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/crown-prince-news/khaled-bin-mohamed-bin-zayed-opens-inaugural-frieze-abu-dhabi-contemporary-art-fair/) reported the inaugural opening on Thursday, 19 November, when admission was by invitation. Public visits run through Sunday, 22 November.

## Pick the time before you book

The [organiser’s ticket page](https://www.friezeabudhabi.com/en/tickets) lists one general-admission window on Friday, **2–9pm**. On Saturday and Sunday, pick either **2–5pm** or **5–9pm**. The organiser lists last admission at 8.30pm each day, so don’t use the 9pm closing time as your arrival deadline. A general-admission ticket is tied to its date and time, and you can’t re-enter on the same ticket. Check the actual slot before making lunch or dinner plans around the fair.

If you’d rather skip the ticketed fair or can’t find the slot you want, [Frieze Sculpture](https://www.friezeabudhabi.com/en/articles/frieze-sculpture-abu-dhabi) is a separate, free outdoor exhibition at Mamsha Beach on Saadiyat Island. It runs through **16 December**. Head to Mamsha Beach for the sculptures, not the fair venue at Manarat Al Saadiyat.
