The [Sheikh Zayed Festival](https://zayedfestival.ae/en) now lists its Eid Al Etihad programme in **Al Wathba, Abu Dhabi, from 1 to 5 December 2026**. That adds Friday and Saturday to the earlier 1–3 December plan. [Emirates News Agency reported on 23 November](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/c3effwo-sheikh-zayed-festival-announces-five-day) that the organising committee plans fireworks on all five nights. The earlier announcement gave an exact fireworks time for the shorter programme, so don’t assume it applies to the added nights.

## Check the last bus before choosing an evening

The festival’s [visitor page](https://zayedfestival.ae/en/plan-your-visit) lists a bus from Abu Dhabi Main Bus Station via Rabdan Mall and the stop opposite Bani Yas Court. Check the return timetable for your date, particularly on a public holiday or if you plan to stay for the fireworks. The same visitor page links to booking. Confirm the admission conditions and the organiser’s show times for your chosen evening before setting out.
