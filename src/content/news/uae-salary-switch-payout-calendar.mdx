Do not put a salary-switch reward into your spending budget just because you opened the account. **Campaign entry, qualification and payment are different events.** A paid reward can also carry conditions which allow the bank to reverse it later.

This Scorecard checks the relevant terms on **5 November 2026**. It adds a payout record to our [same-salary reward comparison](/news/uae-salary-switch-scorecard/), not a new bonus ranking or a prediction of your payment date. The campaign summaries below reuse our verified timelines; they are dated examples, not confirmation that you qualify. Use the [current tracker](/salary-transfer/) before acting.

## Which date are you writing down?

| Record | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign entry deadline | When the required opening, enrolment or first-salary event must happen. Check which event the bank names. |
| Qualification completed | When the required salary credits, activation, savings or eligible product/spend conditions have actually been met. |
| Processing window | What starts the bank's payment clock. It may not be the opening date or first payday. |
| Keep-the-reward conditions | What could forfeit an unpaid reward or reverse one already credited. |

These records are a checklist, not extra bank rules. Confirm the applicable events for your own offer and application route.

## Emirates Islamic: completing the salary test comes first

An optional Covered Card adds its own retail-spend clock; it does not replace the account conditions. Ask the bank to confirm which parts you have completed before treating a processing deadline as overdue. [Emirates Islamic's terms](https://www.emiratesislamic.ae/en/terms-and-conditions/salary-transfer-offer-terms-and-conditions-oct-2026) also require eligibility at fulfilment, so do not assume opening the account settled the whole question.

## FAB: a monthly test, not one upfront payday

<SalaryTransferTimeline offer={campaigns[1].offer} timeline={campaigns[1].timeline} showHistory={false} />

Keep a separate record for each salary month: employer credit, qualifying savings increase or eligible card spend, and cashback credited. The [FAB terms](https://www.bankfab.com/-/media/fab-uds/personal/promotions/2026/20-percent-cashback-on-salary-transfers/terms-and-conditions-en.pdf) do not make a missed month payable retrospectively. A total headline reward is not evidence that the full amount is already owed to you.

## DIB: do not mix the joining bonus and fee reversal

The [DIB XTRA terms](https://www.dib.ae/docs/default-source/pdf/dib-xtra-tc.pdf) give the joining cashback a bundle/spend timetable. The separate home-finance valuation-fee reversal is linked to the finance and first qualifying salary instead. Copying one deadline into the other benefit's row can produce the wrong expected date.

Ask DIB which benefit applies to your exact bundle and what event starts its clock. Our calculator keeps home finance unpriced; a possible fee reversal is not proof of a cheaper finance deal.

## Before payroll moves

- Save the exact offer and terms for your application route, plus any enrolment acknowledgement.
- Ask payroll when the first employer credit can reach the new account; distinguish that from the day you submit the instruction.
- Ask the bank to identify the qualifying event, processing window and any continuing salary or product requirement in writing.
- Track actual credits and completed conditions. If a payment appears late, query it against that record rather than the account-opening date alone.

Use the [salary-transfer calculator](/salary-transfer/calculator/) for a conditional reward comparison. Keep the payout record alongside it: the amount, the timing and the right to keep it are separate parts of the decision.
