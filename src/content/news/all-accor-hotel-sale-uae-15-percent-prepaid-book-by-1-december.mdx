**Accor** is running a global hotel sale, headlined as **"up to 25% off"**,
with bookings open until **1 December 2026**. The headline hides an
important split for UAE stays: the discount and the cancellation rules depend
on the brand, and the offer that covers most UAE hotels is the smaller,
stricter one.

You do not need to be an ALL Accor member. Accor's conditions say the sale is
open to all customers during this period, and its sale page says members get
their Member Rate on top.

## The three offers that matter in the UAE

We read Accor's terms and conditions on 10 November 2026.

| Offer | Brands | Discount | Minimum stay | Cancellation | Stay dates |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hotel Sale** (Middle East, Africa, Asia Pacific, UAE listed) | Premium, midscale and economy: Novotel, ibis, Mercure, Pullman, Mövenpick, Swissôtel, Adagio and others | **15%** | 1 night | **Prepaid, no cancellation, refund or change** | 5 November 2026 to 19 April 2027 |
| **Signature Escapes** (worldwide) | Sofitel, MGallery, Emblems | **25%** | 2 nights | **Flexible**, free cancellation per the hotel's conditions | 8 December 2026 to 28 March 2027 |
| **Stay For More**, Ennismore in the Middle East, Africa and Asia Pacific | Ennismore lifestyle brands such as 25hours, SLS, SO/ and Mondrian | **25%** | 2 nights | **Flexible**, free cancellation per the hotel's conditions | 3 November 2026 to 19 April 2027 |

All three take bookings until 1 December 2026. Each discount is taken off the
hotel's public flexible rate.

The terms also name **Fairmont and Raffles** hotels worldwide under "Stay For
More". The summary tables we read do not give their discount or stay dates,
so check the rate conditions on the booking page for those two brands.

## What to check before you pay

- **Which offer your hotel is in.** Only "participating hotels" are included,
  and Accor links a separate list for each offer. The offer name appears in
  the rate conditions when you book.
- **Whether 15% is worth losing flexibility.** The UAE-wide Hotel Sale rate
  cannot be cancelled or changed. Compare it with the same hotel's flexible
  rate before you commit.
- **The hotel's own terms.** Accor says each hotel's specific conditions
  prevail where they differ from the sale terms.

## What it means for UAE residents

- **A fixed-date staycation at a Novotel, ibis, Pullman or Mövenpick.** The
  15% rate works if your dates will not move. It covers stays through winter
  and into April 2027.
- **A Sofitel or MGallery stay of two nights or more.** This is the better
  offer: a bigger discount and you can still cancel. Stays must fall between
  8 December and 28 March.
- **ALL Accor points.** Accor's terms say that to earn Reward points you must
  give a valid membership number when booking. This sale can also overlap
  with [double points at UAE hotels](/news/all-accor-double-points-uae-hotels-book-by-13-december/);
  where a stay qualifies for several offers, Accor credits only the points
  from the most generous one.

We did not price individual hotels, so we cannot say what any one stay
saves. Our [ALL Accor guide](/hotels/accor-all/) covers how the programme
works.
