Emirates is moving more of its Dubai flights onto the **Airbus A350**, and
with it **Premium Economy**, the cabin between Economy and Business. Two
releases in the last week set the dates:

- **Ho Chi Minh City:** a second daily A350, **EK364/365, from 1 November
  2026**.
- **Hanoi:** the A350 on both daily flights, **EK394/395 and EK388/389, from
  1 December 2026**. This is Premium Economy's first appearance on the
  Hanoi route.
- **Durban:** the A350 on the daily **EK775/776 from 1 March 2027**.

Emirates says that from 1 December **all four daily flights** between Dubai
and Vietnam's two largest cities will be A350s. With Durban, all three of
its South African gateways will have Premium Economy; Cape Town and
Johannesburg got it in September 2025.

## What you get on board

Per Emirates, its A350 carries **298 passengers** in three cabins: Business,
Premium Economy and Economy.

- **Business** is 1-2-1, so every seat has aisle access, with a lie-flat
  bed, a personal minibar and wireless charging.
- **Premium Economy** has leather recliners, in-seat charging and a
  **13.3-inch screen**. The Durban release gives the layout as **2-4-2**.
  Premium Economy passengers flying from Durban get a dedicated check-in
  counter, priority boarding and a **35 kg** checked-baggage allowance plus
  10 kg of cabin baggage.
- **Economy** has a 13.3-inch 4K screen at every seat.

## Which Premium Economy seat?

In September Emirates unveiled a **new** Premium Economy seat with a
full-height electric privacy screen, laid out **2-3-2**, for newly delivered
A350s ([our story](/news/emirates-a350-premium-economy-privacy-screen-seat/)).
Neither of these releases mentions it. Both describe the existing seat, and
the Durban release says 2-4-2. Emirates still has not said which routes get
the new seat, so do not book a Vietnam or Durban flight expecting it.

## For Skywards members

Premium Economy is a separate cabin for both cash fares and Classic Rewards,
so these routes now have a middle option for Miles as well as cash. The
releases say nothing about award pricing or upgrade availability, and we
have not priced either. Our [Skywards hub](/airlines/skywards/) covers how
upgrades with Miles work.

For a family flight to Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi this winter, the change is
simple: from the dates above, every Emirates flight on the route will offer
Premium Economy. Before 1 December, check the aircraft on your Hanoi flight
before you pay for the cabin.
