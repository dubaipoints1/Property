If you live in Al Ain and usually drive to another UAE airport to fly to Pakistan, there’s another option to compare this December. [Fly Jinnah announced on 20 November](https://www.flyjinnah.com/en/about-us/news) that it plans to fly nonstop from Al Ain to Islamabad from **2 December 2026** and to Lahore from **4 December**. The airline says tickets can be booked. Check both legs in its booking engine before making separate plans around the flights.

## Choose the flight day before arranging a pickup

The timetable shows Islamabad flights on **Wednesdays and Sundays**. The outbound flight from Al Ain leaves at **15:20** and is due into Islamabad at **19:15**. Before booking a short visit, make sure one of the scheduled days also works for your return.

Lahore flights are scheduled for **Mondays and Fridays**. The Al Ain flight leaves at **23:30** and is due into Lahore at **03:35 the next morning**, local time. If you book for Friday night, arrange the pickup for Saturday morning. Check the arrival date shown on your booking.

## Search Al Ain, not Abu Dhabi city

[Abu Dhabi Media Office](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/transport/abu-dhabi-airports-reports-growth-in-aircraft-movements-and-cargo-volumes-at-tahnoun-international-airport-in-q3-2026/) identifies Al Ain’s airport as **Tahnoun International (AAN)**. **AUH** is Zayed International, a different airport. Compare the full trip: fare with baggage, the ground journey at both ends and whether the return flights suit your dates. We checked the announced timetable on 22 November, not live fares or seats.
