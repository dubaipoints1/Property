**IHG One Rewards** is running a points promotion to the end of the year
that we had not yet covered. It is a global offer, so it applies to stays at
IHG's UAE hotels, which include InterContinental, Crowne Plaza, Holiday Inn
and Hotel Indigo properties.

The terms below are as reported by
[Head for Points](https://www.headforpoints.com/2026/11/08/best-hotel-promos-for-november-2026/)
in its November promotions round-up, which calls it "the most generous
promotion of the autumn". IHG's own site blocks our reader, so we have not
confirmed them against IHG's terms.

## The offer, per Head for Points

| | |
|---|---|
| Bonus | **Triple base points** on stays booked in the **IHG app** |
| | **Double base points** on stays booked through other qualifying channels |
| Runs | 1 October to **31 December 2026** |
| Registration | Required |
| The catch | The bonus starts on your **second stay** after you register |

## What it means for UAE members

- **Register now, even with one stay planned.** Your first stay after
  registering earns nothing extra but unlocks the bonus for every stay after
  it, up to 31 December. National Day and the winter break are two natural
  occasions for a second.
- **Book in the app.** The difference between triple and double is decided
  by where you book, not where you stay.
- **One stay will not earn the bonus.** If you only expect a single IHG stay
  before the year ends, this promotion does nothing for you.
