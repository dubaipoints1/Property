World of Hyatt is running an autumn promotion: **3,000 bonus points for
every three eligible nights**, up to **36,000 bonus points**, for nights
completed between **29 September and 15 December 2026**. [Head for Points](https://www.headforpoints.com/2026/10/01/world-of-hyatt-launches-autumn-bonus-points-promotion/)
reported it on 1 October.
Every detail below is from Hyatt's own offer page and terms.

## The rules that matter

- **Register first.** Only nights completed **after you register** count.
  Registration is open until 15 December, but a stay before you register
  earns nothing from this promotion.
- **Nights add up across stays.** There is no minimum stay. Three
  one-night stays count the same as one three-night stay.
- **Paid or award nights both count.** An eligible night is one at an
  eligible paid rate, or a free night award.
- **Your own room only.** If you pay for two rooms, only the one you stay
  in counts.
- **Give your membership number** when you book, or at the hotel before
  you check out.
- **The cap is 36,000 points**, which is 36 eligible nights.
- **Allow two to three weeks after checkout** for the points to post.
  They are on top of the usual base points.
- **No elite credit.** Hyatt says the bonus points do not count towards
  elite status.

A stay that starts before 15 December and runs past it still counts for
its nights up to 15 December, provided the stay is completed by 15 June
2027.

## For UAE residents

The promotion covers "participating Hyatt hotels and resorts worldwide".
Hyatt's terms say a hotel must be in World of Hyatt both when you book and
when you stay, and the offer page says the promotion runs at all hotels
participating in World of Hyatt. So a stay at a participating hotel in
Dubai or Abu Dhabi counts the same as one abroad.

Three weekends of one night each between now and mid-December would earn
3,000 bonus points, on top of base points. Book an eligible rate at a hotel
in World of Hyatt, and register before you check in.

Register on [Hyatt's offer page](https://world.hyatt.com/content/gp/en/offers/3k-bonus-points.html).
Hyatt says it can change or withdraw the promotion at any time.
