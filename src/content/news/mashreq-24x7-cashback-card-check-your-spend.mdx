Mashreq lists the [24X7 Cashback Credit Card](https://www.mashreq.com/en/uae/neo/cards/credit-cards/24x7-cashback/) separately from its regular Cashback card in its [November key facts sheet](https://www.mashreq.com/-/jssmedia/pdfs/neo/cards/KFS-TnC/mashreq-cards-consolidated-kfs-en-ar.ashx). If you already have a Mashreq Cashback card, use the name on your statement to find the right terms. The two cards do not have the same earning table.

## Check where the purchase is billed

The 24X7 card lists different cashback rates for dining, streaming, travel, and other spending. The [card’s cashback terms](https://www.mashreq.com/-/jssmedia/pdfs/neo/cards/cards-tncs/credit-card/24x7-cashback-cc-tnc-en.ashx) say the bank uses the merchant category and transaction information it receives. Don’t assume a streaming subscription billed through another company will be treated the same as a charge from the streaming service itself. The terms also exclude utility bills paid through Mashreq’s own payment channels.

If you get the card, check how your usual purchases appear before moving more of your spending. The primary cardholder can redeem accumulated cashback through Mashreq’s app or online banking once the required balance is reached. A redemption reduces the outstanding balance but does not count as the minimum payment due.
