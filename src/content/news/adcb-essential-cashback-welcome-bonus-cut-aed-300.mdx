> **Current application status, checked 18 November 2026.** The [ADCB product page](https://www.adcb.com/en/personal/cards/credit-cards/essential-cashback-credit-card.aspx) still advertises welcome cashback, but its [linked acquisition terms](https://www.adcb.com/en/multimedia/pdfs/2025/may/essential-acquisition-tnc-en.pdf) set an October application window that has closed. We could not confirm the advertised bonus for a new application. Ask ADCB for current, dated terms before counting it.

ADCB's product page advertised **up to AED 300** in our 30 August 2026
check, down from **AED 350**. A second check on 13 September found that
the headline had fallen again, to **up to AED 250**. These were the
figures shown on the page on those dates, not confirmation that a
particular applicant qualified.

## Why it matters

Compare the card without the welcome cashback until you have current
terms. The old flat-rate and cinema-cashback examples in this report
do not describe the current product. See our [card review](/cards/adcb-essential-cashback/)
for the spend tiers, restrictions and separately sourced fees.
