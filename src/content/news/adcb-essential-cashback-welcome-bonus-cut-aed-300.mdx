ADCB has **cut the welcome cashback** on its Essential Cashback Credit Card.
The product page now advertises **up to AED 300** for new-customer
activation on select digital channels, down from **AED 350**. Our
fee/product-page monitor caught the change in a 30 August 2026 check of
ADCB's own page; nothing else on the card moved with it.

## What's the same

- **1% flat cashback** on everyday spend, gated by AED 1,000 of monthly
  card use.
- **AED 70/month** online-cinema cashback, gated by a separate AED 1,500
  of monthly spend.
- **AED 0 annual fee** in year one; ADCB's standard Schedule of Fees from
  year two.
- **AED 5,000 minimum salary**, with salary transfer to ADCB required for
  issuance.

Only the one-off welcome figure changed. The spend trigger and the list
of qualifying digital channels are still not published on the public
page — as before, get both in writing at application.

## Why it matters

The Essential Cashback is ADCB's entry-tier card for the AED 5,000–8,000
salary bracket, positioned below the 365 Cashback for spenders who don't
reliably clear AED 5,000 a month. The welcome credit was never the card's
main draw — the flat 1% rate and the low spend gate are — but it does
lower the year-one arithmetic: a routine user hitting the cinema bonus
and the welcome cashback now nets roughly **AED 1,460** in year one
against the AED 0 fee, AED 50 less than before the cut.

Our [full review](/cards/adcb-essential-cashback/) is updated to the new
figure throughout. This is ADCB adjusting its own offer, not a correction
on our side, so it is not in the [corrections log](/corrections/) — the
