If you have one evening free on **Wednesday, 2 December**, pick the event you want to see before setting off. City Walk and the waterfront both have an **8 pm** event, but they aren’t at the same venue.

## City Walk: Yara at the Central Fountain

[City Walk’s event page](https://www.citywalk.ae/en/whats-on/eid-al-etihad-celebrations-at-city-walk) lists a free Yara concert from **8 pm to 9.30 pm** on 2 December. Look for the Central Fountain. City Walk lists its Eid Al Etihad Village from 1 to 3 December, with workshops and traditional performances; those dates don’t mean Yara is appearing all three evenings.

## Bluewaters or The Beach JBR: choose an earlier or later show

[Visit Dubai](https://www.visitdubai.com/en/festivals-and-events/dubai-events-calendar/eid-al-etihad-light-show) lists the free waterfront light show at **8 pm and 10 pm** on Wednesday. It names both Bluewaters Island and The Beach JBR, so choose the side that suits your journey rather than treating them as separate stops. [Bluewaters’ venue guide](https://bluewatersdubai.ae/en/faqs) describes a pedestrian link to JBR but doesn’t confirm event-night access.

[Emirates News Agency reported on 20 November](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/c3cn5l1-dubai-gears-for-spectacular-55th-eid-etihad) that the show runs on 2 and 3 December. Visit Dubai also describes both dates, but its timed reminder entries name Wednesday. Check the official calendar again if you’re planning for Thursday. The events are free to watch; that doesn’t tell you what parking costs or guarantee a place. Schedules checked on 22 November.
