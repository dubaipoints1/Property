**Etihad** has started flying from **Abu Dhabi (Zayed International)** to
**Red Sea International Airport** in Saudi Arabia's Tabuk Province. The
first flight, an Airbus A320, landed at 12:05 on **Sunday 4 October 2026**,
[Gulf News reports](https://gulfnews.com/business/aviation/etihad-airways-launches-direct-flights-to-saudi-arabias-red-sea-1.500700684).

The route opened with one flight a week. **From 25 October it goes to two**,
according to the state news agency WAM
([via Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/business/travel-tourism/etihad-flies-to-saudi-arabias-red-sea-coast-from-october-4-482466)).

## What changes

- **Route:** Abu Dhabi (AUH) to Red Sea International, nonstop.
- **Now:** Sundays only.
- **From 25 October:** Thursdays and Sundays, per Gulf News.
- **Flight time:** just over three hours. Gulf News quotes Etihad as saying
  about 3 hours 10 minutes.
- **Season:** year-round, per WAM.

It is Etihad's **sixth destination in Saudi Arabia**, after Riyadh, Jeddah,
Dammam, Medina and Al Qassim.

## Why it matters if you live in the UAE

The Thursday flight is the useful part. With one flight a week, the
shortest round trip was a full week away. With Thursdays and Sundays, a
Thursday-to-Sunday break becomes possible, as long as the return flights
run on those same days. Neither report gives the return schedule, so check
the timetable before you book a long weekend around it.

Red Sea International serves The Red Sea and AMAALA resort developments.
Gulf News says the airport already has flights to Riyadh, Jeddah,
**Dubai**, Doha and Milan, so Dubai residents already had a way in. The
Etihad route is the first on that list from Abu Dhabi.

Neither report gives fares or departure times. Check Etihad's booking page
before you plan around a particular day.
