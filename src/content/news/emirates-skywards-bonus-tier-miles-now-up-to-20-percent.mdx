**Emirates** has halved the headline on its standing bonus Tier Miles offer.
The UAE special-offers page now reads **"Get up to 20% bonus Tier Miles when
logged in"**. Until early November the same offer sat at a page address
ending "earn-up-to-40-percent-bonus-tier-miles", and the new page's browser
title still says "Earn up to 40% bonus Tier Miles".

Our offers monitor recorded the change on 4 November 2026. We read the page
ourselves on 10 November.

## What the page says

- The bonus applies to **select flights and fares**.
- Offers are **"exclusively for you"** and appear only when you **log in
  before you search**.
- It is a bonus on **Tier Miles**, the ones that count towards Silver, Gold
  and Platinum status, not the Skywards Miles you spend.

The page does not list an end date, routes or fare types in the text we
could read. Its terms and conditions sit behind a "Show more" control that
our reader did not open, so we cannot say whether every member sees the same
percentage.

## What it means for UAE members

- **Chasing status before your review date?** Log in before every Emirates
  search. A targeted bonus only shows when you are signed in.
- **Do not plan around 40%.** If you were counting on the earlier headline
  to close a gap to the next tier, redo the sum at 20% or less.
- **Check the fare.** The bonus is on "select" fares, so the cheapest fare
  on a route may not carry it.

Our [Skywards guide](/airlines/skywards/) lists the Tier Miles needed for
each tier.
