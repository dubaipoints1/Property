Emirates has relaunched **My Emirates Pass** for winter. Per the terms on
emirates.com, an Emirates boarding pass **to Dubai**, in your own name and
dated **between 1 October 2026 and 31 March 2027**, gets discounts at
partner venues across the UAE for the same period. The pass can be used at
different venues, and more than once at the same one.

**The gotchas, up front.** The programme period **excludes UAE public
holidays**. You need **photo ID** with the pass. Boarding passes cannot be
reprinted after departure, so Emirates suggests **screenshotting a mobile
boarding pass**, which can vanish from the app once you have flown. And the
programme is not valid with any other discount, promotion or loyalty
programme.

## Why a resident should care

The programme is marketed at visitors, but nothing in the terms limits it
to them. The test is a boarding pass to Dubai issued by Emirates, so a UAE
resident flying home from a winter trip qualifies on the inbound leg. If you
fly Emirates back into DXB once between now and March, keep the pass.

There is **no Skywards angle**: the pass earns no Miles and costs none. It
is a discount on spend you make in the UAE, which you may also be able to
stack with nothing else, so check whether a card discount you already hold
beats it before you show the pass.

## What the offers look like

A sample of what the offer list showed on 9 October, in each venue's own
terms:

- **Dining:** 20% off food and beverage at Creek Kitchen (Marriott Marquis
  Dubai Creek) and at &More by Sheraton (Sheraton Jumeirah Beach Resort and
  Sheraton Grand Hotel, Dubai), valid for the pass holder and up to nine
  guests; 30% off the bill at 365 Restaurant, Novotel Dubai Al Barsha.
- **Leisure:** 15% off all Ain Dubai ticket categories; 20% off Dubai
  Sail GP tickets with a promo code; buy one, get one free on a full family
  day pass at The Els Club's Country Club.
- **Spa and wellbeing:** 20% off selected treatments at Talise Spa,
  Jumeirah Emirates Towers, and at Ahasees Spa & Club, Grand Hyatt Dubai.
- **Retail:** 15% off full-price items at several Apparel Group brands,
  including ALDO and Aeropostale, on a single bill.

Each venue sets its own conditions, and Emirates says they can change at
the partner's discretion. Read the venue's line on the
[offers page](https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/experience/my-emirates-pass/)
before you go; several exclude tobacco, shisha and existing packages, and
some require a booking.

## What it changes

Nothing about a booking, a fare or an earn rate. If you are choosing
between carriers for a trip home this winter, it is a small point in
Emirates' favour, not a reason to pay more for the ticket. For how Skywards
prices the flight itself, see the [Emirates Skywards hub](/airlines/skywards/).
