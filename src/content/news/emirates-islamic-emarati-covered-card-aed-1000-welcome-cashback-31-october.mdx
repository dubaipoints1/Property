Emirates Islamic is running a welcome cycle on its **Emarati Covered
Card**, the no-annual-fee card it issues only to UAE nationals. The
[campaign page](https://www.emiratesislamic.ae/en/offers/emarati-credit-card-campaign),
read on 15 September 2026, is headlined "Get a guaranteed AED 1,000
cashback" and its terms table is one line:

> Spend a minimum of AED 15,000 and above within 60 days of card
> issuance — AED 1,000.

The window, from the same terms: cards **sourced from 1 September to
31 October 2026** and approved by 10 November 2026. The
[card page](https://www.emiratesislamic.ae/en/cards/credit-cards/emarati-card)
states the offer the same way.

## Two figures on one page

Halfway down the campaign page, the icon row reads "Welcome Bonus: Up
to 150,000 EI SmartMiles worth AED 1,500", and the hero artwork is
filed under an AED 1,500 name. Everything else on the page, and the
card page, says AED 1,000 in cash. The tile is an earlier cycle's copy
that was not taken down.

We go with the terms. Where a bank's marketing tile and its terms table
disagree, the terms are what the bank will pay against, and here they
say AED 1,000 cashback, not Miles. For the record, by the card page's
own exchange table 150,000 EI SmartMiles would have converted to 15,000
Skywards Miles or 22,500 Etihad Guest Miles; that is not what is on
offer.

## Who qualifies, and who does not

From the terms, in the bank's order:

- **UAE nationals only.**
- **New-to-bank customers only.** Existing Emarati cardholders are out,
  and so is anyone who closed an Emirates Islamic covered card in the
  last six months.
- The card must be **activated**, and the AED 15,000 must be **retail
  spend** within 60 days of issuance. Supplementary-card spend counts
  towards it; fees, cash, balance transfers, reversals and the like do
  not.
- **Not combinable** with any other acquisition offer on the card.
- Cashback is awarded **after the 60 days**, once the spend condition
  is met.

One interaction worth knowing about: Emirates Islamic's current
[salary-transfer campaign](/salary-transfer/emirates-islamic/) has a
card component of its own, and its terms exclude the Emarati card from
it. A national moving a salary and taking this card gets this offer,
not both.

## What the card is once the bonus is spent

No annual fee, at all, which is what makes the bonus clean. Earn is in
EI SmartMiles at "up to" 3.75 per AED 1 abroad and "up to" 2.5 per AED 1
at home, with the usual category carve-outs behind the "up to"; the
[card page](https://www.emiratesislamic.ae/en/cards/credit-cards/emarati-card)
links its own rate table. Talabat is 50 per cent off twice a month,
valet parking twice a month, and lounge access is unlimited.

## The practical read

AED 15,000 in 60 days is AED 7,500 a month of card spend. If that is
your normal run rate, the AED 1,000 is a 6.7 per cent return on it,
once, on a card that costs nothing to hold afterwards. If it is not
your normal run rate, the bonus is being bought with spend you would not
otherwise make, and the arithmetic stops working. Apply before
31 October; the approval deadline of 10 November leaves little slack
after that.
