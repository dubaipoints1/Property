Emirates Islamic is running a banded salary-transfer cashback campaign
to **30 September 2026**, and it is now live on our
[tracker](/salary-transfer/emirates-islamic/). We verified the
[offer page](https://www.emiratesislamic.ae/en/offers/salary-transfer-cashback)
and the campaign
[terms and conditions](https://www.emiratesislamic.ae/en/terms-and-conditions/salary-transfer-offer-terms-and-conditions)
on 22 August 2026; the window opened 1 June.

## What it pays

Two components. First, **CASA cashback** into the salary account, by
band — for non-UAE nationals: **AED 50** at salary AED 5,000–7,499,
rising through AED 100, 400, 600 and 1,500 to **AED 3,000** at
AED 50,000+. UAE Nationals earn more at every band, topping out at
**AED 5,000** at 50,000+.

Second, a **Covered Card spend reward** of up to **AED 1,000** — a new
EI Covered Card plus AED 6,000–18,000 of spend (scaling by band)
within 60 days of the card being issued. Sharia-compliant throughout;
these are Covered Cards, not conventional credit cards. Stacked, the
expat headline is AED 4,000 at the top band — but the guaranteed
no-card figure there is AED 3,000. Full band tables are on the
[tracker entry](/salary-transfer/emirates-islamic/).

## The four traps

**New-to-Group only.** Emirates Islamic is an Emirates NBD Group bank,
and the T&C defines eligibility as no salary-transfer relationship
with the **Group** — Emirates NBD *or* Emirates Islamic — in the six
months before your first salary credit. An ENBD salary customer
hopping across to the Islamic brand earns nothing. This is the
disqualifier we expect to catch the most readers, because it is
invisible on the offer page's headline.

**Enrolment is a form, not a default.** You must submit the campaign
enrolment form on the offer page — new and existing customers alike.
Transfer the salary without enrolling and the reward simply never
tracks.

**Lowest-month banding.** Three consecutive monthly salary credits
qualify you, and if the amounts differ, the band is computed on the
**lower of the second and third months** — the same shape as RAKBANK's
rule. One light month re-bands the entire payout.

**Exclusions with teeth.** Salaries landing in Super Savings Skywards
or Etihad Guest accounts disqualify the whole campaign, as does
holding an active Personal Finance facility at payout time. Cashback
lands within 60 days of completing the three-month run — call it
roughly five months after the first salary — and can be reversed if
the account then goes three consecutive months without a salary credit
within the following year.

## Where it sits in the market

With [RAKBANK's cash reward closing 31 August](/news/rakbank-salary-cash-reward-closes-31-august/),
this becomes the longest-running verified banded offer on the
[live tracker](/salary-transfer/) — and unlike RAKBANK, the bottom
bands are open to everyone, not just payroll-scheme enrollees. The
trade: smaller guaranteed sums at most bands, and the new-to-Group
rule closes it to a large slice of the switching population.
