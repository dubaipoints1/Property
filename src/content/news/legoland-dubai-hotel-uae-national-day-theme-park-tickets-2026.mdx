If you’re considering a National Day stay at LEGOLAND Hotel Dubai, check the park tickets before you compare it with a room-only rate. The [hotel’s offer terms](https://www.legoland.com/dubai/legoland-hotel/hotel-offers/uae-national-day-hotel-offer/) include LEGOLAND Dubai Theme Park tickets and list 2 December 2026 as the last date for both bookings and stays. The hotel directs guests to book on its website with code `UAE55`, subject to room availability.

At least one guest on the booking must be a UAE national or resident. Bring a valid UAE passport or Emirates ID for that guest at check-in. Breakfast is included only if your selected package lists it.

The [resort treats the theme park and Water Park as separate admissions](https://www.legoland.com/dubai/about-us/faq/). Check how many tickets and which park your selected room rate includes, then compare the total for the same family and dates. Don’t add the value of Water Park entry to your comparison unless your booking shows it.
