ADCB has rebalanced the earn table on the **365 Cashback Credit Card**,
its flagship everyday cashback Visa. The changes surfaced in our July
verification of the product page and were confirmed against the Schedule
of Fees (Ver.46):

- **Groceries & supermarkets: 5% → 3%**
- **Fuel & Salik: 3% → 5%**
- **New 5% categories:** home digital-entertainment subscriptions
  (Netflix, Prime, Disney+ and peers) and AI subscriptions (ChatGPT,
  Copilot, Gemini) when paid directly to the merchant
- **Dining stays at 6%**, including online food orders
- **1% on everything else** and on international retail purchases

The card's gating mechanics are unchanged and still decide who this card
is for: **no cashback at all in a month below AED 5,000 of spend**, and a
**AED 1,000 monthly cashback cap**. The welcome offer now runs on a
45-day trigger — **AED 365 back on AED 5,000 of spend within 45 days of
issuance**, paid within 90.

## What the rebalance means in dirhams

For a household running AED 3,000 of dining and AED 2,000 of groceries a
month — exactly clearing the floor — the change trims the monthly earn
from AED 280 to **AED 240** (AED 2,880/year). Shift AED 500 of that
grocery spend to fuel, Salik or a 5% subscription category and the same
AED 5,000 floor pays AED 250. In short: the card got slightly worse for
grocery-heavy households and slightly better for commuters and
subscription stacks.

Grocery-first spenders now have a genuine reason to compare alternatives
— the [cashback rankings](/cards/cashback/) line the options up by net
AED value. The [full 365 Cashback review](/cards/adcb-365-cashback/) has
been rewritten around the new table, with the worked examples and the
break-even maths.
