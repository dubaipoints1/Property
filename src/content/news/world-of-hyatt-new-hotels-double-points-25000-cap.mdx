**World of Hyatt** has changed how it rewards stays at its newest hotels.
Since **1 October 2026**, members earn **double base points** at
participating new hotels, up to **25,000 bonus points per stay**,
[per Milesopedia](https://milesopedia.com/en/news/hospitality/world-of-hyatt-new-hotels-double-points/)
and [Upgraded Points](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/2x-hyatt-points-new-hotels/).
The offer it replaces paid a flat **500 bonus points a night**.

We could not read Hyatt's own [offer page](https://world.hyatt.com/content/gp/en/offers/new-hotels.html):
it returned a bot wall when we tried on 13 October. Every rule below comes
from those two reports, which agree on the substance.

## The rules that matter

- **Book the right rate.** The bonus applies only to the rate called
  **"New Hotel Member Bonus Offer"**. There is no registration, but any
  other rate earns nothing extra.
- **Paid stays only.** Award nights earned the old 500-point bonus; they
  earn nothing under the new one. Group rates are excluded.
- **Base points only.** You earn 5 extra points per USD 1 on top of the
  usual 5. Elite bonus points are not doubled, and Upgraded Points notes
  that taxes do not count.
- **One cap per stay.** Consecutive nights at one hotel on the rate count
  as a single stay, so 25,000 points is the most one trip can earn. You
  reach it at **USD 5,000** (about AED 18,360) of eligible spend.
- **Take Hyatt points.** Choosing airline miles for the stay cancels the
  bonus, per Milesopedia. Up to three rooms count.
- **Dates are set hotel by hotel.** Milesopedia says you must check in
  within the hotel's offer period; Upgraded Points says check out. Read
  the hotel's own terms before you book.

Points post two to four weeks after checkout.

## Better or worse?

It depends on the room rate. On a **USD 200** night (about AED 735) the
new offer pays 1,000 bonus points, double the old 500. At USD 100 a night
the two are level. Below that, and on any award night, the old offer paid
more.

## For UAE members

No UAE hotel is on the current list as Upgraded Points published it. The
nearest is **Grand Hyatt The Red Sea** on Shura Island in Saudi Arabia
(Category 6). **Hyatt Regency London Olympia** (Category 5) is also on it.
The list changes monthly and usually covers the current month and the next
three, so check it again before a booking.

If you are paying cash at either hotel anyway, book the bonus rate rather
than a member or flexible rate. Do not book a new hotel for the bonus
alone. We have not seen whether it stacks with Hyatt's
[autumn 3,000-points-per-three-nights promotion](/news/world-of-hyatt-3000-bonus-points-every-three-nights-15-december/),
which counts paid and award nights to 15 December.

Hyatt points are also on Qatar Privilege Club's points-exchange list; see
our [Qatar Avios transfer-bonus report](/news/qatar-avios-transfer-bonus-uae-banks-31-october/)
before moving any. For the hotel programmes we cover in depth, see
[our hotels section](/hotels/).
