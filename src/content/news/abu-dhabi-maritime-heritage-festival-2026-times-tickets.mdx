Abu Dhabi’s Maritime Heritage Festival is on the Corniche through **Sunday, 29 November**. If you’re taking children, start with the sail-making workshop or the maritime puppet show. Check the day’s programme for the activities you want; the festival opening hours aren’t a timetable for every session.

The [event’s ticket page](https://abu-dhabi.platinumlist.net/event-tickets/maritime-heritage-festival) lists **4–11pm Monday to Thursday** and **4pm to midnight Friday to Sunday**. It also says that free admission for eligible visitors, including People of Determination and young children who meet the age rule, is handled at the on-site box office. Check the criteria and bring proof of eligibility before buying a ticket online.

If you’re driving, head to Gold Parking on **Al Hosn Street**, not the festival entrance, for the complimentary shuttle available to visitors with a valid ticket. The ticket page lists a separate paid self-parking option closer to the venue. [Abu Dhabi Media Office](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/arts-culture/5th-maritime-heritage-festival-to-take-place-in-abu-dhabi/) has the fuller programme of workshops and performances.
