If you’re pricing a winter trip from the UAE, [Accor’s regional Black Friday terms](https://all.accor.com/a/en/offers/ena/black-friday-offer.html) give you two checks before you start: the hotel must be participating and your stay must be three to seven nights. The eligible country list includes the UK, France, Italy and Morocco, but not the UAE. Don’t use this offer to price a Dubai staycation.

Bookings opened on 26 November and close at 23:59 Paris time on 1 December. The listed stay period runs from 8 December 2026 to 28 March 2027. Accor says there is no online prepayment at booking but requires a valid credit card to guarantee the booking at the start of the stay. The property’s own cancellation policy applies.

Compare the same room and meal plan, then look at the full total including any tourist tax, and the last date you can cancel. Accor measures the advertised saving against a flexible rate, not necessarily the cheapest rate you could book.
