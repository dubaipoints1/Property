**flydubai plans to resume nonstop flights from Dubai to Riga, Salzburg and
Vilnius in December.** The airline's [10 November announcement](https://news.flydubai.com/flydubai-expands-european-network-with-direct-flights-to-riga-salzburg-and-vilnius)
lists three flights a week on each route from Dubai International (DXB)
Terminal 3.

- **Riga:** from 11 December 2026, on Tuesdays, Fridays and Saturdays.
- **Salzburg and Vilnius:** from 12 December 2026, on Tuesdays, Thursdays
  and Saturdays.

## Compare the ticket, not just the headline fare

Check both legs against the [timetable](https://www.flydubai.com/en/flying-with-us/timetable/)
before committing to hotel dates. Riga has a Friday departure in the
announced schedule; Salzburg and Vilnius have Thursday instead.

If you need a checked bag, compare what you would pay with that bag
included. flydubai's [fare rules](https://www.flydubai.com/en/flying-with-us/fare-types/)
list checked baggage and seat selection as extras on its Lite fare.
The airline also plans Emirates codeshares on these routes. Check the
allowance and change rules on the ticket you are considering; do not
assume an Emirates-marketed fare follows flydubai's Lite conditions.

If you find cash and award quotes for the same trip, compare them with
our [cash-or-miles calculator](/calculator/cash-or-miles/). We did not
check seats, fares or award space for a particular departure.
