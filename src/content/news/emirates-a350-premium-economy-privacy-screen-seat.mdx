Emirates has launched a new Premium Economy seat with an electrically
operated, full-height privacy screen between every seat — a feature the
airline describes as the first of its kind in any Premium Economy cabin.
It debuts on **newly received Airbus A350s**, in a **2-3-2** layout of
**28 seats**.

## What is actually in the seat

From Emirates' own release, dated 3 September 2026:

- **Full-height adjustable privacy divider** between every seat, raised or
  lowered at the touch of a button.
- **20-inch seat width**, pitch **up to 39 inches** on the A350.
- Recline into a **cradle position** that does not intrude on the row
  behind.
- **U-Dream headrest** by Safran Seats, with adjustable wings.
- **Electrically deployable leather footrest.**
- **Wireless charging** — new to the Premium Economy cabin.
- Tray table with an **integrated phone holder**, usable as a second
  screen.

## The catch is supply, not the seat

The seat is going onto **newly received** A350s, not retrofitted across
the fleet, and the release names no routes. For a Dubai-based reader the
question is not whether the hardware is good — 28 seats at 2-3-2 with a
powered divider is a genuine step up — but how often you will actually be
sitting in one. Emirates has not published a rollout schedule, a fleet
count for the new configuration, or which destinations get the aircraft
first, so we cannot tell you your odds on any given booking.

Emirates' existing four-class A380s and earlier A350 deliveries carry the
**previous** Premium Economy seat. Both will be sold as "Premium Economy"
in the same booking flow. If the divider is the reason you are paying the
premium, the aircraft type on your specific flight is the only thing that
settles it, and that can change after booking.

## For Skywards members

seat, and we are not assuming one. Premium Economy Classic Rewards

## On the "world's first" claim

That is Emirates' description of its own product, not a verified industry
survey, and we have not tested it against every competitor cabin. What is
checkable — the dimensions, the configuration, the seat count and the
feature list above — comes directly from the airline's published release.
