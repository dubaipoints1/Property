If you hold a flydubai ticket between Dubai and Tel Aviv, your flight is
not operating for now. flydubai has **suspended its flights to and from
Israel** while authorities investigate an incident on flight **FZ 1073**
on 30 September.

## What flydubai has said

flydubai has issued three statements on its newsroom. In order:

1. FZ 1073, from **Dubai International (DXB)** to **Ben Gurion (TLV)** on
   30 September, "experienced an incident while en route". The aircraft
   landed safely at **Tabuk (TUU)** in Saudi Arabia, and all passengers
   are safe.
2. "An altercation occurred in the flight deck". On-duty flydubai crew
   travelling on the flight secured the aircraft, diverted it and landed
   it safely at Tabuk. All passengers **and crew** are safe and accounted
   for. flydubai sent **two replacement aircraft** to Tabuk for the
   passengers and crew. It said the incident had no impact on the rest of
   its network, and that the reasons and motives "are unknown and remain
   subject to a formal investigation".
3. In coordination with the relevant authorities, flydubai flights **to
   and from Israel** are **suspended** while the investigation continues.
   flydubai says it will review the suspension as more information
   becomes available.

flydubai has not said how long the suspension will last.

## If you are booked

flydubai says it is "working to provide support and alternative
arrangements where possible" for passengers whose plans are affected. It
has not published rebooking or refund terms for the suspension. Contact
flydubai directly about your booking, and check its newsroom before
making new plans on the route.

Several outlets are reporting much more about what happened on board,
attributed to Israeli officials and Israeli media. flydubai has not
confirmed those accounts, and it has asked everyone to avoid speculating
while the investigation runs. We are reporting only what the airline has
said.
