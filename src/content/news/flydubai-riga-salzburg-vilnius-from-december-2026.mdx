**flydubai** is bringing back three European winter routes from Dubai in
December: **Riga** in Latvia, **Salzburg** in Austria and **Vilnius** in
Lithuania. Each gets **three flights a week** from **Terminal 3 at DXB**. The
airline announced the routes on 10 November 2026 and calls them reinstated
services, part of its winter schedule.

For UAE travellers this means a nonstop to the Austrian Alps for the ski
season and two Baltic capitals in time for their Christmas markets.

## Dates, days and fares

All figures below are flydubai's own, from its release.

| Route | Starts | Days | Flight | Return Economy Lite from DXB | Return Business from DXB |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dubai–**Riga** (RIX) | 11 December 2026 | Tue, Fri, Sat | FZ 1441/1442 | from **AED 2,442** | from AED 10,012 |
| Dubai–**Salzburg** (SZG) | 12 December 2026 | Tue, Thu, Sat | FZ 1449/1450 | from **AED 2,699** | from AED 13,175 |
| Dubai–**Vilnius** (VNO) | 12 December 2026 | Tue, Thu, Sat | FZ 1263/1264 | from **AED 3,112** | from AED 10,301 |

These are "from" fares, so the price on your dates may be higher, and
December school-holiday dates usually are. Economy Lite is flydubai's
lowest fare type; check what baggage it includes before comparing it with
another airline's price.

## What it means for UAE travellers

- **Skiers.** flydubai describes Salzburg as a gateway to the Ski Amadé
  region. A Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday pattern suits a long weekend or a
  full week.
- **Skywards members.** The three routes operate under flydubai's codeshare
  with Emirates, on single tickets with bags checked through. flydubai
  flights earn and spend Skywards Miles; our
  [Skywards guide](/airlines/skywards/) covers how.
- **Connecting passengers.** flydubai highlights onward connections through
  Dubai to Bangkok, Krabi, Colombo, Kathmandu, the Maldives and Zanzibar.

flydubai says Business Class on its Boeing 737s has lie-flat seats, and that
it is rolling out free Starlink WiFi across the fleet. The release does not
say which aircraft on these routes already have it.

Bookings are open on flydubai.com and the flydubai app. The full timetable,
with departure times, is on flydubai's site; the release gives days but not
times.
