RAKBANK's **Salary Transfer Cash Reward** — up to **AED 4,000**, no
credit card required — ends **31 August 2026**. We re-verified the
[accounts landing page](https://www.rakbank.ae/en/landing-page/accounts/accounts-lp)
on 22 August, and the bank's own copy is not being coy about it:
"Hurry! This offer is only until 31st August 2026."

Nine days, and the deadline is harder than it looks. Per the
[campaign T&C](https://www.rakbank.ae/globalassets/rakbank/all-pdfs/001---campaign/c-transfer-salary-cash-reward-july2026.pdf),
your **first salary credit must land inside the window** (or within 60
days of opening, for new accounts). Filing the HR salary-transfer form
this week and getting paid in September does not qualify. If your
payday is end-of-month, you are cutting it to the day.

## Why this one matters

On the [live tracker](/salary-transfer/) this is currently the
market's **best pure-cash offer with no credit card attached** — the
comparable headline figures elsewhere either require card spend (the
[Emirates Islamic banded offer](/salary-transfer/emirates-islamic/)
needs AED 18,000 of Covered Card spend to reach its AED 4,000 expat
headline) or pay in instalments (Mashreq spreads its joining cashback
over 12 months). RAKBANK pays one lump sum within 60 days of the
promotion ending or your third salary credit, whichever is later.

The bands, from the T&C table: **AED 1,000** at salary
AED 10,000–19,999, **AED 2,000** at AED 20,000–49,999, **AED 4,000**
at AED 50,000+. The **AED 500** bottom band (AED 5,000–9,999) exists
only for customers enrolled under RAKempower Payroll or RAKmore — a
standard applicant needs AED 10,000/month minimum. Full mechanics and
exclusions are on the [RAKBANK tracker page](/salary-transfer/rakbank/).

## The two traps

**Three consecutive months, banded on the lowest.** The reward is
computed on the **lowest** of your three qualifying salary credits. A
joining pro-rata month or a stretch of unpaid leave re-bands the whole
payout — AED 50,000, AED 50,000, AED 18,000 pays AED 1,000, not
AED 4,000.

**New-to-salary only.** Anyone whose salary hit a RAKBANK account
before 1 July 2026 is out, and the salary credit's narration must
actually say "Salary" — self-employed transfers do not count.

## Move now or wait?

Waiting is a real bet, not a delay. Both prior RAKBANK salary-transfer
cycles **404'd after close** — the campaign PDFs came down and nothing
announced a successor. As of the 22 August fetch there is no successor
published on the landing page either. RAKBANK has historically come
back with new cycles, but on no stated schedule and at no guaranteed
band. If the bands above work for your salary and your payday clears
31 August, this is the week to file the paperwork.
