ADIB has launched two Marriott Bonvoy co-branded **Covered Cards** — the
Sharia-compliant structure ADIB uses across its card range. Both are live
on ADIB's site now, and the headline welcome offer runs **27 August to
30 November 2026**.

This is the first Marriott Bonvoy co-brand issued in the UAE. For a market
where the hotel co-brand shelf has been thin, that is the story — more than
any single number in it.

## The two cards

| | **Visa Infinite** | **Visa Signature** |
|---|---|---|
| Annual fee | **AED 2,500** | **AED 499** |
| Welcome points | **200,000** | **50,000** |
| Bonvoy hotel spend | 8 pts / US$1 | 3 pts / US$1 |
| International spend | up to 4 pts / US$1 | up to 2 pts / US$1 |
| UAE spend | up to 3 pts / US$1 | up to 1.5 pts / US$1 |
| Elite status | Gold | Silver |
| Status pathway | Platinum at US$25,000/yr | Gold at US$15,000/yr |
| Elite Night Credits | 15/year | 10/year |
| Anniversary award | Free Night Award valued at 85,000 pts | 35,000 pts |

Both carry a **3.75% monthly profit rate (45.63% APR)** per ADIB's Key
Facts Statement, and ATM cash withdrawals cost 3% with a **AED 99**
minimum.

## What the welcome offer actually requires

The Infinite's 200,000 points is two separate hurdles, not one:

- **100,000 points** on card issuance plus a first retail transaction
  within **30 days**.
- **A further 100,000 points** for **US$15,000** of retail spend in the
  first **90 days** — about **AED 55,100** at the pegged rate.

At our ratified Bonvoy baseline of **2.5 fils per point** (AED 25 per
1,000), the full 200,000 is **AED 5,000** of value.

Read the halves separately, because they behave differently:

- The **first 100,000 is AED 2,500** — it covers the first year's fee
  exactly, for one transaction. That half is close to free money.
- The **second 100,000 is also AED 2,500**, but it costs AED 55,100 of
  spend inside 90 days to unlock. On UAE spend at 3 pts/US$1 you would
  also earn roughly 45,000 points along the way, so the tranche is worth
  about **AED 3,625 on AED 55,100** — a ~6.6% return. Good, if you were
  going to spend it anyway. If you are manufacturing spend to reach it,
  you are paying for points.

## Year two is where it tightens

The Infinite's renewal benefit is a Free Night Award "valued at 85,000
points". At our 2.5-fils baseline that is **AED 2,125** against an
**AED 2,500** fee — so the certificate alone does not cover the renewal,
and Free Night Awards are capped in value rather than guaranteed to reach
it. Whether year two pays depends on the 8 pts/US$1 at Marriott
properties and on Gold status being worth something to you.

The Signature's arithmetic is gentler: **AED 499** for 50,000 welcome
points (**AED 1,250**) and a 35,000-point anniversary award
(**AED 875**), which does clear the fee on its own.

## What we could not verify

ADIB does not publish an FX margin or a minimum salary for these cards in
the product pages, Key Facts Statement or Schedule of Charges we read on
10 September 2026 — a gap our own scraper has logged repeatedly against
ADIB. **For a card whose best earn rate is on international hotel spend,
the FX margin is the missing number that decides the whole case**, and we
are not going to guess it. Ask for it in writing before you apply.

The Infinite's US$100 property credit applies at The Ritz-Carlton and
St. Regis on a direct booking of two nights or more on a special rate —
about **AED 367**, and only if you were staying at those brands anyway.

We have not added these cards to our

confirmed in writing, and a full review — which is a separate job from
