Emirates Skywards is running two overlapping summer promotions, and
they carry different deadlines: an enhanced-redemption push across its
non-flight channels **until 30 September 2026**, and a Dubai Summer
Surprises raffle that requires **AED 200+ per qualifying transaction**
with Skywards Everyday partners or Skywards Miles Mall UAE **until
30 August 2026**.

## The redemption side — to 30 September

The enhanced rates apply across Skywards Everyday, Skywards Miles
Mall, the Emirates Official Store, Dubai Duty Free and marhaba Meet &
Greet. The headline number is on Skywards Everyday: during the promo,
**350 Skywards Miles convert to AED 10 back** at hundreds of
participating retail, dining and lifestyle partners across the UAE —
Emirates names Careem, Costa Coffee, MMI, BinSina Pharmacy, Al Jaber
Optical, The Body Shop and NARS among them.

Elsewhere: Miles Mall digital gift cards (Amazon AE, Deliveroo,
Shukran, Apple, NIKE) start from **2,000 Skywards Miles**, and
GCC-issued Emirates Skywards Visa credit cardholders get up to 20% off
selected event redemptions on Skywards Exclusives, under the
"Co-Brand Offer" label.

The 350-for-AED-10 rate is the desk's reason to cover this. It works
out to roughly **2.86 fils per Skywards Mile** — above the 2-fil
cost-basis baseline we use on the
[Skywards programme page](/airlines/skywards/) and in
[our valuations](/valuations/). Cashback conversions usually undercut
that baseline, which is why our standing guidance is to avoid them.
While this promo runs, the arithmetic flips: Everyday cashback is an
honest floor-value redemption for Miles you would otherwise let
expire.

## The DSS raffle — to 30 August

Each qualifying transaction of AED 200 or more with Skywards Everyday
partners or Skywards Miles Mall UAE earns a raffle entry. The pot is
**5,000,000 Skywards Miles, split as 500 winners × 10,000 Miles** — a
prize worth AED 200 per winner at the cost-basis valuation. Treat it
as a small kicker on spend you were making anyway, not a reason to
spend.

Emirates is also promoting up to 1 Skywards Mile per US$1 spent at
Dubai Mall, depending on membership tier (AED 3.6725 to the dollar at
the peg), plus weekly challenges on the Skywards Everyday app.

## What it means for UAE members

If you hold a small Skywards Miles balance that will never reach an
award flight, the window to 30 September is the time to burn it at
2.86 fils rather than the sub-baseline rates that normally apply.
Members with balances earmarked for flight redemptions should do
nothing — no flight award pricing changes here, and 2.86 fils is
still below what a well-chosen flight redemption returns. The raffle
requires no change of behaviour beyond noting the AED 200 transaction
threshold before 30 August.
