[Etihad’s UAE White Friday page](https://www.etihad.com/en-ae/offers/black-friday-deals) says the sale ends on **29 November 2026**. The carrier advertises discounted flights, but a sale banner doesn’t tell you whether your dates and fare are included. We didn’t find a closing time on the campaign page when we checked on 23 November.

## Compare the fare you would actually buy

Enter the UAE departure airport and the dates you want. Compare the full amount in AED with another flight you would genuinely consider. Match the cabin and add the baggage you need before comparing totals. Etihad’s [fare guide](https://www.etihad.com/en-ae/help/faq/fares) sets out separate conditions by fare type, so read the rules for the fare shown at checkout if you may need to change plans.

Check both legs on a return itinerary. The cheapest result on the first screen isn’t necessarily the cheapest ticket once you add a checked bag or choose a fare you can change.
