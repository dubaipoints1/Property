**The extra Calgary flights are for October 2027, not this winter.** Etihad said in its [16 November announcement](https://www.etihad.com/en/news/from-four-to-seven-etihad-announces-daily-calgary-flights-just-three-weeks-after-launch) that it plans to expand its non-stop Abu Dhabi–Calgary service from four flights a week to daily from 15 October 2027.

## Check both legs before you book

If your trip starts before the daily schedule and ends after it, search each direction on its own dates. The new timetable also shows the flight back from Calgary reaching Abu Dhabi the following day. Use that arrival date for airport pick-up or a separate onward flight, rather than the date printed against the departure from Canada.

Use [Etihad’s UAE route page](https://www.etihad.com/en-ae/flights/flights-from-abu-dhabi-to-calgary) to check what the airline is selling for your dates. A planned daily schedule doesn’t tell you what you’ll pay or whether an Etihad Guest reward seat will be available. DubaiPoints checked the published plan, not ticket or reward inventory.
