**If you’re paying for Premium Economy on a winter trip from Dubai, check the cabin on the way back as well as the way out.** In its [10 November winter update](https://www.emirates.com/media-centre/emirates-ramps-up-premium-economy-across-its-european-network-and-offers-more-ways-to-travel-to-popular-winter-sun-destinations/), Emirates said the cabin was available on flights to 31 of its 41 destinations across Europe and the UK. That is a count of destinations, not a promise about every flight.

Paris is a useful example. Emirates [announced a retrofitted A380 on EK071 and EK072](https://www.emirates.com/media-centre/emirates-deploys-premium-economy-to-paris-offering-next-generation-cabins-across-france/) in September, with the cabin due from 25 October. Start there when [checking your dates](https://www.emirates.com/english/destinations/dxb/cdg/flights-from-dubai-to-paris/), then read the cabin against each flight in the itinerary.

## What it means if you’re using Skywards Miles

[Emirates offers Premium Economy Classic Rewards and upgrades](https://www.emirates.com/media-centre/emirates-skywards-introduces-flight-rewards-in-premium-economy/) where available. They have [separate allocations](https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/help/faq-topics/emirates-skywards/faq/what-is-emirates-skywards/): availability for one does not guarantee the other. Check the cash charges on the reward you find and the rules of the cash fare before relying on an upgrade. DubaiPoints checked the published network and programme terms, not seats on sale.
