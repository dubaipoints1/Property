The biggest headline number in the UAE salary-transfer market — Al
Hilal Bank's **"up to AED 15,000"** welcome campaign — **ended on
4 August 2026**. You would not know it from the
[campaign page](https://www.alhilalbank.ae/en/salary-transfer-campaign),
which we fetched on 22 August: it still states "The campaign will be
valid from 4th May 2026 till 04th August 2026" in the terms, carries no
expiry banner, and — eighteen days after closing — **still ranks #1**
in organic search for the salary-transfer query set.

That combination is a trap. The validity dates are there if you read
the terms, but the page presents as a live offer, and a reader who
transfers a salary today on the strength of it earns **AED 0**.

## What the campaign paid, for the record

Since the page will keep surfacing in search results, here is the
structure as published — dated, expired, not enrollable:

- **Salary-transfer component:** 10% of your monthly salary as a
  welcome bonus, capped at **AED 5,000**. A transfer of AED 60,000 or
  more earned an additional AED 5,000.
- **Card-spend tiers:** an extra **AED 500 / 1,200 / 2,000 / 3,000**
  at AED 15,000 / 25,000 / 40,000 / 55,000 of card spend respectively.
- **Finance kickers:** further bonuses attached to taking personal
  finance and home finance products, stacking toward the AED 15,000
  headline.

The full AED 15,000 was never a salary-transfer reward — it was a
salary-plus-heavy-card-spend-plus-two-finance-products reward. But
even the honest AED 5,000–10,000 middle of it is gone.

## Why we are filing this

Reader protection, plainly. An expired campaign page that outranks
every live offer is a market-level misdirection, whoever is at fault
for it, and "the terms said so if you scrolled" is not much comfort
after your employer has processed a salary-transfer letter. Salary
transfers are the slowest financial product to unwind — HR paperwork,
a payroll cycle, often a new account — so the cost of acting on a dead
page is measured in months, not clicks.

Al Hilal (an ADCB Group digital-first Islamic bank) may well launch a
successor; banks that advertise this loudly rarely go quiet for good.
If it does, we will file. Until then, treat any Al Hilal salary
headline you find in search as expired unless the terms show a window
that includes today's date.
