If you’re planning a short break on Palm Jumeirah before Christmas, compare [Six Senses The Palm, Dubai’s Discover package](https://www.sixsenses.com/en/hotels-resorts/middle-east-africa/united-arab-emirates/the-palm-dubai/offers/discover-palm-dubai) with a room-only stay. The resort lists **23 December 2026** as the last booking date and **24 December** as the end of the stay period, subject to availability and a two-night minimum. Check that your whole stay fits inside the window.

The package includes daily breakfast, a wellness screening and an Earth Lab activity. It lists a signature massage and a biohacking session for each of two adults. The resort says to book included wellness activities in advance, so check the slots you want before committing.

Compare the total for the same suite and dates, including taxes and charges. Only count treatments you’d otherwise book. The resort requires a full deposit 48 hours before arrival and says cancellation less than three days before arrival costs the full stay. Check the deadline on your own reservation before paying.
