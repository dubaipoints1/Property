**Minor Hotels** runs Anantara, Avani, NH, nhow, Tivoli and several other
brands. Its stays earn **Avios**, and
[Head for Points reports](https://www.headforpoints.com/2026/09/24/earn-avios-with-minor-hotels/)
that the earning rate is **doubled until 31 October 2026**.

For UAE residents, the key point is where that applies. Iberia's own
partner page says Minor is "a valid partner for collecting Avios across all
its hotel brands in **Europe and America**." So Anantara's hotels in the
UAE do not earn Avios. The offer is only useful on a trip to Europe or the
Americas.

## What you earn

Per Head for Points, Minor stays earn **200 to 450 Avios a night**,
depending on the hotel. With the double offer that becomes **up to 900 Avios
a night** until 31 October. Iberia's page confirms the partnership and the
regions, but it does not show the rates or the promotion, so those figures
rest on Head for Points.

## How to collect

Head for Points says the Avios go only to an **Iberia Club** account. You
give your Iberia Club number at the hotel, and a British Airways Club
number is not accepted. It adds that you can then move the Avios to British
Airways with Iberia's "Move Avios" tool.

Neither source says anything about Qatar Privilege Club, which is the
Avios account many UAE residents use. We could not confirm that Avios
earned this way can be moved to a Qatar account, so do not assume it.

## Worth doing?

Only if you are staying at a Minor hotel in Europe or the Americas before
31 October and already collect Avios with Iberia or British Airways. Head
for Points notes that Minor is also part of the GHA DISCOVERY scheme, which
it says may be worth more than the Avios. Compare the two before you
choose.
