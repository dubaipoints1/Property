If you have an Anantara stay in Abu Dhabi or Dubai coming up, this offer
does not cover it. It is worth knowing about for a trip to Europe or the
Americas.

**Minor Hotels** runs Anantara, Avani, NH, nhow, Tivoli and several other
brands. Its stays earn **Avios** through Iberia's loyalty programme, and
[Head for Points reports](https://www.headforpoints.com/2026/09/24/earn-avios-with-minor-hotels/)
that the rate is **doubled until 31 October 2026**.

## Where it applies

Iberia's own partner page says Minor is "a valid partner for collecting
Avios across all its hotel brands in **Europe and America**." On Iberia's
wording, Anantara's UAE hotels are not covered. Head for Points puts it
more bluntly: only hotels in Europe and the Americas earn, and Minor's
hotels in Asia and Australasia are excluded.

## What you earn

Per Head for Points, a night at a Minor hotel earns **200 to 450 Avios**,
depending on the hotel, and up to **900** with the double offer.

Those figures rest on Head for Points. Its link to Iberia for the rates
leads to the same Iberia partner page we read, which shows no rates. And
neither Iberia's page nor Minor's own offers page, both read on 27
September, mentions the double-Avios offer. We have not been able to
confirm it with either company. Head for Points also does not say whether
31 October is the last date to stay or to book.

## How to collect

Head for Points says the Avios go only to an **Iberia Club** account: you
give your Iberia Club number at the hotel, and British Airways Club
numbers are not accepted. From Iberia Club, it says, you can move the
Avios to British Airways with the "Move Avios" feature online.

Many UAE residents keep their Avios with **Qatar Privilege Club**. Neither
Iberia's page nor Head for Points' article mentions Qatar, and we could not
confirm that Avios earned this way can be moved there. Do not assume they
can.

## Worth doing?

Only with a Minor stay in Europe or the Americas while the offer runs, and
only if you already collect Avios with Iberia or British Airways. Minor
also belongs to the GHA DISCOVERY scheme; Head for Points says it "may be
more valuable than earning Avios". Price both before you give a number at
check-in.
