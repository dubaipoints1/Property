If you were already considering a new SHARE Visa Covered Card from Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank (ADIB), read the [bank’s digital campaign terms](https://www.adib.ae/-/media/project/adib/adibsite/docs/offer/2026/nov/share-digital-offer-tcs.pdf) before counting on the coffee machine. The updated terms set the qualifying spend at **AED 20,000** for applications from 25 November, the day the campaign began.

Apply through the ADIB or SHARE mobile app during the campaign. ADIB must approve the application by **31 December 2026** and issue the card by **15 January 2027**. Eligible retail purchases must **post within 60 days of issuance**, not 60 days from approval. Existing holders of this card and customers who closed or cancelled it in the 12 months before the campaign and reapply during it are excluded. Refunds can take you back below the threshold.

The listed reward is a Nespresso CitiZ D113 for the primary cardholder, subject to availability and possible substitution. It isn’t cash or points. Wait for Majid Al Futtaim or its fulfilment partner to tell you which Carrefour store to visit. Bring your SHARE membership number and a valid Emirates ID. The terms give you **60 days from the first successful notification** to collect it; that’s separate from the spending window.

Judge the card on its fees and ongoing benefits first. If your normal eligible spending won’t reach the threshold, don’t spend extra for the machine.
