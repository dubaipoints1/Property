If you already have a Hyatt stay in mind before year-end, check whether it can earn an extra credit towards Hyatt status. Hyatt announced the offer on 17 November as part of its new partnership with Delta. You do not need to fly Delta or already hold elite status for this particular offer.

Link your World of Hyatt and Delta SkyMiles accounts, then [register for the offer](https://world.hyatt.com/content/gp/en/offers/delta-tier-qualifying-night-credit.html). Choose Hyatt Base Points rather than airline miles as your base earning and complete your first qualifying stay after registration **by 31 December 2026**. An eligible paid rate or a free-night award can qualify. Add your Hyatt membership number to the booking or give it to the hotel before checkout.

This is **one extra qualifying-night credit, not a free night**. Only the room you occupy counts, and there is a maximum of one extra credit under the offer. The terms cover participating properties worldwide; check your UAE hotel and rate before you rely on it. If you were going to book anyway, it is worth registering. Don’t pay for a stay just for the credit unless you know which status or Milestone Reward it would help you reach.
