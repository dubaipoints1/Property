**Etihad Guest** is adding **bonus miles of up to 40%** to points converted
from its partner programmes, on transfers made between **1 and 31 October
2026**, per the offer page on etihad.com. The bigger the transfer, the higher
the rate.

## The tiers

Etihad sets the bonus by how many Etihad Guest Miles the transfer is worth:

| Transfer worth | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Up to 9,999 miles | **10%** |
| 10,000 to 49,999 miles | **20%** |
| 50,000 miles or more | **40%** |

A transfer worth 50,000 miles therefore earns 20,000 bonus miles. Etihad's
terms describe each tier by the size of a transfer. They do not say whether
separate transfers are added together, so if you are aiming for the 40% rate,
make it one transfer of 50,000 miles or more.

## Which partners count

The participating partners on Etihad's page include these UAE banks and
programmes:

- **Emirates NBD** (UAE), **First Abu Dhabi Bank**, **Dubai Islamic Bank**,
  **Emirates Islamic**, **HSBC**, **Mashreq** and **RAKBANK**
- **Ajman Bank**, **DEEM Finance** and **Smiles by e&**

Two hotel programmes many residents hold are on the list too: **ALL – Accor
Live Limitless** and **Shangri-La Circle**. The rest of the list is
**Revolut** and banks and programmes elsewhere, including Saudi Arabia,
Kuwait, Bahrain, Egypt, India, China and Brazil. Each partner's usual transfer ratio
and minimum still apply. The bonus is added on top.

## The rules that matter

- **The window is UAE time.** Transfers count from 00:00 on 1 October to
  23:59 on 31 October 2026, UTC+4.
- **No undo.** Etihad's terms say points transfers cannot be reversed.
- **The bonus arrives later.** Etihad says the bonus miles will reach your
  account within 30 business days of the end of the campaign, so expect them
  some weeks after 31 October, not straight away.
- **One offer at a time.** The bonus is not valid with any other offer, and
  Etihad can change or withdraw it at any time.
- **Get your number right.** You need a valid Etihad Guest number, and the
  terms make entering it correctly your responsibility.

## Should you transfer?

Only for a redemption you can already see. A 40% bonus makes a transfer
cheaper, but it does not make an unplanned one worthwhile. The miles sit in
Etihad Guest once they move, and bank points can no longer go anywhere else.

Two checks before you transfer:

1. **Find the award first.** Search Etihad Guest availability for your dates,
   then work out how many miles you need with the bonus included.
2. **Compare with Qatar.** Several of the same banks are also on Qatar
   Airways' October offer, which pays up to 35% bonus Avios
   [until 31 October](/news/qatar-avios-transfer-bonus-uae-banks-31-october/).
   If your points can go to either programme, compare the cost of the
   redemption in each before choosing.

Our [Etihad Guest programme page](/airlines/etihad-guest/) covers how we value
the miles.
