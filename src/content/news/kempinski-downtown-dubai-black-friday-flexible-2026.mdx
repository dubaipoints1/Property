[Kempinski The Boulevard Dubai](https://www.kempinski.com/en/the-boulevard-dubai/overview/offers/black-friday-offer) and [Kempinski Central Avenue Dubai](https://www.kempinski.com/en/central-avenue-dubai/overview/offers/black-friday-offer) both list Black Friday offers for bookings through **2 December 2026**. Each advertises a discount off its best available room-only rate. Meals are not included. The published stay window runs through **31 December 2027**, but both hotels exclude **26 December 2026 to 3 January 2027** and list further blackout dates in 2027.

Both property pages list free cancellation up to **24 hours before arrival**. Check the precise deadline and any deposit shown for the rate you select; the offer pages don’t establish the payment timing for your particular booking.

Compare the two hotels for the same dates and number of guests. Check the total before paying: applicable taxes, service charge and Tourism Dirham still apply. Both offers are subject to availability and can’t be combined with other offers. A quote at either hotel tells you nothing about the price or availability at the other.
