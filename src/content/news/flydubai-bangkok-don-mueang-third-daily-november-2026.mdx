[flydubai said on 25 November](https://news.flydubai.com/flydubai-ramps-up-bangkok-operations-with-third-daily-flight-amid-strong-demand) that it will add a third daily Dubai–Bangkok service on **27 November 2026**. All three flights are scheduled to depart from Terminal 3 at Dubai International (DXB) for Don Mueang (DMK).

## Check the airport code and arrival date

Don Mueang is not Suvarnabhumi (BKK). If a separate onward ticket leaves from BKK, plan for a transfer across Bangkok. [Airports of Thailand lists an airport shuttle](https://donmueang.airportthai.co.th/service/transportation/detail/1093) and sets its own eligibility rules. Compare the airport codes before you buy either ticket.

The airline’s schedule includes a late-evening Dubai departure that arrives in Bangkok the following morning. If you choose it, use the arrival date, not the departure date, for the hotel or onward flight. Check the date and terminal again on the ticket you actually buy.
