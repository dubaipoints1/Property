If your Maldives trip doesn't end in Malé, you have another way to use [Etihad Guest miles](/airlines/etihad-guest/). In its [27 November announcement](https://www.etihad.com/en-sg/news/etihad-guest-launches-redemption-partnership-with-maldivian), Etihad said members can now redeem miles for eligible flights operated by Maldivian, including onward flights from Malé to other islands. In [September](https://www.etihad.com/en-sa/news/etihad-guest-to-launch-partnership-with-maldivian) it had only said the option was coming.

Start with your accommodation, not the miles balance. Ask which airport to use and what transfer you’ll still need after landing. [Visit Maldives](https://visitmaldives.com/en/maldives/travel-information) lists speedboats, seaplanes and domestic flights as different transfer options. A seat on an island flight does not tell you what the final leg to the resort will cost.

Before transferring bank points or buying miles, check award availability in both directions and ask for the total in miles and cash. Etihad’s [general partner-booking FAQ](https://www.etihad.com/en/help/faq/etihad-guest) lists online and phone bookings, but the Maldivian launch announcement does not specify the booking channel or publish a price table for this partner. Don’t assume the Abu Dhabi–Malé flight and your island award will be issued on one ticket: check before committing, especially if baggage and a tight connection matter.
