[Air Arabia Egypt](https://press.airarabia.com/air-arabia-egypt-launches-new-non-stop-flights-from-cairo-to-al-ain-in-the-uae/) announced on 19 November that it plans to start nonstop flights between Cairo International Airport and Tahnoun International Airport in Al Ain from 2 December. The airline says bookings are open. If you’re travelling from Al Ain, search for **AAN**, not Abu Dhabi’s **AUH**. [Abu Dhabi Airports](https://www.adairports.ae/en/Partners/Airlines) lists them as separate airports.

## Check the date on the Al Ain leg

The published timetable has Cairo departures at **18:10 on Wednesdays and Saturdays**, arriving in Al Ain at **23:35**. Flights from Al Ain are scheduled for **00:25 on Thursdays and Sundays**, arriving in Cairo at **02:45**. All times are local.

If you book the Thursday 00:25 flight, plan to go to the airport on **Wednesday night**, not Thursday night. The same applies to Sunday’s flight: that means Saturday night at the airport. Check both dates and what your fare includes for baggage before paying.
