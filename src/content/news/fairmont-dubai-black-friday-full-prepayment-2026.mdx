If you’re planning at least two nights near Dubai World Trade Centre, [Fairmont Dubai’s Black Friday rate](https://www.fairmont.com/en/hotels/dubai/fairmont-dubai/offers/Fairmont-Dubai-Black-Friday.html) is worth comparing with a flexible booking. The catch: the hotel takes the full cost upfront and keeps it if you cancel.

This is the [Fairmont on Sheikh Zayed Road](https://www.fairmont.com/en/hotels/dubai/fairmont-dubai.html). Its offer lists 1 December 2026 as the last booking date, for stays from 28 November 2026 to 31 May 2027. You need at least two consecutive nights. The discount applies to the room rate, so compare what you would actually pay rather than assuming the same saving on the full bill.

Pull up the same room, dates and guest count at a flexible rate. Compare the final totals and read the cancellation deadline for the flexible booking. If your plans might change, decide whether the saving is worth giving up the option to cancel. We checked the published terms, not availability or prices for a specific stay.
