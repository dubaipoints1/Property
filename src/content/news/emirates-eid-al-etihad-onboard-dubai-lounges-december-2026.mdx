If you’re flying Emirates out of Dubai on 2 or 3 December, you don’t need to change cabins for the Eid Al Etihad sweets. In its [26 November announcement](https://www.emirates.com/media-centre/emirates-celebrates-the-uaes-55th-eid-al-etihad-with-a-taste-of-tradition/), Emirates said it would serve them in every cabin on its Dubai departures on those dates. The airline also included selected return flights from the Gulf and Middle East but didn’t name the individual flights. Don’t assume your return is included.

Emirates lists the special Dubai lounge menu for 2 December. That’s a menu change, not a new lounge benefit: check [Emirates’ usual access rules](https://www.emirates.com/english/experience/our-lounges/paid-lounge-access/) before making plans around it. On board, look for the Eid Al Etihad collection under ‘Dubai & The UAE’ on ice.
