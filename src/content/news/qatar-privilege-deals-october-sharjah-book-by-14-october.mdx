Qatar Airways Privilege Club has a new batch of **Privilege Deals**, its
monthly discount on Avios award flights. Qatar's own offers page promises
savings of **up to 25% on award flights**, for bookings made **by 14 October
2026** for travel **by 30 November 2026**.

This month's list includes a UAE airport. [Head for Points](https://www.headforpoints.com/2026/10/08/qatar-airways-october-avios-discount-deals/)
reported the October routes on 8 October, and **Sharjah** is on its economy
list.

## What Qatar confirms, and what it doesn't

When we read Qatar's page on 8 October, it confirmed the "up to 25%" figure
and both dates. Its route lists did not load. **The routes below come from
Head for Points, not from Qatar.** Check the booking screen, which shows
Privilege Deals prices directly, before you rely on them.

## The October routes, per Head for Points

Head for Points says the discount applies when your trip **starts or ends**
in one of the listed cities, so the other end can be anywhere Qatar flies
via Doha.

- **Economy:** Casablanca, Dammam, Entebbe, Istanbul, Muscat, Nice,
  **Sharjah**, Singapore and Stockholm.
- **Business:** Amman, Ankara, Bucharest, Jeddah, Medina, Salalah, Tunis,
  Warsaw, Yerevan and Zanzibar.

## Why it matters from the UAE

- **Sharjah starts count, in economy.** Under the rule as Head for Points
  describes it, an economy award that begins in Sharjah, with any destination
  beyond Doha, should price at the discounted rate. Dubai and Abu Dhabi are
  not on either list.
- **Business to Jeddah, Medina, Amman or Salalah.** These cities qualify as
  the end of a business-class trip, so a business award from the UAE to one
  of them via Doha should also be discounted. For residents planning Umrah,
  Jeddah and Medina are the useful ones.

## The small print

Per Head for Points:

- The discount does not apply to Qatar's double-price "Flexi" award seats.
- It covers flights operated by Qatar Airways only, not partner airlines.
- Privilege Deals cannot be booked through British Airways. You need to
  book on qatarairways.com, moving Avios across first if your balance is
  with BA.

Avios cover the fare only. Taxes and carrier charges are paid on top, and
Qatar's offers page does not show them. Price the full award on the booking
screen against the cash fare for your dates before committing.
