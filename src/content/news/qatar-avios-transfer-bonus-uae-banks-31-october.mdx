Qatar Airways Privilege Club is paying a **bonus of up to 35% in Avios**
on reward points converted from its partners **by 31 October 2026**, per
the offer page on qatarairways.com. The bonus is tiered by the size of
**a single transaction**, so one large transfer earns more than several
small ones.

**Register-first rules:** none are stated on the offer page, and Head
for Points' five-rule summary of the terms lists no registration step
either. The page says only that terms and conditions apply; the terms
themselves did not load when we read it, so the posting date below comes
from a secondary source and is marked as such.

## The tiers

Per Qatar Airways, the bonus depends on how many Avios one conversion
produces:

| Avios from one transaction | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Up to 49,999 | **15%** |
| 50,000 to 99,999 | **25%** |
| 100,000 or more | **35%** |

Converting 100,000 Avios' worth of partner points in one go would arrive
as 135,000 Avios. The same points split across two 50,000 transfers would
earn 25% each, or 125,000 Avios.

## The UAE partners

The offer page lists eligible regions for each partner. Four UAE
programmes qualify:

- **Emirates NBD** (United Arab Emirates)
- **Dubai Islamic Bank** (United Arab Emirates)
- **HSBC** (Qatar and United Arab Emirates)
- **Mashreq Vantage** (United Arab Emirates)

Global hotel programmes on the list: **ALL Accor**, **IHG Hotels &
Resorts**, **Shangri-La Circle** and **World of Hyatt**. **Marriott
Bonvoy is not on the list**, which matters because Bonvoy is the hotel
currency most UAE readers hold and the one with a published Qatar
conversion rate in our [transfer-ratios guide](/guides/uae-transfer-ratios-2026/).

We have not published the conversion rates from the four UAE bank
programmes into Qatar Avios, and the offer page does not state them.
Check the rate inside your bank's rewards portal before converting; the
bonus is applied to the Avios that land, not to the bank points you
spend.

## Who should care

- **Readers with a large Emirates NBD, DIB, HSBC UAE or Mashreq Vantage
  balance** who were going to move it to Avios anyway. Waiting to
  convert in one transaction, rather than in instalments, is the whole
  game here.
- **Readers planning a Qsuite redemption.** The
  [Qatar Privilege Club hub](/airlines/qatar-privilege-club/) sets out the
  Business-class sweet spots from Doha; a 35% uplift on a 100,000-Avios
  conversion is a meaningful part of a long-haul Business ticket.
- **Almost nobody holding only hotel points.** Head for Points, which
  flagged the offer on 3 October, argues that hotel points are usually
  worth more as hotel nights than as Avios even with the bonus, and that
  Accor points still do better moved to Iberia than to Qatar. We have
  not re-derived that comparison, and HfP's own Accor-to-Qatar rate is
  stated two ways in its article, so treat it as their view and check the
  rate in your Accor account.

## What we could not confirm

Per Head for Points, bonus Avios post **by 30 November 2026**, and the
qualifying date is "the transaction date notified by the relevant
Participating Partner" (HfP quoting Qatar's terms), so a conversion
started on 31 October may not count. Qatar's own terms did not load for
us, so we have not confirmed either point from the issuer.
