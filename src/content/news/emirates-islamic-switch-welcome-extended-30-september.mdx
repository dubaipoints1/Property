Emirates Islamic has quietly extended the welcome offer on the
**Switch Cashback Credit Card**: the published validity now runs to
**30 September 2026**. We caught the new date in our July re-verification
of the card page — no announcement, just a changed line in the terms.

The mechanics are unchanged. New cardholders earn **AED 500 welcome
cashback** after **AED 15,000 of retail spend within 60 days of card
approval**. That is a real spend gate — AED 7,500 a month for two months —
so the welcome pays out for people whose card would carry that load
anyway, not as free money for a wallet-drawer card.

The card's standing economics are what they were when we
[reviewed it](/cards/emirates-islamic-switch-cashback/): **8% cashback on
fuel**, a **2.54% foreign-currency fee** (confirmed against the Schedule
of Charges), and an **AED 313.95 annual fee from year two**. The welcome
covers most of two years of fees if you trigger it.

## Who should care

If you were already considering the Switch for its fuel rate, the
extension removes the deadline pressure that existed when the offer was
due to lapse mid-summer — there are now two more months to plan an
application around the 60-day spend window. If your monthly retail spend
sits well under AED 7,500, the welcome should not be the reason you
apply; judge the card on its category rates alone.

As always: the bank can change or withdraw the offer before the published
