**Marriott Bonvoy** is testing a feature its elite members have asked for
for years: a screen in the app that shows **where you stand in line for a
complimentary room upgrade**. For now it is a beta at a small number of
hotels. The next step reported is the **US and Canada**. Nothing reported so
far puts a date on the UAE.

Marriott announced it after its Members Day event in Bethesda,
[The Points Guy reported on 12 October](https://thepointsguy.com/hotel/marriott-bonvoy-complimentary-upgrade-list-new-lounge-experience/).
We could not find a Marriott press release on the upgrade list; its news
centre gave our reader no article text. Everything below comes from three
reports, credited as we go.

## What the screen shows

The screen is called **"Your Complimentary Upgrade Status"** and appears on
the reservation shortly before arrival, per
[Live From A Lounge](https://livefromalounge.com/marriott-bonvoy-to-roll-out-an-upgrade-list-on-its-app-letting-members-see-their-estimated-chances-of-an-upgrade/)
and [Milesopedia](https://milesopedia.com/en/news/hospitality/marriott-bonvoy-upgrade-waitlist/).
It gives two numbers:

- **Estimated upgrades available** for your stay, counting only rooms that
  match your dates and booked bed type.
- **Your current position in line** among eligible members.

The app says it will tell you **one day before arrival** if an upgrade is
confirmed. Both reports stress that your position can change and the count
is an estimate, not a promise. In the screenshot Milesopedia describes, the
hotel showed 0 upgrades available and the member was second in line.

**Nightly Upgrade Awards go first.** Rooms set aside for members using these
certificates are not counted in the estimate. Milesopedia says a member
applying one moves ahead of everyone on the complimentary list.

The Points Guy says the feature is for members with **Gold status and
above**.

## Where and when

- **Now:** a beta at a small number of hotels. Milesopedia says members first
  spotted it on 11 October.
- **By November:** Marriott says it will expand to **more than 250 hotels in
  the US and Canada**, according to The Points Guy. Live From A Lounge puts
  it at "early November 2026".
- **Everywhere else:** Live From A Lounge says Marriott has not announced a
  wider international rollout.

The reports do not fully agree on how official this is. The Points Guy
describes it as a Marriott announcement; Milesopedia says Marriott has made
no official announcement.

## For UAE members

On current reporting, a stay at a Marriott hotel in Dubai or Abu Dhabi is
unlikely to show the new screen: no UAE hotel is named in the beta or the
November expansion. Where it is useful is a trip to North America: if
you hold Gold status or higher and have a stay at a US or Canadian hotel
from November, look for the screen on the reservation in the app a few days
before you arrive.

The same Members Day brought a second announcement that also skips this
region. Marriott will launch a **Marriott Bonvoy Lounge** at 100
premium-level hotels in Asia-Pacific from February 2027, with 220 more in Greater
China in early 2027, per The Points Guy. No Middle East hotels are named.

If an upgrade on a particular stay really matters, a Nightly Upgrade Award
remains the more reliable route. Our [Marriott Bonvoy hub](/hotels/marriott-bonvoy/)
covers how elite benefits work.
