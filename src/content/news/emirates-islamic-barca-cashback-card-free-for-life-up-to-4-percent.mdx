**Emirates Islamic** has an offer page up for a **Barça Cashback** covered
card, co-branded with FC Barcelona. Our offers monitor picked it up on the
bank's offers list on 9 November 2026, and we do not yet have a review of
the card.

## What Emirates Islamic says

All of this is from the bank's own offer page, read on 10 November 2026.

| | |
|---|---|
| Annual fee | **None.** The page calls it a "free-for-life card with no annual fees" |
| Cashback | **Up to 4% a month** on "key lifestyle spends including sports, fuel, supermarket, and dining" |
| Club perks | FC Barcelona giveaways such as match tickets and fan experiences |
| Launch draw | Spend **AED 2,000 or more between 22 October and 22 December 2026** for the chance to be one of **4 winners** of 2 VIP tickets to a Barça match |

The bank notes that "credit card" here means a covered card, structured under
Shariah principles.

## What the page does not say

"Up to 4%" is a ceiling, and the page gives none of the detail that decides
what you would really earn:

- the cashback rate for each category
- any monthly cap or minimum spend
- the minimum salary to apply
- the foreign-currency fee

Those figures will be in the card's Key Facts Statement, which the page
links to. We have not read it yet, so we are not publishing a rate table or
a verdict.

## What it means for UAE cardholders

- **A no-fee cashback card is worth a look,** but compare it on the capped,
  per-category rates, not the headline. Our
  [card finder](/cards/finder/?prefer=cashback) ranks the cashback cards we
  have already checked.
- **The ticket offer is a prize draw.** Four winners in total, so do not
  apply for the tickets.
- **Already with Emirates Islamic?** Its
  [Switch Cashback card](/cards/emirates-islamic-switch-cashback/) is the
  bank's existing cashback card that we have reviewed.

We will add the card to the site once we have read its Key Facts Statement.
