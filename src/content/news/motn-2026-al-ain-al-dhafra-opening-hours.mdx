If you’re planning to visit the Mother of the Nation Festival over Eid Al Etihad, choose the venue before buying a ticket. The [organiser’s schedule](https://www.motn.ae/about26) puts Al Ain and Al Dhafra first, from **28 November to 2 December 2026**. The Abu Dhabi Corniche edition doesn’t start until **9 December**.

## Choose the park or the bay

- **Al Ain:** the [Al Jahili Park edition](https://www.motn.ae/ar/homepagealain26) lists daily hours from **4 pm to midnight**.
- **Al Dhafra:** the [Al Mugheirah Bay edition](https://www.motn.ae/homepagealdhafra26) lists **4 pm to 10 pm**. If you’re coming after dinner, don’t plan around Al Ain’s later close.

Those are festival hours, not confirmed last admission. Open the venue’s own listing, check the date and ticket type before paying, and look at what the ticket includes if there’s a particular attraction you want. We haven’t checked live ticket stock, prices or parking at either regional venue.

The [Abu Dhabi Media Office announcement](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/tourism/organised-by-department-of-culture-and-tourism-abu-dhabi-10th-motn-festival-to-take-place-across-emirate/) gives the Corniche run as **9 December 2026 to 3 January 2027**. If that’s the one you want, don’t buy for the earlier opening weekend. We checked the organiser’s schedule on 22 November.
