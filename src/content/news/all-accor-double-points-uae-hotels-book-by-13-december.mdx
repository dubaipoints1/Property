A week after its [triple-points offer](/news/all-accor-triple-points-uae-hotels-book-by-25-october/)
closed, **ALL Accor** has opened another. Called the "2X Stay ALL Accor
Festive Offer 2026", it pays **double Reward points** on stays of **two
nights or more**, and on **dining**, at participating hotels in the UAE and
about fifty other countries across the Middle East, Africa and
Asia-Pacific.

The dates that matter:

- **Register and book** between **27 October and 13 December 2026**
  (11:59 p.m. Central European Time).
- **Stay** any time up to **31 March 2027**.
- **Dine** and pay between 27 October and **13 December 2026**.

Everything below comes from Accor's own
[offer page and conditions of sale](https://all.accor.com/a/en/loyalty-program/promotions-offers/meapac-bonus-points-offer.html)
(offer reference 20330) and its
[participating-hotel list](https://all.accor.com/a/content/dam/all/hubs/meapac/offers-campaigns/2026/2026-10/2x-stay---dine/2X_Stay_dine_Hotellist.pdf),
read on 31 October. [LoyaltyLobby reported the offer first](https://loyaltylobby.com/2026/10/31/all-accor-double-points-for-stays-at-select-brands-in-asia-pacific-middle-east-africa-through-march-31-2027-book-by-december-13/).

## Which UAE hotels

By our count, Accor's list carries **46 UAE hotels**: 36 in Dubai, 5 in
Abu Dhabi, 3 in Sharjah and 2 in Ras Al Khaimah. They come from the
mid-range brands: ibis, ibis Styles, Mercure, Grand Mercure, Novotel,
Mövenpick, Pullman and Swissôtel, plus the Queen Elizabeth 2 in Dubai.

As LoyaltyLobby notes, the luxury brands are left out. **Sofitel,
Fairmont, Raffles and Rixos** are not on the list, so this is not the offer
for a Palm or Saadiyat staycation. Search the PDF for your exact hotel
before you book.

## The rules that matter

- **Register first.** Only bookings made after you register count.
- **Two nights minimum**, consecutive, at the same hotel. A booking for
  several rooms counts as one stay.
- **Book direct.** Accor's websites and app, the ALL call centre, the hotel,
  or a travel agent connected to Accor's booking system. Expedia,
  Booking.com and other third-party sites are excluded. Give your ALL number
  when you book.
- **Any status, any country of residence.** The bonus is worked out on the
  points a Classic member would earn.
- **No stacking.** Where a stay qualifies for several offers, you get the
  most generous one only.
- **Dining:** pay the restaurant or bar directly, with your ALL number. A
  bill charged to your room does not count, and nor do orders through
  Talabat, Careem or Deliveroo. The minimum spend is EUR 1.

Accor's worked example: a stay that earns 250 Reward points gets **an
additional 250**. Points are credited within 10 days of checkout.

## What it is worth

Accor redeems **2,000 points for EUR 40** off a stay, so a point is worth
about **8 fils** at our working in the [ALL Accor hub](/hotels/accor-all/).
The bonus simply doubles whatever your stay would earn. On a stay that
earns 500 points, the extra 500 are worth about **AED 40**.

That makes it a reason to book a winter staycation or a Dubai Shopping
Festival weekend at a mid-range Accor hotel directly rather than through a
booking site. It is not a reason to book a stay you did not want.

If you would rather turn the points into airline miles, Emirates Skywards
is paying a 25% bonus on ALL Accor conversions until 30 November; see
[our Skywards story](/news/emirates-skywards-double-miles-hotels-car-rental-accor-bonus-30-november/).
At our baselines the hotel discount is still worth more.
