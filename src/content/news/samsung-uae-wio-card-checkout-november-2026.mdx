Samsung’s [UAE online-store terms](https://images.samsung.com/is/content/samsung/assets/ae/terms-and-conditions/deals/SGE_04.November.2026_E-store_General_Offer.pdf) list a Wio card offer through **30 November 2026** on selected products. The bank-card terms are separate from Samsung’s [Black Friday sale page](https://www.samsung.com/ae/offer/black-friday/). Don’t assume every sale model is included, or that the two prices can be combined.

## Check the payment option and final total

For the card offer, Samsung says to select “Credit or Debit Card”. It excludes instalment plans, digital wallets and Etihad Guest Pay. Samsung lists eligible card identifiers and says the discount applies automatically at checkout when the card and product qualify. Those published card terms do not list a code.

If you are already shopping for a device, compare the same model and configuration at the final price. If the discount isn’t shown, don’t pay expecting a later credit: Samsung describes this as a discount at checkout.
