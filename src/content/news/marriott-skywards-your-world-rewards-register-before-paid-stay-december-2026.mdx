Already paying for a Marriott stay in the UAE by **15 December**? Check whether Marriott addressed its Your World Rewards offer to you. The promotion isn’t automatic for everyone who has linked Bonvoy and Emirates Skywards. If you received the offer, register through [Marriott’s official offer page](https://travel-partner.marriott.com/emirates/en-gb/offer) before you stay. A booking you made before the promotion started can still count if the eligible paid stay falls within the promotion window and you register first.

## Check the rate, not just the hotel

The bonus needs a paid stay that earns Bonvoy points. A points-earning rate alone isn’t enough: Marriott also excludes some properties and bookings, including Marriott Executive Apartments and Homes & Villas by Marriott Bonvoy. Free Night Awards and Cash + Points awards don’t count, and certain prepaid third-party bookings don’t qualify. If you’ve chosen to earn miles rather than points with Bonvoy, the offer doesn’t apply. Consecutive nights at the same property count as one stay, even if you make separate bookings or check out and back in.

Marriott lists **15 December 2026** as the last date in the stay window and gives a separate registration cut-off in US Eastern Standard Time. If your stay crosses the final date, check the terms before relying on the bonus. Register for an eligible stay you already wanted; don’t book extra nights just to chase the offer.
