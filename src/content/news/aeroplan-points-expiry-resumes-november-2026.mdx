[Air Canada says Aeroplan’s expiry pause runs through 29 November](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/aeroplan/news/points-expiry-suspended.html). Its usual inactivity rules apply again from **30 November 2026**. If your points would already have expired without the pause, complete an eligible transaction before that date. The notice does not give a cutoff hour.

This is about your Aeroplan balance, not Emirates Skywards miles. It matters in the UAE because [Air Canada lists eligible earning and redemptions on Emirates-operated flights](https://www.aircanada.com/media/air-canada-and-emirates-to-extend-strategic-partnership-with-major-multi-year-expansion-agreement/).

## Check the inactivity date first

Open the **Activity** tab in your Aeroplan account and look for the points inactivity date. [Aeroplan’s account guide](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/aeroplan/your-aeroplan/inactivity-policy.html) lists earning and redeeming points, including eligible donations, among ways to stay active. It also describes exceptions for some members, so check your own account before doing anything.

If you need an eligible transaction, choose one you can complete in time and check that it appears in your activity. Don’t assume that an unposted transfer or a flight you’ve only booked will count. If your balance is not at risk, there’s no reason to buy points just because the pause is ending.
