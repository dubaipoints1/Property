flydubai has started a **daily non-stop** between **Dubai International
Terminal 3** and **Pokhara (PHH)**, its second destination in Nepal after
Kathmandu. The first flight left DXB on **23 September 2026**. flydubai
says it is the first direct air link between Dubai and Western Nepal. Its release
calls Pokhara the principal gateway to the Annapurna range.

flydubai calls itself the first airline to fly scheduled international
services to Pokhara. The release adds that Pokhara is Nepal's third airport
with scheduled international flights.

## The schedule and the fares

From flydubai's release of 25 September 2026:

| Flight | From | To | Departs | Arrives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FZ 1165 | Dubai (DXB) | Pokhara (PHH) | 12:00 | 18:15 |
| FZ 1166 | Pokhara (PHH) | Dubai (DXB) | 19:15 | 22:00 |

Times are local. Nepal is 1 hour 45 minutes ahead of the UAE, so each
direction is scheduled at about **four and a half hours**.

Fares from Dubai, **return**:

- **Economy** from **AED 1,950**
- **Business** from **AED 5,400**

These are "from" prices. They can change, and flydubai gives no end date
for them.

## What it changes for UAE travellers

flydubai pitches the route as a way to reach Western Nepal without
"multiple transfers or extended road journeys". The midday departure lands in Pokhara at 18:15 local time, the
same day you leave Dubai.

Kathmandu still has four flydubai flights a day. With Pokhara added,
flydubai runs **35 flights a week** to Nepal.

The route is also sold under flydubai's **codeshare with Emirates**. That
means one ticket, with bags checked through, when you connect in Dubai
from the Emirates network.

**Business Class.** The release does not say which aircraft flies the route
or which Business Class seat it carries. The two lead-in fares are AED 3,450
apart. If the seat is why you would pay that, check the seat map on your own
booking first.
