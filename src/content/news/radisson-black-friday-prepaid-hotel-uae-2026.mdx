If you already have dates for a UAE stay, [Radisson’s Black Friday offer](https://www.radissonhotels.com/en-us/hotel-deals/black-friday) is worth checking alongside the hotel’s flexible rate. The main terms give a booking window of 17 November to 2 December 2026 and a stay window of 12 December 2026 to 31 December 2027. The Middle East is included at participating hotels. That is not confirmation that a particular UAE property or date has the offer.

## Compare the same stay before you pay

Start with a property from [Radisson’s UAE directory](https://www.radissonhotels.com/en-us/destination/united-arab-emirates). On Radisson’s website or app, enter your exact dates and guest count and look for the named Black Friday rate. Compare it with a flexible rate for the same room and meal plan. Use the final total, including any taxes and guest charges shown, rather than the discount in the banner.

The promotion requires full payment at booking. Its main terms say you generally cannot cancel or change the reservation after paying and tell you to read the property’s own prepaid cancellation conditions. A lower total can be useful if the trip is fixed; if your dates might move, compare what you could lose with the saving. Don’t cancel an existing reservation until you know what it would cost and can see the new rate for your dates.

*Radisson’s campaign terms and UAE hotel directory were checked on 20 November 2026. DubaiPoints did not verify room inventory or complete a booking.*
