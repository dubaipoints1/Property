[Avani Ibn Battuta Dubai Hotel](https://www.avanihotels.com/en/ibn-battuta-dubai/offers/avani-ibn-battuta-stay-longer-special) lists a Black Friday upgrade when you book its Stay Longer rate by **1 December 2026** for stays through **31 March 2027**. The advertised benefit is the next room category up, capped at a One-Bedroom Suite. A suite is not promised for every booking.

## Compare with the room you would actually book

The hotel lists separate Stay Longer rates for three and five nights. Check the total for the number of nights you need, including taxes and fees, and confirm the upgraded category before paying. Do not add nights just to get the upgrade: compare the package with the same dates on a regular rate, and with the cost of booking the room you want outright.

Avani’s terms require full, non-refundable prepayment when you book. If your dates might move, check the flexible rate before committing. We did not verify availability for any particular night or room category.
