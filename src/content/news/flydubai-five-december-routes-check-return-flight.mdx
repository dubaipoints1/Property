If you’re planning a December trip from Dubai, check the return flight before committing to a hotel. Four of flydubai’s five additions have just two departures a week in the [announced schedule](https://news.flydubai.com/flydubai-adds-five-destinations-to-its-network-from-december).

Atyrau starts on **18 December**, Aktau and Tyumen on **19 December**, and Shymkent resumes on **25 December**. Samarkand returns on **29 December** with three flights a week. The carrier says it has opened bookings.

Atyrau and Aktau are listed from Dubai International **Terminal 3**, while Tyumen is listed from **Terminal 2**. Check the terminal on your own booking, especially if you’re connecting in Dubai.

Emirates will codeshare on all five routes. If you need an onward Emirates flight, search for the whole trip as one itinerary and check where your bag will be tagged. Don’t assume the same baggage arrangements apply if you buy two separate tickets.
