**Hilton Honors** is selling points with a **120% bonus**. Buy 5,000 points
and your account receives 11,000. Hilton's own
[offers page](https://www.hilton.com/en/offers/) confirms the headline:
"Buy Points now and get 120% more". Hilton's purchase page refused our
request on 26 October, so the dates, cap and price below come from the
terms as reproduced by
[LoyaltyLobby](https://loyaltylobby.com/2026/10/07/hilton-honors-buy-points-120-bonus-sale-increased-limit-ocotber-7-november-21-2026/)
and from
[The Points Guy](https://thepointsguy.com/deals/hilton-best-ever-buy-points-promotion/).
We have not read them on Hilton's own site.

## The terms, as reported

- **Deadline: 21 November 2026**, 11:59 p.m. US Eastern time. That is
  8:59 a.m. on 22 November in the UAE. The sale opened on 7 October.
- **Minimum 5,000 points** for the bonus to apply, per both outlets.
- **Cap: 240,000 points a calendar year** before the bonus, which is
  528,000 with it. LoyaltyLobby's own text also mentions 300,000, but the
  terms it quotes say 240,000.
- **Non-refundable.** Points post within 48 hours. Bought points do not
  count towards status.
- **Your offer may differ.** Both outlets say the bonus may be targeted.
  Log in to see your own offer before you assume 120%.

The terms as quoted carry no country restriction. The sale is priced in US
dollars.

## What a point costs in dirhams

TPG puts the normal price at **USD 50 for 5,000 points**, one US cent each.
With the bonus, that buys 11,000 points, or about **0.45 US cents** a
point. At the 3.6725 peg, USD 50 is **AED 183.63**, which works out at
roughly **1.7 fils a point**. Buying the full 240,000 points at that base
price would cost about **USD 2,400 (AED 8,814)** for 528,000 points.

We value Hilton points at
[1.5 fils](/hotels/hilton-honors/). At about 1.7 fils, **you pay more for
each point than we think the average point is worth.** Buying makes sense
only when you have a specific Standard Reward that you have priced in
points, and its cash rate works out above 1.7 fils a point. That usually
means a peak-date room where the cash rate is high. Do not buy to hold.

If you will be staying at Hilton hotels before the end of the year anyway,
the free [More Points to Play promotion](/news/hilton-honors-more-points-to-play-uae-hotels-31-december/)
pays 1,000 or 3,000 bonus points a stay at UAE hotels, with no purchase
needed.
