Citibank UAE has **doubled the joining bonus** on its Cash Back Credit Card.
The welcome statement credit is now **AED 300**, up from AED 150, and the
qualifying condition is unchanged: **AED 6,000 of spend within 60 days**.

The card's own product page states it in three places, including the hero
and the apply CTA.

## The condition that actually disqualifies people

The spend threshold is the easy part. The restriction worth reading twice
is who qualifies at all:

> The welcome offer is applicable only to new Citi Credit Card customers
> who apply/submit a lead directly through Citi website. Applicants
> reaching Citi website through a web search advert or any third party
> link do not qualify.

Two traps in one sentence. You must be **new to Citi credit cards** — an
existing Citi cardholder taking a second card does not qualify. And you
must arrive at the application **directly**, not via a search advert or a
comparison-site link. Clicking a sponsored result for "best UAE cashback
card" and applying from there can cost you the AED 300, and nothing on the
application flow will warn you.

Type the Citi address into the browser yourself.

## What it changes about the card

Not much, and that is the honest answer. AED 300 is a one-time credit on a
card whose case rests on its ongoing rates — 3% on non-AED spend, 2% on
groceries and supermarkets, 1% on everything else, uncapped.

Our [full review](/cards/citi-cashback/) treats the welcome credit as
excluded from the steady-state value for that reason. The doubling makes
year one better; it does not change whether the card earns its place in
year three.

It is worth noting what the 3% non-AED rate actually nets: Citi charges a
**2.99% foreign-currency fee**, so spending abroad earns roughly nothing
after the fee. The cashback that matters here is the 2% on groceries and
the 1% floor, both in dirhams.

## The fee, unchanged

No annual fee in year one. From year two the AED 300 fee (AED 315 with 5%
VAT) is waived if you spend AED 9,000 in the year — a threshold most
regular users clear without trying.
