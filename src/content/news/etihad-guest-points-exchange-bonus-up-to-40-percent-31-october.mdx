Etihad Guest is paying **up to 40% bonus Etihad Guest Miles** on points
transferred in from partner programmes **between 1 and 31 October 2026**
(00:00 to 23:59 UAE time), per the offer page on etihad.com. The bonus is
tiered by the size of the transfer, so one large conversion earns more than
several small ones.

**No registration step** is stated in the terms. The catches sit
elsewhere: **transfers cannot be reversed**, the offer is **not valid with
any other offer**, and the bonus Miles post **within 30 business days of
the campaign ending**, not on the day you transfer.

## The tiers

Per Etihad's terms, the bonus depends on how many Etihad Guest Miles a
transfer is worth:

| Transfer worth | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Up to 9,999 Miles | **10%** |
| 10,000 to 49,999 Miles | **20%** |
| 50,000 Miles or more | **40%** |

The step from 20% to 40% at 50,000 Miles is the one that matters. A
transfer worth 50,000 Miles lands 20,000 bonus Miles; at our published
cost-basis valuation of 2 fils per Etihad Guest Mile, that bonus is worth
about **AED 400**. Two transfers of 25,000 each would earn 20% apiece, or
10,000 bonus Miles in total, about AED 200.

The terms describe the tiers per transfer but do not say in so many words
how Etihad treats several transfers made on the same day. Do it in one go
if you can.

## The UAE partners

The offer page lists every participating programme. The UAE ones:

- [Emirates NBD](/banks/emirates-nbd/) (listed as "Emirates NBD UAE")
- [First Abu Dhabi Bank](/banks/fab/)
- [Dubai Islamic Bank](/banks/dib/)
- [Emirates Islamic](/banks/emirates-islamic/)
- [HSBC](/banks/hsbc/) (listed as "HSBC", without saying which country's programme)
- [Mashreq](/banks/mashreq/)
- [RAKBANK](/banks/rakbank/)
- Ajman Bank, DEEM Finance and Smiles by e&

Revolut is also listed, again without a country.

Two programmes many UAE readers hold are **missing**: **ADCB TouchPoints**
is not on the list, and neither is **Marriott Bonvoy**, the hotel currency
with the best published Etihad ratio in our
[transfer-ratios guide](/guides/uae-transfer-ratios-2026/). The hotel
programmes that do qualify are **ALL Accor** and **Shangri-La Circle**.

Etihad states a ratio for only one partner on the page: **1,000 Ajman Bank
MORE rewards convert to 909 Etihad Guest Miles**. For everyone else it says
"transfer ratios and minimum thresholds apply". Check the rate in your
bank's rewards portal before you convert; the bonus is paid on the Miles
that land, not on the bank points you spend.

## What it means for UAE members

- **You have a large bank balance and an Etihad redemption in mind.** This
  is the month to move it, in one transfer worth 50,000 Miles or more. The
  [Etihad Guest hub](/airlines/etihad-guest/) sets out where Miles go
  furthest from Abu Dhabi; Business Saver redemptions are where the
  40% bonus earns its keep.
- **You hold Accor points.** Our transfer guide records Accor converting
  to Etihad Guest at 2:1, so 100,000 Accor points would arrive as 50,000
  Miles plus 20,000 bonus. Whether that beats using Accor points on a
  hotel stay is the same question it always is; the bonus narrows the gap,
  it does not close it on every route.
- **You have no redemption planned.** Do nothing. Miles moved without a
  use in sight are exposed to whatever Etihad changes next, and a transfer
  cannot be undone.
