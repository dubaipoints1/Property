Citi UAE says it will reduce the annual airport lounge and dining allowances on its **Citi Premier** and **Citi Rewards** Mastercards from **1 January 2027**. The [bank’s airport lounge page](https://www.citibank.ae/credit-cards/travel/lounge-access) sets out the future rules for each card. The published annual cap for Premier goes from **14 to eight**, and for Rewards from **eight to four**. The cap covers lounge visits and eligible airport dining together. It does not require switching to a different app.

## A restaurant offer can use a lounge visit

For each of these cards, an eligible airport dining offer and a lounge entry draw from the same allowance. Citi’s [Premier change notice](https://www.citibank.ae/content/dam/cgcpc/ae/prelogin/www-citibank-ae/doc/pdf/mclounge.pdf?icid=AECACCCSCCAR03) also says that guest visits, where included, use that allowance. If you’re travelling with someone on Premier, check what a visit for both of you would use before choosing between the lounge and a meal.

This is a **Mastercard Travel Pass** benefit. [The app’s UAE listing](https://apps.apple.com/ae/app/mastercard-travel-pass/id1493489553) explains that eligibility depends on the issuer. Seeing a restaurant in [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/conditions-of-use) does not mean your Citi card covers it in Mastercard Travel Pass.

## What to check before you go

In Mastercard Travel Pass, look up the terminal you’ll actually use and read the conditions for the particular lounge or dining offer. Citi’s January rules also list a qualifying international transaction to unlock access. Check your card’s activation and allowance in the app; the published cap is not a promise of free entry for your account. For a trip around New Year, use the rules for your travel date, not the date you bought the ticket. Our [UAE airport lounge guide](/guides/uae-airport-lounges-card-access-2026/) explains how to find the right terminal and app.
