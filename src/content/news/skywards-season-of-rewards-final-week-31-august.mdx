Emirates Skywards' **Season of Rewards** closes on **31 August 2026**,
nine days from now. Before anything else, the registration gotchas: the
flight-side offers require tickets **booked and issued between 21 May
and 11.59pm on 31 August (Dubai time)** with **outbound travel between
21 May and 31 August inclusive**, and anyone not yet a Skywards member
must **enrol by 11.59pm Dubai time on 31 August** to qualify at all.

We covered the campaign's four components at launch — doubled
Cash+Miles, 20% bonus Tier Miles, the partner-earning lift, and the
easyJet/Jet2 reward-flight discount — in
[our 22 May coverage](/news/emirates-skywards-season-of-rewards-2026/).
This is the closing reminder, so here is only what is still actionable
from the UAE this week.

## The travel window is what bites now

Because the terms pair a book-by date with an outbound-travel window
ending the same day, **booking this week only counts if you also depart
by 31 August**. A ticket issued today for a September trip earns
nothing from this campaign. If you have a genuine reason to fly Emirates
or flydubai before month-end — a last summer-holiday leg, a
back-to-school return — book it inside the window. If your next trip
departs 1 September or later, there is no action to take; do not move a
trip for this.

## Partner bonuses: the cap shapes the maths

The 50% bonus Skywards Miles on travel partners — airlines, hotels, car
rentals and Emirates Holidays — is **capped at 10,000 bonus Miles per
partner**, worth about **AED 200** at the 2-fils cost-basis valuation we
use on the [Skywards programme page](/airlines/skywards/). That cap
means a partner hotel stay or car rental you were making anyway this
week is worth routing through Skywards; it is not worth manufacturing
spend for. Per our launch coverage, this component requires
registration first and the bonus posts within 60 days after the
campaign ends — so do not expect the Miles in September's statement.

## What it means for UAE members

Fly before month-end: book inside the window and, per our launch
coverage, use the doubled Cash+Miles rate if you are burning a small
balance on the fare or extras. Not flying until September: nothing here
applies to you, and the separate
[enhanced-redemption push](/news/skywards-summer-redemption-push-dss-5-million-miles/)
runs to 30 September on its own deadline. Emirates NBD Skywards
co-brand cardholders flying in the window still stack card earning on
top, unchanged from launch.
