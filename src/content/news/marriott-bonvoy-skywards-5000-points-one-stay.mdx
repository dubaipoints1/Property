Marriott Bonvoy is running a promotion under its Emirates Skywards
partnership: **5,000 bonus Bonvoy points** after **one eligible paid
stay** completed between **15 July and 30 September 2026** — first
reported by [Live From A Lounge](https://livefromalounge.com/marriott-bonvoy-offering-5000-bonus-points-on-your-next-stay-through-emirates-partnership/).
The ordering rule is the whole story: you must **link your Bonvoy and
Skywards accounts and register for the campaign before the qualifying
stay**. Register first, stay second — a stay completed before
registration earns nothing.

## The mechanics, as reported

Per Live From A Lounge's reporting, one eligible paid stay in the
window triggers the 5,000 points; the window closes 30 September 2026
at 11:59pm EDT, and the stay must be booked through Marriott.com or
other eligible channels at eligible rates. **Which rates and which
properties qualify is defined by the Marriott registration page** —
check it before assuming a prepaid third-party or corporate rate
counts.

## The AED case

At our 2.5-fil Bonvoy cost-basis baseline, 5,000 points is
**AED 125** — for a registration that takes a few minutes, against a
stay many UAE readers already have booked this summer. That is the
honest framing: AED 125 on a staycation you were taking anyway is
free money; a stay manufactured to chase 5,000 points is not.

## The standing partnership, for context

The promotion sits on top of the established Bonvoy–Skywards
partnership, whose mechanics are unchanged: Bonvoy points transfer to
Skywards Miles at **3:1**, with a **5,000-Mile bonus per 60,000
points transferred**; Skywards Silver, Gold and Platinum members earn
**1 Skywards Mile per USD** on eligible Marriott stays; and Bonvoy
Gold Elite members and above earn **3 points per USD** on eligible
Emirates flights. Details are on our
[Marriott Bonvoy hub](/airlines/marriott-bonvoy/) and the
[Emirates Skywards partner page](https://www.emirates.com/ae/english/skywards/partners/marriott-bonvoy/).

## What it means for UAE members

If any paid Marriott-family stay sits in your calendar before
30 September — a summer staycation or a work trip landing at a Bonvoy property — link the accounts and
register today, then forget about it. The cost of registering and not
staying is zero; the cost of staying unregistered is AED 125 of
points left on the table.

Holders of the Emirates NBD Marriott Bonvoy cards are the natural
audience here: the balance the 5,000 points lands in is the one their
card spend already feeds. Nothing about the cards themselves changes.

One stay is the full promotion — there is no cap to allocate against
and no reason to split nights. Book the stay you were booking; just
do the registration first.
