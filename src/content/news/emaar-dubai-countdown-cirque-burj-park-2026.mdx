Emaar says Cirque du Soleil will take part in the Downtown Dubai countdown to 2027 on **31 December 2026**. The organiser’s 17 November announcement confirms that Burj Park will again host a separate paid event. It did not give a ticket release date or prices.

If the live performance is why you want to go, start with the [official event site](https://mydubainewyear.emaar.com/en/). When DubaiPoints checked on 18 November, it still said Burj Park tickets were coming soon. Wait for Emaar to publish the current ticket categories and what each one includes before committing to an evening around the show.

If you mainly want to see the midnight countdown, Emaar lists designated free public viewing areas and an official livestream separately. Don’t assume a free viewing area gives the same view of the performers as Burj Park. Check the event site again closer to the date for access details.
