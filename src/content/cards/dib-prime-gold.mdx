## What to check before applying

Start with the annual fee block and the salary floor in the facts panel. If you expect to use the card abroad, check the bank’s charges separately: a card with no standard annual fee can still cost money to use.

Compare Gold with [Prime Platinum](/cards/dib-prime-platinum/) before choosing. The two are separately named products; meeting the salary floor on this page does not tell you which card the bank will approve.
