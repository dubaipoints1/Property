## Watch out for

- **The AED 1,575 annual fee from year two has no waiver.** Only the joining fee is
  refundable. Model year-two MAF spend before you apply.
- **SHARE Points are closed-loop.** Useful only at MAF — no airline, hotel, or cash
  redemption flexibility. If your loyalty drifts away from Carrefour and City Centre,
  the points strand.
- **Outside-network spending.** Compare the ordinary and reduced-category rows against your own merchants rather than assuming a market-wide advantage.
- **Groceries outside Carrefour earn just 0.375%**; fuel and utilities earn 0.15%.
  Treat this as a category card, not an everywhere card.

## Bottom line

A SHARE-network card with a payable joining fee and a conditional refund. Outside-network categories and geography have lower rates and native points caps. It makes sense only if your real purchases fit those conditions and the benefits you can actually use justify the cost. Confirm pending qualification and benefit terms before applying.

Compare the [Signature](/cards/emirates-nbd-share-visa-signature/) separately: the
earning schedules, fees and application terms are not interchangeable. If most of
your spending falls outside the partner network, compare source-qualified
alternatives without treating a general headline as applying to every purchase.
