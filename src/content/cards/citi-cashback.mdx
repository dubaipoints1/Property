## What you get — per-benefit value

### Cashback on eligible domestic spend

Use the source-qualified earning rows for your actual transactions. The assigned
credit limit and exclusions prevent an unrestricted, linearly scaled annual-return
estimate. No minimum-spend gate does not mean that every purchase earns cashback.

### 3% on non-AED spend

The published international processing margin is not proof of an exact net overseas return. Check the full conversion and processing terms rather than subtracting one issuer percentage from the cashback headline.

### 8 lounge visits (Mastercard Travel Pass)

Unlocked by a non-AED purchase. At the AED 200 walk-in equivalent:
**Value to me: AED 800/year** on four flown trips, **AED 1,600/year** at the
full ration.

### Careem ride and Talabat discount

One Careem airport ride a year (up to AED 84) and 20% off one Talabat order
a month (up to AED 14) — AED 84 + AED 168 at full utilisation.
**Value to me: AED 0–252/year.** Small numbers, honestly stated.

### AED 300 welcome credit

Statement credit advertised on Citi’s product page after AED 6,000 of qualifying spend
within 60 days. **Value in this example: AED 300 once**, if those terms apply and you
meet them.

No combined annual package value is quoted while reward or benefit conditions
remain unconfirmed. Check the current fee and waiver separately rather than
assuming that advertised perks recover the cost.

## Watch out for

- The published international processing margin is not proof of an exact net overseas return. Check the full conversion and processing terms rather than subtracting one issuer percentage from the cashback headline.
- **Different reward conditions.** Compare eligible categories, credit-limit restrictions and exclusions rather than contrasting isolated headline percentages.
- **Lounge gating.** You need a non-AED purchase to unlock the lounge visits.
- **Utility exclusion.** Paying DEWA/etc. through Citi's channels earns no cashback.

## Is it worth it?

Consider it if automatic cashback on eligible purchases fits your needs and you
can meet the fee-waiver conditions without extra spending. Confirm the assigned
credit limit, exclusions, eligibility and benefit terms; this is not cashback
without rules or a market-wide recommendation.

Calibrate expectations on overseas spending: the complete conversion cost needs
checking before the cashback can be treated as a net return. Compare the eligible
domestic reward with any category card you would actually use, including its caps
and qualification conditions.

## Bottom line

A no-minimum-spend cashback card subject to the assigned credit limit and exclusions.
**Consider it** for straightforward eligible domestic purchases. **Look elsewhere**
if you need a guaranteed overseas profit or a higher category headline without
checking its caps and conditions.
