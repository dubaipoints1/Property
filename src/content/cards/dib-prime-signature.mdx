## What to check before applying

DIB’s Signature page refers to the Prime Infinite card in parts of its benefits copy. Do not assume those benefits carry over. If a particular benefit matters to you, look for terms that name Signature before deciding.

[Prime Infinite](/cards/dib-prime-infinite/) is a separate product. Compare their published salary floors and the costs that matter to you; neither profile establishes eligibility.
