## Which CBD card is this?

This is the conventional Super Saver Visa Signature credit card from Commercial Bank of Dubai (CBD), not the separate CBD Al Islami Super Saver card.

## First-year annual fee and standard renewal

CBD’s Key Facts Statement waives the primary card’s annual fee in the first year and lists the standard renewal amount including VAT. That is not an all-in cost or confirmation of a later waiver.
