## Earn rates

<EarnRateTable card="adcb-365-cashback" />

Eligible home digital entertainment and AI subscriptions are narrow categories, not a boost for all online or entertainment spending. Pay the eligible provider directly. Third-party platforms, app stores and aggregators do not qualify for the subscription rate. Salik must be paid directly or through Dubai Now. Bill payments, school fees and transactions through ADCB channels do not earn cashback, though they can count towards qualifying spend.

## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="adcb-365-cashback" />

The current ADCB Schedule of Fees supports the annual-membership amount shown above. Relationship concessions are not universal waivers. Fees are inclusive of VAT where applicable. No separate joining-fee amount has been established by this review.

The foreign-currency percentage is an issuer charge, not a promise of the total conversion cost. The fee guide separately states that card-scheme processing is additional.

## Welcome bonus

The captured product states a welcome cashback subject to qualifying spend and a time window. The structured offer should be read with those conditions, not treated as an unconditional payment.

## Perks worth using

The current review does not attach a cash valuation to lifestyle or insurance headlines. Confirm current eligibility, availability and booking terms before relying on a benefit.

## Key conditions to know

The current product offers a salary **or** credit-limit qualification route. Neither route guarantees approval. Salary-transfer, residency and employment conditions have not all been confirmed, so the previous qualification summary remains unconfirmed rather than presented as a complete rule. Confirm the requirements for the exact product and application channel with ADCB.

This is a bounded field review, not a new whole-card verification date. A retained source date does not certify every historical statement or every linked benefit. Pending terms are set out in the source notes above.

## Watch out for

A quiet month below the cashback qualification gate can change the return materially. Do not estimate an annual return by applying the highest rate to every purchase.

## Is it worth it?

Consider it for a household whose eligible dining, grocery, fuel and direct-subscription spending genuinely matches the categories. Keep a separate view of excluded transactions and renewal cost. A universal all-spend return is not established.
