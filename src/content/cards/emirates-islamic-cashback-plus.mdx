## Which Emirates Islamic card is this?

This is the Cashback Plus Visa Signature Covered Card, not the bank’s Switch Cashback card. The fee below applies to the profit-based card named in the issuer schedule.

## Standard primary annual fee

This is not a joining fee, a personalised first-year or renewal quote, or confirmation of a waiver.
