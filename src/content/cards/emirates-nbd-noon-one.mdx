<HotTip>
noon Credits are a captive reward, not cash, statement credit or transferable miles.
Credit earned on a qualifying order does not promise the same percentage discount
on a later order. Check redemption conditions and count only purchases you would
make anyway. Off-platform rewards have separate exclusions; this is not a flat
cashback rate on every purchase.

## Watch out for

The foreign-currency fee shown is the issuer’s charge, not a total conversion-cost quote. The issuer fee guide states that scheme processing and any acquirer charges may be additional. A merchant’s AED-conversion offer can add a different cost; an issuer-fee waiver does not remove every possible overseas charge.

## Bottom line

Rewards for named noon platforms, not every restaurant or online purchase. The welcome statement cashback is separate from noon Credits earned on spend. It makes sense only if your real purchases fit those conditions and the benefits you can actually use justify the cost. Confirm pending qualification and benefit terms before applying.
