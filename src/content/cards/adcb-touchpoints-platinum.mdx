## Earn rates

ADCB’s [product page](https://www.adcb.com/en/personal/cards/credit-cards/touchpoint-platinum-cc) advertises **up to 1 TouchPoint per AED 1**. That is the highest advertised rate, not what you earn on every purchase.

- **0.4 TouchPoints per AED 1** on eligible domestic purchases at supermarkets, car dealers and fast-food restaurants.
- **0.2 TouchPoints per AED 1** on eligible domestic purchases in fuel, education, government services, real estate, telecoms and transport.
- **0.40 TouchPoints per AED 1 equivalent** on qualifying retail purchases in what ADCB calls its UK and Europe category. The bank refers to the European Economic Area (EEA) in its description; merchant category and EEA classification follow the applicable Visa or Mastercard rules. Don’t assume every UK or European purchase qualifies.

ADCB separately advertises **10,000 bonus TouchPoints** for **AED 10,000 in qualifying retail spend in a calendar month**. This is not a welcome bonus. Under the [card terms, section 7 (PDF)](https://www.adcb.com/en/multimedia/tnc/cb-tnc-touchpoints-credit-card.pdf), ADCB deducts reversals and refunds and excludes disputed purchases and cash withdrawals.

The [programme terms, Schedule B (PDF)](https://www.adcb.com/en/multimedia/tnc/cb-tnc-touchpoints-reward-program.pdf) limit earning each month to spend up to your assigned credit limit. The product page names charity and insurance categories, but the programme terms exclude charitable donations and specified insurance payments. Don’t count donations; ask ADCB whether a particular insurance payment qualifies. These selected rates do not establish a universal return.

## Fee summary

The current ADCB Schedule of Fees supports the annual-membership amount shown above. Relationship concessions are not universal waivers. Fees are inclusive of VAT where applicable. No separate joining-fee amount has been established by this review.

The foreign-currency percentage is an issuer charge, not a promise of the total conversion cost. The fee guide separately states that card-scheme processing is additional.

## Welcome bonus

No current welcome award has been established by the reviewed material. A monthly reward trigger is not a welcome offer, and an absent visible welcome section does not establish that all application channels offer none.

## Perks worth using

The page advertises VOX, talabat and travel benefits, including a lounge-access heading. The previous categorical no-lounge statement is not supported. Confirm the network variant and its actual entitlement conditions before use.

## Key conditions to know

The current product offers a salary **or** credit-limit qualification route. Neither route guarantees approval. Salary-transfer, residency and employment conditions have not all been confirmed, so the previous qualification summary remains unconfirmed rather than presented as a complete rule. Confirm the requirements for the exact product and application channel with ADCB.

This is a bounded field review, not a new whole-card verification date. A retained source date does not certify every historical statement or every linked benefit. Pending terms are set out in the source notes above.

## Watch out for

The salary figure is one route in an alternative qualification sentence, not a guaranteed approval threshold. The recurring fee depends on relationship tier. Do not value cinema or food-delivery headings as fully utilised cash.

## Is it worth it?

A possible TouchPoints option for an applicant who confirms the qualification route, card variant and current earn schedule. Do not choose it using the former unverified annual-return examples.
