## Perks worth using

The page advertises VOX, talabat and travel benefits, including a lounge-access heading. The previous categorical no-lounge statement is not supported. Confirm the network variant and its actual entitlement conditions before use.

## Key conditions to know

The current product offers a salary **or** credit-limit qualification route. Neither route guarantees approval. Salary-transfer, residency and employment conditions have not all been confirmed, so the previous qualification summary remains unconfirmed rather than presented as a complete rule. Confirm the requirements for the exact product and application channel with ADCB.

This is a bounded field review, not a new whole-card verification date. A retained source date does not certify every historical statement or every linked benefit. Pending terms are set out in the source notes above.

## Watch out for

The salary figure is one route in an alternative qualification sentence, not a guaranteed approval threshold. The recurring fee depends on relationship tier. Do not value cinema or food-delivery headings as fully utilised cash.

## Is it worth it?

A possible TouchPoints option for an applicant who confirms the qualification route, card variant and current earn schedule. Do not choose it using the former unverified annual-return examples.
