## Watch out for

- Eligibility for selected merchants depends on how the transaction is made and posted.
- **The AED 5,000 gate.** Spend under AED 5,000 in a month and the cashback doesn't pay.
- **Unpublished rate.** The per-merchant cashback percentage isn't on the product page;
  we've recorded the monthly cap and flagged the field.

## Is it worth it?

CBD One is worth investigating if your spending regularly reaches the named merchants.
Check the qualifying spend, eligible channels and merchant-credit terms against your
actual purchases. Unconfirmed credits and costs are not included in a return estimate.


## Bottom line

Compare CBD One on the merchants you actually use, and check the current terms with CBD before relying on any estimate.
