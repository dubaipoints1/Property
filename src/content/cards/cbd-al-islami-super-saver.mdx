## What the fee documents say

<FeeBlock card="cbd-al-islami-super-saver" />

The [Key Facts Statement](https://www.cbd.ae/docs/revcbdislamilibraries/default-document-library/kfs-sa_islamic_credit-card_en-ar311032ff458f66deb8c5ff0b00b6b24a.pdf) lists the primary-card annual fee from year two. The [Islamic fee schedule](https://www.cbd.ae/islami/general/fees-and-charges) gives different renewal terms for some existing cardholders, including an older application cohort. If you already hold this card, check your own terms rather than using the amount above as your charge.

CBD mentions a spending-based renewal waiver in its Key Facts Statement, but the sources checked do not establish the qualifying annual spend. A separate joining fee, salary requirement, welcome offer, foreign-spending cost and reward rate are not verified here. The bank lists this product, but we have not confirmed new applications. This reference profile is not included in Finder, Compare or the fee tables.
