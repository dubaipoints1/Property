## Which Amazon card is this?

This is the Mastercard Platinum version of Emirates Islamic’s Amazon card, not the [Mastercard World version](/cards/emirates-islamic-amazon-world/). The issuer lists both tiers. A published annual fee is not an eligibility decision or a promise of which tier the bank will offer.
