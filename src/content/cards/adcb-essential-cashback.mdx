## Earn rates

<EarnRateTable card="adcb-essential-cashback" />

The general rate changes with monthly spend, while issuer-defined select categories earn a reduced rate. The product wording overlaps at the spend-tier boundary; confirm that boundary with ADCB rather than inferring how it is applied. The merchant guide includes EU purchases and named everyday categories. Bill payments, school fees and transactions through ADCB channels are excluded from cashback, although they count towards qualifying spend.

## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="adcb-essential-cashback" />

The current ADCB Schedule of Fees supports the annual-membership amount shown above. Relationship concessions are not universal waivers. Fees are inclusive of VAT where applicable. No separate joining-fee amount has been established by this review.

The foreign-currency percentage is an issuer charge, not a promise of the total conversion cost. The fee guide separately states that card-scheme processing is additional.

## Welcome bonus

The product page still advertises welcome cashback, but its linked acquisition terms describe an application period that ended before this review. That mismatch remains open. Do not apply on the assumption that the headline is a live offer for a new application; request current, dated terms from ADCB.

## Perks worth using

The current page describes Reel buy-one-get-one tickets, not the former online-cinema cashback allowance. It also describes conditional Mastercard Travel Pass lounge access. Register and check eligibility: the initial entitlement and later non-AED transaction requirement are different. The visible lounge count is not labelled with a yearly period.

## Key conditions to know

The current product offers a salary **or** credit-limit qualification route. Neither route guarantees approval. Salary-transfer, residency and employment conditions have not all been confirmed, so the previous qualification summary remains unconfirmed rather than presented as a complete rule. Confirm the requirements for the exact product and application channel with ADCB.

This is a bounded field review, not a new whole-card verification date. A retained source date does not certify every historical statement or every linked benefit. Pending terms are set out in the source notes above.

## Watch out for

The old cinema-cashback allowance, no-lounge assertion and unknown-year-two-fee narrative have been removed. The current fee schedule expressly lists a free annual membership. A cinema promotion can expire independently of the card.

## Is it worth it?

Consider it if your eligible purchases and monthly spend fit the current tiers. Treat the welcome offer as pending, and compare real cashback after exclusions rather than a flat rate on every transaction.
