## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="emirates-nbd-darna-select-visa" />

Read the joining fee, renewal fee and any promotional waiver separately. A renewal figure is not the complete first-year cost, and a current application promotion is not a guarantee that every applicant receives it. The fee table carries the source-qualified VAT treatment. The foreign-currency fee shown is the issuer’s charge, not a total conversion-cost quote. The issuer fee guide states that scheme processing and any acquirer charges may be additional. A merchant’s AED-conversion offer can add a different cost; an issuer-fee waiver does not remove every possible overseas charge.

## What you get — per-benefit value

Darna is one of the few UAE programmes with a published conversion — 10 Darna Points =
AED 1 — so the earn can carry honest AED figures. The caveat on every line below: the
value is only realisable inside the Darna partner network. No airline transfer, no
cash-out.

## Bottom line

An entry-level Aldar/Darna card: useful only where your purchases qualify for the partner rate, not a general rent or education rebate. It makes sense only if your real purchases fit those conditions and the benefits you can actually use justify the cost. Confirm pending qualification and benefit terms before applying.

Compare Select only on purchases that qualify for the settled rows. Its captive redemption network and disputed non-partner rows make an unrestricted cashback comparison inappropriate.
