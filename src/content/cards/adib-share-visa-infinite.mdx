## How the rewards work

The detail pages and shared terms do not fully resolve the reach of qualifying SHARE ecosystem spending. We retain that conflict openly rather than extend a programme reward to every purchase.

The displayed rows are source-qualified observations. They are not a universal
base rate, a cash conversion or a total-return ranking. Native reward limits and
purchase-channel conditions remain part of the issuer terms.

## What the fee figure does and does not cover

<FeeBlock card="adib-share-visa-infinite" />

The displayed annual fee is the standard primary-card charge from the reviewed
source row. It is not a first-year or total annual cost. Joining charges, any
waiver and profit charges remain Pending; an omitted field does not mean zero.
Welcome incentives and perks have not been valued or automatically typed.

## Before applying

Confirm qualifying SHARE purchases and how the contractual ecosystem limitation applies before relying on a projected reward.

Read the current issuer product page, KFS and linked programme conditions. Salary,
residency, employment and transfer requirements are not fully reconciled in this
profile. Any income amount retained in the source record does not establish
complete eligibility or guarantee approval.

Use [the comparison tool](/cards/compare/?cards=adib-share-visa-infinite,adib-cashback-visa) to inspect
the source-qualified terms beside the existing Cashback profile, without assuming
that unlike reward units or unresolved charges are equivalent.
