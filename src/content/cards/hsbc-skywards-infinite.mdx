The HSBC Emirates Skywards Infinite Credit Card earns Skywards Miles directly.
That is useful only when the currency and redemption fit your plans; it does not
make every purchase or reward seat good value.

## Earning Miles

<EarnRateTable card="hsbc-skywards-infinite" />

These rates have a USD-equivalent spending basis. The product page distinguishes
AED spending, non-AED transactions and reduced-rate AED categories. Do not read
the reduced domestic rates as a separate rule for all overseas purchases.

HSBC also advertises a boost for direct Emirates and flydubai bookings. This
profile does not apply that carrier-specific boost to all airfare. Check the
issuer's current earning terms before relying on it for a booking.

## Cost and eligibility

<FeeBlock card="hsbc-skywards-infinite" />

The product page and KFS agree on the standard annual fee and published income
threshold. The issuer says an HSBC account is not required. Its credit assessment
still applies, so a listed minimum is not guaranteed approval.

The foreign-currency fee shown is the issuer processing charge including VAT.
Separate scheme charges and the exchange rate can add cost. Welcome incentives
and travel benefits have conditions of their own and are not priced into this
standing profile.

## A useful next check

Read the [Skywards guide](/airlines/skywards/) and compare a real, available
redemption with the cash ticket you would otherwise buy. Do that before treating
a large Miles balance as a cash saving.
