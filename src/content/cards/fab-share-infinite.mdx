## What to check before applying

This is First Abu Dhabi Bank’s Infinite tier, not an Emirates NBD SHARE card. The salary figure is the minimum FAB publishes. It is not confirmation that you meet the rest of the bank’s requirements.

## Annual membership fee

<FeeBlock card="fab-share-infinite" />

The schedule lists the standard primary-card annual membership fee including VAT. Check any offer made to you separately, including when the fee is charged. We have not confirmed a joining fee or fee waiver.

For a lower published annual fee, compare [FAB SHARE Signature](/cards/fab-share-signature/) or [FAB SHARE Platinum](/cards/fab-share-platinum/). Look at how your own purchases would qualify, not just the name of the tier. SHARE points can be used within the programme; they are not a cash payout.
