## Check which RTA plan the bank is offering

This profile uses the RTA Platinum entry under Profit-Based Cards in Emirates Islamic’s Schedule of Charges. The RTA column of the bank’s Key Facts Statement corroborates that annual fee. The bank publishes separate terms for a Fee-Based plan; do not use the annual fee below to price that plan.

## Standard primary annual fee

<FeeBlock card="emirates-islamic-rta" />

This is an annual fee for the primary card, not an assessment of joining charges, eligibility, foreign-currency costs or the cost of carrying a balance. No cashback rate or promotional benefit is established here. Check the Key Facts Statement for the plan named in any offer you receive.
