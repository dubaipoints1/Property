## Digital welcome offer: cards issued by 31 December 2026

**Issuer terms checked 16 November 2026.** The Titanium offer pays **AED 500 cashback** after **AED 5,000 in qualifying spend within 60 days of card issuance**. You must start the application in the RAKBANK app and have the card issued through that channel from **31 August 2026 to 31 December 2026**, both dates inclusive. This dated offer is separate from the card’s ongoing cashback and is not counted in our comparisons.

The offer covers the first eligible card for new RAKBANK customers or existing customers without a RAKBANK credit card. Previous cardholders must have closed their old card at least six months before applying. RAKBANK staff and self-employed customers do not qualify. Applications started through a sales agent or another channel are excluded; the bank’s digital team can help finish an application that started in the app.

Qualifying spend excludes transfers, cash-like transactions, fees, instalment transactions and reversed, refunded or disputed purchases. You cannot combine this offer with another spend-based promotion on the same card unless RAKBANK permits it. Cards that are delinquent, blocked, over limit or inactive during the promotion or fulfilment period do not qualify. RAKBANK says it credits the cashback within 90 business days after the promotion ends and may take it back if you cancel the card in the first 12 months.

If your card is issued after 31 December 2026, do not count on this offer. See [RAKBANK’s digital onboarding terms (PDF)](https://www.rakbank.ae/globalassets/rakbank/all-pdfs/001---campaign/tc---new-customer-dob-feb-2025.pdf) and the [issuer’s Titanium page](https://www.rakbank.ae/en/cards/credit-cards/titanium-credit-card) for any changes.
