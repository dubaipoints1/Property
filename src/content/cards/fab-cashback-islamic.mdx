## What to check before applying

This is FAB’s Islamic Cashback Mastercard, not the [conventional FAB Cashback Card](/cards/fab-cashback/). The minimum monthly salary FAB publishes is a starting point. It does not tell you whether you qualify or whether FAB will approve an application.

## Annual fee and cashback

<FeeBlock card="fab-cashback-islamic" />

Use the standard primary annual fee including VAT when comparing costs. That figure does not establish a separate joining charge or a waiver.

FAB’s published cashback terms differ on minimum spend. We don’t quote a rate, cap or estimated return on that evidence. Before counting on cashback, check which purchases qualify and which spend rules apply to you. We have not confirmed a current welcome offer.
