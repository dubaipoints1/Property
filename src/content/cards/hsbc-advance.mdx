## What to check before applying

HSBC links this card to an Advance Account. Check that you have the matching account before applying. If you are considering opening the account to get the card, assess the costs and conditions of the two products separately.

Advance and [Premier](/cards/hsbc-premier/) both use HSBC Rewards points, but the matching account is different. A shared programme does not establish equal earn rates, benefits or eligibility. If you value direct cashback instead, see the [HSBC card line-up](/banks/hsbc/).
