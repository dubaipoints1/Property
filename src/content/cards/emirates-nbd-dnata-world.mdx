The Emirates NBD dnata World Credit Card is the premium tier in ENBD's
dnata co-brand pair, co-branded with dnata Travel, the Emirates Group's
UAE travel agency. It earns **dnata Points** redeemable directly against
bookings made through dnata Travel — flights, hotels, holidays — and is
positioned for households that route the bulk of their travel spend
through the agency anyway.

## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="emirates-nbd-dnata-world" />

Read the joining fee, renewal fee and any promotional waiver separately. A renewal figure is not the complete first-year cost, and a current application promotion is not a guarantee that every applicant receives it. The fee table carries the source-qualified VAT treatment. The foreign-currency fee shown is the issuer’s charge, not a total conversion-cost quote. The issuer fee guide states that scheme processing and any acquirer charges may be additional. A merchant’s AED-conversion offer can add a different cost; an issuer-fee waiver does not remove every possible overseas charge.

## What you get — per-benefit value

The source describes percentage rewards paid in dnata Points. Redemption remains limited to the programme’s channels. This partial review does not assign a fixed cash value or fee-offset guarantee to miles, points, status or conditional benefits. Value depends on the qualifying terms and the redemption you actually make.

### Welcome bonus

Up to 2,500 dnata Points: 1,000 on payment of joining fee and 1,500 on AED 25,000 retail spend in the first 3 billing statements.

### Issuer foreign-currency-fee waiver

The foreign-currency fee shown is the issuer’s charge, not a total conversion-cost quote. The issuer fee guide states that scheme processing and any acquirer charges may be additional. A merchant’s AED-conversion offer can add a different cost; an issuer-fee waiver does not remove every possible overseas charge.

### dnata Travel and partner earn

No total annual value is quoted while reward qualification, caps or benefit allowances remain unresolved. Use only the benefits you can verify and actually use; an advertised upper bound is not guaranteed annual value.

## Watch out for

The issuer product page states a minimum monthly salary of AED 20,000. The reviewed product, Key Facts Statement and shared credit-card terms do not establish a universal salary-transfer requirement. Confirm the conditions of your own application; we do not assume a transfer-related clawback or that none can apply.

## Bottom line

A dnata-partner card with an issuer foreign-currency-fee waiver. That is not a promise that every currency-conversion or scheme charge disappears. It makes sense only if your real purchases fit those conditions and the benefits you can actually use justify the cost. Confirm pending qualification and benefit terms before applying.
