## Standard primary annual fee

<FeeBlock card="al-masraf-mastercard-platinum" />

The [conventional credit-card Key Facts Statement](https://www.almasraf.ae/pdf/kfs-credit-card.pdf) directs readers to the [retail tariff](https://www.almasraf.ae/pdf/schedule-of-charges-retail-banking.pdf) for the amount. The bank still links its 2023 retail tariff, which lists the standard Platinum fee before VAT. This does not establish what you would pay at opening or each renewal, or whether a separate joining fee or waiver applies.

The bank’s [fee-waiver campaign page](https://www.almasraf.ae/free-for-life) lists 14 June 2026 as the last day of that offer. Treat the page as historical, not a current waiver. The bank’s product pages share an enquiry link; we have not confirmed intake or eligibility for this card. This reference is not included in the active catalogue, comparisons or fee tables.
