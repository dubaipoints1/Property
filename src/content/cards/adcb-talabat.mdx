## Earn rates

All earn pays as **talabat credit**, a closed-loop balance redeemable only inside
the talabat app, against food, talabat Mart, grocery and pharmacy orders:

<EarnRateTable card="adcb-talabat" />

<HotTip>
The 35% on talabat orders is three conditions deep. It covers only the **first 10
talabat orders** in a calendar month, pays at most **AED 35 an order** and **AED 350 a
month**, and needs **AED 2,500 of retail spend** on the card that month. Miss the
spend and every talabat order that month earns 1.25%. An order of AED 100 or more
earns the full AED 35, so the cap bites on large baskets.
</HotTip>

ADCB credits the month's talabat credit to the talabat Pay account in the first week
of the following month, and counts purchases by the date they are billed, not the
date of the transaction.

## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="adcb-talabat" />

The card carries no annual fee and no spend threshold to keep it free. The cost to
watch is the **2.99% non-AED foreign-currency markup** in line with the ADCB schedule
of fees, market-standard for UAE cards, with no FX advantage on overseas spend. All
published fees include 5% VAT.

## What you get — per-benefit value

talabat credit is AED-denominated: ADCB states the caps and the welcome in dirhams
("up to AED 35 per order as talabat credit"), so the maths is direct, with one caveat
on every line. The value only realises if you keep ordering on talabat. Counted
against the AED 0 annual fee.

### 35% on talabat orders

**Value to me: AED 2,688/year** for a household placing eight AED 80 orders a month
(AED 28 back on each, AED 224 a month), provided the card also carries AED 2,500 of
retail spend every month. At ten orders of AED 100 or more the AED 350 monthly cap
binds, which is **AED 4,200/year** at most.

### Welcome bonus

Up to AED 750 in talabat credit on AED 5,000 of spend in the first 45
days, digital-channel applications only; AED 200 for existing ADCB
cardholders.
**Value to me: AED 750, one-time** at face for a new-to-bank applicant
who orders regularly; **AED 200** if you already hold an ADCB card.

### Everyday earn

1.25% on most domestic and international retail; 0.35% on supermarkets, fuel,
education, government services, real estate, telecoms, charity, transport, auto
dealers, insurance and UK/EEA merchants.
**Value to me: AED 235/year** on the AED 2,500 a month the 35% rate needs, if
AED 1,200 of it earns 1.25% (AED 180) and AED 1,300 falls in the 0.35% categories
(AED 55). Paid as talabat credit, not cash.

### talabat pro free delivery

Unlimited free delivery on qualifying pro orders (AED 50+ food,
AED 100+ mart/grocery/pharmacy).
**Value to me: the delivery fee saved on every qualifying order.**
talabat prices delivery dynamically by area and basket, so we don't book
an annual AED figure.

**Total package:** roughly **AED 3,670 in year one** for a new-to-bank household
placing eight AED 80 talabat orders a month and putting another AED 2,500 a month on
the card (AED 2,688 + AED 750 + AED 235), plus the unpriced delivery savings, against
no fee. In later years the same household collects roughly **AED 2,900/year**. A
household that cannot reach AED 2,500 of monthly spend loses the 35% rate and
collects a fraction of this.

## Watch out for

- **The AED 2,500 spend condition.** The 35% rate is the card. A month under AED 2,500
  of retail spend drops every talabat order that month to 1.25%.
- **The caps.** Ten orders and AED 350 a month. A household ordering daily earns 1.25%
  on everything after the tenth order.
- **The 0.35% list is long.** Supermarkets, fuel, telecoms and government payments are
  much of a typical household's spend, and they earn 0.35% here.
- **Captive-currency payout.** talabat credit redeems only inside the talabat app; stop
  ordering there and the balance traps the rewards.
- **Existing-customer welcome is much weaker.** AED 200 for existing ADCB cardholders
  versus AED 750 new-to-bank; this card is sharpest as a first ADCB card.

## Bottom line

For a household that orders on talabat most weeks and can put AED 2,500 a month on the
card, the 35% rate is worth up to AED 350 a month in talabat credit on a card with no
fee, and the welcome adds up to AED 750 more. The maths collapses for an occasional
talabat user or anyone who cannot meet the monthly spend, because the everyday earn is
1.25% at best and 0.35% on supermarkets, fuel and bills. **Apply** if talabat is a
weekly habit and the card can carry AED 2,500 a month. **Skip** if it is not; a
flat-rate AED-denominated cashback card is the cleaner hold for everyday spend.
