## Earn rates

<EarnRateTable card="adcb-talabat" />

Additional talabat orders and months below the qualifying-spend gate use a lower rate. Other eligible retail has a separate rate, with reduced domestic categories and EEA merchants. The terms also exclude specified transaction types and limit eligible earning to the assigned credit limit. Use the source-qualified rows rather than one universal base.

## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="adcb-talabat" />

The current ADCB Schedule of Fees supports the annual-membership amount shown above. Relationship concessions are not universal waivers. Fees are inclusive of VAT where applicable. No separate joining-fee amount has been established by this review.

The foreign-currency percentage is an issuer charge, not a promise of the total conversion cost. The fee guide separately states that card-scheme processing is additional.

## Welcome bonus

The nested product terms distinguish new ADCB cardholders from existing cardholders, not simply customers who are new to the bank. Digital-channel, qualifying-spend and time-window conditions apply. Conversion and repeat-issuance exclusions must not be omitted.

## Perks worth using

Free delivery is limited to eligible talabat pro orders and selected vendors, with separate order minima for food and for other listed categories. The terms govern registration, account standing, restricted redemption, expiry and possible forfeiture. talabat credit cannot be exchanged for cash or used to pay ADCB charges.

## Key conditions to know

The current product offers a salary **or** credit-limit qualification route. Neither route guarantees approval. Salary-transfer, residency and employment conditions have not all been confirmed, so the previous qualification summary remains unconfirmed rather than presented as a complete rule. Confirm the requirements for the exact product and application channel with ADCB.

This is a bounded field review, not a new whole-card verification date. A retained source date does not certify every historical statement or every linked benefit. Pending terms are set out in the source notes above.

## Watch out for

A monthly maximum is not a guaranteed reward. Order eligibility, billing dates, merchant classification and qualifying retail spend all matter. Lounge and cinema headings do not establish a complete entitlement schedule.

## Is it worth it?

Consider it for routine eligible talabat use only if the spending gate is realistic without extra purchases. Compare the restricted credit you would actually redeem, not a headline rate on all card spending.
