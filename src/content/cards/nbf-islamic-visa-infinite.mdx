## Which eligibility route applies to you?

The bank lists salary or NBF Priority status as alternatives. Its key facts statement also describes the possibility of applying against a fixed deposit. Treat these as separate routes to ask about, not as short cuts around the bank’s underwriting.

If you are comparing the two tiers, see [Platinum Exclusive](/cards/nbf-islamic-platinum-exclusive/) for its own published salary terms. Its criteria should not be carried over to this card.

## Fees and charges

For overseas purchases, ask the bank for the cost of a transaction in its original currency and the cost of a purchase abroad billed in dirhams. The issuer’s foreign-currency processing fee does not establish the total cost of either payment. See the [NBF card line-up](/banks/nbf/) for the two Islamic profiles.
