The Liv Cashback is the free-for-life half of Liv's two-card lineup — a
Mastercard Platinum product from Emirates NBD's digital brand, built on
the same tier-by-total-monthly-spend mechanic as its paid sibling, at
lower rates and zero fee.

## Earn rates

<EarnRateTable card="liv-cashback" />

The 0.75% in the table is the **guaranteed base tier** — what you earn in
a month where total spend stays under AED 5,000. The full ladder, per
Liv's feature terms:

<HotTip label="How the tiers actually work">
Your rate is set by **total spend across the card in the calendar
month**, and once a tier is reached it applies to **all** of that
month's spend: **0.75%** from AED 0–4,999 of total spend, **1.5%** from
AED 5,000–9,999, and **2%** at AED 10,000+. It is a retroactive monthly
multiplier, not a category table. Low-margin merchant categories —
government, utilities, bills — earn **0.1%** whatever tier you reach.
Cashback is capped at **AED 750/month** and additionally bounded by a
percentage of your credit limit, so a modest limit can set a lower
ceiling than the cap itself.
</HotTip>

## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="liv-cashback" />

Free for life — no joining fee, no annual fee, ever. The 1.99% FX
margin is the standout number: most UAE issuers charge 2.5–3.5%, and
almost none pair a sub-2% FX rate with a zero fee. The monthly finance
rate is 3.49% (41.88% APR) per the Liv charges page — as always, a
number that only matters if you carry a balance, which no cashback
arithmetic survives.

## Skywards conversion

Rewards accrue as points with two exits: cashback against the card, or
**Emirates Skywards Miles — up to 5,000 Miles a month** on this card,
with a **500-point minimum** per redemption in either direction. A
fee-free card that feeds Skywards through ENBD's first-party
relationship is a rarity; for a light spender building a Skywards
balance without paying an annual fee anywhere, that alone is the case
for this card.

## Key conditions to know

<dl class="dp-conditions">
  <dt>Tier-by-total-spend</dt>
  <dd>The advertised 2% requires AED 10,000+ of total spend in the
  calendar month; 1.5% requires AED 5,000. The guaranteed floor is
  0.75%. The achieved tier applies to all of that month's spend.</dd>

  <dt>Two caps, not one</dt>
  <dd>AED 750/month cashback cap, plus a bound tied to a percentage of
  your credit limit. The lower of the two governs.</dd>

  <dt>Redemption</dt>
  <dd>500-point minimum per redemption, as cashback or as Skywards
  Miles (up to 5,000 Miles/month on this card).</dd>

  <dt>One card per customer</dt>
  <dd>Liv issues either the Cashback or the Cashback+ to a given
  customer, never both. Applying for this card is choosing it over the
  higher-earning paid alternative.</dd>

  <dt>Eligibility</dt>
  <dd>No minimum salary is published on Liv's product page, feature
  terms or KFS — application and approval run through the Liv X app,
  which is why our spec table shows the salary bar as
  <strong>"Pending"</strong> rather than a number we cannot source. UAE
  residency required.</dd>
</dl>

## Cashback vs Cashback+

<CardComparison cards={["liv-cashback", "liv-cashback-plus"]} />

Because of the one-card rule this is the decision, not a comparison.
The [Cashback+](/cards/liv-cashback-plus/) costs AED 700 a year for
double the top-tier rate (4% at AED 15,000+ vs 2% at AED 10,000+),
double the cap (AED 1,500 vs 750), double the Skywards ceiling (10,000
vs 5,000 Miles/month) and a much deeper perk stack. Below AED 7,000 of
monthly spend both cards sit at their floors and the free card wins
outright. Above AED 15,000, the Cashback+'s top tier is worth up to
AED 300 a month more — before its perks. In the AED 7,000–15,000 band,
run your own statements before choosing.

## Watch out for

- **The floor, not the headline.** Budget at 0.75% and treat 1.5%/2% as
  upside in the months you genuinely clear the tiers.
- **The credit-limit bound.** Your real cashback ceiling may be lower
  than AED 750 if your limit is modest.
- **Low-margin categories.** Government and utility payments earn 0.1%
  — they count toward your tier but barely earn.
- **The one-card rule.** You cannot hold this and later add the
  Cashback+ alongside it; switching means choosing again.

## Bottom line

The strongest argument for the Liv Cashback is everything it doesn't
charge: no fee, ever, with a 1.99% FX rate and a Skywards exit that
fee-free cards almost never offer. **Apply** as a first UAE card or a
fee-free keeper if your monthly spend sits under AED 10,000. **Take the
[Cashback+](/cards/liv-cashback-plus/) instead** if you reliably clear
AED 15,000 a month — the one-card rule means you must pick now. **Look
elsewhere** if you want a published salary bar, category-based rates,
or a card whose rate doesn't depend on how the month's total came out.
