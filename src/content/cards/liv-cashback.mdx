The Liv Cashback is the free-for-life half of Liv's two-card lineup — a
Mastercard Platinum product from Emirates NBD's digital brand, built on
the same tier-by-total-monthly-spend mechanic as its paid sibling, at
lower rates and zero fee.

## Earn rates

<EarnRateTable card="liv-cashback" />

The 0.75% in the table is the **base tier on qualifying general retail** — what you earn in
a month where total spend stays under AED 5,000. The full ladder, per
Liv's feature terms:

<HotTip label="How the tiers actually work">
Your rate is set by **total spend across the card in the calendar
month**, and once a tier is reached it applies to that month’s qualifying
general-retail purchases: **0.75%** from AED 0–4,999 of total spend, **1.5%** from
AED 5,000–9,999, and **2%** at AED 10,000+. It is a retroactive monthly
multiplier, not a category table. Low-margin merchant categories —
government, utilities, bills — earn **0.1%** whatever tier you reach.
Cashback is capped at **AED 750/month** and additionally bounded by a
percentage of your credit limit, so a modest limit can set a lower
ceiling than the cap itself.
</HotTip>

## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="liv-cashback" />

Liv’s published foreign-currency transaction percentage is an issuer component, exclusive of applicable VAT and separate Mastercard processing. It must not be compared with another card’s all-in fee or used to promise a precise overseas net return. The base tier applies to qualifying retail, not every transaction; reduced categories and the assigned-credit-limit cap still apply.

## Skywards conversion

Rewards accrue as points with two exits: cashback against the card, or
**Emirates Skywards Miles — up to 5,000 Miles a month** on this card,
with a **500-point minimum** per redemption in either direction. A
fee-free card that feeds Skywards through ENBD's first-party
relationship is a rarity; for a light spender building a Skywards
balance without paying an annual fee anywhere, that alone is the case
for this card.

## Key conditions to know

<dl class="dp-conditions">
  <dt>Tier-by-total-spend</dt>
  <dd>The advertised 2% requires AED 10,000+ of total spend in the calendar month; 1.5% requires AED 5,000. The base qualifying-retail tier is 0.75%. The achieved tier applies to qualifying general retail, while reduced low-merchant rates remain applicable.</dd>
  <dt>Two caps, not one</dt>
  <dd>AED 750/month cashback cap, plus a bound tied to a percentage of your credit limit. The lower of the two governs.</dd>
  <dt>Redemption</dt>
  <dd>500-point minimum per redemption, as cashback or as Skywards Miles (up to 5,000 Miles/month on this card).</dd>
  <dt>One card per customer</dt>
  <dd>Liv issues either the Cashback or the Cashback+ to a given customer, never both. Applying for this card is choosing it over the higher-earning paid alternative.</dd>
  <dt>Eligibility</dt>
  <dd>The minimum salary above comes from <a href="https://www.liv.me/-/media/liv/website/pdf/2024/kfs/apr/liv-credit-card-key-facts-statement-kfs-english.pdf">Liv's Key Facts Statement</a>. UAE residency is required. A salary match does not guarantee approval; Liv assesses applications in the Liv X app.</dd>
</dl>


## Cashback vs Cashback+

<CardComparison cards={["liv-cashback", "liv-cashback-plus"]} />

Because of the one-card rule this is the decision, not a comparison.
The [Cashback+](/cards/liv-cashback-plus/) costs <CardFeeAmount card="liv-cashback-plus" /> a year for
double the top-tier rate (4% at AED 15,000+ vs 2% at AED 10,000+),
double the cap (AED 1,500 vs 750), double the Skywards ceiling (10,000
vs 5,000 Miles/month) and a deeper perk stack. Their spend-tier thresholds
differ, so do not assume both cards remain on their base tiers in the same
month. Compare your qualifying general retail, reduced-category spend,
assigned limit and fees together rather than using an unrestricted top-tier gap.

## Watch out for

- **The floor, not the headline.** Budget at 0.75% and treat 1.5%/2% as
  upside in the months you genuinely clear the tiers.
- **The credit-limit bound.** Your real cashback ceiling may be lower
  than AED 750 if your limit is modest.
- **Low-margin categories.** Government and utility payments earn 0.1%
  — they count toward your tier but barely earn.
- **The one-card rule.** You cannot hold this and later add the
  Cashback+ alongside it; switching means choosing again.
