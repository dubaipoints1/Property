## Joining and renewal fees

<FeeBlock card="emirates-nbd-u-by-emaar-signature" />

The bank’s key facts statement separates the joining fee from renewal. Treat the joining fee as one part of your opening costs, and confirm its VAT treatment and the total due in the first year with the bank. The UPoints programme name does not tell you the return on your own spending. Check eligible categories and redemption terms before assigning the points a value.
