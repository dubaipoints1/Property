## What you get — per-benefit value

This partial review does not assign a fixed cash value or fee-offset guarantee to miles, points, status or conditional benefits. Value depends on the qualifying terms and the redemption you actually make.

### Welcome bonus

Up to 60,000 Etihad Guest Miles: 20,000 on joining-fee payment and 40,000 on AED 60,000 spend within 6 months. Silver fast-track requires an eligible return flight within 6 months.

### Earn on AED 100,000 of card spend

No total annual value is quoted while reward qualification, caps or benefit allowances remain unresolved. Use only the benefits you can verify and actually use; an advertised upper bound is not guaranteed annual value.

### Silver-tier fast-track

Status privileges have their own qualification and validity conditions. An eligible return flight, card activation or spend target may be required; the benefit is not treated as an unconditional cash payment.

## Bottom line

An accessible Etihad co-brand, but current product and special-terms reward rates disagree. Do not choose it using a single assumed base return. It makes sense only if your real purchases fit those conditions and the benefits you can actually use justify the cost. Confirm pending qualification and benefit terms before applying.

**Consider it** if Etihad is your main airline and you can meet the actual qualifying conditions. Compare alternative co-brands on confirmed current terms, without treating the contested base rate as settled.
