## What you get — per-benefit value

This partial review does not assign a fixed cash value or fee-offset guarantee to miles, points, status or conditional benefits. Value depends on the qualifying terms and the redemption you actually make.

### Welcome bonus

Up to 200,000 Etihad Guest Miles: 40,000 on joining-fee payment; 60,000 on AED 90,000 spend within 6 months; and 100,000 on AED 120,000 spend within 6 months. Check exact campaign eligibility. Gold fast-track requires an eligible return flight within 6 months.

### Visa Airport Companion lounge access

A named benefit is not evidence of unlimited visits, guest admission or an unrestricted booking allowance. Use the current issuer/provider conditions for the qualifying spend, booking channel, fees and usage limit; unconfirmed allowances are not used for benefit matching.

### 50% redemption-cost voucher on AED 150,000 spend

No total annual value is quoted while reward qualification, caps or benefit allowances remain unresolved. Use only the benefits you can verify and actually use; an advertised upper bound is not guaranteed annual value.

### Earn on AED 100,000 of card spend

No total annual value is quoted while reward qualification, caps or benefit allowances remain unresolved. Use only the benefits you can verify and actually use; an advertised upper bound is not guaranteed annual value.

### Year-one Etihad Guest tier status

Status privileges have their own qualification and validity conditions. An eligible return flight, card activation or spend target may be required; the benefit is not treated as an unconditional cash payment.

## Watch out for

The issuer product page states a minimum monthly salary of AED 30,000. The reviewed product, Key Facts Statement and shared credit-card terms do not establish a universal salary-transfer requirement. Confirm the conditions of your own application; we do not assume a transfer-related clawback or that none can apply.
