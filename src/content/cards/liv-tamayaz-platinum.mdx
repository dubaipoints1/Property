## What your rewards can buy

You earn Tamayaz Points on eligible spending and redeem them at Union Coop, in store or online. Liv calls the reward cashback in its comparison table, but the value goes into your Tamayaz loyalty account rather than your card statement. Check which purchases qualify, the limits, and how your Tamayaz membership affects redemption. This profile does not calculate a general return.

## What to check before applying

The published salary figure is one part of the bank’s assessment. Liv’s Tamayaz guidance says salaried and self-employed readers can apply, but does not make this salary figure a qualification test for self-employed applicants. The bank also considers age, debt burden and credit history.

Liv lists its website and Liv X app as application routes for the Tamayaz range. If you do not already have a Liv account, Liv directs you to its website. Select **Platinum** when the bank asks which card you want.

## Fees and charges

Both issuer disclosures list no standard first-year joining fee and no standard annual renewal fee under Platinum. This does not establish the cost of foreign-currency purchases or other usage charges. If you prefer a card with a different rewards currency, see the [Liv card line-up](/banks/liv/).
