## Earn rates

ADCB’s [product page](https://www.adcb.com/en/personal/cards/credit-cards/touchpoint-ic) advertises **up to 1.5 TouchPoints per AED 1**. That is the highest advertised rate, not what you earn on every purchase.

- **0.4 TouchPoints per AED 1** on eligible domestic purchases at supermarkets, car dealers and fast-food restaurants.
- **0.2 TouchPoints per AED 1** on eligible domestic purchases in fuel, education, government services, real estate, telecoms and transport.
- **0.40 TouchPoints per AED 1 equivalent** on qualifying retail purchases in what ADCB calls its UK and Europe category. The bank refers to the European Economic Area (EEA) in its description; merchant category and EEA classification follow Visa’s rules. Don’t assume every UK or European purchase qualifies.

ADCB separately advertises **15,000 bonus TouchPoints** for **AED 15,000 in qualifying retail spend in a calendar month**. This is not a welcome bonus. Under the [card terms, section 7 (PDF)](https://www.adcb.com/en/multimedia/tnc/cb-tnc-touchpoints-credit-card.pdf), ADCB deducts reversals and refunds and excludes disputed purchases and cash withdrawals.

The [programme terms, Schedule B (PDF)](https://www.adcb.com/en/multimedia/tnc/cb-tnc-touchpoints-reward-program.pdf) limit earning each month to spend up to your assigned credit limit. The product page names charity and insurance categories, but the programme terms exclude charitable donations and specified insurance payments. Don’t count donations; ask ADCB whether a particular insurance payment qualifies. These selected rates do not establish a universal return.

## Fee summary

The current ADCB Schedule of Fees supports the annual-membership amount shown above. Relationship concessions are not universal waivers. Fees are inclusive of VAT where applicable. No separate joining-fee amount has been established by this review.

The foreign-currency percentage is an issuer charge, not a promise of the total conversion cost. The fee guide separately states that card-scheme processing is additional.

## Welcome bonus

The Expedia voucher requires annual-fee payment. It is restricted to the designated booking link, eligible properties, the required stay pattern and a validity period. The FAQ says it applies to base room charges, not taxes, fees or multiple rooms. It is not cash and must not be described as automatically offsetting the fee.

## Perks worth using

The page advertises lifestyle and travel benefits. The reviewed state does not establish every guest, lounge, golf or insurance entitlement, so no generic premium-network allowance or cash package value is assigned.

## Key conditions to know

The current product offers a salary **or** credit-limit qualification route. Neither route guarantees approval. Salary-transfer, residency and employment conditions have not all been confirmed, so the previous qualification summary remains unconfirmed rather than presented as a complete rule. Confirm the requirements for the exact product and application channel with ADCB.

This is a bounded field review, not a new whole-card verification date. A retained source date does not certify every historical statement or every linked benefit. Pending terms are set out in the source notes above.

## Watch out for

Confirm your relationship fee, application route and actual benefits before applying. Do not rely on the former universal no-salary-transfer claim or unsupported competitor-return comparison.

## Is it worth it?

Keep it on a shortlist only where the confirmed fee and restricted benefits fit your plans. This partial profile is not a certified all-spend rewards calculation.
