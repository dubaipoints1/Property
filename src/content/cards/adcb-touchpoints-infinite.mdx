## Welcome bonus

The Expedia voucher requires annual-fee payment. It is restricted to the designated booking link, eligible properties, the required stay pattern and a validity period. The FAQ says it applies to base room charges, not taxes, fees or multiple rooms. It is not cash and must not be described as automatically offsetting the fee.

## Perks worth using

The page advertises lifestyle and travel benefits. The reviewed state does not establish every guest, lounge, golf or insurance entitlement, so no generic premium-network allowance or cash package value is assigned.

## Key conditions to know

The current product offers a salary **or** credit-limit qualification route. Neither route guarantees approval. Salary-transfer, residency and employment conditions have not all been confirmed, so the previous qualification summary remains unconfirmed rather than presented as a complete rule. Confirm the requirements for the exact product and application channel with ADCB.

This is a bounded field review, not a new whole-card verification date. A retained source date does not certify every historical statement or every linked benefit. Pending terms are set out in the source notes above.

## Watch out for

Confirm your relationship fee, application route and actual benefits before applying. Do not rely on the former universal no-salary-transfer claim or unsupported competitor-return comparison.

## Is it worth it?

Keep it on a shortlist only where the confirmed fee and restricted benefits fit your plans. This partial profile is not a certified all-spend rewards calculation.
