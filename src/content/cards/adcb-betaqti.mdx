ADCB markets Betaqti specifically to Emiratis. It is a paid TouchPoints card with source-qualified earn categories, a welcome gift and a spend-linked anniversary ladder; it is not a universally high-rate card.

## Earn rates

<EarnRateTable card="adcb-betaqti" />

The headline is an advertised upper bound. Domestic supermarkets, auto dealers, insurance and quick-service restaurants have a different rate from fuel, education, government, real estate, telecoms, charity and transport. EEA merchant classification is separate. The former review incorrectly described reduced categories as earning the headline rate.

## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="adcb-betaqti" />

The current ADCB Schedule of Fees supports the annual-membership amount shown above. Relationship concessions are not universal waivers. Fees are inclusive of VAT where applicable. No separate joining-fee amount has been established by this review.

The foreign-currency percentage is an issuer charge, not a promise of the total conversion cost. The fee guide separately states that card-scheme processing is additional.

## Welcome bonus

The current source calls the welcome award a gift when you get the card. It does not establish the former activation wording or a guaranteed absence of qualifying conditions. Anniversary rewards use annual-spend tiers; do not confuse them with the base earning rate.

## Perks worth using

The captured lounge benefit is conditional. Registration, entitlement checks and a qualifying non-AED transaction condition apply; accompanying guests beyond the entitlement may be charged. The visible visit count is not labelled as an annual quota, so this review does not invent that period.

## Key conditions to know

The current product offers a salary **or** credit-limit qualification route. Neither route guarantees approval. Salary-transfer, residency and employment conditions have not all been confirmed, so the previous qualification summary remains unconfirmed rather than presented as a complete rule. Confirm the requirements for the exact product and application channel with ADCB.

This is a bounded field review, not a new whole-card verification date. A retained source date does not certify every historical statement or every linked benefit. Pending terms are set out in the source notes above.

## Watch out for

The restricted nationality scope, annual cost and reduced-category earn all matter. Do not turn an approximate redemption illustration into a guaranteed TouchPoints cash value or add unrelated perk valuations.

## Is it worth it?

A candidate for an eligible Emirati applicant who wants TouchPoints and can use the actual benefit conditions. Compare the annual fee with benefits you expect to use, rather than treating every advertised perk as cash.
