## Earn rates

<EarnRateTable card="adcb-shukran" />

Named Shukran brands, other eligible retail, reduced UAE merchant categories and EEA merchants have distinct rates. The higher other-retail rate must not be applied to reduced categories. Rewards are credited into the Shukran membership account for redemption within the participating ecosystem.

## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="adcb-shukran" />

The current ADCB Schedule of Fees supports the annual-membership amount shown above. Relationship concessions are not universal waivers. Fees are inclusive of VAT where applicable. No separate joining-fee amount has been established by this review.

The foreign-currency percentage is an issuer charge, not a promise of the total conversion cost. The fee guide separately states that card-scheme processing is additional.

## Welcome bonus

The current product distinguishes applicants with no existing ADCB credit or covered card from existing cardholders, and distinguishes UAE Nationals from other residents. The offer has a qualifying spend window and a crediting timeline. The former fixed early-cancellation clawback assertion is not established by the reviewed source.

## Perks worth using

The current source describes a Shukran Platinum tier upgrade and Reel cinema benefits. Do not treat status or cinema access as guaranteed cash value; booking, scope and promotion expiry still matter.

## Key conditions to know

The current product offers a salary **or** credit-limit qualification route. Neither route guarantees approval. Salary-transfer, residency and employment conditions have not all been confirmed, so the previous qualification summary remains unconfirmed rather than presented as a complete rule. Confirm the requirements for the exact product and application channel with ADCB.

This is a bounded field review, not a new whole-card verification date. A retained source date does not certify every historical statement or every linked benefit. Pending terms are set out in the source notes above.

## Watch out for

The product page says UAE Nationals have no annual fee, while the fee table expresses nationality concessions within relationship columns. That scope mismatch is unresolved. The resident base amount and first-year waiver are supported; do not assume the broader permanent exemption without confirmation.

## Is it worth it?

Most relevant if you already shop the participating Shukran brands and can use the captive rewards. Check the applicable annual-fee profile before relying on a permanent waiver.
