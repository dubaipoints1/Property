The Emirates NBD U by Emaar Infinite is the bank's co-branded Visa Infinite with the U by
Emaar loyalty programme. It is built for the resident whose discretionary spend genuinely
runs through Emaar's footprint — Dubai Mall, the Address/Vida/Rove hotel families, and
Emaar F&B — and earns 7.5% back as UPoints across that universe.

## Perks worth using

Qualifying Emaar spend: 7.5% as U by Emaar; Qualifying other spend: 1.5% as U by Emaar. These are qualifying merchant/category rates, not a single rate for every purchase. Use the exact rows in the table and check the issuer exclusions and caps.

### 7.5% UPoints on Emaar spend

No total annual value is quoted while reward qualification, caps or benefit allowances remain unresolved. Use only the benefits you can verify and actually use; an advertised upper bound is not guaranteed annual value.

### Automatic U by Emaar Platinum status

U by Emaar status has its own activation, validity and participating-venue conditions. Check the current hotel, dining and spa entitlements; status is not an unconditional cash payment.

### Suite upgrades (max 7 nights/year) at Address/Vida/Rove

No total annual value is quoted while reward qualification, caps or benefit allowances remain unresolved. Use only the benefits you can verify and actually use; an advertised upper bound is not guaranteed annual value.

### Visa Airport Companion lounge access — confirm current allowance

The product names Visa Airport Companion access. Confirm the current registration, qualifying-spend, visit and guest conditions for this card; no cash value is assigned to an unconfirmed allowance.

### Reel Cinemas BOGO (AED 5,000 monthly spend gate)

No total annual value is quoted while reward qualification, caps or benefit allowances remain unresolved. Use only the benefits you can verify and actually use; an advertised upper bound is not guaranteed annual value.

### Welcome bonus: up to 25,000 UPoints

Up to 25,000 Upoints: 15,000 on activation and joining-fee payment, plus 10,000 on USD 10,000 qualifying retail spend in the first 3 billing statements. Grand Drive valet is redirected to China Town while expansion work continues.

### Visa Infinite chrome (concierge, valet, EPP)

The issuer states that Grand Drive valet access is unavailable during expansion work and redirects eligible holders to the China Town valet area. Use the current app and venue instructions. Cinema, lounge and other benefits retain their own spend, booking and usage conditions.

## Bottom line

An Emaar-focused card with separate joining and renewal fees, staged welcome rewards and location-specific benefits. The old Grand Drive valet promise is not current. It makes sense only if your real purchases fit those conditions and the benefits you can actually use justify the cost. Confirm pending qualification and benefit terms before applying.
