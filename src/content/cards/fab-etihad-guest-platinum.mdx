## What to check before applying

This is FAB’s conventional Visa Platinum card in its Etihad Guest range, not the Islamic tier. FAB publishes a minimum monthly salary for the named card. That figure does not settle the rest of your eligibility or guarantee approval.

## Annual membership fee

<FeeBlock card="fab-etihad-guest-platinum" />

The published figure is the standard annual fee for the primary card, including VAT. It is not a first-year quote, and we have not included a joining fee, waiver or promotional miles in this profile.

Compare it with [FAB Etihad Guest Signature](/cards/fab-etihad-guest-signature/) and [FAB Etihad Guest Infinite](/cards/fab-etihad-guest-infinite/). Check which purchases qualify, how you would use the miles and what any optional accelerator would cost before valuing rewards.
