## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="emirates-nbd-visa-flexi" />

Read the joining fee, renewal fee and any promotional waiver separately. A renewal figure is not the complete first-year cost, and a current application promotion is not a guarantee that every applicant receives it. The fee table carries the source-qualified VAT treatment. The foreign-currency fee shown is the issuer’s charge, not a total conversion-cost quote. The issuer fee guide states that scheme processing and any acquirer charges may be additional. A merchant’s AED-conversion offer can add a different cost; an issuer-fee waiver does not remove every possible overseas charge.

## Key conditions to know

<dl class="dp-conditions">
  <dt>Plus Points earn structure</dt>
  <dd>Native Plus Points and source-stated percentage rows are not unconditional cash returns. Cashback redemption is AED 0.75 per point, while selected non-cash redemptions are AED 1. Per-statement caps and exclusions apply.</dd>

  <dt>Salary transfer</dt>
  <dd>The issuer product page states a minimum monthly salary of AED 12,000. The reviewed product, Key Facts Statement and shared credit-card terms do not establish a universal salary-transfer requirement. Confirm the conditions of your own application; we do not assume a transfer-related clawback or that none can apply.</dd>

  <dt>Annual fee</dt>
  <dd>AED 0 on the standing promo. SoC standard renewal AED 735 — confirm with ENBD that the waiver applies to your application before relying on the AED 0 figure long-term.</dd>

  <dt>Foreign-currency fee</dt>
  <dd>The published foreign-currency percentage is the VAT-inclusive issuer fee, not an all-in conversion cost; scheme processing and any acquirer charges may be additional.</dd>

  <dt>Category MCC mapping</dt>
  <dd>Groceries map to MCC 5411 (LuLu, Carrefour, Spinneys, Choithrams). Fuel runs on 5541 / 5542 (ADNOC, ENOC, EPPCO). Verify with a small test transaction before assuming a hybrid merchant codes the way you expect.</dd>

## Watch out for

A current welcome incentive is not assumed unless its value, eligibility and qualification period are established in the issuer terms. Any pending offer is excluded from a guaranteed return estimate.

## Bottom line

A selectable-benefit Plus Points card. Conflicting old and new benefit menus do not establish that every listed perk can be held together. It makes sense only if your real purchases fit those conditions and the benefits you can actually use justify the cost. Confirm pending qualification and benefit terms before applying.
