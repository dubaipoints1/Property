## What to check before applying

The bank publishes a minimum salary, but meeting that figure is not approval. Review the documents and other requirements on the named issuer page. Make sure you are looking at Emirates NBD’s Platinum tier: another SHARE card or issuer can have different terms.

## Membership fees

<FeeBlock card="emirates-nbd-share-visa-platinum" />

No standard joining or annual membership fee is not the same as no card charges. The issuer lists different SHARE points terms by category, so check how your intended purchases earn and where you can redeem before comparing this card with one that pays cash.
