The Liv Cashback+ is the premium half of Liv's two-card lineup — a
Mastercard World product from Emirates NBD's digital brand whose entire
proposition rests on one mechanic: your cashback rate is a tier set by
your total spend that month, applied to everything you spent.

## Earn rates

<EarnRateTable card="liv-cashback-plus" />

The 1% in the table is the **guaranteed base tier** — the rate you earn
in a month where total spend stays under AED 7,000. The full tier ladder,
per Liv's feature terms:

<HotTip label="How the tiers actually work">
Your rate is set by **total spend across the card in the calendar
month**, and once a tier is reached it applies to **all** of that month's
spend: **1%** from AED 0–6,999 of total spend, **2%** from AED
7,000–14,999, and **4%** at AED 15,000+. This is not a category table —
it is a retroactive monthly multiplier. Spend AED 14,500 and the whole
month earns 2%; spend AED 15,100 and the whole month earns 4%. Low-margin
merchant categories — government, utilities, bills — earn **0.1%**
whatever tier you reach. Cashback is capped at **AED 1,500/month**, and
additionally bounded by a percentage of your credit limit: Liv's own
illustration is that an AED 20,000 limit caps cashback at AED 800/month,
so your real ceiling may be lower than AED 1,500.
</HotTip>

## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="liv-cashback-plus" />

AED 700 joining fee and AED 700 annual fee. The joining fee is waived
with an active Liv Max subscription. The 1.99% FX margin is genuinely
low for the UAE market — most issuers sit between 2.5% and 3.5% — and
the monthly finance rate is 3.25% (39% APR), with an AED 175 late fee
and AED 25 card replacement per the Liv charges page.

## Skywards conversion

Rewards accrue as points with two exits: cashback against the card, or
**Emirates Skywards Miles — up to 10,000 Miles a month** on this card.
Every redemption, either direction, needs a **500-point minimum**. This
is the feature that separates the Cashback+ from the rest of the UAE
cashback field: an entry-tier product whose earnings can leave as
airline miles through ENBD's first-party Skywards relationship. If you
value Skywards Miles above one fil each, run the conversion before
taking the cash.

## Key conditions to know

<dl class="dp-conditions">
  <dt>Tier-by-total-spend</dt>
  <dd>The advertised 4% requires AED 15,000+ of total spend in the
  calendar month; 2% requires AED 7,000. The guaranteed floor is 1%.
  The achieved tier applies to all of that month's spend.</dd>

  <dt>Two caps, not one</dt>
  <dd>AED 1,500/month cashback cap, plus a bound tied to a percentage of
  your credit limit — Liv illustrates an AED 20,000 limit capping at
  AED 800/month. The lower of the two governs.</dd>

  <dt>Redemption</dt>
  <dd>500-point minimum per redemption, as cashback or as Skywards Miles
  (up to 10,000 Miles/month on this card).</dd>

  <dt>One card per customer</dt>
  <dd>Liv issues either the Cashback+ or the Cashback to a given
  customer, never both. Applying for this card is choosing it over the
  free-for-life alternative.</dd>

  <dt>Eligibility</dt>
  <dd>No minimum salary is published on Liv's product page, feature
  terms or KFS — application and approval run through the Liv X app,
  which is why our spec table shows the salary bar as
  <strong>"Pending"</strong> rather than a number we cannot source. UAE
  residency required.</dd>

## Cashback+ vs Cashback

<CardComparison cards={["liv-cashback-plus", "liv-cashback"]} />

The comparison is really a bet on your own spend. The free
[Liv Cashback](/cards/liv-cashback/) tops out at 2% (at AED 10,000+ of
monthly spend) with an AED 750 cap; the Cashback+ costs AED 700 a year
and tops out at 4% (at AED 15,000+) with an AED 1,500 cap plus the
bigger perk stack. At AED 15,000 of monthly spend the 2-point rate gap
is worth up to AED 300 a month — comfortably clearing the fee. At AED
6,000 of monthly spend both cards pay their floor rates and the fee
buys you lounges and insurance, not cashback.

## Watch out for

- **The floor, not the headline.** Budget your expected cashback at 1%
  and treat the 2%/4% tiers as upside in months you genuinely clear them.
- **The credit-limit bound.** A modest credit limit can cap your
  cashback well below AED 1,500 regardless of spend.
- **Low-margin categories.** Government, utility and bill payments earn
  0.1% and still count toward your tier — spend them here for the tier,
  but don't expect earnings from them.
- **The one-card rule.** You cannot trial the free card first and
  upgrade later while holding both; it is one or the other.

## Bottom line

For the AED 15,000+/month household, this is one of the UAE's strongest
flat-earn cards — 4% on everything, a low 1.99% FX fee, a real perk
stack and a Skywards exit. **Apply** if your spend clears the top tier
most months and your credit limit will support the cap. **Take the free
[Liv Cashback](/cards/liv-cashback/)** if your spend sits below AED
7,000 — same mechanic, no fee. **Look elsewhere** if you need a
published salary bar and predictable category rates rather than an
app-assessed approval and a spend-tiered rate.
