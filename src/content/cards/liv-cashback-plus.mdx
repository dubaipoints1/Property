The Liv Cashback+ is the premium half of Liv's two-card lineup — a
Mastercard World product from Emirates NBD's digital brand whose entire
proposition rests on one mechanic: your cashback rate is a tier set by
your total spend that month, applied to qualifying general retail rather than every transaction.

## Earn rates

<EarnRateTable card="liv-cashback-plus" />

The 1% in the table is the **base tier on qualifying general retail** — the rate you earn
in a month where total spend stays under AED 7,000. The full tier ladder,
per Liv's feature terms:

<HotTip label="How the tiers actually work">
Your rate is set by **total spend across the card in the calendar
month**, and once a tier is reached it applies to that month’s qualifying
general-retail purchases: **1%** from AED 0–6,999 of total spend, **2%** from AED
7,000–14,999, and **4%** at AED 15,000+. This is not a category table —
it is a retroactive monthly multiplier for qualifying general retail. Low-margin
merchant categories — government, utilities, bills — earn **0.1%**
whatever tier you reach. Cashback is capped at **AED 1,500/month**, and
additionally bounded by a percentage of your credit limit: Liv's own
illustration is that an AED 20,000 limit caps cashback at AED 800/month,
so your real ceiling may be lower than AED 1,500.
</HotTip>

## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="liv-cashback-plus" />

Liv’s published foreign-currency transaction percentage is an issuer component, exclusive of applicable VAT and separate Mastercard processing. It must not be compared with another card’s all-in fee or used to promise a precise overseas net return. The base tier applies to qualifying retail, not every transaction; reduced categories and the assigned-credit-limit cap still apply.

## Skywards conversion

Rewards accrue as points with two exits: cashback against the card, or
**Emirates Skywards Miles — up to 10,000 Miles a month** on this card.
Every redemption, either direction, needs a **500-point minimum**. This
is the feature that separates the Cashback+ from the rest of the UAE
cashback field: an entry-tier product whose earnings can leave as
airline miles through ENBD's first-party Skywards relationship. If you
value Skywards Miles above one fil each, run the conversion before
taking the cash.

## Key conditions to know

<dl class="dp-conditions">
  <dt>Tier-by-total-spend</dt>
  <dd>The advertised 4% requires AED 15,000+ of total spend in the calendar month; 2% requires AED 7,000. The base qualifying-retail tier is 1%. The achieved tier applies to qualifying general retail, while reduced low-merchant rates remain applicable.</dd>
  <dt>Two caps, not one</dt>
  <dd>AED 1,500/month cashback cap, plus a bound tied to a percentage of your credit limit — Liv illustrates an AED 20,000 limit capping at AED 800/month. The lower of the two governs.</dd>
  <dt>Redemption</dt>
  <dd>500-point minimum per redemption, as cashback or as Skywards Miles (up to 10,000 Miles/month on this card).</dd>
  <dt>One card per customer</dt>
  <dd>Liv issues either the Cashback+ or the Cashback to a given customer, never both. Applying for this card is choosing it over the free-for-life alternative.</dd>

## Cashback+ vs Cashback

<CardComparison cards={["liv-cashback-plus", "liv-cashback"]} />

The comparison is really a bet on your own spend. The free
[Liv Cashback](/cards/liv-cashback/) tops out at 2% (at AED 10,000+ of
monthly spend) with an AED 750 cap; the Cashback+ has the annual fee shown above
and tops out at 4% (at AED 15,000+) with an AED 1,500 cap plus the
bigger perk stack. Their spend-tier thresholds differ; a single monthly
spend figure does not place both cards on the same tier. Compare qualifying
general retail, reduced categories, the assigned-credit-limit bound and fees
before deciding whether the paid card offers more usable value.

## Watch out for

- **The floor, not the headline.** Budget your expected cashback at 1%
  and treat the 2%/4% tiers as upside in months you genuinely clear them.
- **The credit-limit bound.** A modest credit limit can cap your
  cashback well below AED 1,500 regardless of spend.
- **Low-margin categories.** Government, utility and bill payments earn
  0.1% and still count toward your tier — spend them here for the tier,
  but don't expect earnings from them.
- **The one-card rule.** You cannot trial the free card first and
  upgrade later while holding both; it is one or the other.
