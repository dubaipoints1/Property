The HSBC Emirates Skywards Signature Credit Card is a direct Miles-earning
product. Its standing earning and fee terms can be compared, but its
employer-dependent income requirements are not represented as a universal salary
qualification.

## Earning basis and exclusions

<EarnRateTable card="hsbc-skywards-signature" />

The rates use USD-equivalent spending, not a Miles-per-AED basis. Reduced-sector
rates in this profile apply to the issuer's specified AED categories. The higher
direct Emirates and flydubai earning opportunity is not treated as a rate for all
airfare, and welcome incentives are not included in the standing calculation.

## Fees

<FeeBlock card="hsbc-skywards-signature" />

The listed annual fee includes VAT. The foreign-currency amount is the issuer
processing fee including VAT, not the total cost of conversion. Scheme charges
may also apply.

## Eligibility must be checked with HSBC

The product page distinguishes employees of approved companies from other
applicants. The KFS also states a salary requirement. This profile therefore
leaves the universal salary field Pending and does not put the card into
salary-qualified recommendations.

That is not a claim that the card has no minimum. Ask HSBC which route applies to
your employer and application, and read its current documents before applying.
You can still [compare the published terms](/cards/compare/?cards=hsbc-skywards-signature,hsbc-skywards-infinite)
with the Infinite card without treating either profile as an approval decision.
