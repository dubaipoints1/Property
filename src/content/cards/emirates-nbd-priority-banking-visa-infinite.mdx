## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="emirates-nbd-priority-banking-visa-infinite" />

Read the joining fee, renewal fee and any promotional waiver separately. A renewal figure is not the complete first-year cost, and a current application promotion is not a guarantee that every applicant receives it. The fee table carries the source-qualified VAT treatment. The foreign-currency fee shown is the issuer’s charge, not a total conversion-cost quote. The issuer fee guide states that scheme processing and any acquirer charges may be additional. A merchant’s AED-conversion offer can add a different cost; an issuer-fee waiver does not remove every possible overseas charge.

Plus Points accrual is not a cash percentage. Read the exact native-unit rows, reduced categories and statement cap alongside the redemption channel; statement cashback is below the selected non-cash redemption value.

### Plus Points earn (1% / 2% / 5% by transaction size)

No total annual value is quoted while reward qualification, caps or benefit allowances remain unresolved. Use only the benefits you can verify and actually use; an advertised upper bound is not guaranteed annual value.

### Up to 4 valet visits a month (Abu Dhabi)

A named benefit is not evidence of unlimited visits, guest admission or an unrestricted booking allowance. Use the current issuer/provider conditions for the qualifying spend, booking channel, fees and usage limit; unconfirmed allowances are not used for benefit matching.

### Airport lounge access (Visa Infinite)

The issuer names Visa Airport Companion. Confirm the visit allowance, qualifying
spend, guest treatment and registration conditions with your relationship manager.
No cash value is assigned to an unconfirmed allowance.

### Airport transfers, golf, courier

Plus Points are not cash at a single universal rate. The issuer currently states AED 0.75 per point for statement cashback, while selected Rewards Hub, participating-store and other non-cash channels offer AED 1 per point. Do not value a cashback example at par.

## Watch out for

The issuer restricts this card to existing Priority Banking customers. It does not publish a numerical minimum salary on this product page; this is not a zero-salary eligibility claim. Confirm the applicable relationship and application conditions.

## Bottom line

A relationship product for existing Emirates NBD Priority Banking customers, not a
standalone approval route. Check your personalised fee and the current benefit
conditions against services you actually use. Neither relationship status nor a
list of advertised perks establishes that the card recovers its cost. Do not open
a wider banking relationship solely on the basis of unconfirmed card benefits.
