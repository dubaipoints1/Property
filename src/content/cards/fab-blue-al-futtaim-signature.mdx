## Which Blue FAB card is this?

This is the conventional Signature tier in the Blue FAB range by Al-Futtaim, not the Islamic card. Compare it with [Blue FAB Infinite](/cards/fab-blue-al-futtaim-infinite/) and [Blue FAB Platinum](/cards/fab-blue-al-futtaim-platinum/) if you’re choosing between the conventional tiers.
