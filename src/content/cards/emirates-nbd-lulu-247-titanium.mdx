## LuLu 247 Titanium vs Platinum

Titanium has a lower published salary threshold and lower headline LuLu rewards than Platinum. Compare the settled categories and each card’s joining and renewal costs; a higher rate does not automatically recover a fee.

Airport lounge access was discontinued from 1 May 2025. LuLu member benefits remain subject to their own conditions; free shipping is not an unconditional entitlement.

## What you get — per-benefit value

The linked rewards terms value 1 LuLu Point at AED 1 when redeemed through the specified LuLu channels. It is a closed-loop shopping reward, not transferable cash or airline miles. Billing-cycle caps and qualifying categories still apply.

## Bottom line

The entry-level LuLu co-brand is a narrower fit for a household that shops at LuLu and meets the published eligibility profile. Platinum has different fees and headline rewards, but reduced-category conflicts and caps prevent a blanket claim that every meaningful return doubles. Compare your qualifying spend before choosing.
