The LuLu 247 Titanium is the entry-level half of Emirates NBD's co-branded Mastercard pair
with LuLu Hypermarkets — a free-for-life card that opens LuLu cashback to households on
modest salaries who still do their weekly shop at LuLu.

## Earn rates

All earn is paid as **LuLu Points**, redeemable 1:1 at the till at any LuLu Hypermarket,
LuLu Express or on luluhypermarket.com:

<HotTip>
A household putting **AED 1,000 a month** through LuLu earns AED 35 a month in points;
**AED 2,000 a month** earns AED 70; **AED 3,000 a month** earns AED 105 — roughly AED
1,260 a year in groceries-back at the higher spend benchmark, on a card with no annual
fee. The cap on value is the captive currency: those points only spend at LuLu.
</HotTip>

## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="emirates-nbd-lulu-247-titanium" />

The card is free for life — AED 0 annual fee, no spend threshold, no clawback. The costs
that bite are a **1.99% foreign-currency fee** and a **3.69% monthly interest rate** if you revolve a balance. All published fees include 5% VAT.

## LuLu 247 Titanium vs Platinum

The Titanium is the lower-salary sibling. If you can clear AED 12,000 a month on salary,
the doubled earn covers the gap easily — twice the LuLu rate, though the Platinum is not
free (AED 525 to join, AED 262.50 a year):

| Feature | Titanium | [Platinum](/cards/emirates-nbd-lulu-247-platinum/) |
|---|---|---|
| Min salary | **AED 5,000** | AED 12,000 |
| LuLu earn rate | 3.5% | **7%** |
| Fuel earn rate | 2% | **4%** |
| Utilities earn rate | 1% | **2%** |
| Other spend earn rate | 0.35% | 0.7% |
| Lounge access | None (ended 1 May 2025) | 1,000+ lounges (Travel Pass) |
| Annual fee | Free for life | AED 262.50 (AED 525 to join) |

## What you get — per-benefit value

LuLu Points carry a published face value — 1 point = AED 0.01 at the till
— so the earn prices cleanly. Counted against the AED 0 fee (free for
life).

### 3.5% LuLu earn

**Value to me: AED 420/year** on AED 1,000 a month of LuLu spend;
**AED 1,260/year** at AED 3,000 a month. Half the Platinum's rate on the
identical shop — paid as captive till credit.

### Fuel earn

2% as LuLu Points.
**Value to me: AED 120/year** on AED 500 a month of fuel.

### Utilities earn

1% as LuLu Points on utility bill payments.
**Value to me: AED 120/year** on AED 1,000 a month of bills.

### Lounge access

None. The product page states: "Effective 1st May 2025, Airport Lounge
Access will be discontinued."

**Total package:** roughly **AED 660/year** for a household putting
AED 1,000 a month through LuLu, AED 500 on fuel and AED 1,000 on bills,
against no fee. Every line is upside — but a holder above the AED 12,000
salary bar doubles the two biggest lines by moving to the
[Platinum](/cards/emirates-nbd-lulu-247-platinum/) at the same AED 0.

## Watch out for

- **Opportunity cost vs Platinum.** You earn half the points the Platinum does on LuLu,
  fuel and base spend. If your salary clears AED 12,000, the Platinum is the better hold
  once your LuLu spend clears its AED 262.50 annual fee.
- **Captive currency.** LuLu Points only redeem inside the LuLu ecosystem. If you shop
  mostly at Carrefour, Spinneys or Union Coop, a flat-rate cashback card is the simpler hold.
- **Weak base earn.** 0.35% on spend outside LuLu, fuel and utilities is the lowest base rate in the ENBD
  card stack — pair this card with a broad cashback card for everything outside groceries
  and petrol.
- **No lounge access.** It ended on 1 May 2025; older comparisons that list it are out of date.

## Bottom line

The entry-level LuLu co-brand: half the rates of its Platinum sibling, half the salary bar,
and unlike the Platinum, free for life. For a LuLu-loyal household earning between AED 5,000 and AED
12,000 a month, this is the card that fits the slot — AED 1,000 of monthly LuLu spend
returns AED 35 in points, AED 3,000 returns AED 105. **Apply**
if LuLu is your weekly shop and your salary sits under the AED 12,000 Platinum gate.
**Skip** if you earn above that — the [Platinum](/cards/emirates-nbd-lulu-247-platinum/)
doubles every meaningful rate for AED 262.50 a year — or if you shop elsewhere.
