## Watch out for

## Bottom line

A Skywards card with a payable joining fee and rewards limited by named partner categories, merchant codes and statement limits. Silver status follows card validity. It makes sense only if your real purchases fit those conditions and the benefits you can actually use justify the cost. Confirm pending qualification and benefit terms before applying.
