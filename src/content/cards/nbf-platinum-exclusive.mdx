## What to check before applying

If you are salaried, use the published figure as an initial check. NBF can also assess an application against a fixed deposit. Ask the bank what it would require for that route rather than treating the salary figure as an approval test.

[Visa Infinite](/cards/nbf-visa-infinite/) has its own Priority alternative. That wording does not establish a Platinum Exclusive route. For the separate Islamic card, read [NBF Islamic Platinum Exclusive](/cards/nbf-islamic-platinum-exclusive/).
