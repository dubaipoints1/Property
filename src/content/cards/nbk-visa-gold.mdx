## Compare the ongoing fee

Gold’s published renewal fee sits between Classic and Platinum. Compare that ongoing charge with the benefits you expect to use, rather than deciding on the first-year annual fee alone.

<FeeBlock card="nbk-visa-gold" />

The bank advertises the first-year annual fee as waived. That is not a statement that joining or other first-year costs are zero. The linked disclosure gives the standard renewal charge before VAT.

## Check the account requirement

NBK UAE says you need an NBK account to request the card. The disclosure describes different ways to qualify, so confirm with the bank which route applies to you. There is no standalone salary minimum verified for our Finder. This review does not price rewards, a welcome offer or foreign spending.
