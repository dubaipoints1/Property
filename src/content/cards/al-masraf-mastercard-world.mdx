## Standard primary annual fee

<FeeBlock card="al-masraf-mastercard-world" />

The [conventional credit-card Key Facts Statement](https://www.almasraf.ae/pdf/kfs-credit-card.pdf) directs readers to the [retail tariff](https://www.almasraf.ae/pdf/schedule-of-charges-retail-banking.pdf) for the amount. The tariff, which the bank still links and which carries a 2023 effective date, lists the standard World annual fee before VAT. That does not establish what you would pay at opening or renewal, or whether a separate joining fee or waiver applies.

The bank’s [fee-waiver campaign page](https://www.almasraf.ae/free-for-life) lists 14 June 2026 as the last day of the offer. Do not rely on it as a current waiver. The bank uses a shared enquiry link; new applications for this tier and individual eligibility remain unconfirmed. This reference is not included in the active catalogue, comparisons or fee tables.
