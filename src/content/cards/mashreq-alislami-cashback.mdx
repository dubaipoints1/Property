## Which Mashreq cashback card is this?

This is the base Cashback Visa credit card from Mashreq Al Islami, Mashreq’s Islamic banking division. It is separate from the [conventional Mashreq Cashback Credit Card](/cards/mashreq-cashback/) and from both 24X7 Cashback products.

## Standard primary annual fee

<FeeBlock card="mashreq-alislami-cashback" />

Mashreq’s Islamic Key Facts Statement and its card charges schedule list no recurring annual membership fee for the primary card. That does not establish the total cost of using the card. Joining charges, foreign-currency costs, eligibility, rewards and welcome offers are not verified in this profile.
