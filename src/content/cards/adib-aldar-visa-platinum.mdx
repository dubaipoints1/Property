The terms name Aldar Education, Properties, Malls and Hospitality categories, but also impose a Participating Aldar Ecosystem limitation whose interaction with overseas rows remains unresolved.

## What the fee figure does and does not cover

The displayed annual fee is the standard primary-card charge from the reviewed
source row. It is not a first-year or total annual cost. Joining charges, any
waiver and profit charges remain Pending; an omitted field does not mean zero.
Welcome incentives and perks have not been fully verified or assigned a cash value.

## Before applying

A named Aldar Education or Properties rate is not a rate for all UAE spending. Confirm the ecosystem condition and merchant classification before choosing the card.

Read the current issuer product page, KFS and linked programme conditions. Salary,
residency, employment and transfer requirements are not fully reconciled in this
profile. Any income amount retained in the source record does not establish
complete eligibility or guarantee approval.

Use [the comparison tool](/cards/compare/?cards=adib-aldar-visa-platinum,adib-cashback-visa) to inspect
the source-qualified terms beside the existing Cashback profile, without assuming
that unlike reward units or unresolved charges are equivalent.
