The du Titanium name appears in FAB’s fee schedule; the bank markets it as the du Credit Card. The [card-specific terms](https://www.bankfab.com/-/media/fab-uds/personal/terms-and-conditions-consolidated/card-specific/ducreditcard_tnc.pdf?view=1) say you do not have to be a du customer to apply. That is separate from eligibility for a reward on du spending.
