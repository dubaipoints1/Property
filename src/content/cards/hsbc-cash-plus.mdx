HSBC Cash+ is a cashback credit card with a standing everyday rate rather than a
category-led headline. Its simple base should not be confused with an unlimited
return: HSBC limits cashback-eligible purchases by the assigned credit-card limit.

## What the base rate covers

<EarnRateTable card="hsbc-cash-plus" />

The issuer describes eligible purchases and a credit-limit restriction. Your own
limit is not published in the product documents, so we do not invent a cap or
calculate a total cashback return from unrestricted spending.

Cash+ also advertises periodic bonus cashback tied to spending conditions. That
is not part of the base rate shown here. Compare those conditions with spending
you would make anyway, not extra purchases made to reach a reward.

## Fees and conditions

<FeeBlock card="hsbc-cash-plus" />

The standard annual fee and introductory waiver are separate. The profile does
not assume an ongoing waiver whose qualifying conditions have not been fully
reconciled. The foreign-currency figure is the VAT-inclusive issuer processing
fee, not a total conversion cost; scheme charges may also apply.

## Before applying

Check the current product page and KFS, your own credit limit and the conditions
for any bonus or fee waiver. HSBC says an account with it is not required for this
card. Meeting the listed income and age conditions does not guarantee approval.
