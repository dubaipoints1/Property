## What to check before applying

This Islamic World Mastercard earns RAKrewards and is separate from the [conventional RAKBANK World Credit Card](/cards/rakbank-world/). The published minimum is for salaried applicants. The Key Facts Statement sets out different financial conditions for self-employed applicants, plus residence and security-cheque requirements. The salary figure alone does not establish eligibility.

## Primary annual fee and other charges

<FeeBlock card="rakbank-islamic-world" />

The standard annual fee shown here is for the primary card only. RAKBANK’s published documents describe supplementary-card fees differently. Confirm what it would charge for the additional cards you need.

We have not confirmed an all-in foreign-currency cost, a RAKrewards earn rate or a current welcome offer. Check the applicable terms for your purchases before comparing value.
