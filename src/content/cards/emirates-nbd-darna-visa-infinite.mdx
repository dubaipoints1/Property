## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="emirates-nbd-darna-visa-infinite" />

Read the joining fee, renewal fee and any promotional waiver separately. A renewal figure is not the complete first-year cost, and a current application promotion is not a guarantee that every applicant receives it. The fee table carries the source-qualified VAT treatment. The foreign-currency fee shown is the issuer’s charge, not a total conversion-cost quote. The issuer fee guide states that scheme processing and any acquirer charges may be additional. A merchant’s AED-conversion offer can add a different cost; an issuer-fee waiver does not remove every possible overseas charge.

## What you get — per-benefit value

Darna publishes its conversion — 10 Darna Points = AED 1 — so the earn carries honest AED
figures, with the standing caveat that the value only exists inside the Darna partner
network.

### 10% back at Darna partners

No total annual value is quoted while reward qualification, caps or benefit allowances remain unresolved. Use only the benefits you can verify and actually use; an advertised upper bound is not guaranteed annual value.

## Watch out for

The issuer product page states a minimum monthly salary of AED 30,000. The reviewed product, Key Facts Statement and shared credit-card terms do not establish a universal salary-transfer requirement. Confirm the conditions of your own application; we do not assume a transfer-related clawback or that none can apply.

## Bottom line

An Aldar-focused premium card with separate joining and renewal charges. Reduced categories, exclusions and the monthly cap matter alongside the headline rate. It makes sense only if your real purchases fit those conditions and the benefits you can actually use justify the cost. Confirm pending qualification and benefit terms before applying.
