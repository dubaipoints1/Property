## What to check before applying

HSBC links this card to a Premier Account. If you do not have that account, check its requirements and costs with HSBC before treating the card as an option. Having the account does not guarantee that the bank will issue the card.

This card earns HSBC Rewards points, not Emirates Skywards Miles directly and not direct cashback. If you are deciding between Premier and a card with a different reward currency, see the [HSBC card line-up](/banks/hsbc/) and compare what you expect to redeem before checking the current earn and redemption terms.
