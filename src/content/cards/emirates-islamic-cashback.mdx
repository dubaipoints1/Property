## Which Emirates Islamic Cashback card is this?

This is the bank’s base Cashback Visa Covered Card, called Cashback Platinum in the profit-based fee schedule. It has separate terms from [Cashback Plus](/cards/emirates-islamic-cashback-plus/) and [Switch Cashback](/cards/emirates-islamic-switch-cashback/).
