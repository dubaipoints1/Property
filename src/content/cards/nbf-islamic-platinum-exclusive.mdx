## What to check before applying

Use the published minimum as an initial check if you are salaried. The bank can assess applications against income or a pledged fixed deposit. Ask which route it would consider and what additional conditions it applies.

[Visa Infinite](/cards/nbf-islamic-visa-infinite/) publishes a separate Priority alternative. Do not assume it applies to Platinum Exclusive.
