## Which CBD card is this?

This is the conventional Visa Infinite credit card from Commercial Bank of Dubai (CBD). Its annual-fee terms are specific to this card, not the bank’s Visa Signature products.

## First-year annual fee and standard renewal

<FeeBlock card="cbd-visa-infinite" />

CBD’s Key Facts Statement waives the primary card’s annual fee in the first year. The standard renewal amount shown includes VAT. The waiver does not establish the total cost of opening or using the card. Joining charges, foreign-currency costs, eligibility, rewards and welcome offers are outside this profile’s verification scope.

CBD’s product page also describes a separate spend-based renewal waiver. Check its current conditions before assuming you will pay less than the standard renewal fee.
