Mashreq Cashback is a dining-led Visa card paying cashback in AED. That does not
make its qualifying spending unlimited or every local purchase eligible for the
same rate.

## Earn rates and source conditions

<EarnRateTable card="mashreq-cashback" />

The current product page restricts cashback-eligible spending in a statement
cycle to the assigned credit-card limit. Your own limit is not a public source
value, so it is not entered as an invented numeric cap.

The product page describes other local spending with an upper bound; the KFS
states an exact other-UAE rate. The stored base remains withheld pending
reconciliation. A reduced sector rate also does not override the explicit
exclusion for bill payments through Mashreq Online and Mobile Banking App.

## Fees

<FeeBlock card="mashreq-cashback" />

The fee figures retain their prior source record; this is not a whole-card
re-verification. Check the current KFS for the applied foreign-currency charges,
repayment terms and other costs before applying. Carrying a balance can outweigh
cashback.

## Welcome offer

<WelcomeBonusCallout card="mashreq-cashback" />

A welcome incentive is separate from standing cashback. Read its current
customer, spend-window and eligible-transaction conditions; do not spend extra
merely to qualify.

## Is it useful for your spending?

It may be a useful dining card if the issuer's merchant classification, your
assigned limit and the eligible payment channel match how you actually spend.
It is not presented as a set-and-forget household return or as a guarantee that
every restaurant, bill or local purchase earns a headline reward.

No per-benefit annual values or unrestricted-spend totals are shown while the
cap and local-rate conditions remain unresolved. Use [Compare](/cards/compare/?cards=mashreq-cashback,citi-cashback)
to read the standing terms and source notes beside another card.
