## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="emirates-nbd-webshopper" />

Read the joining fee, renewal fee and any promotional waiver separately. A renewal figure is not the complete first-year cost, and a current application promotion is not a guarantee that every applicant receives it. The fee table carries the source-qualified VAT treatment. The foreign-currency fee shown is the issuer’s charge, not a total conversion-cost quote. The issuer fee guide states that scheme processing and any acquirer charges may be additional. A merchant’s AED-conversion offer can add a different cost; an issuer-fee waiver does not remove every possible overseas charge.

## Watch out for

The foreign-currency fee shown is the issuer’s charge, not a total conversion-cost quote. The issuer fee guide states that scheme processing and any acquirer charges may be additional. A merchant’s AED-conversion offer can add a different cost; an issuer-fee waiver does not remove every possible overseas charge.

## Bottom line

An online-only card with separate joining and renewal fees. It cannot replace a normal card for in-store payments or cash withdrawals. It makes sense only if your real purchases fit those conditions and the benefits you can actually use justify the cost. Confirm pending qualification and benefit terms before applying.
