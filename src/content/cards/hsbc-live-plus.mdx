## Cashback

Cashback is paid monthly into the card. The accelerated rates apply only when you spend
at least AED 3,000 in the month, and each category is capped:

<EarnRateTable card="hsbc-live-plus" />

<HotTip>
Two conditions shape the cashback. First, you must spend at least **AED 3,000** in a
month, or the bonus categories drop to the 0.5% base. Second, each bonus bucket is
capped at **AED 200** per statement cycle — **6% dining**, **5% fuel**, and **2%
groceries and entertainment combined** — so the most you can earn on the accelerated
categories is **AED 600 a month**. The 6% dining rate therefore tops out at about AED
3,300 of restaurant spend.
</HotTip>

## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="hsbc-live-plus" />

There's no annual fee in the first year, then **AED 313.95** (incl. VAT) — waived if you
spend at least **AED 12,000** in the prior 12 months. The fee block above shows the
VAT-inclusive issuer processing fee. It is not a total conversion cost: HSBC's KFS
says standard scheme processing charges may also apply. The KFS publishes APR, but a
separate monthly rate has not been corroborated and is excluded from payoff defaults.

## What you get — per-benefit value

### Category cashback

Worked at AED 1,500 of dining (6% = AED 90), AED 800 of fuel (5% = AED 40)
and AED 2,000 of groceries (2% = AED 40) a month — clearing the AED 3,000
floor, all inside the AED 200 caps: AED 170/month.
**Value to me: ~AED 2,040/year** on that profile. The full-cap ceiling is
AED 600/month — **AED 7,200/year** — needing about AED 3,300 of dining,
AED 4,000 of fuel and AED 10,000 of groceries-and-entertainment a month.

### 12 lounge visits (Visa Airport Companion)

Unlocked by one USD 1+ international purchase a year. At the AED 200 walk-in
equivalent: **Value to me: AED 800/year** on four flown trips, up to
**AED 2,400/year** at the full ration.

### Careem Plus + Zomato Gold (12 months each)

Both are memberships HSBC doesn't price on the product page, so we don't
invent a figure. **Value to me: the subscription price you'd otherwise have
paid — AED 0 if you wouldn't have.**

### Cinema buy-one-get-one (AED 6,000 monthly spend gate)

At the AED 40–50 typical UAE ticket, one paired visit a month saves one
ticket. **Value to me: AED 480–600/year** in months you clear the AED 6,000
gate.

**Total package:** ~AED 2,040 of cashback plus ~AED 800 of four-trip lounge
value and ~AED 480–600 of cinema saving is roughly **AED 3,300–3,450 a
year** against a fee of AED 0 in year one and AED 313.95 after — waived at
just AED 12,000 of annual spend, which the worked profile clears several
times over. The memberships stack on top for readers who'd have paid for
them anyway.

## Watch out for

- **The AED 3,000 gate and AED 200 caps.** The headline rates suit moderate, spread
  spend — a single big category maxes its AED 200 cap fast, and a quiet month drops you
  to 0.5%.
- **0.5% base is weak.** Spend outside dining, fuel, groceries and entertainment earns
  almost nothing — pair Live+ with a flat-rate card for the rest.
- **Eligibility conflict.** The current product page and KFS publish different
  requirements. Confirm the rule HSBC will apply to your employer and application.
- **Lounge condition.** You must make a small international purchase each year to keep
  the complimentary lounge visits.

## Is it worth it?

For a UAE resident who spends across dining, fuel and groceries and travels a few times
a year, Live+ is one of the better mainstream picks: high (if capped) category cashback,
a separately stated issuer FX fee, no first-year fee, and a perk stack — Careem Plus, Zomato Gold, lounge
access, travel insurance — that many fee-charging cards don't match. New arrivals get a
genuine head start through HSBC's international credit-history sharing.

It's a weaker fit for heavy single-category spenders (the caps bite) or anyone whose
spend sits mostly outside the bonus categories (0.5% base). As ever with a cashback card,
the value assumes you clear the balance in full each month.

## Bottom line

A category-cashback option worth checking when the qualifying spend and caps fit.
Confirm eligibility with HSBC before applying, and compare the full foreign-currency
cost rather than the issuer processing fee alone. **Skip** if you spend heavily in
one category or mostly off-category.
