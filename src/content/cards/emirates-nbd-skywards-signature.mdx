## Earn rates

Earn rates are credited as **Skywards Miles per USD 1** of qualifying spend. Keep the source denomination when comparing cards.

Emirates and named partner categories: 1.5 Skywards Miles per USD 1; Qualifying international spend: 1 Skywards Miles per USD 1; Qualifying domestic spend: 0.75 Skywards Miles per USD 1. These are qualifying merchant/category rates, not a single rate for every purchase. Use the exact rows in the table and check the issuer exclusions and caps.

Miles post directly to your Emirates Skywards account — no transfer step.

## Fee summary

Read the joining fee, renewal fee and any promotional waiver separately. A renewal figure is not the complete first-year cost, and a current application promotion is not a guarantee that every applicant receives it. The fee table carries the source-qualified VAT treatment. The foreign-currency fee shown is the issuer’s charge, not a total conversion-cost quote. The issuer fee guide states that scheme processing and any acquirer charges may be additional. A merchant’s AED-conversion offer can add a different cost; an issuer-fee waiver does not remove every possible overseas charge.

## Welcome bonus breakdown

Up to 40,000 Skywards Miles: 15,000 on joining-fee payment; 15,000 on USD 10,000 spend within the first 3 billing statements; 10,000 on USD 3,750 Emirates spend within the first 12 billing statements. Subject to current offer terms.

## Bottom line

Choose between Signature and Infinite using their actual joining and renewal costs, settled reward rows and benefits you will use. A conditional welcome award is not a guaranteed fee offset.

A lower-fee Skywards card with a payable joining charge. Named partner categories are not general travel or dining, and the EU/UK rate needs confirmation. It makes sense only if your real purchases fit those conditions and the benefits you can actually use justify the cost. Confirm pending qualification and benefit terms before applying.
