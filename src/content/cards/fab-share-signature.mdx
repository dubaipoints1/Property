## What to check before applying

First Abu Dhabi Bank publishes a minimum salary for Signature. Meeting it does not establish that you qualify. The card earns SHARE points, not cash; check the category rules and caps for the places you actually shop.

## Annual membership fee

<FeeBlock card="fab-share-signature" />

The standard primary annual membership fee includes VAT. It does not verify a separate joining fee or confirm a waiver or promotion.

Compare the fee first with [FAB SHARE Platinum](/cards/fab-share-platinum/) and [FAB SHARE Infinite](/cards/fab-share-infinite/). Do not assume that published terms for those tiers apply to Signature.
