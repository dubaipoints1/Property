## What to check before applying

If you are considering the card for travel, check how DIB treats a purchase in a foreign currency and a purchase abroad billed in dirhams. The annual fee does not tell you what either transaction will cost.

Compare [Prime Signature](/cards/dib-prime-signature/) as a separate tier rather than carrying over its application or benefits. The published salary floor here does not guarantee DIB approval.
