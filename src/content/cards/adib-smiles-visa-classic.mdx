## How the rewards work

The contractual Smiles rows distinguish app purchases from other qualifying expenditure. Smiles Points are not presented here as cash and no redemption conversion is invented.

The displayed rows are source-qualified observations. They are not a universal
base rate, a cash conversion or a total-return ranking. Native reward limits and
purchase-channel conditions remain part of the issuer terms.

## What the fee figure does and does not cover

<FeeBlock card="adib-smiles-visa-classic" />

ADIB lists no standard annual fee for the primary card. That does not establish
the total cost of opening or using it. Confirm any joining charges and applicable
profit charges with ADIB before applying.

<h2 id="adib-smiles-fx-terms">What ADIB lists for payments abroad</h2>

The [schedule of charges](https://www.adib.ae/-/media/project/adib/adibsite/docs/personal/cards/adib-covered-cards-schedule-of-charges-and-fees.pdf#page=1) has two rows for this card:

- **Non-AED transactions:** **2.3% before applicable VAT**, plus the network conversion rate and card-network fee described in Note 5.
- **The separate row labelled AED international transactions:** **2.75% before applicable VAT**. Note 6 says the fee includes a Visa charge but describes overseas payments in foreign currencies. The wording does not clearly distinguish when this row applies instead.

The rows are not a combined quote. Ask ADIB which one applies and check any conversion the merchant offers separately.

<h2 id="adib-smiles-welcome-terms">Who gets the welcome Smiles Points?</h2>

ADIB’s [covered-card terms](https://www.adib.ae/-/media/project/adib/adibsite/docs/personal/cards/adib-covered-cards-terms-and-conditions.pdf#page=44) list **5,000 Smiles Points for the non-self-funded card** and **2,500 Smiles Points for the self-funded card**. Both require a qualifying purchase within **30 days of receiving the card**. The sign-up award is one-time only; reapplication and the upgrades named in the terms do not earn another award.

Cash withdrawals, card payments, fees, balance transfers and above-limit transactions are among the exclusions. Quarterly rewards are separate. Confirm the funding arrangement with ADIB; neither number is presented as cash or as an unconditional award.

## Before applying

Read the Smiles-app purchase scope, reduced categories and issuer rules. A large points figure is not directly comparable with another programme’s currency.

The issuer publishes a salary threshold in the product’s required documents.
That is not confirmation of its residency, employment or salary-transfer rules,
nor does it guarantee approval. Check the linked issuer documents before applying.

Use [the comparison tool](/cards/compare/?cards=adib-smiles-visa-classic,adib-cashback-visa) to inspect
the source-qualified terms beside the existing Cashback profile, without assuming
that unlike reward units or unresolved charges are equivalent.
