## What you get — per-benefit value

The linked rewards terms value 1 LuLu Point at AED 1 when redeemed through the specified LuLu channels. It is a closed-loop shopping reward, not transferable cash or airline miles. Billing-cycle caps and qualifying categories still apply.

No total annual value is quoted while reward qualification, caps or benefit allowances remain unresolved. Use only the benefits you can verify and actually use; an advertised upper bound is not guaranteed annual value.

### Welcome bonus

Up to 500 LuLu Points: 250 on joining-fee payment and first transaction; 250 on AED 8,000 spend in the first 2 billing statements. Qualifying balance-transfer conditions apply separately.

## Watch out for

- **Restricted redemption.** Value depends on purchases you actually want to make through the specified LuLu channels. Do not treat the reward as unrestricted cash.
- **Different category rules.** General spend, named LuLu purchases and reduced categories are separate rows. Disputed reduced rates remain pending; no market-average comparison is asserted.
- **Benefit conditions.** Check Mastercard Travel Pass registration, qualifying spend, visit and guest rules, and your departure terminal before relying on access.

## Bottom line

Consider this card if LuLu is already part of your regular spending and its current redemption and benefit terms fit your needs. Compare the joining and renewal costs with the rewards you can actually use. The source conflicts and unresolved allowances prevent a guaranteed annual value or automatic fee-recovery claim. If you want unrestricted rewards, compare other products on their own confirmed terms.
