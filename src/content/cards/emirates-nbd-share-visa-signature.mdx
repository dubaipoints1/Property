## Watch out for

- **SHARE Points are closed-loop.** Useful only at MAF — no airline, hotel, or cash
  redemption flexibility. If your loyalty drifts away from the ecosystem, the points strand.
- The welcome award has a qualifying-spend condition in the first three months. Do not replace calendar months with a fixed day count.
- **Non-MAF earning is qualified.** Reduced merchant categories, geography and
  exclusions prevent one universal off-network return.
- **Groceries outside Carrefour earn 0.25%; fuel and utilities 0.10%.** Treat as a
  category card, not an everywhere card.

## Bottom line

A Free-for-Life SHARE card whose headline reward applies inside the specified network. Reduced categories and geography still matter. It makes sense only if your real purchases fit those conditions and the benefits you can actually use justify the cost. Confirm pending qualification and benefit terms before applying.

**Consider it** if your existing purchases qualify within SHARE and you can use the
restricted rewards. For other spending, compare a companion card's current category,
payment-channel and credit-limit conditions; no card here is recommended as an
unrestricted rate on everything outside the ecosystem.
