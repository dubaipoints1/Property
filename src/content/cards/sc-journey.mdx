## What to check before applying

Check the published monthly income in the facts panel and whether you already have a current or savings account with Standard Chartered. These are not an approval or a universal salary requirement. The bank’s named application page is listed below.

Journey earns 360° Rewards points, not airline miles outright. If you want direct cashback, compare [Platinum X](/cards/sc-platinum-x/) on the [Standard Chartered card line-up](/banks/standard-chartered/) and check the separate conditions of each card.

## Fees and charges

<FeeBlock card="sc-journey" />

Treat the first-year waiver for new card applicants and later renewals as separate decisions. The Key Facts Statement lists the standard fee separately for existing cardholders. The bank’s product page and Key Facts Statement differ at the renewal spend boundary. Confirm with the bank before budgeting for a fee-free renewal. Current earning rates, the welcome offer and overseas purchase costs are not confirmed in this profile.
