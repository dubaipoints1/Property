## The plan is the price

The account plan is part of the cost of using this credit facility. Standard Plan customers do not earn cashback; other plans have separate fees or qualification conditions. The captured fee schedule does not show an annual-card-fee row, which is not itself an explicit zero-fee promise.

- **Salary plan — free**, with a minimum monthly salary transfer of
  **AED 15,000** (reducible for employees of Wio partner companies).
- **Plus plan — AED 49/month**, waived when you hold **AED 35,000+**
  in balances.
- **Family plan — AED 49/month**, waived at AED 35,000+ in balances
  or one member's AED 15,000+ salary.
- **Standard plan — AED 25/month** (free at AED 3,000+ in balances),
  and **no cashback at all**.

So the effective price of the earning card is either a salary
transfer you may have been planning anyway, or AED 588 a year of Plus
subscription — comparable to a mid-tier annual fee elsewhere, but
buying the whole banking plan, not just the card. Do that arithmetic
before comparing the account-plan cost and the unresolved annual card fee
with another product.

## Earn rates

<EarnRateTable card="wio-credit" />

The headline rate applies to qualifying general retail, not every transaction. The KFS reduces named government, real-estate and utility categories, names DU specifically, and excludes quasi-cash. Plan eligibility and the combined Credit/MyMoney cap still apply.

<HotTip label="Where 2% quietly becomes 0.5%">
Government, real-estate and utility payments — including Easy Cash —
earn **0.5%**, not 2%, per the KFS. Cash advances, quasi-cash and
crypto transactions are excluded from cashback entirely. For a
household whose biggest card lines are DEWA, rent and school-adjacent
government fees, the blended rate will sit well below the headline.
</HotTip>

## Paying other banks' cards

Wio describes settling **another UAE bank's credit card** through Wio Credit
and earning **0.5% cashback** on the payment.
For a household carrying a high-fee card elsewhere, that is cashback
on a bill you were paying anyway. The conditions, straight from the
KFS: the 0.5% is bounded by **0.5% of your Wio Credit limit**, and the
clause carries a **minimum spend of AED 5,000 of retail transactions
in the same cycle** — so the mechanic rewards people already using Wio
Credit as a primary card, not a parking spot for other banks' bills.

## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="wio-credit" />

The captured tariff has no annual-card-fee row, but that absence is not an explicit zero-fee promise. Include the account-plan cost in your comparison.
Check the **2% FX margin** alongside the complete conversion and processing
costs. Interest is where the plan matters a second time: roll-over on Standard, Plus
and Family is **3.25%/month (39% APR)**, cut to **2.29%/month (27.48%
APR)** on the Salary plan. These are plan-specific charges, not a
market-wide cheapest-rate ranking or a reason to carry a balance.
Interest-free period runs up to 60 days, minimum payment is 5% or AED
100, late payment is AED 199, card replacement AED 26.25.

## Easy Cash, honestly priced

Easy Cash lets you borrow up to 50% of your credit limit at **AED 1
per day per AED 1,000 borrowed**, for up to 90 days. The framing is
friendly; the arithmetic is not small: Wio's own Schedule of Fees
states the equivalent rate as **36.5% APR**. The flat-dirham wording does
not establish an advantage over another borrowing product: it remains
a 36.5% APR product per the bank's own
figure, not a cheap loan. Easy Installments (3–48 months) are the
planned-purchase alternative: AED 52.50 processing on the 3-month
tenure, longer tenures free, at rates from 0% up to your roll-over
rate.

## Key conditions to know

<dl class="dp-conditions">
  <dt>Plan gate</dt>
  <dd>Cashback requires the Salary, Plus or Family plan. Standard-plan customers earn nothing. The plan subscription — or the AED 15,000 salary transfer — is the card's effective price.</dd>
  <dt>One cap, broadly drawn</dt>
  <dd>AED 2,500/month maximum cashback, counted across Wio Credit purchases <em>and</em> "My Money" current-account transactions.</dd>
  <dt>Rate demotions</dt>
  <dd>Government, real-estate and utility payments (including Easy Cash) earn 0.5%. Cash advances, quasi-cash and crypto are excluded from cashback.</dd>
  <dt>Other-bank-card payments</dt>
  <dd>0.5% cashback, up to 0.5% of your Wio Credit limit, conditioned on AED 5,000+ of retail transactions in the same cycle.</dd>
  <dt>Salary-plan interest discount</dt>
  <dd>Roll-over at 2.29%/month (27.48% APR) on the Salary plan versus 3.25%/month (39% APR) on every other plan. First month's roll-over is waived; your applied rate is set out in your Offer.</dd>
  <dt>Eligibility</dt>
  <dd>The key facts statement (effective August 2026, re-read 3 November 2026) asks for a <strong>monthly salary of AED 7,000 or above</strong>, ages 21 to 65, UAE residency and employment, and at least 3 months in your job. Approval is still subject to Wio's own credit assessment in the app.</dd>
</dl>


## Watch out for

- **The Standard-plan trap.** Hold the card on the Standard plan and
  you have a 0% cashback card with a monthly subscription. Confirm
  your plan before counting any earnings.
- **The cap's reach.** AED 2,500/month sounds generous, but it pools
  card spend with current-account transactions — heavy My Money users
  will hit it sooner than the card spend alone suggests.
- **The 0.5% categories.** Utilities, government and real-estate
  payments are exactly the big recurring lines many readers put on a
  cashback card. Here they earn a quarter of the headline.
- **Easy Cash's true cost.** AED 1/day per AED 1,000 is 36.5% APR by
  the fee schedule's own statement. Use it as a bridge, not a loan.

## Bottom line

Wio Credit’s recurring value depends on the qualifying account plan, reduced
categories, actual fee terms and your use of the other-bank-card facility.
**Apply** if you qualify for the Salary plan — or if AED 49/month of
Plus is already worth it to you for the banking. **Skip** if you would
sit on the Standard plan, if your spend is dominated by the 0.5%
categories, or if you need guaranteed approval rather than an app-assessed decision.
