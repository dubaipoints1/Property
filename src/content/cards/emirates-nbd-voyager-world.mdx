The headline rate is the **6 Voyager Miles per AED 1 on the Voyager Travel Portal** —
ENBD's own flight-and-hotel booking platform inside the ENBD X app. That rate applies
only to portal bookings; "all other travel" earns 3 and everyday retail earns just 0.5.
The table above shows the general-spend rates — the 6× is portal-only, so the card

## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="emirates-nbd-voyager-world" />

Emirates NBD markets the Voyager World as **Free for Life** — no joining fee, no annual
fee. For the record, the February 2026 Schedule of Charges lists a standard annual fee
of **AED 315** for the Voyager World tier (and AED 1,575 for the higher Voyager World
Elite); the lifetime-free offer waives it. Confirm the Free-for-Life terms on your own
application before relying on them.

## Watch out for

Native miles-per-AED units are confirmed. Reduced categories/EU-UK earn 25% or 10% of base; native per-statement cap applies. No unconditional all-spend or total annual return is calculated.

## Bottom line

Consider it if your existing travel plans suit the Voyager Travel Portal and its
current reward and booking terms. Confirm the Free-for-Life application offer and
benefit conditions rather than treating the advertised package as an automatic saving.

Reduced categories and the statement cap matter for everyday purchases. Compare the
specific earning and redemption routes with alternatives you can actually use;
neither the welcome award's stated value nor the portal headline establishes a
universal annual return.
