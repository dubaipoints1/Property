## Cashback

The issuer uses monthly-spend tiers and separate channel caps. A top-tier headline is not a universal comparison rate; the source-qualified tier schedule and unresolved model conditions control any return estimate.

<EarnRateTable card="sc-platinum-x" />

<HotTip>
Two rules define this card. **Channel:** cashback is earned only on **AED online spend,
non-AED foreign-currency spend, and AED mobile-wallet (Apple/Samsung/Google Pay) taps** —
a normal in-store swipe of the physical card earns **nothing**. **Tier:** the rate steps
up with your total monthly spend — **0% under AED 2,500, 3% from AED 2,500, 5% from AED
7,500, and 10% only from AED 15,000**. At the top tier each channel is capped (AED 400 online, AED 400
foreign-currency, AED 200 mobile-wallet), so the most you bank is roughly **AED 1,000 a
month**. Complete foreign-currency costs must be checked separately; an issuer
upper bound plus additional scheme processing does not establish an exact net return.
</HotTip>

## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="sc-platinum-x" />

The card is free in year one, then AED 525 (any ongoing waiver requires current product-specific confirmation).
The foreign-currency fee is up to 2.99%, and the cash-advance fee is 3.15% (min AED 105).
The retail monthly finance charge is recorded from the current product-specific fee
block. The captured shared price guide did not establish this product's annual rate,
so that figure is withheld rather than calculated from the monthly charge. As with any
cashback card, clear the balance in full.

## What you get — per-benefit value

### Channel cashback (online / foreign-currency / mobile wallet)

Worked at the middle tier: AED 8,000 of total monthly spend (5% rate), of
which AED 4,000 online (AED 200, within the applicable channel cap) and AED 2,000 by
mobile wallet (AED 100, inside the AED 200 cap): AED 300/month.
**Value to me: ~AED 3,600/year** on that profile. At the top tier —
AED 15,000+ of monthly spend with all three channel caps maxed — the ceiling
is ~AED 1,000/month, **~AED 12,000/year**. An in-store swiper earns AED 0
regardless of spend.

### Foreign-currency cashback

Foreign-currency cashback depends on the spend tier and channel cap. The issuer
publishes an FX upper bound and says scheme processing is additional. That does
not establish an exact all-in fee, so no precise net overseas profit or break-even
claim is included in this review.

### Unlimited lounge access (20+ lounges)

A smaller network than the 1,000+ Visa Airport Companion lists, but
uncapped. At the AED 200 walk-in equivalent: **Value to me: AED 800/year**
on four trips that route through covered lounges; scales with your actual
travel.

**Total package:** ~AED 3,600 of channel cashback at the worked middle tier
plus ~AED 800 of lounge value is roughly **AED 4,400/year** against the
AED 525 standard annual fee (the current product confirms the first-year waiver;
an ongoing spend waiver is not established). The maths holds only if your spend genuinely lives online
and in the mobile wallet — for a physical-card spender the package is the
lounge access and nothing else.

## Watch out for

- **The swipe trap.** If you mostly tap or insert the physical card in shops, this card
  earns nothing — its cashback lives entirely online, abroad, and in the mobile wallet.
- **The 10% mirage.** 10% only kicks in above AED 15,000 of monthly spend; below that
  lower tiers apply. Foreign-currency cashback is not an exact net profit after all fees.
- **Caps.** ~AED 1,000/month total ceiling across the three channels.
- **Finance charges.** The fee block uses the current product-specific retail
  monthly charge; no unsupported annual figure is inferred from it.

## Is it worth it?

For a heavy spender whose purchases run through online checkouts, foreign-currency
transactions and mobile-wallet taps — and who clears AED 7,500-15,000+ a month — the
Platinum X offers channel-specific cashback, subject to the applicable tiers and caps.
Check its lounge conditions and published eligibility separately; this is not a
market-wide return ranking.

For everyone else it underwhelms: a typical in-store swiper earns nothing, the 10% needs
heavy spend, and the full cost of foreign-currency spending needs checking. It's a
specialist card, not a default one.

## Bottom line

A high-ceiling, high-condition cashback card for online/mobile-wallet-heavy big spenders.
**Apply** if your spend fits the three channels and you'll hit the higher tiers. **Skip**
if you mostly swipe a physical card in-store — it earns you nothing.
