## Check the annual fee

<FeeBlock card="united-arab-bank-mastercard-world" />

The first-year annual fee is separate from renewal. UAB’s linked application and Key Facts Statement describe an annual spending waiver; check what spending counts and when the bank measures it. Do not use the first-year annual fee to assume joining or other costs are zero. This is the conventional World profile; do not borrow terms from other Mastercard tiers or UAB’s Islamic products.

## What the application establishes

UAB lists this Mastercard as a separate primary card type on its [application paperwork](https://www.uab.ae/help-center/forms-and-policies). That does not establish how an online enquiry is routed or whether you qualify. No salary minimum, exchange charge, welcome reward or comparable earn rate is confirmed in this profile.
