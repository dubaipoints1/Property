## Which Blue FAB card is this?

This is the conventional Platinum tier in the Blue FAB range by Al-Futtaim, not the Islamic card. Compare it with [Blue FAB Infinite](/cards/fab-blue-al-futtaim-infinite/) and [Blue FAB Signature](/cards/fab-blue-al-futtaim-signature/) if you’re choosing between the conventional tiers.

## Standard primary annual membership fee

<FeeBlock card="fab-blue-al-futtaim-platinum" />

FAB’s fee schedule lists no standard annual membership fee for the primary card. This does not mean that joining, foreign-currency or borrowing charges are zero. This profile does not establish who qualifies or estimate the value of rewards or a welcome offer.
