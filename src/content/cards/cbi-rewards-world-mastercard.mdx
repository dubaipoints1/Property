## Standard primary annual fee

<FeeBlock card="cbi-rewards-world-mastercard" />

CBI’s [Key Facts Statement](https://www.cbiuae.com/media/w3laroxq/cbi-credit-cards-key-facts-statement-april-2026.pdf) and [November credit-card tariff](https://www.cbiuae.com/media/uqziuyjp/cbi-credit-card-schedule-of-charges-nov-2026-v1-1.pdf) agree on the primary annual fee; the tariff lists it inclusive of VAT. A joining charge, first-year or renewal quote, and any waiver for an individual account are not established. Do not use benefits labelled for other CBI cards to value this one.

The bank uses a shared enquiry link. New applications and eligibility for this tier remain unconfirmed.
