## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="adcb-lulu-platinum" />

The current ADCB Schedule of Fees supports the annual-membership amount shown above. Relationship concessions are not universal waivers. Fees are inclusive of VAT where applicable. No separate joining-fee amount has been established by this review.

The foreign-currency percentage is an issuer charge, not a promise of the total conversion cost. The fee guide separately states that card-scheme processing is additional.

## Welcome bonus

No current welcome award is established by the material reviewed. Absence of a visible award is not proof that no channel-specific offer exists; confirm any application promotion separately.

## Perks worth using

The page describes Reel cinema and lounge benefits, but a headline does not establish every visit, guest or booking condition. The linked LuLu terms impose good-standing, credit-limit, transaction-exclusion, redemption and expiry conditions. They name Classic, Gold and Platinum, not the separately marketed Titanium card.

## Key conditions to know

The current product offers a salary **or** credit-limit qualification route. Neither route guarantees approval. Salary-transfer, residency and employment conditions have not all been confirmed, so the previous qualification summary remains unconfirmed rather than presented as a complete rule. Confirm the requirements for the exact product and application channel with ADCB.

This is a bounded field review, not a new whole-card verification date. A retained source date does not certify every historical statement or every linked benefit. Pending terms are set out in the source notes above.

## Watch out for

LuLu Points are a restricted reward, not statement cash. The issuer uses merchant-category classification and can exclude transactions. Do not assume every transaction outside LuLu earns the advertised other-store upper bound.

## Is it worth it?

A focused option for a household already buying at LuLu. Its case is the spend you actually make at eligible merchants, not an unsupported claim to the strongest rate across the entire market.
