Earn is expressed as **% value back as Darna Points** (10 Darna Points = AED 1), redeemable
inside the Aldar / Darna partner network — not as miles per AED and not as cashback:

<HotTip>
The 7.5% partner rate is the upgrade over the [Darna
Select](/cards/emirates-nbd-darna-select-visa/)'s 6.25%, but Darna Points have a single
redemption universe: the Aldar / Darna partner network. They are not transferable to
Skywards, Etihad Guest, Marriott Bonvoy or any cash-equivalent. If your landlord, building
manager or weekly dining list does not sit inside that network, inspect the ordinary
and reduced-category earning rows against your purchases. Off-network spending is
not one uniform category, and this is not a guaranteed advantage over another card.
</HotTip>

## Fee summary

<FeeBlock card="emirates-nbd-darna-visa-signature" />

Read the joining fee, renewal fee and any promotional waiver separately. A renewal figure is not the complete first-year cost, and a current application promotion is not a guarantee that every applicant receives it. The fee table carries the source-qualified VAT treatment. The foreign-currency fee shown is the issuer’s charge, not a total conversion-cost quote. The issuer fee guide states that scheme processing and any acquirer charges may be additional. A merchant’s AED-conversion offer can add a different cost; an issuer-fee waiver does not remove every possible overseas charge.

## What you get — per-benefit value

Darna publishes its conversion — 10 Darna Points = AED 1 — so the earn carries honest AED
figures, with the standing caveat that the value only exists inside the Darna partner
network.

## Watch out for

The issuer product page states a minimum monthly salary of AED 12,000. The reviewed product, Key Facts Statement and shared credit-card terms do not establish a universal salary-transfer requirement. Confirm the conditions of your own application; we do not assume a transfer-related clawback or that none can apply.

## Bottom line

Aldar-focused rewards with a limited-time Free-for-Life application offer. The standard fee and promotional waiver are different facts. It makes sense only if your real purchases fit those conditions and the benefits you can actually use justify the cost. Confirm pending qualification and benefit terms before applying.
