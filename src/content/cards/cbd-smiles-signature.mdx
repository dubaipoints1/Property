## Which CBD card is this?

This is the conventional Smiles Visa Signature credit card from Commercial Bank of Dubai (CBD). Smiles is the associated programme; this profile does not verify earn rates or redemption value.

## First-year annual fee and standard renewal

<FeeBlock card="cbd-smiles-signature" />

CBD’s Key Facts Statement waives the primary card’s annual fee in the first year. The standard renewal amount shown includes VAT. The waiver does not establish the total first-year cost. This profile does not verify separate joining charges, foreign-currency costs, personal eligibility or welcome offers.

CBD’s product page also describes a separate spend-based renewal waiver. Check its current conditions before assuming you will pay less than the standard renewal fee.
