## Which route should you ask about?

NBF lists salary or Priority status as alternatives and describes a separate deposit-backed route in the Key Facts Statement. Check which terms would apply to you before changing an account or tying up a deposit.

If you are comparing tiers, [Platinum Exclusive](/cards/nbf-platinum-exclusive/) has its own named conditions. If you intend to apply through NBF Islamic, use the [Islamic Visa Infinite profile](/cards/nbf-islamic-visa-infinite/) instead.
