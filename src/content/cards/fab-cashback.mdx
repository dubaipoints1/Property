## Earn rates

<EarnRateTable card="fab-cashback" />

The cashback structure is direct percent on the bonus categories — not a
points-to-cashback conversion you have to mentally translate:

- **5% cashback** on supermarkets, fashion and dining
- The current source gives an issuer FX upper bound, with separate card-scheme processing charges. It is not a fixed all-in conversion cost; use the qualified fee disclosure above rather than the previously recorded percentage or a precise net-return calculation.
- **1% cashback** on most other spending; a small list of categories drops
  to 0.15%

Cashback pays as **FAB Rewards** redeemable inside the FAB Mobile app —
**AED 0.30 per 100 points** at the cashback-flow redemption rate (the
FAB cashback table publishes 17 FAB Rewards = AED 0.05 in the 5%
category; the same ratio holds across all four cashback tiers).
Not auto-applied as a statement credit; you have to redeem actively,
which is a minor friction versus the Mashreq Cashback or HSBC Cash+
statement-credit model.

## Perks worth using

The current issuer page advertises lounge, delivery, cinema and shopping benefits.
Their usefulness depends on the applicable provider and booking conditions, not
just the headline allowance. No combined annual package value is assigned here.

### Airport lounge access

The issuer advertises annual cardholder and guest visits, subject to a qualifying
card transaction. Check the Mastercard registration, eligible lounges, visit and
guest charging rules, and reset period before travelling. Advertised access is
not an unrestricted or guaranteed cash saving.

### Talabat

The advertised discount has a per-order cap, monthly usage limit and minimum order
value. Confirm the current code, eligible payment and provider terms before
ordering. A maximum monthly discount is not a guaranteed annual reward.

### Cinema and shopping offers

Cinema, Carrefour, airport-coffee and other shopping offers have their own dates,
participating venues and booking rules. These provider conditions have not been
fully reconciled in this review, so no fixed annual benefit valuation or fee-
recovery claim is supplied. Use the current issuer and provider terms.

## Key conditions to know

<dl class="dp-conditions">
  <dt>AED 3,000 prior-month spend gate</dt>
  <dd>You earn cashback in any given month <strong>only if</strong> the previous month's eligible spend was at least AED 3,000. Drop below in one month and the next month earns nothing — not pro-rated, not deferred. Plan around this if your spend is seasonal.</dd>
  <dt>AED 1,000 monthly cashback cap</dt>

## FAB Cashback vs FAB Elite

<CardComparison cards={["fab-cashback", "fab-elite"]} />

The two FAB cards target opposite use cases. **Cashback** rewards everyday
household spending at low fee and low salary bar — the right choice for
AED 5,000–15,000 monthly card spend on dining, supermarkets and fashion.
**Elite** rewards luxury merchants and ADV+ lifestyle perks at AED 40,000+
salary and AED 10,000+ monthly spend — the right choice only when those
gating thresholds clear.

For most readers comparing them: stay on Cashback unless your spend
profile pushes squarely into the Elite's narrow proposition.

## Watch out for

- The current product page advertises a digital-journey Amazon Gift Card promotion. Its campaign terms and application-route conditions still need checking; we do not treat it as an unconditional welcome entitlement or infer a first-year fee waiver.
- **AED 150 category sub-caps.** The 5% bonus rate stops paying past
  AED 3,000 of monthly spend in each bonus category (5% × AED 3,000 =
  AED 150). Over-cap spend earns the 1% floor.
- **Redemption friction.** Cashback pays as FAB Rewards inside the FAB
  Mobile app, not as auto-applied statement credit — unlike Mashreq
  Cashback or HSBC Cash+.

## Is it worth it?

Consider it if your regular eligible purchases fit the categories, prior-month
qualification and caps. Check credit-limit restrictions, reduced categories and
redemption conditions before estimating your return. Unconfirmed benefits are
not counted towards recovering the annual fee.
