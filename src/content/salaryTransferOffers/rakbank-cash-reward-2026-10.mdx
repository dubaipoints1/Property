## Real-world notes

- Verified 13 October 2026 against RAKBANK's **Salary Transfer Cash Reward
  Promotion** terms (October 2026 PDF, read in full), linked from the
  RAKmore account page. Every band figure above is the terms' own table.
  Source text archived at
  `.council/research/2026-10/salary-transfer-source-rakbank-2026-10-13.md`.
- **This is a one-month window.** RAKBANK has run the same table as a
  series of short campaigns: July–August, September and now October, each
  with its own dated terms document. Its accounts page lists the October
  terms as "active" and September's as "concluded". If your first salary
  cannot land in October, expect a successor campaign but do not count on
  its figures until its terms are published.
- **Two small inconsistencies in RAKBANK's own copy.** The English terms
  end the promotion on 31 October; the Arabic column of the same PDF says
  30 October. The landing pages also say 31 October. We use 31 October.
  The terms say claims are paid within 90 days, where the July cycle said
  60.
- **The headline band is steep.** AED 4,000 needs a salary of AED 50,000+;
  AED 10,000–19,999 pays AED 1,000. The AED 500 band is only for RAKempower
  Payroll or RAKmore enrollees, so a standard applicant needs AED 10,000 a
  month.
- **The lowest-month rule is the trap.** One light month among the three
  re-bands the whole reward.
- RAKislamic runs a parallel Sharia-compliant campaign to the same date.
  Its terms are a separate document and are not covered by this entry.
