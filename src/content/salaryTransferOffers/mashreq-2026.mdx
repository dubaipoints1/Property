## Real-world notes

- Re-verified 22 August 2026 against Mashreq's live NEO salary-transfer
  page and its linked
  [salary-transfer bonus T&C](https://www.mashreq.com/-/jssmedia/pdfs/neo/accounts-deposits/TnCs/salary-transfer-bonus-tnc-en-ar.ashx).
  **The offer changed on 1 August 2026**: the base joining bonus re-banded
  to AED 100 / 350 / 1,000 / 2,500 and a new **early-bird** cashback
  (AED 100 / 250 / 800 / 1,500) was added for salaries arriving within 90
  days of account opening — taking the achievable headline to
  **AED 4,000** at 50,000+.
- The two components together are what Mashreq's "up to AED 4,000" banner
  refers to; miss the 90-day early-bird window and the ceiling halves to
  AED 2,500.
- Branded "cashback" by Mashreq, but it is a **fixed AED bonus per salary
  band** paid in monthly instalments — not an ongoing percentage cashback.
- Payout discipline is the long game here: instalments run across 12
  months and a 2-month salary gap inside the first year reverses
  **everything already paid**. This remains the longest-tailed clawback on
  the tracker.
- Mashreq's own page still carries a legacy FAQ describing 6 instalments
  for the older structure; the current offer copy states 12. We flag the
  discrepancy rather than resolve it — confirm the instalment schedule in
  the account-opening T&C before counting on timing.
- No published hard end date; `validUntil` is a conservative year-end
  marker, not a bank deadline.
