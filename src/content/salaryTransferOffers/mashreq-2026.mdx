## Which terms apply?

Mashreq’s [official terms index](https://www.mashreq.com/uae/neo/accounts/terms-and-conditions/)
separates the pre-August offer from the terms for first salaries from
1 August 2026. The 12-instalment schedule above applies only to the
latter group. Do not use it to work out what an earlier switch should pay.

The tracker shows the advertised total and the calculator estimates the
joining bonus only. Check the early-bird timing separately before
counting on the extra. Mashreq does not publish a fixed end date in these
terms; confirm that the offer is still open before arranging payroll.
