## Real-world notes

- Verified 6 August 2026 against HSBC's **New to Bank and Customer
  Relationship Offer** T&C (PDF, read in full) and the Premier
  rewards-and-offers page (footnote 11). Both salary tiers above are the
  T&C's own rows verbatim; dossier at
  `.council/research/2026-08/salary-transfer-hsbc-adcb.md`.
- **This is one campaign with two doors.** The mass-market entry point
  is the Advance tier — two salaries of AED 10,000+ inside three months
  pays AED 750. The AED 2,000 headline needs a Premier-qualifying salary
  of AED 40,000 a month (AED 30,000 for Emirati nationals): a
  premium-segment offer, priced like one.
- **The same welcome offer also pays on money, not salary** — AED 8,000
  to 12,000 cashback for parking AED 500,000 to 2 million+ of
  new-to-bank funds. Those legs are funding-based, sit outside a
  salary-transfer tracker, and don't stack with a same-tier salary
  reward; they're noted here so the "up to AED 14,000" marketing
  headline reads correctly.
- **The 180-day rule is the real tenure.** Formally you need two
  consecutive qualifying salaries inside three months; practically, an
  interrupted salary or a closed account inside 180 days of the first
  credit un-earns the reward. Treat it as a six-month commitment.
- **The quiet clause is real**: the T&C forbids public comment about the
  offer without HSBC's written consent, with clawback attached. We have
  not seen a UAE salary offer put that in writing before.
