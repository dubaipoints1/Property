## Real-world notes

- Verified 22 August 2026 against the live offer page and the campaign
  [Terms and Conditions](https://www.emiratesislamic.ae/en/terms-and-conditions/salary-transfer-offer-terms-and-conditions).
  Band figures are the banks' published tables verbatim; we headline the
  **non-UAE-national** amounts and list the National uplift in the
  requirements.
- **The new-to-Group rule is the trap to know.** Emirates Islamic is an
  Emirates NBD Group bank, so a salary relationship with **Emirates NBD**
  in the last 6 months disqualifies you — switching between the group's
  two brands earns nothing.
- **The lowest-month rule mirrors RAKBANK's**: the band is computed on the
  lower of your 2nd and 3rd months' salaries, so one light month re-bands
  the payout.
- The AED 4,000 expat headline needs a 50,000+ salary *and* AED 18,000 of
  Covered Card spend inside 60 days; the guaranteed no-card figure at that
  band is AED 3,000 (AED 5,000 for Nationals).
- Enrolment is not automatic — submit the campaign form on the offer page
  or the reward will not track. Sharia-compliant throughout; the "cards"
  are EI Covered Cards.
