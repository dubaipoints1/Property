## Real-world notes

- Verified 13 October 2026 against RAKislamic's **Salary Transfer Cash
  Reward Promotion** terms (October 2026 PDF, read in full), linked from
  the RAKislamic salary-transfer page, which carries the same table and
  "only until 31 October 2026". Source text archived at
  `.council/research/2026-10/salary-transfer-source-rakbank-2026-10-13.md`.
- **Same table as the conventional RAKBANK campaign, different terms.**
  RAKislamic pays within 60 days where the conventional terms say 90, and
  its clawback clause covers only error or fraud. The conventional
  version lets RAKBANK claw back if it later decides you were not
  eligible.
- **One typo in the bank's own document.** The Arabic column of the
  table reads AED 20,000 to AED 40,999 for the AED 2,000 band; the English
  column and the RAKislamic page read AED 49,999. We use the English.
- **The lowest-month rule is the trap.** One light month among the three
  re-bands the whole reward.
- This is the only Sharia-compliant RAKBANK offer; the conventional
  campaign to the same date is a
  [separate entry](/salary-transfer/rakbank/).
