## Real-world notes

- Verified 15 October 2026 against Emirates Islamic's **Salary Transfer
  Offer Terms and Conditions (October 2026)**, read in full. Every band
  figure above is the terms' own table. Source text archived at
  `.council/research/2026-10/firecrawl-2026-10-15-emirates-islamic-salary-terms.md`.
- **What changed from the June–September cycle.** The two bottom bands
  (5,000–7,499 and 7,500–9,999) are merged into one; the non-national
  CASA reward at 15,000–34,999 rises from AED 600 to AED 1,000; and two
  new top bands pay AED 6,000 (50,000–79,999) and AED 10,000 (80,000+)
  in CASA cashback alone, for nationals and non-nationals alike.
- **The headline needs the card.** The expat totals above include the
  Covered Card reward, which needs a new EI Covered Card and AED 6,000 to
  18,000 of spend inside 60 days of disbursal. Without the card, the
  guaranteed figure is the CASA amount alone.
- **The new-to-Group rule is the trap to know.** Emirates Islamic is an
  Emirates NBD Group bank, so a salary relationship with **Emirates NBD**
  in the last 6 months disqualifies you, and so does an EI salary reward
  in the last 12 months.
- **Six months, not one.** Unlike RAKBANK's month-by-month campaigns,
  this cycle runs to 31 March 2027.
- Enrolment is not automatic. Sharia-compliant throughout; the "cards"
  are EI Covered Cards.
