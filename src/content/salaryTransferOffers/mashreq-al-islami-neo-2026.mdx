## Use the Islamic salary terms

The [Mashreq Al Islami salary account page](https://www.mashreqalislami.com/uae/neo/accounts/current-accounts/salary-transfer/)
identifies the first-salary cohort. The Early Bird terms list separate
salary and non-salary activities. The figures above are the salary
cashback plus the salary-specific early-bird amount, not the maximum for
all activities or a second bonus on top of conventional Mashreq NEO.
These terms do not publish a fixed campaign end date; 31 January 2027 is
the tracker’s next check.
