## Salary reward, not the combined headline

The [Arrive programme page](https://www.adib.ae/en/personal/new-to-country)
combines salary and deposit benefits in its headline. The table above uses
only the salary column in the bank’s terms. The deposit profit and the
covered-card fee waiver are separate and are not counted here.

The record starts from the bank’s [10 November 2026 announcement](https://www.adib.ae/en/news/2026/nov/adib-launches-adib-arrive-a-new-banking-proposition-for-new-uae-residents),
not from an issuer enrolment deadline. ADIB does not publish a fixed
campaign end date in these terms. The tracker’s 31 January 2027 date is
for the next check.
