## Who should check this

This is for a specific group of existing Premier customers, not a
welcome offer. If you do not already hold the qualifying account, see
the separate new-to-bank offer on this page. Do not assume the two
offers combine or that the new-to-bank salary or foreign-currency
rules apply to this one.
