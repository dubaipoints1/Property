## Real-world notes

- Verified 1 October 2026 against ENBD's
  [offer page](https://www.emiratesnbd.com/en/promotions/payroll-cashback)
  and its Salary Transfer Welcome Bonus terms and conditions (PDF, read in
  full). The two bands and both cashback tables are the T&Cs' own figures;
  sources are archived in `.council/research/2026-10/news-sources-2026-10-01.md` §3.
- **The headline "up to AED 6,000" is the loan-included top band.** The
  table above shows the cashback for the salary transfer plus the
  mandatory credit card: AED 1,500 or AED 2,000. The extra AED 500 or
  AED 4,000 needs a personal, auto or home loan, or a wealth product.
  Price that product on its own terms before counting the cashback.
- **This replaces ENBD's earlier bonus-interest cycle** (1 February – 30
  June 2026, now in the
  [offer history](/salary-transfer/history/emirates-nbd/)). That cycle
  paid interest on a balance; this one pays a fixed cash sum.
- **Twelve months is the real tenure.** The bonus pays out after about
  five months, but ENBD can reverse it until you have received twelve
  consecutive salaries. Full write-up:
  [our news post](/news/emirates-nbd-salary-transfer-cashback-up-to-aed-6000-24-december/).
