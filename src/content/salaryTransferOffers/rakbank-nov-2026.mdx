## Before you switch

For an eligible account opened before November, telling HR is not enough:
the first salary has to reach RAKBANK during November. The separate
60-day allowance is for eligible accounts opened in November. If
your salary varies, use the lowest of the three credits when estimating
your reward.

These are the **conventional** terms. The
[RAKislamic terms](https://www.rakbank.ae/globalassets/rakbank/all-pdfs/002-campaign/islamic-salary-transfer-tc-nov-2026.pdf)
have a different payout clock and inconsistent English and Arabic
salary bands. We do not rank the Islamic offer. The prize draw and
referral reward on RAKBANK’s site are not part of this cash estimate.
