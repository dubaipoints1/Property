## Card spend is required

First Abu Dhabi Bank’s (FAB) [Islamic campaign page](https://www.bankfab.com/en-ae/islamic-banking/promotions/20-percent-salary-transfer-cashback)
and the operative clauses require a new FAB Islamic credit card and
qualifying monthly spend. The terms have a “Save or Spend” heading but
specify only the spend route for this offer. Do not use the conventional
FAB offer’s savings route as a substitute.

The cashback amounts are the examples in the English terms at the listed
example salaries, not quotes for every salary between them. The
published terms take precedence over the rounded marketing examples.
