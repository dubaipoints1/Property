## What to check before switching

The [current bank terms](https://av.sc.com/ae/content/docs/sc-ae-wsdepositscampaign.pdf)
set out two salary cashback tiers in clause 9. Opening a qualifying account
and receiving the first salary are separate steps. For new clients the
salary sequence starts in the following month; for eligible existing clients
it needs to start within the campaign window.

Clause 7 covers a separate balance-related cashback. The tracker shows
only the salary cashback, with no deposit or investment reward added. Check
the applicable Priority relationship requirements with the bank before
switching salary or moving money.
