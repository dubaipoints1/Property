## Real-world notes

- Verified 13 October 2026 against HSBC's **New to Bank Customer Cashback
  Offer** terms (PDF, read in full; effective 1 September 2026) and the
  Premier rewards-and-offers page. Both salary tiers above are the terms'
  own figures. Source text archived at
  `.council/research/2026-10/salary-transfer-source-hsbc-2026-10-13.md`.
- **This replaces the January–August terms** at the same URL, now in the
  [offer history](/salary-transfer/history/hsbc/). Three things changed:
  the Advance minimum rose from AED 10,000 to AED 12,500; the Premier
  salary reward doubled from AED 2,000 to AED 4,000; and the Premier
  reward now also needs AED 12,000 of foreign-currency activity in the
  first three months.
- **The AED 4,000 is a premium-segment reward.** It needs a salary of
  AED 40,000 a month (AED 30,000 for Emirati nationals) plus AED 12,000 of
  non-AED card spend, FX top-up or international remittance. If you send
  money home or travel, that is easy; if you do neither, it is an extra
  hurdle to plan for.
- **The same welcome offer pays on money, not salary.** AED 8,000 to
  12,000 of cashback is available for new-to-bank balances of AED 500,000
  to AED 2 million or more. Those legs are outside a salary-transfer
  tracker; HSBC's "up to AED 16,000" headline adds the AED 12,000 balance
  reward to the AED 4,000 salary reward.
- **The 180-day rule is the real tenure.** Two salaries inside three
  months qualify you; an interrupted salary or a closed account inside 180
  days of the first credit un-earns the reward.
