## Real-world notes

- Verified 6 August 2026 against RAKBANK's **Salary Transfer Cash Reward**
  promotion T&C (July 2026 PDF, linked from the RAKmore account page).
  Every band figure above is the T&C table verbatim.
- **The headline band is steep.** AED 4,000 needs a salary of AED 50,000+;
  at AED 10,000–19,999 the reward is AED 1,000. The AED 500 bottom band
  (salary 5,000–9,999) is **only** for customers enrolled under RAKempower
  Payroll or RAKmore — a standard applicant needs AED 10,000/month
  minimum.
- **The lowest-month rule is the trap to know.** The reward is computed on
  the lowest of your three consecutive salary credits, so a single light
  month (unpaid leave, a joining pro-rata) re-bands the whole reward.
- The same window carries a companion **welcome bonus interest**
  promotion: 6.25% p.a. for three months on balances up to AED 5M for
  new-account salary-transfer customers holding an average balance of
  AED 100,000+ (its own T&C, same 31 August end date). Balance-heavy
  switchers can stack the two; the tracker lists only the guaranteed cash
  reward here.
- No lock-in beyond the three qualifying months is stated, and the
  clawback clause is conditional on ineligibility rather than on stopping
  your salary later — milder than most peers, but the reward is also
  one-time rather than monthly.
