DIB is giving **20% off Air Arabia Ultimate fares and 10% off Value fares**,
booked on the Air Arabia website with promo code **DIB** and paid with a DIB
Visa Covered Card. Bookings must be made **between 1 October and 15 November
2026**, for travel until 31 March 2027.

- **Ultimate fare:** 20% off, up to AED 150 per passenger one-way or AED 300
  per passenger on a round trip
- **Value fare:** 10% off, up to AED 40 per passenger one-way or AED 80 per
  passenger on a round trip
- **Code:** **DIB**, entered when searching for the flight
- **Route condition:** the booking must include a flight originally
  departing from Sharjah (SHJ), Ras Al Khaimah (RKT) or Abu Dhabi (AUH)
- **Cards:** DIB **Visa** Covered Cards; corporate cards excluded
- **Book by:** 15 November 2026
- **Travel by:** 31 March 2027

The discount applies to the base fare, carrier surcharges and ancillaries
bought in the same booking (bags, seats, meals). Taxes, government levies and
airport charges are excluded and paid in full. On an Ultimate fare the
one-way cap is reached at AED 750 of discountable fare (20% of AED 750 is
AED 150).

The terms that matter:

- **Air Arabia website only.** Not the call centre, travel agents or
  third-party sites
- **No Apple Pay or Samsung Pay.** Pay with the card itself
- **Air Arabia flights only** (G9 and 3L). A route that includes another
  airline may not qualify
- **Changes can cost the discount.** If the booking is modified, Air Arabia
  may withdraw the discount on unflown segments
- Seat allotments are limited and blackout dates may apply
