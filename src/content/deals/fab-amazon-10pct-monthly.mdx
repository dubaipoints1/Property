FAB's year-round Amazon.ae offer gives **10% off, capped at AED 50**, once
per calendar month on a spend of **AED 300 or more** with a FAB Mastercard
credit card. The offer runs to **31 December 2026**.

- **Discount:** 10%, up to AED 50 per month
- **Minimum spend:** AED 300 in one order
- **Code:** **FABAUG** for August — the code carries the month's name and
  rotates, so check the [offer page](https://www.bankfab.com/en-ae/personal/credit-cards/offers/amazon-year-round-offer)
  for the current one before checkout
- **Cards:** FAB **Mastercard** credit cards only (Cashback, Rewards Active,
  ADNOC Rewards, Z Card, Rewards Indulge, Elite, du, GEMS World, Travel);
  Visa cards do not qualify
- **Excluded:** Amazon's own sale events — the discount does not stack with
  Prime Day-style promotions, which is exactly when you would want it
- **Expires:** 31 December 2026

Used every month at the cap, this is AED 50 × 12 = **AED 600 a year** for
spend most households have anyway — a better return than most of the
category cashback rates on the cards themselves. The catch is remembering
the monthly code and the AED 300 threshold in a single order.

_Verified against the [FAB offer page](https://www.bankfab.com/en-ae/personal/credit-cards/offers/amazon-year-round-offer)
on 6 August 2026._
