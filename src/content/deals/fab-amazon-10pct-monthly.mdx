FAB's year-round Amazon.ae offer gives **10% off, capped at AED 50**, once
per calendar month on a spend of **AED 300 or more** with a FAB Mastercard
credit card. FAB lists **15 November 2026** as the end date. Check that the
discount applies to your basket before paying.

- **Discount:** 10%, up to AED 50 per month
- **Minimum spend:** AED 300 in one order
- **Code:** a monthly promo code shown on FAB's
  [offer page](https://www.bankfab.com/en-ae/personal/credit-cards/offers/amazon-year-round-offer).
  It changes every month (it read FABOCT on 10 October 2026), so copy the
  current one from that page before checkout
- **Cards:** FAB **Mastercard** credit cards only (Cashback, Rewards Active,
  ADNOC Rewards, Z Card, Rewards Indulge, Elite, du, GEMS World, Travel);
  Visa cards do not qualify
- **Excluded:** Amazon's own sale events — the discount does not stack with
  Prime Day-style promotions, which is exactly when you would want it
- **Published end date:** 15 November 2026

_14 November 2026: The issuer's current offer page lists 15 November as the
end date. This corrects the 31 December date previously shown here._
