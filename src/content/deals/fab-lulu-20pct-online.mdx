FAB is giving **20% off, capped at AED 75**, on Lulu Hypermarket orders of
**AED 300 or more** placed through the Lulu website or app and paid with a
FAB Mastercard credit card. The offer runs to **31 December 2026**.

- **Discount:** 20%, up to AED 75 per order
- **Minimum spend:** AED 300, online only (Lulu website or mobile app)
- **How often:** FAB's offer details say "valid twice per month". The
  listing's strapline reads "Instant Savings Every Thursday"; the two do not
  say the same thing, so read the terms before planning a weekly shop
  around it
- **Cards:** FAB Mastercard credit cards. The offer page lists Rewards
  Active, ADNOC Rewards, Z Card, Cashback, Rewards Indulge, Elite, du,
  GEMS World and Travel
- **Expires:** 31 December 2026

At the cap, twice a month, this is AED 75 × 2 = **AED 150 a month** off
grocery spend. The cap kicks in at AED 375 per order (20% of AED 375 is
AED 75), so an order above that earns nothing more. It sits alongside FAB's
[Amazon.ae offer](/deals/fab-amazon-10pct-monthly/), which runs to the same
end date on the same Mastercard-only rule.

The discount stacks on a card's own earn only if FAB still counts the
discounted amount as eligible spend, and the offer page does not say. For the
underlying earn rates, see our [FAB Cashback review](/cards/fab-cashback/).
