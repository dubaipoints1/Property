HSBC is giving **a complimentary 12-month Careem Plus subscription** to
cardholders in Dubai and Abu Dhabi who add and use an eligible HSBC credit
card on the Careem app. The offer is valid **until 31 December 2026**.

- **Benefit:** 12 months of Careem Plus, free
- **Where:** cardholders based in Dubai and Abu Dhabi
- **Cards:** HSBC Emirates Skywards Infinite, Emirates Skywards Signature,
  Black, Premier, Cash+, Max Rewards, Advance, Live+, Platinum and Platinum
  Select credit cards
- **Limit:** once per Careem user, and once per eligible card
- **Code:** none shown on the page
- **Expires:** 31 December 2026

What Careem Plus includes, as HSBC's page lists it: unlimited free delivery
and member discounts on food and groceries; buy-one-get-one at brunches,
buffets and specials through Careem DineOut; 10% off home services; 10% back
on 10 rides a month (electric and Comfort rides excluded); 10% off laundry;
unlimited free bike rides; and priority support.

HSBC does not put an AED value on the membership, and we do not either: the
benefit is worth whatever you would otherwise spend on delivery fees and
rides.

The page does not say what happens at the end of the 12 months. Check in the
Careem app whether the subscription renews at the paid rate, and cancel
before then if you do not want it.

For one of the eligible cards, see our [HSBC Live+ review](/cards/hsbc-live-plus/).
