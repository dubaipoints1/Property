RAKBANK is giving **a flat 20% off at IGP**, the online gift-delivery shop,
with **no minimum order** and a **maximum discount of AED 100**, using code
**RAK20** and a RAKBANK credit or debit card. The offer is valid **till
31 December 2026**.

- **Discount:** 20%, up to AED 100
- **Minimum order:** none
- **Code:** **RAK20**
- **Where:** ae.igp.com or the IGP app
- **Cards:** RAKBANK and RAKBANK Islamic credit and debit cards
- **Expires:** 31 December 2026

The cap is reached on an order of AED 500 (20% of AED 500 is AED 100); below
that the full 20% applies.

The exclusions are wide: gold, diamond, electronics, international shipping
and the international catalogue, gift cards, "and some branded products".
The code cannot be combined with any other IGP discount.
