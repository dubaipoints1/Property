> **Ends sooner than first listed.** FAB's offer page now says **valid until
> 15 November 2026**. It read 31 December 2026 when we first filed this deal.
> Checked 10 November 2026.

FAB is giving **10% off noon.com, capped at AED 50**, once a month on a spend
of **AED 300 or more** paid with a FAB Mastercard credit card and code
**FAB10** at checkout. The offer runs to **15 November 2026**.

- **Discount:** 10%, up to AED 50
- **Minimum spend:** AED 300
- **How often:** once per month
- **Code:** **FAB10**
- **Cards:** FAB Mastercard credit cards. The offer page lists Rewards
  Active, ADNOC Rewards, Z Card, Cashback, Rewards Indulge, Elite, du,
  GEMS World and Travel
- **Expires:** 15 November 2026

The cap is reached on an order of AED 500 (10% of AED 500 is AED 50), so the
sweet spot is a single order between AED 300 and AED 500. With the earlier
end date, one more use in November is what is left: **up to AED 50**.

Two things FAB's page does not settle. It says the discount applies "on top
categories like home essentials, beauty, and groceries" without saying
whether other categories are excluded, and it does not say whether noon's
own sale events are carved out. Check the basket at checkout before relying
on it.

This is the third FAB Mastercard offer on the same pattern, alongside
[Amazon.ae](/deals/fab-amazon-10pct-monthly/) and
[Lulu online](/deals/fab-lulu-20pct-online/), all now ending 15 November. For
the card's own earn on top, see our [FAB Cashback review](/cards/fab-cashback/).
