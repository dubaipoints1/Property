FAB is paying **3% cashback, up to AED 50**, on wellness products at
**BinSina Pharmacy**, online and in-store at all locations, when you pay with
a FAB Mastercard credit or debit card. The offer runs to **31 December 2026**.

- **Cashback:** 3%, up to AED 50
- **Where:** BinSina Pharmacy, online and in-store, "at all locations"
- **What:** wellness products
- **Cards:** FAB Mastercard credit and debit cards, AED transactions only.
  The offer page lists Cashback, GEMS World, Z Card, Elite, Rewards Indulge,
  Travel, du, Islamic Cashback, Emirati Islamic, Rewards Active and World
  Elite
- **Paid:** to the card used, within 15 days
- **Expires:** 31 December 2026

The AED 50 cap is reached at about AED 1,667 of spend (3% of AED 1,667 is
AED 50). FAB's page does not say whether the cap is per transaction, per
month or for the whole offer, so treat AED 50 as the most you can count on.

Two more limits from the page: **Mastercard only**, so a FAB Visa card does
not qualify, and **AED transactions only**. The page also does not define
"wellness products", so do not assume every item in the shop counts.
