HSBC lists a Visa offer at Farfetch: **save up to AED 500 with code
26VISAME**, paying with an HSBC Visa card, **until 31 December 2026**.

- **Saving:** "up to AED 500"
- **Code:** **26VISAME**, applied at checkout
- **Cards:** HSBC Visa cards
- **Expires:** 31 December 2026

That is the whole of what HSBC's page says about Farfetch. It gives no
percentage, no minimum spend and no tier table, so we cannot tell you what
basket earns the full AED 500. The page notes that "discounts vary by brand
and eligibility (new vs returning customers)" and that merchant terms apply.
Enter the code and read the discount line before paying.

HSBC lists it under Visa's name ("Offers are available only for Visa
cardholders"), so HSBC Mastercard holders are not covered. The same HSBC page lists codes for SHEIN, VogaCloset and iHerb, but those
ended on 30 June and 30 September 2026; only the Farfetch code runs to the
end of the year.

on 20 October 2026._
