Emirates Islamic is giving **up to AED 50 off** a Carrefour order of **AED 300
or more** placed on the Carrefour app or website, every **Saturday and
Sunday** from **17 October to 29 November 2026**. Pay with an Emirates Islamic
Visa debit or credit card and enter code **EI50** before you pay.

- **Discount:** up to AED 50 per order
- **Minimum spend:** AED 300, on the Carrefour app or website
- **How often:** once per card per weekend (Saturday and Sunday)
- **Code:** **EI50**
- **Cards:** Emirates Islamic **Visa** debit or credit cards
- **Runs:** 17 October to 29 November 2026, "or until stocks last, whichever
  is earlier"

Seven weekends fall inside the window. The first (17–18 October) has already
passed, so a household that orders on each of the remaining six is looking
at up to AED 50 × 6 = **AED 300** before the offer ends.

The terms that can trip you up:

- **No Apple Pay.** The page excludes payments made through Apple Pay. Pay
  with the card itself
- **No stacking.** It cannot be combined with vouchers, other coupon codes or
  SHARE points
- **"Up to" AED 50.** The page does not say how the discount is worked out
  below the cap, so a basket just over AED 300 may get less than AED 50.
  Check the total at checkout before you confirm
- **Stock limit.** The campaign can end before 29 November if the allocation
  runs out
- Refunds, reversals and disputed transactions are excluded, and
  wholesalers are not eligible
