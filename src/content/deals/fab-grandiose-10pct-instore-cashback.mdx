FAB is paying **10% cashback on groceries bought in store at Grandiose and 4%
on groceries ordered online**, paid with a FAB Mastercard credit or debit
card. The offer runs to **31 December 2026**.

- **Cashback:** 10% in store, 4% online
- **Where:** all Grandiose locations (FAB lists 13, in Dubai and Abu Dhabi)
  and Grandiose online
- **Cards:** FAB Mastercard credit and debit cards, AED transactions only.
  The offer page lists Cashback, GEMS World, Elite, Rewards Indulge, Travel,
  du and Z Card
- **Paid:** to the card used, within 15 days
- **Expires:** 31 December 2026

The "up to 10%" headline resolves to two fixed rates: 10% applies only at the
till. FAB's page states **no cap and no minimum spend**, which is unusual for
a 10% grocery offer, so check the first statement credit before moving a
whole month's shopping. The cashback comes on top of whatever the card itself
earns.

Grandiose stores are mostly in the newer Dubai communities (Sports City, JVC,
Arjan, Silicon Oasis, Dubai Investment Park, Majan, Victory Heights, Al
Jaddaf) plus Souq Madinat Jumeirah, Souq Al Bahar, B1 Mall and Al Bateen in
Abu Dhabi.

For what the card earns on groceries, see our
[FAB Cashback review](/cards/fab-cashback/).
