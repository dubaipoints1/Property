FAB is paying **1.67% cashback on each monthly salary credit** — capped at
AED 750 a month and AED 9,000 over twelve months — for salaries moved to a
FAB current account during the campaign window, which closes **31 December
2026**. Minimum monthly salary AED 5,000.

- **Reward:** 1.67% of each month's salary, up to AED 750/month, AED 9,000 total
- **Who qualifies:** New FAB customers, or existing ones with no FAB salary
  transfer in the last 12 months
- **The monthly test:** Save 20% of that month's salary (incremental
  month-end balance) *or* spend 20% of it on a new FAB credit card — either
  route qualifies, and no card is needed if you save
- **Lock-in:** None, and no clawback of cashback already paid — genuinely
  rare for a UAE salary-transfer offer
- **Expires:** Campaign entry closes 31 December 2026; cashback then runs up
  to 12 months from your first qualifying salary

The headline "20% cashback" is a twelve-month total, not a rate — the terms
price it at 1.67% per month, and above roughly AED 44,900 in salary the
AED 750 monthly cap means extra salary earns nothing more.

This is the highest-ceiling salary-transfer offer we track — AED 9,000
against DIB's AED 3,000 and Mashreq's AED 2,500. Full band table, clawback
terms and the fine print are on our
[salary-transfer tracker entry](/salary-transfer/fab/), verified against
FAB's own T&C document, which takes precedence over the marketing page and
differs from it in two of the published example rows.
