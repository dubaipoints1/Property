Citi is giving **15% off all products on fnp.ae**, the flowers-and-gifts shop,
with code **CITI15** and a Citi credit or debit card. The offer is valid
**until 31 March 2027**.

- **Discount:** 15% on all products
- **Code:** **CITI15**, entered at checkout on fnp.ae
- **Cards:** Citibank credit and debit cards
- **Public holidays:** included, per the page
- **Expires:** 31 March 2027

The page sets no cap and no minimum order: AED 30 off a AED 200 order, and so
on up. It cannot be combined with any other fnp promotion, so check whether a
sale price already beats it before entering the code.

That the offer runs through public holidays is the useful detail. Gift and
flower offers often black out exactly the days people buy flowers. This one
says it does not.
