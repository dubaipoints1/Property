Citi is giving **15% off at Dubai Parks and Resorts** when you pay with a
Citi credit or debit card. The offer is valid **until 30 June 2027**, which
covers this winter's park season and the next spring.

- **Discount:** 15%
- **Tickets:** up to 8 per transaction; each valid for a single admission on
  the selected day
- **Public holidays:** excluded
- **Code:** none shown on the page
- **Cards:** Citi credit and debit cards
- **Expires:** 30 June 2027

On a family day out the 15% adds up: AED 150 off AED 1,000 of tickets. The
page does not say which parks within the resort are covered, or whether the
discount is taken online or at the gate. Ask before you buy.

The tickets are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged, and the offer cannot
be combined with any other promotion.
