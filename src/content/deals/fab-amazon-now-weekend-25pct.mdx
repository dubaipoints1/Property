FAB is giving **25% off Amazon now, capped at AED 60**, on a spend of **AED
50 or more** every **Saturday and Sunday**, paid with a FAB Mastercard credit
card. The offer runs to **31 December 2026**.

- **Discount:** 25%, up to AED 60
- **Minimum spend:** AED 50
- **When:** every Saturday and Sunday
- **Cards:** FAB Mastercard credit cards. The offer page lists Rewards
  Active, ADNOC Rewards, Z Card, Cashback, Rewards Indulge, Elite, du, GEMS
  World and Travel
- **Expires:** 31 December 2026

The cap is reached on a basket of AED 240 (25% of AED 240 is AED 60). Below
that, the discount is a straight quarter off; above it, every extra dirham is
full price.

What FAB's page does not say: how many times a weekend the discount can be
used, whether a code is needed, and whether any categories are excluded. It
links to terms rather than printing them, so check the discount is applied at
checkout before you pay.

Amazon now is a separate offer from FAB's
[10% monthly Amazon.ae discount](/deals/fab-amazon-10pct-monthly/), which
also runs to 31 December. For the card's own earn on top, see our
[FAB Cashback review](/cards/fab-cashback/).
