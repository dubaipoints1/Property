Emirates NBD is giving **10% to 20% off flights and hotel bookings on the
Trip.com app**, depending on which Emirates NBD Mastercard you pay with. Each
card tier has its own code, its own cap and its own yearly limit. The offer
is valid **until 31 March 2027** for travel until 31 March 2028.

| Card | Discount | Cap | Minimum booking | Code | Uses |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Voyager World Elite Mastercard | 20% | AED 150 | AED 750 | MCTRIP20 | 5 times a year |
| Voyager World Mastercard | 15% | AED 90 | AED 600 | MCTRIP15 | 4 times a year |
| Mastercard Platinum | 10% | AED 60 | AED 600 | MCTRIP10 | 3 times a year |

- **Where:** the Trip.com app, flights and hotels
- **How:** enter the code for your card at checkout and pay with that card
- **Booking deadline:** 31 March 2027
- **Travel by:** 31 March 2028

The caps are set so that each one is reached exactly at the minimum booking:
20% of AED 750 is AED 150, 15% of AED 600 is AED 90 and 10% of AED 600 is
AED 60. In practice that makes the offer a fixed AED 150, AED 90 or AED 60 off
any qualifying booking, however large. Used to its yearly limit, that is
AED 150 × 5 = **AED 750** on the World Elite card, AED 90 × 4 = AED 360 on
Voyager World and AED 60 × 3 = AED 180 on Platinum.

The landing page links to a separate
[terms document (PDF)](https://cdn.emiratesnbd.com/en/assets/file/mastercard-trip-jul15-tnc.pdf)
that we have not read, so exclusions such as fare classes or hotel types are
not covered here. The page does not say whether "a year" means a calendar
year or a rolling twelve months.
