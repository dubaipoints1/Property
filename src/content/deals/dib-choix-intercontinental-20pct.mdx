DIB is giving **20% off à la carte and afternoon tea at Choix**, the patisserie
at InterContinental Dubai Festival City, plus **20% off cakes bought to take
away**, when you pay with a DIB Covered Card. The offer is valid **till
31 December 2026**.

- **Discount:** 20% on à la carte and afternoon tea; 20% on takeaway cakes
- **Party size:** up to 5 guests, including the cardholder
- **Booking:** reservation required on +971 4 701 1127/28 or
  reservation.dfc@ihg.com, quoting the DIB offer
- **Code:** none; mention the offer when booking
- **Cards:** DIB Covered Cards
- **Expires:** 31 December 2026

The page sets no cap, so the 20% applies to the whole eligible bill. Two
lines in the terms sit awkwardly together: the headline includes cakes "for
take away", but a later term says the offer "is applicable on dine-in only".
If the takeaway cake is the reason you are going, confirm it with the hotel
when you book.

Excluded: public holidays and special occasions (Christmas Eve and Day, New
Year's Eve and Day, both Eids), tobacco and retail items, and use alongside
any other promotion.
