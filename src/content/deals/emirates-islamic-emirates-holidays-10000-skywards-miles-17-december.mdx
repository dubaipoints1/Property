Emirates Islamic is giving **10,000 bonus Skywards Miles** on an Emirates
Holidays package of **AED 20,000 or more**, booked online and paid with an
Emirates Islamic Covered (credit) card or debit card. Book **by 17 December
2026** for travel **by 31 March 2027**.

- **Bonus:** 10,000 Skywards Miles per qualifying booking
- **Minimum:** a package (flights and accommodation) from AED 20,000, on any
  fare brand
- **Cards:** Emirates Islamic Covered or Visa debit cards
- **Booking window:** 17 June to 17 December 2026
- **Travel window:** 1 July 2026 to 31 March 2027
- **Credited to:** the Skywards account of the lead traveller, who must be on
  the trip

What it is worth: at our [Skywards valuation](/airlines/skywards/) of 2 fils
a Mile, 10,000 Miles is about **AED 200**, or **1%** of a AED 20,000 package.
That is a modest top-up rather than a reason to book. Compare the package
price with booking the flights and hotel separately first.

The terms that matter:

- **No Apple Pay or other wallets.** Pay with the card itself
- **Online bookings only**
- **No stacking.** It cannot be combined with other Emirates Holidays
  promotions, gift-certificate redemptions or website discounts
- Emirates can add or withdraw packages without notice

The page does not say when the Miles are credited.
