DIB is giving **5% to 20% off on the Dr. Nutrition app**, depending on the
product line, with promo code **DIB15** and a DIB Covered Card. The offer is
valid **till 31 August 2027**.

- **15% off** all DNP packages
- **20% off** tablets from Laperva and Wow Woman
- **15% off** slimming products, snacks and beauty products from Laperva and
  Wow Woman
- **10% off** sports nutrition from Laperva and Wow Woman
- **10% off** products from Optitect, Wowtien, BB, Bio Protection and Applied
  Nutrition
- **5% off** every other brand
- **Code:** **DIB15**
- **Where:** the Dr. Nutrition app only. Not the website, not in store
- **Cards:** DIB Covered Cards (DIB's term for its Shariah-structured credit
  cards)
- **Expires:** 31 August 2027

Despite the code's name, 15% is not the general rate: most brands get 5%, and
the higher tiers are tied to Dr. Nutrition's own lines and a handful of named
brands. The page sets no cap and no minimum spend. It cannot be combined with
any other Dr. Nutrition discount.

For DIB's card line-up, see our [DIB Consumer Cashback review](/cards/dib-consumer-cashback/).
