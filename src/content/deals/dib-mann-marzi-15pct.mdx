DIB is giving **15% off the total bill at Mann Marzi Restaurant** when you pay
with a DIB Covered Card or a Johara Debit Card. There is no code and no
minimum spend on the page.

- **Discount:** 15% on the total bill
- **Code:** none
- **Cards:** DIB Covered Cards and Johara Debit Cards
- **Expires:** 30 November 2026 (see below)

DIB's own pages disagree on the end date. The tile on the
[card-offers index](https://www.dib.ae/offers/card-offers) reads "Valid till
30 November 2026"; the offer's detail page says "Offer is valid till
31/12/2026". We list the earlier date. If you are planning a December meal
around it, check with the restaurant first.

Excluded: public holidays and special occasions (Christmas Eve and Day, New
Year's Eve and Day, both Eids), and use alongside any other discount or
promotion.

and the DIB card-offers index on 20 October 2026._
