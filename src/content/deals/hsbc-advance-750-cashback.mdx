HSBC is paying **AED 750 cashback** for opening a new Advance Account in the
HSBC UAE app by **31 December 2026**. The app must be downloaded and
activated within 30 days of account opening to qualify.

- **Bonus:** AED 750 cashback
- **How:** Open the Advance Account in the HSBC UAE app — in-app opening is
  the qualifying route, and it is English-only (branch opening is available
  but the offer copy is specific to the app)
- **Advance eligibility:** Transfer a monthly salary of at least AED 10,000,
  *or* hold an average monthly balance of AED 100,000 in deposits or
  investments
- **Expires:** 31 December 2026

Worth knowing before you count the AED 750: HSBC's salary definition is
strict — the credit must arrive monthly with a "Salary" (or equivalent)
transaction description, and occasional separate payments do not count. If
you miss the eligibility criteria HSBC reserves the right to charge a
service fee before converting the account, so this is a deal for people who
clear the AED 10,000 salary bar comfortably, not one to stretch into.

Earning at least AED 40,000 a month? The [Premier welcome offer](https://www.hsbc.ae/premier/rewards-and-offers/)
pays AED 2,000 on the salary-transfer route on the same end date — see our
[salary-transfer tracker](/salary-transfer/) for how the UAE's
salary-linked offers compare.
