HSBC is offering **AED 750 cashback** to eligible new-to-bank customers who
open a new Advance Account during the offer period, which ends on
**31 December 2026**. HSBC's product page presents the UAE app as the opening
route; the offer terms also require the app to be downloaded and activated
within 30 days of account opening.

- **Bonus:** AED 750 cashback
- **Who:** New-to-HSBC customers opening an individual Advance Account; HSBC
  staff, existing customers and joint-account applicants are excluded by the
  offer terms
- **Salary route:** Transfer at least two eligible salaries of AED 10,000 to
  AED 39,999 within the first three months of opening the account
- **Balance route:** Maintain an average monthly balance of at least
  AED 100,000 in deposits or investments for four consecutive months
- **App condition:** Download and activate the HSBC UAE app within 30 days of
  opening the account
- **Payment timing:** HSBC says cashback is credited within 90 calendar days
  after the applicable salary or balance conditions are met
- **Expires:** 31 December 2026

Worth knowing before you count the AED 750: HSBC's salary definition is
strict — the credit must arrive monthly with a "Salary" (or equivalent)
transaction description, and occasional separate payments do not count.
Closing the account within 180 days of the first deposit or salary credit,
interrupting the required salary transfers within that period, or failing the
balance or app conditions may make the cashback ineligible or repayable.

Earning at least AED 40,000 a month? The [Premier welcome offer](https://www.hsbc.ae/premier/rewards-and-offers/)
pays AED 2,000 on the salary-transfer route on the same end date — see our
[salary-transfer tracker](/salary-transfer/) for how the UAE's
salary-linked offers compare.
