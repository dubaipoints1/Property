ADIB is giving **20% off Lulu online orders every Thursday**, capped at
**AED 50 per transaction**, on a spend of **AED 200 or more** paid with an
ADIB Visa Covered Card. The offer runs to **31 December 2026**.

- **Discount:** 20%, up to AED 50 per transaction
- **Minimum spend:** AED 200 per transaction
- **When:** Thursdays only, on eligible online purchases
- **How often:** one transaction per card every Thursday
- **Cards:** ADIB **Visa** Covered Cards. The full eligible list sits in the
  terms linked from ADIB's offer page
- **Expires:** 31 December 2026

The cap is reached on an order of AED 250 (20% of AED 250 is AED 50), so the
useful range is a Thursday order between AED 200 and AED 250. There are 12
Thursdays from 15 October to 31 December inclusive, so a household that
shops every week is looking at up to AED 50 × 12 = **AED 600** before the
offer ends.

FAB runs its own [Lulu online offer](/deals/fab-lulu-20pct-online/) on
different terms (AED 300 minimum, AED 75 cap, Mastercard only). If you hold
cards from both banks, the two are separate offers on separate cards; neither
bank's page says anything about using them in the same week.

For ADIB's card line-up, see our [ADIB Cashback Visa review](/cards/adib-cashback-visa/).
We have not confirmed from the terms whether that card is on the eligible list.
