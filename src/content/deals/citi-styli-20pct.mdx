Citi is giving **20% off full-price items, 10% off sale items and 5% off home
and beauty** at Styli, the online fashion shop, when you pay with a Citi
credit or debit card on the UAE site. The offer is valid **until 31 December
2026**.

- **Full-price items:** 20% off
- **Sale items:** 10% off
- **Home and beauty:** 5% off
- **How often:** can be used multiple times by the same customer
- **Code:** none shown on the page
- **Cards:** Citi credit and debit cards
- **Expires:** 31 December 2026

No cap or minimum is stated. The catch is the payment method: the page
excludes **Apple Pay, mada Pay, Samsung Pay, Google Pay and any digital
wallet**. Enter the card number itself at checkout. The privilege cannot be
combined with any other promo code or with gift cards, and applies on the UAE
site only.
