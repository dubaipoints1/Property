Emirates NBD is giving **30% off standard one-day, one-park tickets** to
Ferrari World, Warner Bros. World, SeaWorld and Yas Waterworld on Yas Island,
Abu Dhabi, when the tickets are bought online and paid with an Emirates NBD
credit or debit card. The offer runs to **21 December 2026**.

- **Discount:** 30% off the standard one-day, one-park gate rate
- **Parks:** Ferrari World, Warner Bros. World, SeaWorld Yas Island, Yas
  Waterworld. The offer page also lists **20% off at CLYMB**
- **How:** online only, through each park's Emirates NBD offer page (linked
  from the bank's page)
- **Limit:** up to four discounted tickets per online transaction
- **Cards:** a primary Emirates NBD credit or debit card
- **Expires:** 21 December 2026

The discount is on a single-park day ticket only; multi-park and annual
passes are not covered. Four tickets per transaction covers most families in
one booking.

_Verified against the [Emirates NBD offer page](https://www.emiratesnbd.com/en/promotions/enbd-miral-offer)
and its [terms (PDF)](https://cdn.emiratesnbd.com/en/assets/file/miral-bts-aug24-tnc.pdf)
on 26 October 2026._
