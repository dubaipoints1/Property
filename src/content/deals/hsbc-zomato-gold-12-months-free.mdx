HSBC is giving **a complimentary 12-month Zomato Gold dining membership** to
all HSBC credit and debit cardholders who register their card on the Zomato
app with promo code **HSBCGOLD**. The offer **expires 29 November 2026**.

- **Benefit:** 12 months of Zomato Gold dining membership
- **Code:** **HSBCGOLD**
- **Cards:** all HSBC credit and debit cards
- **Limit:** the code works once per card, per user
- **Expires:** 29 November 2026

HSBC's page describes Zomato Gold as "up to 50% on dining bills, including
brunches, business lunches, buffets, and more at over 4,000 restaurants
across the UAE". It applies to dining bills only, cannot be combined with
other Zomato offers or deals, and exclusion days are at Zomato's discretion.

**It renews automatically.** The terms say plainly: "Your membership will be
auto-renewed after the 12-month membership period." Put a reminder in your
calendar for eleven months from activation if you do not want to pay for the
second year.

The page does not state an AED price for the membership, so we do not put a
value on the free year.
