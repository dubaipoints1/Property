FAB is paying **7% cashback, up to AED 500, on automotive services at
800CarGuru**, paid in store with a FAB Mastercard credit or debit card. The
offer runs to **31 December 2026**.

- **Cashback:** 7%, up to AED 500
- **Where:** in store only, at 800CarGuru's two Dubai branches, Al Quoz 1 and
  Al Quoz 4
- **Cards:** FAB Mastercard credit and debit cards, AED transactions only.
  The offer page lists Cashback, GEMS World, Z Card, Elite, Rewards Indulge,
  Travel, du, Islamic Cashback, Emirati Islamic, Rewards Active and World
  Elite
- **Paid:** to the card used, within 15 days
- **Expires:** 31 December 2026

The AED 500 cap is only reached on about AED 7,143 of work (7% of AED 7,143
is AED 500), so for a routine service the full 7% applies. FAB's page does not
say whether the cap is per transaction or for the whole offer; treat AED 500
as the most you can count on.

on 26 October 2026._
