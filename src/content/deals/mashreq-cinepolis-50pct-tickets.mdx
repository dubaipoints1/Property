Two Mashreq credit cards carry a **Cinepolis offer of 50% off up to eight
movie tickets a month**, valid **until 31 December 2026**. The two product
pages describe it slightly differently.

- **Mashreq Cashback Credit Card:** "50% off on Deluxe-Plus seats, up to 8
  tickets per month. Valid until 31 Dec 2026."
- **Mashreq Platinum Plus Credit Card:** "Get 8 movie tickets each billing
  month at 50% off until 31 December 2026."
- **Code:** none shown on either page
- **Expires:** 31 December 2026

The Cashback page limits the discount to Deluxe-Plus seats; the Platinum
Plus page names no seat type and counts by billing month rather than calendar
month. Both link to terms we have not read, so treat the Cashback wording
(Deluxe-Plus only) as the safe assumption for either card.

Mashreq does not quote a ticket price, so there is no AED figure to put on
this. What the pages guarantee is half off, up to eight tickets a month, for
the rest of 2026.

This is separate from the standing 50% off four tickets a month at VOX, Reel
and Novo that the Platinum Plus page also lists. That one carries no end date,
so we do not list it as a deal.

For the card itself, see our [Mashreq Cashback review](/cards/mashreq-cashback/).
