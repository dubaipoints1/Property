Citi is giving **an instant 10% off plus 10% back in the MakeMyTrip wallet**
on hotel and flight bookings paid with a Citi credit or debit card. The offer
is valid **until 30 June 2027**.

- **Hotels:** instant 10% off, up to AED 200, plus 10% cashback in the MMT
  Wallet, up to AED 200, on select hotel bookings of at least **AED 300**
- **Flights:** instant 10% off, up to AED 50, plus 10% cashback in the MMT
  Wallet, up to AED 50; no minimum transaction
- **How often:** once per user per quarter
- **Code:** none shown on the page
- **Cards:** Citi credit and debit cards
- **Expires:** 30 June 2027

Both halves cap together. On a hotel, the AED 200 instant discount is reached
at a AED 2,000 booking, and so is the AED 200 wallet credit: up to
**AED 400 back** on one stay. On a flight, both caps are reached at AED 500,
for up to AED 100.

Read the wallet half carefully. The cashback lands in the MakeMyTrip wallet,
not on your card, and the page says it is valid for **60 days**. It is only
worth its face value if you book with MakeMyTrip again inside two months.

The hotel discount is limited to "select hotel bookings", which the page does
not list. It cannot be combined with any other MakeMyTrip offer.

For Citi's card line-up, see our [Citi Cashback review](/cards/citi-cashback/).
