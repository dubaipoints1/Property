Emirates NBD is giving **5% back on Urban Company home services** (cleaning,
repairs, beauty, grooming) booked through the noon app and paid with the
**Emirates NBD noon One Visa credit card**. The offer runs to **31 December
2026**.

- **Back:** 5%, paid as **credits, not cash**: 4% as Urban Company credit and
  1% as noon credit
- **Code:** **ENBD** for one-off services; **NOONENBD** for monthly
  subscription services
- **Card:** Emirates NBD noon One Visa credit card only
- **Where:** Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah, on the iOS or Android app only
  (not the website)
- **Cap:** the noon-credit part is capped at AED 2,000 a month per card
- **Credits:** Urban Company credit lands within 24 hours of the service and
  must be used within 60 days
- **Expires:** 31 December 2026

Read this as a 4% voucher for your next Urban Company booking plus 1% noon
credit, not as 5% cashback. If you book a cleaner every week, the Urban
Company credit is easy to use up; for an occasional booking, 60 days may not
be long enough.

The terms say the offer is for cardholders who receive the bank's offer by
email, SMS or app message. The bank's own page presents it to every noon One
cardholder, so try the code, but check the credit after your first booking.
