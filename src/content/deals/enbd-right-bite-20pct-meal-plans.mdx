Emirates NBD is giving **20% off selected Right Bite meal plans** with promo
code **ENBD20**, ordered through the Right Bite app or website and paid with
an Emirates NBD card. The offer is valid **till 31 December 2026**.

- **Discount:** 20%
- **Code:** **ENBD20**
- **What counts:** 20, 40 and 60-day meal plans
- **What does not:** lunch-only plans, and the Plus and Lite plans
- **Cards:** "an Emirates NBD card"; the page names no narrower list
- **Expires:** 31 December 2026

No cap is stated, which matters on a product priced by the plan: 20% comes
off the whole plan, not a fixed voucher amount. The offer cannot be combined
with any other Right Bite promotion or discount, so a plan already on sale
may be cheaper without the code.

The page does not say whether the discount repeats on renewal or applies to
the first purchase only. Ask Right Bite before committing to a 60-day plan on
that assumption.
