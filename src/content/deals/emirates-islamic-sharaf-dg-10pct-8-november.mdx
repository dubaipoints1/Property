Emirates Islamic is giving **10% off, up to AED 300**, on a Sharaf DG order of
**AED 1,000 or more** on eligible home appliances and electronics, paid with
an Emirates Islamic Visa debit or credit card. The offer runs **from 9
October to 8 November 2026**, so there are days left, not weeks.

- **Discount:** 10% off, capped at AED 300 per order, applied automatically
  at checkout
- **Minimum spend:** AED 1,000 on eligible home appliances and electronics
- **Eligible:** includes TVs, soundbars, speakers, headphones and sound
  systems
- **How often:** once per card during the campaign
- **Cards:** Emirates Islamic **Visa** debit or credit cards
- **Ends:** 8 November 2026

The cap is reached at AED 3,000. Above that, the discount stays at AED 300,
so on a AED 6,000 television it is 5%.

The page does not say whether the offer applies in store, online or both, or
list excluded brands. Check that the discount shows at checkout before you
pay.
