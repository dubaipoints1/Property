Standard Chartered is giving Journey Credit Card holders **10% cashback,
capped at AED 100 per statement**, on purchases at duty-free stores in all UAE
airports. The offer runs **from 1 March 2025 to 31 January 2027**.

- **Cashback:** 10%, up to AED 100 per statement
- **Where:** duty-free stores at all UAE airports (merchant category code
  5309)
- **Condition:** at least one airline transaction on the card in the 180
  calendar days before the duty-free purchase, made directly with an airline
  or travel agency (MCC 3000, 3301 and 4722)
- **How it pays:** credited automatically as a cashback credit on the Journey
  statement
- **Cards:** Standard Chartered Journey Credit Card; primary and
  supplementary spend count together
- **Expires:** 31 January 2027

The cap is reached on AED 1,000 of duty-free spend in a statement period
(10% of AED 1,000 is AED 100). There is no code and no registration step on
the page; the qualifying condition is the airline ticket.

That condition is the catch. The ticket has to be bought **on the Journey
card**, directly from an airline or a travel agency, within the 180 days
before you shop. A ticket bought on another card does not unlock it.
