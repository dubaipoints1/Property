FAB is paying **4% cashback on your first Zed ride booking and 2% on every
booking after it**, made online with a FAB Mastercard credit or debit card.
The offer runs to **31 December 2026**.

- **Cashback:** 4% on the first booking, 2% on subsequent bookings
- **Where:** Zed, online bookings only
- **Cards:** FAB Mastercard credit and debit cards, AED transactions only.
  The offer page lists Cashback, GEMS World, Z Card, Elite, Rewards Indulge,
  Travel and du
- **Paid:** to the card used, within 15 days
- **Expires:** 31 December 2026

Read the headline carefully: the "up to 4%" is the first ride only. Every
ride after that earns 2%. FAB's page states no cap and no minimum fare.

For what the card itself earns on top, see our
[FAB Cashback review](/cards/fab-cashback/).
