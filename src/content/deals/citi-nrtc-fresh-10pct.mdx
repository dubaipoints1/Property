Citi is giving **10% off NRTC Fresh online orders** of **AED 80 or more**
paid with a Citi credit or debit card, through NRTC's app or website. The
offer is valid **until 31 March 2027**.

- **Discount:** 10%
- **Minimum order:** AED 80
- **Cap:** none, per the page
- **How often:** no limit on the number of transactions per card per month
- **Where:** NRTC's app or website (nrtcfresh.com)
- **Code:** none shown on the page
- **Cards:** Citi credit and debit cards
- **Expires:** 31 March 2027

This is the rare grocery offer with no cap and no monthly limit. The page
says so in terms: "There is no cap on the discount amount or the number of
transactions per covered card per month." A household that moves its weekly
fruit and vegetable shop to NRTC gets 10% off every order of AED 80 or more:
AED 40 a month on AED 400 of orders.

It does not apply to items already on promotion, or alongside any other NRTC
offer.
