RAKBANK is giving **25% off talabat orders, capped at AED 30**, on an order
of **AED 100 or more** paid with a RAKBANK credit card and code **RAK30**. The
offer can be used twice a month and runs **from 15 April to 31 December 2026**.

- **Discount:** 25%, up to AED 30
- **Minimum order:** AED 100
- **How often:** 2 transactions per customer per month
- **Code:** **RAK30**
- **Cards:** RAKBANK credit cards
- **Validity:** 15 April 2026 to 31 December 2026

The cap is reached on an order of AED 120 (25% of AED 120 is AED 30), so the
best value is an order between AED 100 and AED 120. Two capped orders a month
is **up to AED 60 a month**; across November and December that is AED 120,
plus whatever is left of October.
