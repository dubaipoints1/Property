Emirates Islamic is giving **20% off Air Arabia Ultimate fares and 10% off
Value fares** booked on the Air Arabia website with promo code **EIB** and
paid with an Emirates Islamic Visa credit card. Bookings must be made
**between 16 October and 15 November 2026**.

- **Ultimate fare:** 20% off, up to AED 150 per passenger one-way or AED 300
  per passenger on a round trip
- **Value fare:** 10% off, up to AED 40 per passenger one-way or AED 80 per
  passenger on a round trip
- **Code:** **EIB**
- **Routes:** flights in the Air Arabia network from Sharjah (SHJ), Ras Al
  Khaimah (RKT) or Abu Dhabi (AUH), one-way or return
- **Cards:** Emirates Islamic **Visa credit** cards only
- **Book by:** 15 November 2026

On an Ultimate fare the one-way cap is reached at AED 750 of fare (20% of
AED 750 is AED 150); on a Value fare, at AED 400.

The terms that matter:

- **Air Arabia website only.** New bookings made on the website during the
  promotion period
- **Limited seats.** Each flight has a limited allocation and blackout dates
  may apply
- **Refunds are on the discounted price.** Air Arabia's normal change and
  refund rules apply, and only the amount paid after the discount is refunded
- **No stacking.** It cannot be combined with other promotions, vouchers or
  loyalty programmes
- Air Arabia can withdraw or change the promotion without notice

The page does not give a travel-by date. DIB runs a near-identical Air Arabia
offer with its own code to the same date; see the
[DIB deal](/deals/dib-air-arabia-20pct-ultimate/).
