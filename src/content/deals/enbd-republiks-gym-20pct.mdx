Emirates NBD is giving **20% off club membership at StudioRepublik and
FitRepublik** in Dubai when you pay with an eligible Emirates NBD credit
card. The offer runs to **10 July 2027**.

- **Discount:** 20% on club membership
- **Where:** FitRepublik, Dubai Sports City, and StudioRepublik, Umm Al Sheif
- **Cards:** the terms list Visa Infinite, Skywards Visa Infinite, Marriott
  Bonvoy World Elite Mastercard, U By Emaar Visa Infinite, Priority Visa
  Infinite, Skywards Signature, U By Emaar Signature, Marriott Bonvoy World
  and dnata World. Other Emirates NBD cards are not on the list
- **How:** present the card at the time of payment and verify it as the gym
  asks; without verification the standard rate applies
- **Expires:** 10 July 2027

The bank's page headline says "Emirates NBD Credit Card", but the terms
narrow it to the premium cards above. Check that yours is on the list before
you sign up. The discount cannot be combined with the gym's own promotions.
