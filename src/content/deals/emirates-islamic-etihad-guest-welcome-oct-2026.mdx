Emirates Islamic is offering **up to 200,000 bonus Etihad Guest Miles** to
new holders of its Etihad Guest Covered Cards who apply by **31 October 2026**.
The card must be issued before **5 November 2026**. The bonus comes in two
parts: one tranche when the annual fee is paid, a second for hitting a spend
threshold in the first three months.

| Card | Annual fee (AED) | Miles on fee payment | Spend in first 3 months (AED) | Miles for the spend | Total bonus | Minimum salary (AED) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Etihad Guest Premium | 1,500 | 80,000 | 60,000 | 120,000 | **200,000** | 20,000 |
| Etihad Guest Saqer | 899 | 50,000 | 45,000 | 50,000 | **100,000** | 12,000 |
| Etihad Guest Ameera | 699 | 30,000 | 30,000 | 50,000 | **80,000** | 12,000 |
| Etihad Guest Platinum | 199 | 10,000 | 12,000 | 20,000 | **30,000** | 5,000 |

At our [2-fils cost-basis baseline](/airlines/etihad-guest/) for an Etihad
Guest Mile, the totals are worth roughly AED 4,000 (Premium), AED 2,000
(Saqer), AED 1,600 (Ameera) and AED 600 (Platinum). Net of the first-year fee,
that is about AED 2,500, AED 1,101, AED 901 and AED 401. Redeemed well, Miles
can be worth more than that; the baseline is deliberately conservative.

The spend tranche is the harder half. AED 60,000 in three months on the
Premium card is AED 20,000 a month of retail spend. Emirates Islamic excludes
the annual and joining fees, cash transactions, balance transfers and profit
from the count, and reversed transactions do not count either.

The terms that matter:

- **Not for returning cardholders:** anyone who has held an Emirates Islamic
  Etihad Guest card before is excluded
- **Not on top of other welcome offers:** the campaign Miles are "inclusive
  of any other Welcome bonus" on these cards
- **Timing:** fee-payment Miles arrive within 45 days of paying the annual fee
  and the campaign joining fee; spend Miles within 45 days of hitting the
  threshold. The card must be activated within 30 days of opening
- **Joining fee:** the terms mention a "campaign joining fee" but the offer
  page does not state an amount. Ask before you sign
- **Missed payments:** meeting the spend but missing a minimum payment
  forfeits the bonus

Emirates Islamic's page also lists up to two 60% discount vouchers on Etihad
Guest Miles bookings and a fast track to Gold tier, but says these depend on
card type without saying which card gets which.

_Verified against the [Emirates Islamic offer page](https://www.emiratesislamic.ae/en/offers/bonus-etihad-miles-offer)
on 13 October 2026. Emirates Islamic says it may change or cancel the offer
without notice._
