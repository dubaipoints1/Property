Citi is giving **buy-one-get-one-free movie tickets and 20% off food and
drink** at Reel Cinemas, but only on its two top cards, **Citi Ultima** and
**Citi Prestige**. The offer is valid **until 31 December 2027**.

- **Tickets:** buy one, get one free
- **Food and drink:** 20% off on a minimum purchase of AED 20, at the counter
- **How:** book online at reelcinemas.com or in the Reel Cinemas app, logged
  in to your Reel account; choose at least two tickets
- **Cards:** Citi Ultima and Citi Prestige credit cards only, primary
  cardholder
- **Expires:** 31 December 2027

The eligible formats are listed: Standard 2D and 3D, Standard Plus 2D and 3D,
Preferred View 2D, Platinum 2D and 3D, MAX4D, Dolby Standard, Dolby Plus,
Dolby Preferred View, and Screen X Standard and Standard Plus.

**Use the primary card.** The page warns that booking with a supplementary
card "may result in full charges being levied". Seats are first come, first
served.

Other Citi cards have their own cinema benefits on Citi's movies page, but
those carry no end date, so we do not list them as deals.

on 20 October 2026._
