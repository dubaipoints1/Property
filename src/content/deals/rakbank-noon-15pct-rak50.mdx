RAKBANK is giving **15% off noon, capped at AED 50**, on an order of
**AED 300 or more** paid with a RAKBANK credit card and code **RAK50**. It is
good for one order a month and runs **from 1 August to 31 December 2026**.

- **Discount:** 15%, up to AED 50
- **Minimum order:** AED 300
- **How often:** 1 transaction per customer per month
- **Code:** **RAK50**
- **Cards:** RAKBANK credit cards
- **Validity:** 1 August 2026 to 31 December 2026

The code name suggests a flat AED 50; it is not. At the AED 300 minimum the
discount is 15% of AED 300 = **AED 45**. The full AED 50 needs an order of
about AED 334 (15% of AED 333.33 is AED 50). Above that, every extra dirham is
at full price. One capped order in each of October, November and December is
AED 50 × 3 = **up to AED 150**.

The landing page's own terms section did not load when we read it, so
category exclusions and the treatment of noon's sale events are not
confirmed here.
