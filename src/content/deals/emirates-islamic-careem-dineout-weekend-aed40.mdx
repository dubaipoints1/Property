Emirates Islamic is giving **AED 40 off** a Careem DineOut bill of **AED 200
or more**, once per weekend, when you pay with an Emirates Islamic Visa
Covered Card. The discount is applied automatically at checkout; there is no
code. The offer runs **until 29 November 2026**.

- **Discount:** AED 40, applied automatically
- **Minimum spend:** AED 200
- **How often:** one transaction per user per weekend (Friday to Sunday)
- **Where:** the "DineOut" section of the Careem app
- **Cards:** Emirates Islamic **Visa** Covered Cards (Emirates Islamic's term
  for its Shariah-structured credit cards)
- **Expires:** 29 November 2026

A flat AED 40 is worth the most on a bill close to the AED 200 minimum: 20% of
AED 200, falling to 10% of AED 400. From the weekend of 23–25 October there
are six weekends left, so a regular user is looking at up to AED 40 × 6 =
**AED 240** before the offer ends.

The terms that matter:

- **No digital wallets.** The page excludes Apple Pay and other wallet
  transactions. Pay with the card itself
- **No stacking** with any other promotion, discount or voucher
- **Weekends only:** Friday to Sunday
- The page also says the offer is "valid on one transaction per vertical",
  without saying which Careem verticals count. For DineOut, the weekly limit
  above is the one that applies
- Careem and Emirates Islamic can change or withdraw the offer at any time

Emirates Islamic runs a separate DineOut offer for its Amazon Mastercard (15%
off up to AED 100 on a bill of at least AED 500, code MC15), but that one ends
on 31 October 2026 and covers a single card, so we do not list it.
