Emirates Islamic is giving new credit-card customers a **noon.com gift card
worth up to AED 1,000**, paid once they reach a spend target in the first 60
days. Apply **by 31 December 2026** and have the card issued **by 31 January
2027**.

| Card tier | Spend within 60 days of issue | noon gift card |
|---|---|---|
| Emarati or any Infinite card | AED 10,000 | **AED 1,000** |
| Any Signature card | AED 7,500 | **AED 750** |
| Any Platinum card | AED 5,000 | **AED 500** |

Each tier works out at 10% of the spend target, paid as a noon voucher rather
than cash.

- **Who qualifies:** new Emirates Islamic credit-card customers who apply
  digitally through the offer page
- **Not eligible:** anyone with an active Emirates Islamic credit card, or who
  closed one in the last six months; business and corporate cards
- **Apply by:** 31 December 2026, card issued by 31 January 2027

The terms that matter:

- **Retail spend only.** The target is a "minimum retail purchase". The page
  does not define what counts as retail, so check the card's Key Facts
  Statement before you rely on a large payment hitting the target
- **Slow payout.** The voucher arrives by SMS "within 90 days after the end
  of the campaign period". The campaign ends on 31 December, so expect it by
  the end of March 2027
- **Short shelf life.** The voucher is valid for 3 months from issue and is
  redeemed at noon.com
- **One card only.** Apply for two and the offer applies to the higher-value
  one. It "is not valid in conjunction with any other promotion", which we
  read as ruling out stacking it with the [Switch Cashback welcome bonus](/deals/emirates-islamic-switch-cashback-welcome-aed500/)

For our review of Emirates Islamic's cashback card, see
