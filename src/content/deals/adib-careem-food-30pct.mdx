ADIB is giving **30% off Careem Food orders**, capped at **AED 50 per
order**, when you pay with an ADIB Visa Covered Card and enter promo code
**ADIB30**. The campaign runs from **5 October 2026 to 5 January 2027**.

- **Discount:** 30%, up to AED 50 per order
- **Minimum spend:** AED 100 per order
- **How often:** two orders a month during the campaign period
- **Code:** **ADIB30**
- **Cards:** ADIB **Visa** Covered Cards. ADIB's page shows the eligible
  cards as an image only; the full list is in the
  [campaign terms (PDF)](https://www.adib.ae/-/media/project/adib/adibsite/docs/offer/2026/oct/careem/careem-food-2026-tc.pdf)
- **Runs:** 5 October 2026 to 5 January 2027

At the cap, the discount is worth up to AED 50 × 2 = **AED 100 a month**.
The cap is reached on an order of about AED 167 (30% of AED 166.67 is
AED 50), so the best value comes from orders between AED 100 and roughly
AED 167. Above that, every extra dirham is at full price.

For ADIB's card line-up, see our [ADIB Cashback Visa review](/cards/adib-cashback-visa/).
We have not confirmed from the terms whether that card is on the eligible list.
