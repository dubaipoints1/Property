RAKBANK is giving **15% off LuLu online orders, capped at AED 50**, on a
spend of **AED 150 or more**, once every Sunday, when you pay with a RAKBANK
credit card on Lulu.com or the Lulu app. The page shows no promo code. The
offer runs **from 26 April to 27 December 2026**.

- **Discount:** 15%, up to AED 50
- **Minimum order:** AED 150
- **How often:** 1 transaction per customer, every Sunday
- **Where:** Lulu.com and the Lulu app
- **Code:** none shown on the page
- **Cards:** RAKBANK credit cards
- **Validity:** 26 April 2026 to 27 December 2026

At the AED 150 minimum the discount is AED 22.50. The cap needs an order of
about AED 334 (15% of AED 333.33 is AED 50). From Sunday 25 October to the
last valid Sunday, 27 December, there are ten Sundays, so a weekly LuLu shop
could save up to AED 50 × 10 = **AED 500** before the offer ends.

The page does not say how the discount is applied without a code, whether
automatically at payment or as a later credit, and its terms section did not
load when we read it. Check the order total on your first Sunday before
relying on it.

Part of the same RAKBANK campaign as [talabat](/deals/rakbank-talabat-25pct-rak30/),
[noon](/deals/rakbank-noon-15pct-rak50/) and
[Careem Food](/deals/rakbank-careem-food-25pct-rak25/).
