RAKBANK is giving **25% off Careem Food and Careem Quik orders, capped at
AED 30**, on an order of **AED 100 or more** paid with a RAKBANK credit card.
Each service has its own code: **RAK25** for Food and **RAKQ25** for Quik. The
offer runs **from 1 May to 31 December 2026**.

- **Discount:** 25%, up to AED 30
- **Minimum order:** AED 100
- **How often:** 2 transactions per customer per month
- **Codes:** **RAK25** (Careem Food), **RAKQ25** (Careem Quik)
- **Cards:** RAKBANK credit cards
- **Validity:** 1 May 2026 to 31 December 2026

As with the talabat leg of the same campaign, the cap is reached at AED 120
(25% of AED 120 is AED 30), so orders between AED 100 and AED 120 get the full
percentage. Two capped orders a month is **up to AED 60 a month**.

The page says "2 transactions per customer per month" once, for both codes.
It does not say whether that is two in total or two on each service. Assume
two in total until RAKBANK's terms say otherwise; the terms section did not
load when we read the page.

Part of the same RAKBANK campaign as [talabat](/deals/rakbank-talabat-25pct-rak30/),
[noon](/deals/rakbank-noon-15pct-rak50/) and
[LuLu online](/deals/rakbank-lulu-sundays-15pct/).
